# IELTS for admission

Half the language question is answered by one number: the score your programme asks for. The other half is that many universities let you skip the certificate altogether. Below are checked requirements at the universities we work with.

## How the exam works

IELTS has four parts: listening, reading, writing and speaking. Each part and the overall result are scored from 0 to 9 in half-band steps. The certificate is valid for **two years** — the same rule applies to TOEFL and PTE, while Cambridge certificates do not expire.

Admission almost always means the **Academic** module, not General Training. Some universities accept both — Politecnico di Milano and NABA in Italy count General as well. If you are choosing what to sit, sit Academic: it is accepted everywhere.

## When you do not need the certificate

- **The university's own English exam.** CIU and NEU in North Cyprus, and several Turkish universities, run their own test on campus or online.
- **An online interview.** Budapest Metropolitan in Hungary admits without a certificate on the strength of an interview.
- **School or a degree taught in English.** Many universities exempt graduates of English-medium schools and programmes. Malaysia also counts Year 11 English at school and the national MUET exam.
- **A preparatory language year.** If your score falls short, universities in Türkiye, North Cyprus and Malaysia enrol you on an English foundation year and move you to year one once you pass it.

## Alternatives to IELTS

TOEFL iBT is accepted almost everywhere, PTE Academic at most universities, the Duolingo English Test less often (IBS in Hungary takes it from 105), Cambridge B2 First and C1 Advanced frequently and without expiry, MUET in Malaysia.

One warning: **online formats are not universally accepted**. Medipol counts neither IELTS Online nor TOEFL Home Edition — only a test centre sitting. Check this before you pay the exam fee.

## Five mistakes that force a retake

1. **Sitting General instead of Academic.** For a university that is usually the wrong module.
2. **Hitting the overall score but not the per-part minimum.** Bocconi asks 6.5 with 6.0 in every part, Middlesex 6.0 with 5.5 — the overall band alone will not carry you.
3. **Sitting online where only a test centre counts.**
4. **An expired certificate.** The two years run to the application date, not to the start of the programme.
5. **Sitting too late.** Results arrive within days, but the university needs time to verify them. Leave a month before the application deadline.

## How to prepare in three months

- Take a mock test at the start: it shows which part drags you down, and the plan follows from there.
- The weak part is usually writing, and what it needs is structure — an introduction, two argued paragraphs, a conclusion — rather than vocabulary.
- Speaking improves only by speaking: 15 minutes out loud a day beats an hour of theory.
- In the last month switch to full timed mock tests: the exam runs for nearly three hours, and stamina decides as much as language.
