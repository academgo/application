# 9 study destinations compared

All prices below come from official university price lists, with the year stated. Universities publish 2027/28 prices closer to intake, so treat these as ranges, not quotes.

## Tuition and living cost

| Destination | Tuition | Living |
|---|---|---|
| Türkiye | from $500 a year at public, from $4,000 at private universities (2025/26) | from $700 a month (Istanbul) |
| UAE | about AED 60,000–80,000 a year at branch campuses (2026/27) | from AED 5,500 a month (Dubai) |
| Malaysia | about RM 35,000–45,000 a year at private universities (2026) | from RM 2,000 a month (Kuala Lumpur) |
| Italy | from €160 a year at public universities, income-based (ISEE), 2026/27 | about €1,100–1,400 a month in Milan |
| Spain | about €1,200–8,200 a year at public universities; private cost more (2026/27) | from €600 a month (IPREM legal minimum: €7,200 a year) |
| Hungary | about €6,000–10,000 a year in English (2025/26–2026/27) | about €800–1,300 a month (Budapest) |
| Georgia | MD in English: $5,900–6,500 at private, $8,000 at public universities (2026/27) | about GEL 1,730 a month without rent (Tbilisi, Numbeo) |
| North Cyprus | $4,450–6,400 a year at EMU (public); €2,800–4,500 at private universities after the standard discount (2026/27) | depends on the university: campus housing is published per year in USD |
| Cyprus | about €9,000–11,820 a year at private universities; medicine €23,000–24,000 (2026/27) | rent €600–1,000 plus €600–900 a month for other costs |

## Language, entry and work

| Destination | Language of study | Entry and status | Work while studying |
|---|---|---|---|
| Türkiye | English and Turkish | student visa, then a student residence permit (ikamet) | only with a work permit (çalışma izni) |
| UAE | English | student visa sponsored by the university | limited, only through approved schemes |
| Malaysia | English | Student Pass through EMGS, approved before you travel | up to 20 hours a week during breaks, with approval |
| Italy | Italian and English | type D visa via Universitaly, then a residence permit | up to 20 hours a week and 1,040 hours a year |
| Spain | Spanish; some programmes in English | student visa, then a TIE card | up to 30 hours a week, no separate permit needed |
| Hungary | English; Hungarian is needed for clinical work | type D visa, then a residence permit | up to 30 hours a week; full time during breaks, max 90 days a year |
| Georgia | English | visa-free entry for many countries, then a student residence permit | depends on your status |
| North Cyprus | English | TRNC student residence permit | check with your university |
| Cyprus | English | entry permit applied for by the university | up to 20 hours a week in term time, 38 during breaks, in permitted sectors |

## Degree recognition — read before you pay

| Destination | What matters |
|---|---|
| Türkiye | YÖK-accredited universities; recognition depends on your home country |
| UAE | at branch campuses the degree is awarded by the UK or Australian home campus |
| Malaysia | MQA accreditation; check recognition in your home country |
| Italy, Spain, Hungary, Cyprus | EU degree |
| Georgia | NCEQE accreditation, WFME-recognised until 11.2028; Georgian is required to practise medicine |
| North Cyprus | the TRNC is recognised only by Türkiye. Degrees are not recognised in Europe directly. The working route: a YÖK-listed programme and legalisation of the degree through Turkish authorities |

Most agencies stay quiet about North Cyprus. We say it plainly: it is the most affordable English-taught start with easy entry — and it carries a recognition limit you should know about before the first payment.
