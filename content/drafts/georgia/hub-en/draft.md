If you plan to study in Georgia in 2027/28, you are most likely looking at an English-taught MD in Tbilisi. Tuition for the six-year medical program is about $5,900–8,000 a year, and international applicants enter without Georgian national exams. Admission runs through three steps: the university's consent, recognition of your school certificate by the national center NCEQE, and an enrollment order from the Ministry of Education.

Georgia suits you if you want an English-taught degree in medicine, dentistry, business or IT at a moderate fee, and you are ready to handle translations, document legalization and a visa. Citizens of Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman can enter without a visa for one year. Applicants from India, Pakistan, Nigeria and Egypt need a D3 study visa.

Georgia is a weaker fit if you plan to practise medicine independently in Georgia without learning Georgian, or if you need a local job to pay for living costs. Indian medical applicants should check NMC rules before applying, not after enrollment. Below you will find costs, requirements, the visa route and the catches, based on official sources checked in September 2026.

<!-- block: textContent -->
Over six years, MD tuition alone adds up to roughly $35,400 (SEU) to $48,000 (TSMU), if prices stay the same. That total excludes housing, food and flights.

Plan for a few official fees on top of tuition. NCEQE charges 40 GEL to recognize a school certificate within one month, or 80–250 GEL for faster processing. The enrollment procedure without national exams costs 30 GEL (one month) or 60 GEL (15 working days). The D3 visa fee is 20 USD, plus the VFS service fee if you apply through VFS. A study residence permit costs 300, 450 or 600 GEL, depending on how fast you need a decision. For a full breakdown by university, see our guide to the [cost of studying in Georgia](/study-in-georgia/cost-of-studying-in-georgia).

## Admission requirements

International applicants do not sit Georgian national exams. Instead, the university gives its consent, NCEQE checks that your school certificate is genuine and matches Georgian standards, and the Minister of Education issues an order that enrolls you. Full details are on our [admission requirements](/study-in-georgia/admission-requirements) page.

<!-- block: processBlock title="Admission to a Georgian university, step by step" -->
1. Apply to the university. You send a passport copy, your school certificate, proof of English and an application form. Most universities then hold an online interview; UG adds online English and subject tests, and Alte holds a 5-minute interview on English and motivation.
2. Translate and legalize your documents. Every foreign-language document needs a notarized translation into Georgian, and your school documents need an apostille or consular legalization, depending on your country.
3. Receive the university's acceptance or consent letter. NCEQE will not process your file without it.
4. Get your school certificate recognized by NCEQE. The standard fee is 40 GEL with a decision within one month; faster options cost 80–250 GEL.
5. Wait for the Ministry of Education order. SEU says this usually takes 4–5 weeks after the NCEQE decision; Alte says results are normally available within one month.
6. Apply for your visa and travel. Applicants from India, Pakistan, Nigeria and Egypt apply for a D3 visa; GCC citizens enter visa-free and apply for a residence permit in Georgia.

<!-- block: textContent -->
Start early. Recognition alone can take up to a month, and the ministry order and a D3 visa come after it. As a rough guide, begin the procedure about 2–3 months before the semester starts, and earlier if your documents need consular legalization.

English requirements differ by university. SEU and TSMU ask for at least B1, while UG, Alte and TSU (for its MD) ask for B2. You do not always need IELTS: UG and Alte offer an internal English test, and Alte also accepts proof of at least one year of study in English. Programs taught in English or Russian do not require Georgian; for Georgian-taught programs, the university may require a preparatory year.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Document legalization by country" -->
- **India:** all education documents need an MEA apostille, as the Georgian embassy's D3 checklist requires. MD applicants also need NEET results.
- **Pakistan:** Pakistan joined the Apostille Convention on 9 March 2023, so documents are apostilled. You need a D3 visa.
- **Nigeria and Egypt:** neither country is part of the Apostille Convention. Documents usually go through consular legalization: the foreign ministry of the issuing country, then a Georgian consulate.
- **Saudi Arabia:** apostille (member since 7 December 2022); citizens enter visa-free for one year.
- **UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman:** not part of the Apostille Convention, so documents need consular legalization; citizens enter visa-free for one year.
- **Indian and Pakistani residents of the UAE:** the Georgian embassy in the UAE also asks for an online student entry permission (registration.gov.ge) and a UAE residence permit or multiple-entry visa valid 90 days beyond the requested visa.

## Student visa and documents

Students from India, Pakistan, Nigeria and Egypt need a D3 visa, a long-term category for study. You apply online at geoconsul.gov.ge; in India, documents are submitted through VFS Global. The consular fee is 20 USD, plus the VFS service fee.

The Indian D3 checklist includes the Ministry of Education enrollment order, the university's admission order, proof of accommodation, proof of funds, insurance for the full visa period and, for MBBS/MD, NEET. The checklists do not set a fixed financial amount. You show your own funds or a sponsor package: a notarized sponsor affidavit, the sponsor's ID, a 3-month bank statement and a salary certificate, or proof of a grant or scholarship.

The D3 visa is issued for 90 days. Once in Georgia, you apply for a study residence permit through the Public Service Development Agency (SDA): at a Public Service Hall, an SDA office or online. Apply no later than 40 calendar days before your legal stay ends. You need funds of at least two monthly subsistence minimums for each month of the permit. This amount changes monthly, so we calculate it for you at the time of application.

The e-Visa portal issues short-stay category C visas only, such as tourism or business. It is not a route to study. GCC citizens skip the D3 step: they enter visa-free for one year and apply for the residence permit inside Georgia. Step-by-step details are in our [Georgia student visa guide](/study-in-georgia/georgia-student-visa).

## What to know before you apply

AcademGo tells you what many agencies leave out. None of these points is a reason to rule Georgia out, but each one changes how you should plan.

### Independent medical practice in Georgia requires Georgian

Georgian law states that knowledge of Georgian is mandatory for independent medical practice in Georgia. Patients in clinics speak Georgian, which is why universities include Georgian courses in the MD curriculum, even though teaching is in English. Residency programs at TSMU are offered only in Georgian. If you want to work as a doctor in Georgia, treat Georgian as a core subject from year one. If you plan to return home, focus on your home-country licensing exam.

### Graduates start as junior doctors

After the MD, a graduate receives junior doctor status without extra exams. A junior doctor may perform a doctor's duties but works under the responsibility of an independent doctor and has no right to independent practice, except in emergencies. The path to a full licence in Georgia is a post-diploma exam, residency and a unified state certification exam. Nationality does not block this path, but the language requirement does.

### WFME recognition of Georgia's accreditor runs to November 2028

WFME recognizes NCEQE, the agency that accredits Georgian medical programs, until November 2028. WFME recognizes agencies, not universities, so be careful with claims like "WFME-approved university". A student starting in 2027 graduates around 2033, after the current recognition period ends. Renewal is likely to be a matter for the future, and no one can guarantee it. This matters most if you plan to take the USMLE or register in a country that relies on WFME recognition.

### Check home-country licensing rules first

For India, the NMC Foreign Medical Graduate Licentiate Regulations 2021 (FMGL 2021) apply. They require at least 54 months of study plus 12 months of internship at the same university, teaching in English, the whole course in one country, a licence in the country of study at par with its citizens, a further 12-month internship in India and NExT. In the Georgian model, the internship is part of the 6 years, not an extra year after graduation. Georgia's Ministry of Education states that its MD complies with NMC rules, but that is Georgia's position, not an NMC decision. An NMC advisory from November 2024, as reported, warns that any deviation in duration, language, program, clinical training or internship may lead to disqualification from registration in India. NEET is mandatory for Indian citizens who plan to study medicine abroad.

For Pakistan, PM&DC requires an MDCAT score of at least 50% and a university listed in the ECFMG/WDOMS lists. Nigerian and Egyptian applicants should confirm how their regulator treats a Georgian MD before applying. We check each program against your home rules before you pay any fee.

### No fixed amount for visa funds

There is no official fixed sum for the D3 visa. Any website quoting an exact figure is not quoting the embassy checklist. What matters is a clean, consistent sponsor package, and we help you assemble and check it.

### Working while studying needs a separate permit

Since 1 March 2026, according to KPMG and local law firms, a foreigner without permanent residence needs a separate work authorization to work for a Georgian employer. A study residence permit does not give that right by itself. Plan your budget without counting on earnings in Georgia.

If you need a degree from an EU university instead, compare this route with [studying in Poland](/study-in-poland).

Housing depends on the university. TSU runs the Lisi dormitory with 320 places; enrolled students apply through the university administration, and the fee is around 150 GEL a month. UG has no dormitory yet and offers temporary housing support on request. SEU plans a dormitory but has none, and Alte helps with hostel places and shared or single apartments through its relocation services.

On work, the rule since 2026 is simple: a part-time job with a Georgian employer requires a separate permit. Build your budget on family funds or a scholarship, not on local income.

Student life for international students is centered in Tbilisi. SEU, for example, runs Holi and Diwali events, a cricket club and culture days, and Indian students form a large community on several campuses.

<!-- block: accordionBlock title="Frequently asked questions" -->
### Is Georgia a good choice for MBBS for Indian students?
It can be, if the program fits NMC FMGL 2021. English MD tuition is about $5,900–8,000 a year, and the program lasts 6 years with the internship built in. Check the NMC rules and NEET before applying, because the Georgian model differs from the NMC structure in several points.

### Do I need NEET to study medicine in Georgia?
NMC rules make NEET mandatory for Indian citizens who study medicine abroad. The Georgian D3 visa checklist in India also lists NEET for MBBS/MD applicants. Some universities, such as SEU and TSU, ask for NEET results at admission.

### Do I need IELTS?
Not always. SEU and TSMU ask for B1 English, while UG, Alte and TSU's MD ask for B2. UG and Alte offer an internal English test, and Alte also accepts proof of one year of study in English.

### Do I need to take an entrance exam?
You do not sit Georgian national exams. Universities run their own checks instead: an online interview, and at some universities an English or subject test. The Ministry of Education then issues an enrollment order.

### How long does admission take?
Recognition by NCEQE takes up to one month at the standard fee of 40 GEL. The ministry order usually follows within about 4–5 weeks, according to SEU. Start about 2–3 months before the semester, and earlier if you need consular legalization or a D3 visa.

### Can I work in Georgia while studying?
Since 2026, working for a Georgian employer requires a separate work authorization, and a study residence permit does not grant it. Plan your finances without income in Georgia.

### Can I work as a doctor in Georgia after graduation?
You receive junior doctor status, which means working under an independent doctor's responsibility. Independent practice requires residency, a state certification exam and Georgian language skills by law.

### Are universities in Georgia recognized by WHO?
WHO does not recognize universities. The correct check is whether the medical school is listed in WDOMS and whether its accreditor, NCEQE, is recognized by WFME, which holds until November 2028.
