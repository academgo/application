<!-- block: textContent -->
International students enter universities in Georgia without sitting Georgian national exams. Instead, you need three approvals in a fixed order: the university's consent, recognition of your school certificate by NCEQE (the National Center for Educational Quality Enhancement), and an enrollment order from Georgia's Ministry of Education.

This page walks through each step for the 2027/28 intake: which documents to prepare, how to legalize them in your home country, what NCEQE charges and how long it takes. It is written for applicants from India, Pakistan, Nigeria, Egypt and the Gulf. Most of it applies to both MD (MBBS) and non-medical programs.

Most delays happen before the file ever reaches Georgia: a missing apostille, a translation that is not notarized, a visa file without the ministry order. If you plan for these early, the process is predictable.

<!-- block: textContent -->
## How admission to universities in Georgia works

The university decides whether to accept you. The state confirms your school certificate and issues the enrollment order. Only then can students from India, Pakistan, Nigeria and Egypt apply for a study visa.

<!-- block: processBlock title="Admission steps, from application to residence permit" -->
1. Apply to the university. Each university sets its own entry requirements. At [Tbilisi State Medical University (TSMU)](/study-in-georgia/tbilisi-state-medical-university), for example, you send your documents, fill in a questionnaire, pass an online interview on Zoom and then receive an offer letter.
2. Get the university's written consent. For enrollment without national exams, NCEQE needs a written consent signed by the rector of your chosen university.
3. Legalize and translate your documents. Put an apostille or consular legalization on your school documents at home, then get a notarized translation into Georgian.
4. Apply to NCEQE for recognition of your school certificate. NCEQE checks that the certificate is authentic and matches Georgian standards.
5. Receive the ministry order. NCEQE forwards your file to the Ministry of Education, the minister issues an order, and the university enrolls you.
6. Get your entry route. Citizens of India, Pakistan, Nigeria and Egypt need a D3 study visa. Citizens of Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman enter visa-free for one year.
7. Apply for a student residence permit in Georgia. File the application no later than 40 calendar days before your visa or visa-free stay ends.

## NCEQE recognition: fees and processing times

NCEQE recognition is mandatory for every foreign school certificate. You choose the speed, and the fee depends on it.

<!-- block: tableBlock -->
| Processing time | NCEQE recognition fee |
|---|---|
| 1 month | 40 GEL |
| 15 working days | 80 GEL |
| 5 working days | 120 GEL |
| 1 working day | 250 GEL |

<!-- block: textContent -->
If you submit by mail, add 5 GEL. Enrollment without national exams is a separate NCEQE service: 30 GEL for 1 month or 60 GEL for 15 working days.

You can submit the file in four ways:

- in person at NCEQE, 2 Merab Aleksidze Street, Tbilisi;
- online through my.gov.ge, which requires a Georgian ID card and an electronic signature, so it is usually not an option for foreign applicants;
- through a representative with a power of attorney;
- by mail.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Documents NCEQE asks for" -->
- Application form.
- Copy of your passport with a notarized translation into Georgian.
- Notarized translation of your school certificate.
- Written conclusion or consent from the admitting university (for the no-exam procedure, signed by the rector).
- Copy of the certificate with grades and proof of your last two years of study (for enrollment without national exams).
- Payment receipt.
- Power of attorney, if a representative submits for you.

## Documents for the university and translation rules

Universities ask for a shorter list first. TSMU asks for a passport, a high school certificate and a language certificate; applicants under 18 add a translated birth certificate and their parents' passports.

Translation is the strict rule. Any foreign-language document goes to NCEQE only with a notarized Georgian translation. TSMU asks for notarized Georgian translations in two original copies.

<!-- block: textContent -->
## Apostille or consular legalization: rules by country

Georgia has been a party to the Hague Apostille Convention since 14 May 2007. If your country is also a party, an apostille is enough. If it is not, your documents need consular legalization.

<!-- block: tableBlock -->
| Country of issue | Hague Apostille Convention | What your documents need |
|---|---|---|
| India | Party | Apostille. For the D3 visa, the Georgian embassy requires an MEA apostille on all education documents |
| Pakistan | Party since 9 March 2023 | Apostille |
| Saudi Arabia | Party since 7 December 2022 | Apostille |
| Nigeria | Not a party | Consular legalization |
| Egypt | Not a party | Consular legalization |
| UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman | Not a party | Consular legalization |

<!-- block: textContent -->
Consular legalization usually runs in two steps: the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the issuing country, then the Georgian consulate. After the apostille or legalization, you still need the notarized Georgian translation.

## University requirements: language, interview and NEET

### Language certificate and interview

TSMU asks for an internationally recognized certificate at B1 level or higher in the language of the program, English or Russian. It also holds an online interview on Zoom before issuing an offer letter. Other universities set their own level and format.

### Do you need Georgian or a preparatory year?

English- and Russian-taught programs do not require Georgian. For Georgian-taught programs, a university may require a preparatory year. TSMU, for example, runs a preparatory course.

### NEET for Indian MBBS applicants

A Georgian university may not require NEET for enrollment, but NMC requires NEET for Indian citizens who want to study medicine abroad. The Georgian embassy in India also lists NEET in its D3 visa checklist for MBBS students. The MD program in Georgia lasts 6 years (360 ECTS), with the final year fully clinical. More on programs and universities is on our [MBBS in Georgia](/study-in-georgia/mbbs-in-georgia) page.

## Timeline for the 2027/28 intake

Georgian universities publish exact 2027/28 application dates closer to the intake. Plan backward from the semester start.

As a guide, start the NCEQE and ministry procedure at least 2–3 months before the semester begins.

<!-- block: tableBlock -->
| Stage | Standard option | Faster option |
|---|---|---|
| NCEQE recognition | 1 month, 40 GEL | 1 working day, 250 GEL |
| Enrollment without national exams (ministry order) | 1 month, 30 GEL | 15 working days, 60 GEL |
| D3 study visa | Consular fee 20 USD, plus the VFS service fee if you apply through VFS | Depends on the embassy |
| Student residence permit, after arrival | Decision on day 30, 300 GEL | Day 20 for 450 GEL, day 10 for 600 GEL |

<!-- block: textContent -->
### The D3 visa file

You apply online on geoconsul.gov.ge; in India, documents go through VFS Global. The key items are the Ministry of Education order, the university's enrollment order, NEET (MBBS only), proof of accommodation and funds, insurance for the full visa period, and an MEA apostille on education documents. The D3 visa is issued for 90 days. Full checklist: [Georgia student visa](/study-in-georgia/georgia-student-visa).

The Georgian embassy in the UAE adds an online student entry permit (registration.gov.ge) and a UAE residence permit or multiple-entry visa valid 90 days beyond the requested visa.

## What to know before you apply

Several points matter more than brochures suggest, especially for future doctors.

### Practicing medicine in Georgia requires Georgian

After the MD, graduates receive junior doctor status without extra exams. A junior doctor has no right to independent medical practice and works under a licensed doctor. Georgian law makes the Georgian language mandatory for independent practice, and TSMU runs its residency programs only in Georgian (per its website, September 2026). Universities include Georgian courses so students can talk to patients. If you plan to work as a doctor, plan your licensing exam at home or in a third country from year one.

### WFME recognition has an end date

WFME recognizes agencies, not universities. Georgia's accreditor, NCEQE, holds WFME recognition until November 2028. A student starting in 2027 graduates around 2033. Renewal is a question for the future, and nobody can guarantee it. Check the status again before each major step, such as ECFMG registration.

### Check your home-country rules before you pay

For India, the rulebook is the NMC Foreign Medical Graduate Licentiate Regulations (FMGL) 2021. They set conditions on course length, internship, language of instruction and licensing in the country of study. In Georgia, the clinical internship sits inside the 6-year MD, not after it. Georgia's Ministry of Education states that its MD is in line with the new NMC rules. That is Georgia's position, not an NMC decision. Read the current FMGL text and compare it with your specific program before paying a deposit. Applicants from Pakistan, Nigeria and Egypt should do the same with their national medical regulator.

### No fixed bank balance for the visa

The Georgian embassy checklists do not set a fixed amount for the D3 visa. You show your own funds or a sponsor package: a notarized sponsor affidavit, the sponsor's ID, a 3-month bank statement and a salary certificate. A grant or scholarship letter also works.

### Do not plan on working in Georgia

Since 2026, part-time work for a Georgian employer requires a separate permit. Plan your budget without counting on earnings in Georgia. Tuition and living costs are on our [cost of studying in Georgia](/study-in-georgia/cost-of-studying-in-georgia) page.

### Do I need an entrance exam to study in Georgia?
No Georgian national exam is required for international students. Admission goes through university consent, NCEQE recognition of your school certificate and a Ministry of Education order. Universities may still ask for a language certificate and an interview.

### How long does NCEQE recognition take?
It depends on the fee you pay. The standard option takes 1 month for 40 GEL. Faster options are 15 working days (80 GEL), 5 working days (120 GEL) and 1 working day (250 GEL).

### Do my documents need an apostille?
If your country is a party to the Hague Apostille Convention, yes: India, Pakistan and Saudi Arabia use an apostille. Nigeria, Egypt, the UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman are not parties, so documents need consular legalization. In both cases, you then need a notarized translation into Georgian.

### Is NEET required for MBBS in Georgia?
A Georgian university may not require NEET for enrollment, but NMC requires NEET for Indian citizens who study medicine abroad. The Georgian embassy in India also lists NEET in the D3 visa checklist for MBBS students.

### Do I need to speak Georgian?
Not to study in an English- or Russian-taught program. Georgian-taught programs may require a preparatory year. Medical students still take Georgian courses for work with patients, and independent medical practice in Georgia requires Georgian by law.

### How much money do I need to show for the Georgian student visa?
The embassy checklists do not set a fixed amount. You provide proof of your own funds or a sponsor package: a notarized affidavit, the sponsor's ID, a 3-month bank statement and a salary certificate.

### When should I start applying for the 2027/28 intake?
Universities publish exact 2027/28 dates closer to the intake. As a guide, start the NCEQE and ministry procedure at least 2–3 months before the semester begins.
