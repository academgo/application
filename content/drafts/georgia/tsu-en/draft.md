Ivane Javakhishvili Tbilisi State University is a public, multi-faculty university in Georgia's capital, founded in 1918. It has seven faculties, including a Faculty of Medicine that has trained doctors since 1918, and runs an English-taught Medical Doctor (MD) program for international students. According to the university, it has about 22,000 students, including more than 660 from abroad.

TSU suits applicants who want an English-taught MD at a state university and have a clear plan for licensing at home, or an English bachelor's in economics, computer science or engineering.

It is a weaker fit if you need a guaranteed dormitory place, intake dates fixed well in advance, or a degree already confirmed by your home regulator.

## Tbilisi State University tuition fees

The English MD costs $8,000 per year, or $4,000 per semester. This is the fee listed on the program page in September 2026; the page does not state the academic year. TSU sets fees each academic year, and 2027/28 fees have not been published yet.

## Programs and the degree you receive

The main program for international students is **Medicine (English)**. Graduates receive the Medical Doctor (MD) qualification. It is a one-cycle program of 6 years and 362 ECTS.

In Georgia, the MD includes clinical training inside the 6 years. Clinical rotations take the last three semesters, and the sixth year is fully clinical.

English-taught bachelor programs, all 4 years:

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Bachelor programs in English and French" -->
- Economics (Bachelor of Economics) at the International School of Economics at TSU (ISET)
- Chemistry / Biochemistry
- Computer Science
- Computer Science (SDSU), run jointly with San Diego State University
- Computer Engineering
- Electrical Engineering
- Computer Science, a double degree with Université Paris 8 (taught in French and Georgian)

At master's level, TSU offers an English-taught MA in Economics through ISET.

## Admission requirements for international students

International students enter Georgian universities without Georgian national exams. TSU gives consent, then Georgia's National Center for Educational Quality Enhancement (NCEQE) and the Ministry of Education confirm your enrollment.

<!-- block: processBlock title="How to apply to TSU for 2027/28" -->
1. Check your school results. You need a completed secondary school certificate. For the MD, TSU asks for proof that you passed biology, chemistry and English at school. TSU does not publish minimum grades by curriculum, so we assess your transcript against the program before you apply.
2. Prove your English. The MD requires a B2 certificate (IELTS or TOEFL). English-taught bachelor programs also ask for an English certificate.
3. Prepare documents. For the MD: application form, school certificate with a notarized Georgian translation, proof of biology, chemistry and English, passport copy, English certificate. Indian citizens add their NEET result.
4. Legalize and translate. Indian and Pakistani documents need an apostille; for India's D3 visa, education documents need an MEA apostille. Documents from Egypt, Nigeria and the UAE need consular legalization. Every foreign document then needs a notarized translation into Georgian.
5. Apply online. MD applications go through uni-can.tsu.ge. After you submit, TSU holds a video interview to confirm your English.
6. Receive the letter of consent. The faculty reviews your file and may set an exam or interview. If you pass, TSU issues a letter of consent.
7. NCEQE recognition and ministry order. Your documents go to NCEQE, which checks your school certificate. The fee is 40 GEL for 1 month or 80 GEL for 15 working days. The Ministry of Education then issues the enrollment order.
8. Sign the contract and apply for the visa. You sign a contract with TSU. Applicants under 18 need a parent or guardian to sign, with a notarized copy of the birth certificate.

<!-- block: textContent -->
TSU does not publish intake dates or deadlines on its international pages. For the 2027/28 intake, plan to start the whole process at least 2–3 months before the semester begins: NCEQE recognition alone can take up to a month, and a D3 visa comes after the ministry order. Exact dates are published by the university closer to the intake, and we track them for you.

## What to know before you apply

These limits are easier to handle before you pay any fees.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Honest limitations" -->
- **Recognition in India is not automatic.** NMC rules (FMGL 2021) require at least 54 months of study plus a 12-month internship at the same university, and a license in the country of study on par with citizens. Georgia's Ministry of Education states that its MD equals an MBBS and follows NMC rules; this is Georgia's position, not an NMC decision. In Georgia the internship sits inside the 6 years, and an NMC advisory warns that such deviations may lead to refusal of registration.
- **Patients speak Georgian.** Teaching is in English, but Georgian universities include Georgian language courses so students can talk to patients in clinics.
- **No independent practice in Georgia without Georgian.** Graduates become junior doctors working under a licensed doctor, and independent practice requires Georgian by law. Plan to take licensing exams at home or in a third country.
- **WFME recognition has an end date.** NCEQE's WFME recognition runs until November 2028, before a 2027 starter graduates, so renewal matters.
- **Pakistan.** PM&DC expects the university to appear in the ECFMG/WDOMS lists and an MDCAT score of at least 50%. We check the current WDOMS listing with you.
- **Do not plan on working.** From 2026, part-time work for a Georgian employer requires a separate work permit. Build your budget without income earned in Georgia.

## Rankings and accreditation

TSU is a state university authorized in Georgia. Its English MD was accredited in 2011, the year the first international students were admitted. In Georgia, MD programs are accredited or authorized by NCEQE.

NCEQE received WFME recognition in 2018 for 10 years, until November 2028. WFME recognizes agencies, not universities, so the accurate wording is "Georgia's accrediting agency is WFME-recognized".

The rankings TSU quotes on its own pages date from 2019–2020, so we do not repeat them. If rankings matter to you, we check current figures together.

## Housing and student life

Enrolled students can apply to the TSU administration for a dormitory place. The main option is the Lisi dormitory near Lisi Lake: three four-story buildings with hotel-type rooms and a sports ground, 320 places in total. We found no guarantee of a room for the full study period.

TSU pages show a fee of around 150 GEL per month for Lisi; we confirm the current rate before you apply. Places are limited, so many international students rent a room or a shared flat.

Numbeo (September 2026) puts a single person's monthly costs in Tbilisi at about 1,730 GEL without rent, and a one-bedroom flat outside the center at about 1,163 GEL. This reflects an expat lifestyle; students in a dormitory or shared room usually spend noticeably less.

## Student visa

Citizens of India, Pakistan, Nigeria and Egypt need a D3 study visa. You apply online at geoconsul.gov.ge; in India, documents are submitted through VFS Global. The consular fee is $20, plus the VFS service fee. The e-Visa is for short visits only and cannot be used for study.

The D3 checklist includes the Ministry of Education order, TSU's enrollment order, proof of accommodation, proof of funds and insurance for the whole visa period. No fixed amount of funds is set; a 3-month bank statement or a sponsor package is required.

GCC citizens enter Georgia visa-free for one year. Holders of a valid visa or residence permit from one of 50 listed countries, including GCC states, can enter for 90 days in any 180 days. In Georgia, all students then apply for a study residence permit at least 40 days before their legal stay ends. Full details are on our [Georgia student visa page](/study-in-georgia/georgia-student-visa).

### How much does the MD at TSU cost for international students?
TSU lists the English MD at $8,000 per year, or $4,000 per semester, as of September 2026. TSU sets fees each academic year, so we confirm the 2027/28 fee before you apply.

### How long is the MD at TSU?
The program page states 6 years, 12 semesters and 362 ECTS. Clinical training, including the internship, is part of those 6 years.

### Do Indian students need NEET for TSU?
Yes. TSU lists NEET among the MD documents for Indian citizens. NMC also requires NEET for Indian citizens who study medicine abroad, so without it you cannot register to practice in India later.

### Is IELTS required for TSU?
For the MD, TSU asks for a B2 certificate, such as IELTS or TOEFL. After you apply, TSU also holds a video interview to confirm your English. English-taught bachelor programs require an English certificate as well.

### Is TSU the same as Tbilisi State Medical University?
No. They are separate universities with separate admissions and fees. TSU has seven faculties, and medicine is one of them.

### Does TSU guarantee a dormitory?
No guarantee was found. Enrolled students can apply for a place in the Lisi dormitory, which has 320 places. Many international students rent a room or shared flat in Tbilisi.

### Can I work in Georgia while studying at TSU?
From 2026, working for a Georgian employer requires a separate work permit, and a study residence permit does not give that right by itself. Plan your budget without income earned in Georgia.
