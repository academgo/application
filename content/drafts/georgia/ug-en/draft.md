University of Georgia Tbilisi (UG) is a private university that teaches bachelor's, master's and PhD programs in English and Georgian. International applicants know it mainly for its six-year Medicine (MD) program, but UG also offers English-taught degrees in computing, engineering, business, pharmacy and nursing. Admission is open year-round, with fall and spring intakes, and foreigners do not sit Georgia's national entrance exams.

UG suits you if you want an English-taught MD at a private university with a clear price list, or an IT or business bachelor's for $4,000–5,000 a year. It is a weaker fit if you need a guaranteed dormitory: university housing is still under construction. It is also the wrong plan if you want to practice medicine independently in Georgia without learning Georgian, because Georgian law requires it.

## Programs and the degree you receive

UG teaches in English at every level, from bachelor's to PhD. Medicine (MD) and Dentistry are one-cycle programs at the School of Health Sciences.

In Georgia, the MD lasts 6 years (360 ECTS) and includes the internship. Clinical rotations take the last three semesters, and the sixth year is fully clinical. Georgian language courses are part of the curriculum, because patients speak Georgian. An MD graduate becomes a junior doctor in Georgia, a status without the right to independent practice.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="English-taught programs at UG" -->
- Bachelor's in technology: Civil Engineering, Cybersecurity Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Electronic and Communication Engineering.
- Bachelor's in business and humanities: Business Administration and Business Analytics (3- and 4-year tracks), English Philology, Graphic Design.
- Bachelor's in health: Nursing, Pharmacy.
- Master's (120 ECTS, 60+60): Management of IT, Computer Science, Blockchain – Distributed Information Systems, Cybersecurity, International Business Law, Security Studies, Global Governance, Education Administration, English Philology, Financial Risk Management, MBA.
- PhD: Education Sciences.

## Admission requirements for international students

Foreigners skip Georgia's national exams. The university accepts you, NCEQE (the education quality center) recognizes your school certificate, and the Ministry of Education issues an enrollment order.

<!-- block: processBlock title="How to apply to UG for 2027/28" -->
1. Check eligibility. UG does not publish minimum grades by curriculum, so we check your transcript against the program first. Indian citizens applying for MD must qualify NEET: NMC requires it, and the D3 visa checklist in India lists it for MBBS students.
2. Collect documents: passport, school certificate (bachelor's diploma for master's), a 2-minute video about yourself, a 3×4 photo and the application fee receipt. UG's FAQ also mentions a transcript and a medical application.
3. Certify documents. India, Pakistan and Saudi Arabia use an apostille (an MEA apostille for Indian education documents). Egypt, Nigeria, the UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman need consular legalization. NCEQE needs a notarized Georgian translation of every foreign document.
4. Apply through UG's online portal.
5. Wait for Ministry confirmation: 3–4 weeks, according to UG. NCEQE recognition takes about 3 weeks, then the minister's order follows within 2 weeks. Faster recognition costs more: from 40 GEL for 1 month to 250 GEL for 1 working day.
6. Take UG's online exams in English and your subject. UG asks for B2 English or equivalent, otherwise you take its English exam; native speakers are exempt. No IELTS or TOEFL minimums are published.
7. Get your admission decision, pay part of the fee and receive the invitation. UG quotes up to 3 weeks for admission and up to 4 weeks for the visa.
8. Arrive, pay the rest of the first year, sign the contract, and show health insurance within one month.

<!-- block: textContent -->
UG accepts applications year-round and says there is no admission deadline. Intake dates for 2027/28 are not announced yet; UG publishes them closer to each intake. As a rough guide, start 2–3 months before your semester. There is no age limit, though UG asks applicants over 30 to consult it first.

## What to know before you apply

These limits affect UG applicants most, and each has a practical fix.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Risks and how to handle them" -->
- **No dormitory.** University housing is under construction. UG offers temporary housing support on request and an accommodation letter for the visa. We help you find a rental before you fly.
- **Full first year before the contract.** If your visa is refused, UG states that "paid tuition fees will be refunded upon request", without mentioning deductions. We clarify this with UG in writing before you pay.
- **NMC decides MD recognition in India.** NMC's 2021 rules require at least 54 months of study plus a 12-month internship at the same university, among other conditions. In Georgia, the internship sits inside the 6 years. Georgia's Ministry of Education states that its MD complies with NMC rules, but that is the Georgian side's position. We review the NMC conditions with you; see our [MBBS in Georgia](/study-in-georgia/mbbs-in-georgia) guide.
- **WFME recognition has an end date.** NCEQE, which accredits Georgian MD programs, is recognized by WFME until November 2028. A 2027 starter graduates around 2033, and renewal cannot be guaranteed today.
- **Georgian matters in medicine.** Patients speak Georgian, and independent practice in Georgia requires Georgian by law. Plan to take licensing exams at home or in a third country.
- **No part-time job to count on.** Since 2026, work for a Georgian employer requires a separate permit. Budget without earnings in Georgia.

## Rankings and accreditation

We do not quote rankings for UG: the ranking claims on its website are undated, and we could not verify them.

In Georgia, NCEQE accredits and authorizes MD programs, and NCEQE is recognized by WFME until November 2028. WFME recognizes agencies, not universities, so "WFME-recognized university" or "WHO-recognized degree" is inaccurate for any Georgian school. Treat "NMC approved" claims with the same caution: NMC replaced the Medical Council of India in 2020 and keeps no list of approved foreign universities. Before you apply for MD, we check UG's listing in the World Directory of Medical Schools (WDOMS) and the current NCEQE decision with you.

## Housing and student life

UG has no dormitory, so international students rent apartments or rooms in Tbilisi. According to Numbeo (September 2026), a one-bedroom apartment costs about 1,746 GEL a month in the center and about 1,163 GEL outside it. One person's expenses without rent average about 1,730 GEL a month. These are expat-level figures; a student sharing a flat usually spends noticeably less. A monthly transport pass costs 40 GEL.

Local and international students study together across UG's six schools. Health insurance is compulsory for the whole program.

## Student visa

Citizens of India, Pakistan, Nigeria and Egypt need a D3 study visa. You apply online at geoconsul.gov.ge; in India, documents go through VFS Global. The consular fee is $20, plus the VFS service fee. The checklist includes the Ministry of Education order, UG's enrollment order, proof of housing, insurance and NEET for MBBS applicants. There is no fixed amount of money: you show a 3-month bank statement or a sponsor's affidavit, ID, statement and salary letter.

GCC citizens enter visa-free for one year and apply for a student residence permit in Georgia (300–600 GEL, depending on speed), at least 40 days before their legal stay ends. UAE residents with a valid UAE residence permit can enter visa-free for 90 days in any 180 days. UG helps with the residence permit on request. Full details are on our [Georgia student visa](/study-in-georgia/georgia-student-visa) page.

### Is UG a public or private university?
UG is a private university in Tbilisi, registered as The University of Georgia LTD. It teaches bachelor's, master's and PhD programs in English and Georgian.

### How much does MD cost at UG?
UG's price list shows $6,500 a year for the English-taught MD, as of September 2026. The page names no academic year, and 2027/28 fees are not published. Six years of tuition come to about $39,000 if the price stays the same.

### Does UG have a dormitory?
No. University housing is under construction; UG offers temporary housing support on request and an accommodation letter for your visa. Most students rent in Tbilisi.

### Is the UG MD recognized by NMC in India?
NMC keeps no list of approved foreign universities, so no Georgian university is "NMC approved". Your degree is judged against NMC's rules on course length, internship, English teaching and licensing. Georgia's internship sits inside the 6-year MD, so review this before applying.

### When can I apply for the 2027/28 intake?
UG accepts applications year-round for fall and spring intakes, with no admission deadline. Start 2–3 months before your semester to leave time for recognition, the Ministry order and your visa.

### What English level do I need?
UG asks for B2 English or an equivalent; without it, you take UG's English exam. Native speakers are exempt, and no IELTS or TOEFL minimums are published.

### Can I work in Georgia while studying at UG?
Since 2026, working for a Georgian employer requires a separate permit; a student residence permit alone does not give that right. Plan your budget without earnings in Georgia.
