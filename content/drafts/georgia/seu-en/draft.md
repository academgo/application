SEU Georgia, officially Georgian National University SEU, is a private university in Tbilisi with four faculties: Business and Technology, Law, Social Sciences and Medicine. International students come mainly for its English-taught Medical Doctor (MD) program, dentistry (DMD), nursing and business degrees. SEU is authorized by Georgia's quality agency NCEQE and admits foreign students without Georgian national exams.

SEU suits applicants who want an English-taught MD at the lower end of Georgian fees, and Indian students who already hold a NEET result. It is a weaker fit if you need a dormitory from day one, or plan to practice independently in Georgia without learning Georgian.

For the full country overview, see our [Study in Georgia](/study-in-georgia) guide.

## Programs and the degree you receive

The MD is a single-cycle, six-year program of 360 ECTS, entered straight after high school. The last three semesters are clinical, and the sixth year is fully spent in the clinic, with American Hospital Tbilisi as SEU's affiliated clinic.

Georgia's Ministry of Education states that the Georgian MD is equivalent to MBBS and follows the new NMC rules. This is the Georgian side's position; the decision on registration in India belongs to NMC.

Three details matter for Indian students:

- **Internship is inside the six years.** Georgia has no separate 12-month internship after graduation, while NMC's FMGL 2021 rules require 12 months of internship on top of at least 54 months of study.
- **Patients speak Georgian.** Teaching is in English, but Georgian courses are part of the curriculum so you can talk to patients in the clinic.
- **Licensing in Georgia.** An MD graduate becomes a junior doctor, who works only under the responsibility of an independent doctor. Independent practice in Georgia requires further exams, residency and knowledge of Georgian by law.

The other English-taught degrees are listed in the fee table above. Programs in Information Technologies and Data Science & AI are marked "Under Accreditation" by SEU; we do not recommend them until accreditation is confirmed on the NCEQE website.

## Admission requirements for international students

Foreign applicants enter SEU without Georgian national exams. The process runs through the university, then NCEQE (recognition of your school certificate), then an enrollment order from the Ministry of Education. You pay tuition only after that order is issued.

<!-- block: processBlock title="How to apply to SEU, step by step" -->
1. School certificate — Higher Secondary, Intermediate, Senior or GCE A-Level (3 subjects). Grade requirements depend on the program; we check yours first.
2. English proof — at least B1 for bachelor's, MD and DMD; B2 for master's programs. Without a certificate, you take SEU's English test after the interview.
3. NEET for Indian MD applicants — required by SEU and by NMC for Indian citizens studying medicine abroad.
4. PDF scans to admissions@seu.edu.ge — passport, certificate, application form and English proof. Incomplete applications are not reviewed.
5. Interview — SEU schedules an interview after it accepts your documents; the English test follows if needed.
6. Notarized translation and acceptance letter — your passport and school certificate are translated into Georgian and notarized (two copies, one stays with SEU), and SEU issues an acceptance letter.
7. Recognition at NCEQE — NCEQE checks that your certificate is genuine and matches Georgian standards. The fee depends on speed: 40 GEL for 1 month, 80 GEL for 15 working days, 120 GEL for 5 working days. The student, a representative or SEU (under a separate service agreement) can file it.
8. Ministry order — usually 4–5 weeks after the NCEQE decision.
9. Payment and enrollment — pay when SEU's Student Affairs office (iss@seu.edu.ge) asks you; enrollment follows within 2–3 working days. Then apply for your visa.

<!-- block: textContent -->
For the 2027/28 intake, start at least 2–3 months before the semester: recognition can take a month, and the ministry order and D3 visa add several weeks. We confirm the 2027/28 dates with SEU's admissions office for each applicant. Transfer students follow the same path after SEU evaluates their transcript.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Document legalization by country" -->
- India and Pakistan: apostille. For India, the Georgian embassy requires the MEA apostille on all education documents for the D3 visa.
- Nigeria, Egypt, UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman are not in the Apostille Convention: documents need consular legalization.
- Saudi Arabia: apostille.
- Every foreign document goes to NCEQE with a notarized Georgian translation.

## What to know before you apply

**Prices are from the 2025 list.** SEU has not published 2027/28 fees. The risk is a price change after you plan your budget. We ask SEU for the current fee for your program in writing before you pay anything.

**Check recognition at home.** NCEQE's WFME recognition runs until November 2028, while a 2027 starter graduates around 2033; renewal cannot be guaranteed in advance. Indian students should compare the NMC FMGL 2021 conditions with the Georgian structure, especially the internship rule. We go through this with you, including the World Directory of Medical Schools (WDOMS).

**No dormitory.** Arrange a room or flat before arrival: the D3 visa requires proof of accommodation.

**No income from part-time work.** Since 2026, working for a Georgian employer requires a separate permit, and a student residence permit alone does not give that right. Plan your budget without earnings in Georgia.

## Rankings and accreditation

We do not quote rankings for SEU, because we could not confirm a sourced one. Accreditation can be checked. According to SEU, the NCEQE Authorization Council granted it unconditional authorization on June 10, 2021, and its English-taught MD is accredited by NCEQE. NCEQE itself is recognized by the World Federation for Medical Education (WFME) until November 2028.

Online claims such as "SEU is fully recognized by NMC and WHO" are not made by SEU itself. WFME recognizes agencies, not universities, and WHO does not recognize degrees.

## Housing and student life

SEU has no dormitory yet, so students rent in the city. According to Numbeo (September 2026), a one-bedroom flat in Tbilisi costs about 1,746 GEL a month in the center and 1,163 GEL outside it, and living costs without rent are about 1,730 GEL. These are average expat figures; students sharing a flat spend noticeably less.

Health insurance is mandatory: SEU partners with the insurer PSP, and you choose the policy. Besides the pool and sports halls, student life is shaped around the Indian community, with Holi, Diwali, a cricket club and Culture Days.

## Student visa

Citizens of India, Pakistan, Nigeria and Egypt need a D3 study visa. You apply online on geoconsul.gov.ge; in India, documents go through VFS Global. The consular fee is $20, plus the VFS service fee.

Key documents are the Ministry of Education order, SEU's enrollment order, proof of accommodation, insurance and proof of funds. There is no fixed amount: a 3-month bank statement or a sponsor package works. Indian MD applicants also attach NEET.

GCC citizens enter visa-free for one year and apply for a student residence permit inside Georgia, no later than 40 days before their legal stay ends. Full details are in our [Georgia student visa guide](/study-in-georgia/georgia-student-visa).

### What are SEU Georgia's MD fees?
According to SEU's price list of June 2025, the English-taught MD costs $5,900 per year for 6 years. SEU had not published 2027/28 fees as of September 2026. We confirm the current fee with the university before you pay.

### Does SEU require NEET from Indian students?
Yes. SEU states that Indian nationals applying to the MD program must provide NEET results. NMC also requires NEET from Indian citizens who study medicine abroad and later want to practice in India.

### Is the SEU MD equal to MBBS in India?
Georgia's Ministry of Education states that the Georgian MD is equivalent to MBBS. The final decision on registration in India belongs to NMC, which sets its own conditions for foreign graduates, including the internship rule. Check these conditions before you apply.

### Is SEU recognized by WFME or WHO?
WFME recognizes agencies, not universities: Georgia's agency NCEQE is WFME-recognized until November 2028, and SEU is authorized by NCEQE. WHO does not recognize universities or degrees, so the phrase "WHO-recognized" is not accurate for any school.

### Does SEU have a hostel?
No. SEU says a new dormitory will be built near the campus, but students rent private housing in Tbilisi. Arrange it before arrival, because the D3 visa requires proof of accommodation.

### When do I pay SEU tuition?
Only after the Ministry of Education issues your enrollment order, when SEU's Student Affairs office asks for payment. Enrollment follows within 2–3 working days after payment.
