MBBS in Georgia is a six-year, English-taught Doctor of Medicine (MD) program that costs about $5,900–8,000 a year at the main universities. Georgia's Ministry of Education describes the MD as equal to an MBBS, but whether your degree lets you practice at home depends on your own country's regulator, not on Georgia.

That second point surprises most applicants. India's National Medical Commission (NMC) has strict rules for foreign medical graduates, and Georgia's own law limits what a graduate can do there without the Georgian language. Below: fees by university, accreditation, the India question and the 2027/28 admission route. For the wider picture, see our [guide to studying in Georgia](/study-in-georgia).

<!-- block: textContent -->
## How the MD program in Georgia works

Medicine in Georgia is a one-cycle MD program: 6 years and 360 ECTS, with the internship built into the 6th year. There is no separate 12-month internship after graduation in the Georgian system. Clinical rotations take up the last 3 semesters, and the 6th year is fully clinical.

MD programs are accredited or authorized by Georgia's National Center for Educational Quality Enhancement (NCEQE). Before you apply, check that the university has a valid NCEQE decision for the exact program you want.

In the clinic, foreign students have the same rights as Georgian students. You can examine patients and carry out some procedures under a teacher's supervision and with the patient's consent.

Teaching is in English, but patients speak Georgian. According to the Ministry of Education, universities include Georgian language courses in the curriculum so students can talk to patients. The level each university expects differs, so ask the admissions office directly.

## Accreditation: what WFME recognition really means

Georgia's accrediting agency, NCEQE, is recognized by the World Federation for Medical Education (WFME). WFME recognizes agencies, not individual universities. So the correct statement is "Georgia's accreditor is WFME-recognized," not "this university is WFME-approved." The WHO does not recognize diplomas at all, so treat "WHO-recognized university" claims with caution.

NCEQE received WFME recognition in 2018 for 10 years, until November 2028. A student starting in 2027 will graduate around 2033. Renewal is a future decision, and no one can guarantee it today.

This matters for the USMLE and ECFMG route in the United States, which looks at whether your program is accredited by a WFME-recognized agency. Choose a program with a valid NCEQE accreditation decision on eqe.ge, and check the university's listing in the World Directory of Medical Schools (WDOMS) before you apply.

## Is MBBS in Georgia valid in India?

Only the NMC can decide whether a foreign degree qualifies you to register in India. Georgia's Ministry of Education stated on 9 August 2024 that its MD equals an MBBS and that medical education in Georgia complies with the new NMC rules. That is the Georgian side's position, not an NMC decision. The NMC does not publish a list of approved foreign universities.

Under the NMC's Foreign Medical Graduate Licentiate Regulations 2021 (FMGL 2021), you need:

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Key NMC FMGL 2021 conditions" -->
- a course of at least 54 months plus 12 months of internship at the same university;
- teaching in English;
- the whole course in one country and one university;
- a license to practice in the country that issued the degree, on the same terms as its citizens;
- a further 12 months of internship in India and the NExT exam.

<!-- block: textContent -->
Two points need attention in Georgia's case. First, the internship sits inside the six-year MD rather than after it, so ask how the university documents it. Second, a Georgian graduate becomes a junior doctor, not an independent practitioner (see below). Only the NMC interprets how these fit its rules.

NMC rules also require Indian citizens and OCIs who study medicine abroad to qualify NEET. A Georgian university may admit you without NEET, but NMC requires it for later registration in India. The embassy in India also lists NEET for MBBS visa applicants.

According to press reports of an NMC advisory dated 27 November 2024, any deviation in duration, language, curriculum, clinical training or internship may lead to refusal of registration in India. Check the latest NMC notices before you sign anything.

Pakistan's PM&DC requires the university to appear in the ECFMG/WDOMS lists and an MDCAT score of at least 50%. For Nigeria, Egypt and other countries, confirm the rules with your home medical regulator before applying.

## Admission and visa route for the 2027/28 intake

International students enter Georgian universities without Georgian national exams. The route runs through the university, NCEQE and the Ministry of Education. Plan to start 2–3 months before the semester begins, because recognition alone can take up to a month. Full details are on our [admission requirements](/study-in-georgia/admission-requirements) page.

<!-- block: processBlock title="How to enroll in an MD program in Georgia" -->
1. Choose a university and program — confirm the NCEQE accreditation, WDOMS listing and the fee for your intake.
2. Get the university's consent — the rector's written consent is needed for enrollment without national exams.
3. Legalize your documents — India and Pakistan use the apostille (India: MEA apostille on all education documents); Nigeria, Egypt, the UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman need consular legalization.
4. Recognition of your school certificate by NCEQE — NCEQE checks that it is genuine and matches Georgian standards. Fees: 40 GEL (1 month), 80 GEL (15 working days), 120 GEL (5 working days) or 250 GEL (1 working day). All foreign documents need a notarized Georgian translation.
5. Ministry of Education order — NCEQE passes your file to the Ministry, the minister issues an enrollment order, and the university enrolls you.
6. Apply for a D3 study visa — apply online at geoconsul.gov.ge; in India, submit documents through VFS Global. The consular fee is $20, plus any VFS service fee.
7. Apply for a student residence permit in Georgia — through the Public Service Development Agency (Public Service Hall or online), no later than 40 calendar days before your legal stay ends.

<!-- block: textContent -->
Citizens of India, Pakistan, Nigeria and Egypt need a D3 visa. The embassy checklist in India includes the Ministry of Education order, the university's enrollment order, NEET for MBBS, proof of accommodation, proof of funds and insurance for the whole visa period. The D3 is issued for 90 days, which is why the residence permit step follows soon after arrival.

GCC citizens enter Georgia visa-free for one year. Indians and Pakistanis living in the UAE with a valid residence visa can enter visa-free for 90 days in any 180, but the Georgian embassy in the UAE still issues study visas with its own extra requirements. See our [Georgia student visa guide](/study-in-georgia/georgia-student-visa) for the full checklist.

## What to know before you apply

Other agencies tend to skip these points. Each has a practical answer if you know it early.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Risks and how to handle them" -->
- **Independent practice in Georgia requires Georgian.** After the MD, graduates become junior doctors without extra exams. A junior doctor works under an independent doctor's responsibility and has no right to independent practice, except in emergencies. To practice independently, you need a post-diploma exam, residency and a state certification exam. The law makes knowledge of Georgian mandatory for independent practice, and all residency programs at TSMU are taught only in Georgian. What to do: plan your license at home or in a third country from day one.
- **Nationality is not the barrier; language is.** The law gives foreigners the same right to a license as Georgians.
- **WFME recognition of NCEQE runs to November 2028.** Your graduation will come later. What to do: check the recognition status each year and keep your WDOMS and NCEQE documents on file.
- **Home-country rules decide your career.** For India, check FMGL 2021, NEET and the latest NMC notices before paying a deposit. For Pakistan, Nigeria and Egypt, check with your own regulator. We walk through this with you before choosing a university.
- **There is no official fixed amount for the D3 visa.** Embassy checklists ask for your own funds or a sponsor package: a notarized sponsor affidavit, the sponsor's ID, 3 months of bank statements and a salary certificate. Be wary of anyone quoting a "required" sum.
- **Do not plan on earning in Georgia.** From 2026, working for a Georgian employer needs a separate work authorization, and a student residence permit alone does not give the right to work. Budget without local income.

### How long does it take to study medicine in Georgia?
The MD program lasts 6 years (360 ECTS). The internship is part of the 6th year, which is fully clinical, with no separate internship year after graduation.

### What are the MBBS in Georgia fees?
English-taught MD tuition at the main universities is about $5,900–8,000 a year: SEU $5,900, Alte $5,950, UG $6,500 and TSMU $8,000, per university websites checked in September 2026. Six years of tuition come to roughly $35,000–48,000, before living costs. Fees for 2027/28 are not yet published.

### Is MBBS in Georgia valid in India?
Georgia's Ministry of Education says its MD equals an MBBS and complies with NMC rules, but only the NMC decides on registration in India. Check the FMGL 2021 conditions, especially the internship and the license in the country of study, against the specific program. Review the latest NMC notices before you apply.

### Do Indian students need NEET for Georgia?
A Georgian university may not require NEET for admission. But NMC rules require Indian citizens and OCIs to qualify NEET to register in India later, and the embassy checklist lists NEET for MBBS visa applicants. Treat it as required.

### Is the program fully in English?
Teaching is in English, but patients speak Georgian, so universities include Georgian language courses in the curriculum. Ask each university which level it expects by the clinical years.

### Can I work as a doctor in Georgia after graduation?
You receive junior doctor status, which lets you work only under an independent doctor's responsibility. Independent practice requires a post-diploma exam, residency and a state certification exam, plus mandatory knowledge of Georgian. Most international graduates plan their license at home or in a third country.

### Is a Georgian university recognized by WFME or the WHO?
WFME recognizes accrediting agencies, not universities: Georgia's NCEQE is WFME-recognized until November 2028. The WHO does not recognize diplomas. Check that your program has a valid NCEQE decision and that the university is listed in WDOMS.

### How much money do I need to show for the Georgian student visa?
Embassy checklists do not set a fixed amount. You show your own funds or a sponsor package with 3 months of bank statements, a notarized affidavit, the sponsor's ID and a salary certificate. For the residence permit, the rule is at least two subsistence minimums per month, a figure that changes regularly.
