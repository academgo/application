Tbilisi State Medical University (TSMU) is a state-run medical university in Georgia's capital. It has been a separate medical institution since 1930. International students study at its International Faculty of Medicine and Stomatology, in English or Russian.

TSMU suits applicants who want a state university, a six-year MD taught in English and a campus in the capital. The English MD costs $8,000 a year at current fees, at the top of Georgia's range of around $5,900–8,000.

It is a weaker fit if you plan to practice independently in Georgia without learning Georgian: residency at TSMU is taught only in Georgian.

## Programs and the degree you receive

The main program for international applicants is the one-cycle Medical Doctor (MD). It runs for 6 years and 360 ECTS, as set nationally in Georgia. Clinical rotations fill the last three semesters, and the sixth year is fully clinical. The internship is built into these six years rather than added after graduation.

The degree awarded is an MD. Georgia's Ministry of Education states that the Georgian MD is equivalent to the MBBS. This is the Georgian side's position, not a decision by India's National Medical Commission (NMC).

Other programs open to international students:

- **Dental Medicine** in English or Russian, at the International Faculty.
- **American MD Program** at the Faculty of Medicine, a separate track with a higher fee.
- **Pharmacy** and **Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation** in English.
- **Public Health and Management**, a joint Georgian-French bachelor's program.
- **Preparatory Course** for students who need a foundation year.

For how Georgian medical degrees compare across universities, read our overview of [MBBS in Georgia](/study-in-georgia/mbbs-in-georgia).

## Admission requirements for international students

Foreign applicants enter TSMU without Georgian national exams. The university reviews your documents and interviews you online. After that, Georgia's education quality agency NCEQE (National Center for Educational Quality Enhancement) recognizes your school certificate, and the Ministry of Education issues an enrollment order. See our [admission requirements](/study-in-georgia/admission-requirements) page for the full process.

<!-- block: processBlock title="How to apply to TSMU for 2027/28" -->
1. Check your grades and curriculum — TSMU asks for a high school certificate (it refers to "A level"). It does not publish minimum grades by curriculum, so we assess your transcript (CBSE, state boards, FSc, WAEC, Thanaweya Amma and others) before you apply.
2. Get a language certificate — an internationally recognized certificate at B1 or higher in the language of your program, English or Russian.
3. Prepare your documents — passport, school certificate and language certificate. Under-18s add a translated birth certificate and parents' passports; transfer students add a transcript.
4. Legalize and translate — Indian and Pakistani documents need an apostille; Indian education documents need the MEA apostille for the visa. Nigeria, Egypt, the UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman are not in the Apostille Convention, so their documents need consular legalization. All documents must be translated into Georgian and notarized, in two original copies.
5. Submit and take the interview — TSMU reviews your file, asks you to complete a form and invites you to an online interview on Zoom. If you pass, you receive an offer letter.
6. NCEQE recognition — NCEQE checks your certificate. The fee depends on speed: 40 GEL for 1 month, 80 GEL for 15 working days, 120 GEL for 5 working days, 250 GEL for 1 working day.
7. Ministry order and visa — after the Ministry of Education issues the enrollment order, applicants who need a visa apply for the D3 study visa.
8. Plan your timeline — TSMU has not published dates for the 2027/28 intake. Start the recognition procedure 2–3 months before the semester.

<!-- block: textContent -->
Indian citizens should note one more rule. TSMU's admission pages do not mention NEET, but NMC requires Indian citizens who study medicine abroad to qualify NEET. Without it, you cannot register as a doctor in India later.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="What to know before you apply" -->
- **Residency is in Georgian only.** All TSMU residency programs are taught in Georgian. Plan your postgraduate path at home or in a third country from day one.
- **A Georgian MD does not mean independent practice in Georgia.** Graduates become junior doctors and may work only under a supervising doctor. Independent practice requires an exam, residency, a state certification exam and knowledge of Georgian by law.
- **Patients speak Georgian.** Teaching is in English, but Georgian universities add Georgian language courses so students can talk to patients in clinics. Take these courses seriously.
- **The NMC question for Indian students.** Georgia includes the internship inside the six years. NMC rules for foreign graduates require 12 months of internship on top of the course. Only NMC decides how it treats a Georgian degree, so check the current NMC rules before you apply.
- **WFME recognition has an end date.** NCEQE, the agency that accredits Georgian MD programs, is recognized by WFME until November 2028. A student starting in 2027 graduates around 2033, and renewal cannot be guaranteed in advance.
- **Many sites use the TSMU name.** Several third-party websites look like university pages, for example with an .in domain. The official site is tsmu.edu; check fees and requirements there.
- **Do not count on local income.** Since 2026, working for a Georgian employer requires a separate permit.

## Rankings and accreditation

We do not quote rankings for TSMU because we have not verified any from an official source. What is confirmed: TSMU is a state university, and MD programs in Georgia are accredited by NCEQE. NCEQE received WFME recognition in 2018 for ten years, until November 2028.

The correct wording matters for your future licensing exams. WFME recognizes accrediting agencies, not universities, so no Georgian university is "WFME-recognized" by itself. The WHO does not recognize universities or diplomas either. Before applying, check the university's listing in the World Directory of Medical Schools (WDOMS).

## Housing and student life

TSMU's official pages do not describe dormitories for international students, so we confirm housing options for your intake directly. Most students rent a room or share an apartment in Tbilisi. The D3 visa also requires proof of accommodation, such as a rental agreement or a notarized letter from your host.

According to Numbeo (September 2026), one person in Tbilisi spends about 1,730 GEL a month excluding rent. A one-bedroom apartment costs about 1,163 GEL outside the center and about 1,746 GEL in the center. A monthly transit pass is 40 GEL. These are average expat figures; students who share a room spend noticeably less.

## Student visa

Citizens of India, Pakistan, Nigeria and Egypt need a D3 study visa. You apply online at geoconsul.gov.ge; in India, documents go through VFS Global. The consular fee is $20, plus the VFS service fee if you use VFS. Proof of funds has no fixed amount: embassies ask for 3 months of bank statements or a sponsor package.

Citizens of Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman enter Georgia visa-free for one year and apply for a study residence permit inside the country. See the full checklist on our [Georgia student visa](/study-in-georgia/georgia-student-visa) page.

### Is TSMU a government university?
Yes. TSMU is a state university in Tbilisi. It has been a separate medical institution since 1930.

### What is the TSMU fee for MBBS (MD) in English?
The English MD costs $8,000 a year, as listed on the TSMU website in September 2026. Fees for 2027/28 are not yet published, so we confirm the fee before you apply.

### Do I need NEET to study at TSMU?
TSMU's admission pages do not mention NEET. However, NMC requires Indian citizens who study medicine abroad to qualify NEET.

### What English level does TSMU require?
TSMU asks for an internationally recognized language certificate at B1 or higher in the language of your program. There is no fixed IELTS score, but there is an online interview.

### How long is the MD program at TSMU?
The MD in Georgia lasts 6 years and 360 ECTS. The final three semesters are clinical, and the internship is included within the six years.

### Is TSMU approved by NMC or recognized by WHO?
NMC does not publish a list of approved foreign universities, and the WHO does not recognize universities. What is confirmed: Georgian MD programs are accredited by NCEQE, which is recognized by WFME until November 2028.

### Can I do residency at TSMU in English?
No. All TSMU residency programs are taught only in Georgian.
