Istituto Marangoni is a private fashion and design school founded in Milan in 1935. It is not a university: in Italy it belongs to the AFAM system of higher arts education, and the Ministry of University and Research (MUR) authorizes it to award academic diplomas. It teaches in Milan and Florence, runs campuses in Paris, London, Dubai and several other cities, and reports over 4,000 students from more than 107 countries.

It suits applicants who want to study fashion design, fashion business, interiors or visual design inside Milan's industry, in English, and whose family can cover about €30,000 a year in fees plus living costs. It suits less well if you need a classic university degree for a public-sector job at home, or if your budget points you toward state universities.

One detail matters more than the brochure suggests. Only some Marangoni courses lead to a state-recognized Italian diploma. Below we show which ones do, what they cost for non-EU students, and how admission and the visa work for the September 2027 intake.

## Istituto Marangoni tuition fees for non-EU students

Fees for non-EU students in Milan and Florence run from about €19,400 to €33,500 a year, plus an enrollment fee. That enrollment fee is charged every year, not once. For a fashion BA, the real annual cost at 2026/27 prices is €29,800, or €89,400 over three years before rent and living costs.

## Programs and the degree you receive

The qualification depends on the campus and the course type, not on the word "BA" on the website.

- **Three-year courses in Milan and Florence** lead to the Diploma Accademico di I livello, 180 ECTS. Italian rules treat it as equivalent to a three-year university bachelor's degree (laurea triennale). The website calls these courses "BA (Hons)", but in Italy you do not receive a British honors degree.
- **One-year "Master" courses** lead to a Master accademico di I livello, 60 credits. It is a postgraduate course taken after a bachelor's. It is not the same as a two-year Italian master's degree.
- **Two-year Master of Arts** (Diploma Accademico di II livello, 120 credits) exists only for selected programs, such as Fashion Design & Innovation (Milan, Florence) and Communication Design (Milano Design).
- **One-Year Intensive, Foundation and short courses** give a school certificate. They are outside the AFAM system and are not recognized by MUR as a degree.

The school's validated bachelor-level courses include:

- Milano Fashion: Fashion Design & Accessories; Fashion Communication & Image; Fashion Management, Digital Communication and Media.
- Milano Design: Interior Design; Product Design; Visual Design.
- Firenze: Fashion Design & Accessories; Fashion Communication & Image; Fashion Product; Arts Curating; Multimedia Arts.

Postgraduate options in Milan include Fashion & Luxury Brand Management, Fashion Buying & Merchandising, Interior Design, Fine Jewellery Design and Digital Art Direction.

Other campuses award different qualifications. London offers BA (Hons) and MA degrees validated by Regent's University London, which is a UK degree with a UK visa. Paris issues French RNCP certificates and Regent's-validated programs. Dubai is accredited by the UAE Commission for Academic Accreditation. Riyadh offers an Advanced Training Diploma, not a bachelor's. Only Milan and Florence award an Italian diploma.

## Admission requirements for international students

Admission to the Italian campuses is portfolio- or essay-based, with no entrance exam. You need 12 years of school for the AFAM courses. School systems in Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, Nigeria and Egypt usually cover 12 years, but we check your certificate individually.

<!-- block: processBlock title="How to apply for September 2027" -->
1. School grades — a 12-year secondary school certificate for the three-year courses. For a Master, you need a bachelor's diploma, a CV and two reference letters.
2. Creative task or essay — creative BA applicants submit "6 creative ideas" (sketches, mood boards, collages on A4/A3), not a full portfolio. Business BA applicants write an essay of about 700 words on a fashion or luxury trend. Creative Master applicants send a portfolio of about 10–12 projects.
3. English — B2 for the BA. The school uses IELTS 5.0 as an example, and its FAQ lists IELTS 4.5–5.0 or TOEFL iBT 42–71. For a Master, plan for IELTS 5.0–5.5. The Foundation year asks for B1, IELTS 4.5.
4. Online application — fill in the form, pay the enrollment fee, upload documents. The school sends a decision within 15 working days.
5. Document certification — for the visa, your school certificate needs either two CIMEA statements (comparability and authenticity) or a Dichiarazione di Valore from the Italian embassy. A CIMEA comparability statement costs around €150 and takes about 60 working days as standard. Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, Saudi Arabia, Oman and Bahrain use the apostille. Nigeria, Egypt, the UAE, Qatar and Kuwait need consular legalization at the Italian embassy.
6. Universitaly pre-enrollment — applicants who need a visa register the chosen program on the official Universitaly portal. Each institution sets its own window, so dates for 2027 will appear closer to the intake. See our [Universitaly guide](/study-in-italy/admission-universitaly).
7. Visa and arrival — classes in Milan start on 20 September 2027. The consulate deadline for bachelor-level visa applications, AFAM included, is 31 October 2027. Plan to apply much earlier.

<!-- block: textContent -->
Marangoni appears to admit on a rolling basis, without a fixed application deadline. In practice, the visa timeline is your real deadline. Since 2026/27, each institution can validate only a limited number of Universitaly applications, so applying early also protects your place in the queue.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="What to know before you apply" -->
- **The cost is high and repeats every year.** A fashion BA costs €29,800 a year at 2026/27 prices, before rent. The visa itself requires proof of at least €10,179.85 a year for living costs, on top of tuition. Count both before you choose.
- **Not every course is a degree.** One-Year Intensive and Foundation courses give a certificate without MUR recognition. If your goal is a recognized diploma, choose a three-year course.
- **The one-year Master is not a two-year master's.** This can matter for PhD applications or public-sector jobs at home. Check with your home country's recognition authority before you enroll.
- **Two course pages disagree with the official list.** Fashion Product and Fragrances & Cosmetics in Milan are described as MUR-recognized on their pages but are missing from the school's Milan validation list. We confirm their status with the school before you apply.
- **The school's English minimum is lower than what the consulate checks.** For English-taught programs, the Italian consulate expects at least B2 in all four skills. Aim for a score that clearly shows B2.
- **The enrollment fee is paid upfront.** Refund terms, including after a visa refusal, are not published. Ask the school in writing before you pay. We help you do this.

## Rankings and accreditation

Marangoni ranks #45 in the world in the QS World University Rankings by Subject 2026 for Art & Design, according to the school. It is the first time it reached the top 50. In Italy, the Milan and Florence campuses operate as AFAM institutions under Presidential Decree 212/2005, with course-level authorizations from MUR. That authorization covers the three-year diplomas and the AFAM master's programs, not the intensive or short courses.

## Housing and student life

Marangoni has no residences of its own. Its Accommodation Service works with partner residences and private apartments, and prices are not published. As a guide, a single room in Milan cost around €704 a month in August 2026, and around €627 in Florence. With rent, living in Milan costs roughly €1,100–1,400 a month.

On a student permit you can work up to 20 hours a week and 1,040 hours a year. Within 8 working days of arrival, you apply for the residence permit (permesso di soggiorno); the first one costs €116.46. Voluntary enrollment in the Italian national health service costs €700 per calendar year. After graduating from an AFAM program, you can apply for a 9–12 month permit to look for work or start a business.

<!-- block: textContent -->
## Student visa

You need a type D study visa. It is valid for 365 days and allows multiple entries. Marangoni sends an admission letter and instructions, but you apply yourself at the Italian consulate in your country of residence. Biometrics are required. The funds can belong to you or to relatives up to the fourth degree, and the consulate checks where the money comes from. The consulate alone decides, and it also looks at whether your chosen program makes sense for your background. Full checklist: [Italy student visa](/study-in-italy/italy-student-visa).

### Is Istituto Marangoni a university?
No. It is a private fashion and design school. In Italy it is part of the AFAM system, and MUR authorizes it to award academic diplomas. Its three-year Diploma Accademico di I livello is treated in Italy as equivalent to a three-year bachelor's degree.

### How much does Marangoni cost for international students?
Non-EU tuition in Milan and Florence ranges from about €19,400 to €33,500 a year, depending on the program. Add an annual enrollment fee of €4,000 for bachelor's courses or €5,500 for a Master. A fashion BA comes to €29,800 a year at 2026/27 prices.

### Is the Marangoni degree recognized?
Three-year courses and AFAM Master's programs in Milan and Florence are recognized by the Italian Ministry of University and Research. One-Year Intensive and Foundation courses give a certificate without that recognition. Recognition in your home country depends on local rules, so check with your national authority.

### What IELTS score do I need?
For bachelor's courses the school asks for B2 and gives IELTS 5.0 as an example; its FAQ lists IELTS 4.5–5.0 or TOEFL iBT 42–71. For a Master, plan for IELTS 5.0–5.5. The Italian consulate checks for at least B2 in all four skills.

### Do I need a portfolio for the bachelor's?
Not a full one. Creative courses ask for "6 creative ideas", such as sketches, mood boards or collages. Business courses ask for an essay of about 700 words on a fashion or luxury trend.

### Are there scholarships at Marangoni?
Yes. The Winter 2027 round gives up to €5,000 off tuition for January–February 2027 starts, with a deadline of 30 November 2026. Students in AFAM programs can also apply for the regional DSU scholarship, which depends on family income.

### Does Marangoni provide student housing?
It has no residences of its own. Its Accommodation Service helps students find rooms in partner residences and private apartments. Budget around €704 a month for a single room in Milan (August 2026 average).

### Can I work while studying at Marangoni?
Yes, within limits. A student residence permit allows up to 20 hours of work a week and 1,040 hours a year. After graduation you can apply for a 9–12 month permit to look for work.
