Bocconi University is a private university in Milan, founded in 1902, focused on economics, management, finance, law and political science. Most bachelor's programs are taught in English, and the university says about 3,700 of its roughly 15,000 students come from 124 countries. There are around 3,000 first-year places, decided mainly by a test score and school grades.

It suits students who want business, economics or finance with strong subject rankings and who can fund the budget or qualify for an income-based waiver. It is a weak fit for engineering, medicine or design, or if your family cannot cover €17,000 a year plus Milan living costs without a waiver.

You submit the application yourself in Bocconi's online portal. AcademGo checks your grades and documents, plans the test, reviews the application before you send it and builds a backup list.

## Bocconi University tuition fees
Bocconi charges one flat fee for EU and non-EU students, with no lower band based on income. Family income matters only if you apply for a tuition waiver. The fee for 2027/28 has not been published yet ("will be defined soon"), so plan with the 2026/27 figures.

<!-- block: tableBlock -->
| Item | Amount (price list 2026/27) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Bachelor's and law, 1st year | €17,000 / year | Same for EU and non-EU students |
| Master of Science, 1st year | €18,550 / year | With a 20% waiver €14,840, with 60% €7,420; indexed to inflation during the program |
| Application fee (bachelor's) | €100 | Non-refundable |
| Bocconi online test | €60 per attempt | Up to 4 attempts per cycle |
| Graduation fee (BSc) | €215 | Paid at graduation |

<!-- block: textContent -->
There is no separate deposit. To confirm your place, you pay an advance on the first installment about three weeks after results; it can be refunded if you withdraw within each session's deadlines. For 2027/28 first-year students, the rest of the first installment is due October 15, 2027, the second January 31, 2028 and the third April 28, 2028.

### Tuition waivers and scholarships
For bachelor's and law, support is income-based. One application, Bocconi4Access to Education, covers 100%, 80% and 60% waivers and 40% and 20% waivers (Boost Your Future), and international first-year candidates can apply. Bocconi reviews your family's income and assets documents and awards waivers from the top level down until the budget runs out. **Income thresholds are not published.**

A 100% waiver covers tuition and fees, not housing. Waivers renew each year if you meet the merit requirements. Early and Winter applicants apply in separate windows, with results about 10 weeks after each deadline. Other options include the Women Award, a sibling discount, a sports scholarship and student loans; master's students can apply for merit awards such as the Bocconi merit award.

## Programs and the degree you receive
Bachelor's programs (BSc) last 3 years. The English-taught options are:

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Bachelor's programs taught in English" -->
- Economics
- Finance
- International Politics and Government (including a HEC-Bocconi double degree)
- Management
- Management and Computer Science
- Management for the Arts and Cultures
- Mathematical and Computing Sciences for Artificial Intelligence
- World Bachelor in Business
- Bachelor in Global Law

<!-- block: textContent -->
Economia aziendale (BSc) and the 5-year Integrated Master of Science in Law are taught in Italian. First-year classes have around 115 students. According to the university, most Master of Science and MA programs are in English.

Bocconi describes itself as a legally recognized non-state university. Graduates receive Italian degrees: a Laurea (bachelor's) and a Laurea Magistrale (master's). For regulated professions at home, check with your national authority how the degree is assessed.

## Admission requirements for international students
For the bachelor's intake of 2027/28, the decision is 55% test score and 45% average of your school grades from the two years before your final year. You apply with those grades; the final diploma is needed by July 2027. You must be at least 17 on September 1, 2027, and 17-year-olds need a legal guardian in Italy.

Bocconi accepts the Bocconi online test, the SAT or the ACT. Law applicants can also use the LSAT or the Bocconi online test – Law. No minimum SAT or ACT score is published.

<!-- block: processBlock title="How to apply for the 2027/28 bachelor's intake" -->
1. Check your grades — We look at how your board's marks (FSc, CBSE, WAEC, Thanaweya Amma) fit Bocconi's average and whether your profile is realistic.
2. Take the test — Booking for the proctored Bocconi online test runs July 13, 2026 – January 19, 2027. It has 50 questions in 75 minutes: 24 math, 11 reading, 6 numerical reasoning, 9 critical thinking. Below 17 points, the application is not assessed.
3. Choose a session — Early: September 2–29, 2026 (15:00 Italian time), results mid-November 2026. Winter: November 25, 2026 – January 26, 2027, results mid-March 2027. Early applications leave more time for the visa.
4. Apply — Submit in Bocconi's portal, pay the €100 fee and file the waiver application in its window.
5. Prove your English — IELTS Academic 6.5 (6.0 in each part), TOEFL iBT 88, Cambridge 180 (CPE) or 173 (CAE/FCE), or Duolingo 110. Needed for enrollment, not for the application.
6. Confirm your place — Pay the first-installment advance and get your diploma by July 2027.
7. Prepare the visa — CIMEA statements or a Declaration of Value, Universitaly pre-enrollment, proof of funds. Visa deadline: October 31, 2027.

<!-- block: textContent -->
Master of Science applicants have four rounds, from October 1, 2026 to April 29, 2027. According to the university, it considers a GMAT, GRE or Bocconi test score, GPA, dossier and motivation.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="What to know before you apply" -->
- **Budget is the main filter.** Tuition is €17,000 a year (2026/27), and Milan costs roughly €1,100–1,400 a month with a room.
- Waivers are not guaranteed and do not cover housing. Have a plan that works without a full waiver.
- The consulate checks money separately: at least €10,179.85 per year in traceable funds, yours or a relative's up to the 4th degree.
- Validation by Bocconi does not guarantee a visa; the consulate decides.
- Universities validate Universitaly applications only up to their quota plus 20%, so apply early.
- Student work is capped at 20 hours a week and will not cover the fees.

## Housing and student life
Bocconi runs 7 residences (Bligny, Bocconi, Castiglioni, Dubini, Isonzo, Javotte, Spadolini) with single rooms only, around 1,700 places. For 2026/27, the full rate is €770–1,050 a month for 10 months, plus a €600 deposit; a reduced rate of €380–650 depends on income. 2027/28 prices come by the end of January 2027.

A private single room in Milan averaged about €704 a month in August 2026. The university reports more than 280 partner universities for exchanges.

<!-- block: textContent -->
## Student visa
Non-EU students pre-enroll on Universitaly, then apply for a type D visa at the Italian consulate in their country of residence, with proof of €10,179.85 for the year. Pakistan, India and Bangladesh use the apostille; Nigeria, Egypt, the UAE, Qatar and Kuwait need consular legalization. The 2027/28 deadline is October 31, 2027, and the residence permit application is due within 8 working days of arrival. Full steps are on our [Italy student visa page](/study-in-italy/italy-student-visa) and in the [Universitaly pre-enrollment guide](/study-in-italy/admission-universitaly).

## Other options
If Bocconi's budget or test is a stretch, compare it with public universities. [Politecnico di Milano](/study-in-italy/politecnico-di-milano) and the [University of Bologna](/study-in-italy/university-of-bologna) charge roughly €160–4,000 a year (2026/27), depending on family income and fixed rates for international students, and you can apply for a [DSU scholarship](/study-in-italy/dsu-scholarship). For design and fashion, [NABA](/study-in-italy/naba) and [Istituto Marangoni](/study-in-italy/istituto-marangoni) cost around €22,000–30,000 a year for non-EU students (2026/27). ### How much does Bocconi cost per year?
For 2026/27, bachelor's and law programs cost €17,000 a year, and Master of Science programs cost €18,550. The fee is the same for EU and non-EU students. The 2027/28 fee has not been published yet.

### Does Bocconi offer scholarships to international students?
Yes. International first-year bachelor's and law students can apply for Bocconi4Access to Education, with income-based waivers of 20% to 100%. Thresholds are not published and funds are limited, so apply early.

### What IELTS score does Bocconi require?
IELTS Academic 6.5 with at least 6.0 in each part; TOEFL iBT 88 and Duolingo 110 are also accepted. You need it for enrollment, so you can apply before you have it.

### When is the Bocconi application deadline for 2027?
For the 2027/28 bachelor's intake, the Early session runs September 2–29, 2026, and the Winter session runs November 25, 2026 to January 26, 2027. Master of Science applicants have four rounds between October 2026 and April 2027.

### Is it hard to get into Bocconi?
It is selective: about 3,000 first-year places, with the decision based on the test score (55%) and school grades (45%). You can use the Bocconi test, SAT or ACT.

No. You submit it yourself in Bocconi's portal. We assess your chances, prepare documents, review the application and build a backup list.
