The University of Bologna (Alma Mater Studiorum, UniBo) dates its founding to 1088, a conventionally accepted year, and calls itself the oldest university in the Western world. Today it has five campuses in Emilia-Romagna, 97,235 students (2024/25) and 97 English-taught bachelor's, single-cycle and master's programs for 2026/27. It also ranks #123 in the QS World University Rankings 2027, third in Italy.

It suits applicants who want a large Italian university, strong humanities, law or economics, and low fees: students from Pakistan, India, Nigeria, Egypt, Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Qatar pay a reduced flat fee of €1,157 a year for a bachelor's (2026/27).

It suits you less if you need one fixed test, one deadline and guaranteed housing. Every program runs its own selection, non-EU seats are limited, dorm places are scarce, and UniBo does not work with agencies, so you apply yourself.

## University of Bologna tuition fees for 2026/27
Standard fees depend on family income (ISEE, an Italian income indicator); without ISEE you pay the maximum. For most of our applicants the better option is the reduced flat fee for non-OECD citizens whose family income is earned only abroad. A declaration of income is enough, with no certificates.

Fees for 2027/28 are not published yet. The reduced flat fee cannot be combined with the No Tax Area exemption, and ER.GO scholarship winners pay no fees at all. Each selection call also charges an application fee: €20 for CLaBE and International Studies, €50 for the International Management master's.

The last UniBo grant for international students, International Talents 2025/26, was master's-only: 30 grants of €6,500 a year, with a GRE requirement. No 2026/27 call has been published. The realistic support is the need-based ER.GO scholarship: up to €7,171.11 a year in 2026/27, see our [DSU scholarship page](/study-in-italy/dsu-scholarship).

## Programs and the degree you receive
UniBo offers 15 English-taught bachelor's and single-cycle programs for 2026/27, mostly in economics and management (6) and political sciences (3), plus pharmacy, sciences, engineering, medicine, statistics and veterinary medicine. At master's level there are 82 programs in English. Examples:
- **Business and Economics (CLaBE)**, Bologna campus, bachelor's.
- **International Studies**, Forlì campus, bachelor's.
- **Medicine and Surgery**, six-year single-cycle program in English, with an admission test.
- **International Management**, master's.

You graduate with an Italian bachelor's (laurea), master's (laurea magistrale) or single-cycle degree. Recognition back home depends on your country's rules, especially for medicine, so check it before you apply. English-taught programs need no Italian.

<!-- block: processBlock title="Admission requirements for international students" -->
1. Check your school certificate — Italy requires at least 12 years of schooling for a bachelor's. Most systems in Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, Nigeria and Egypt have 12 years, but we verify your case. If your country has a compulsory university entrance exam, you need its result too.
2. Read the call (bando) of your program — there is no single UniBo test. CLaBE ranks applicants only by SAT score, with no minimum. International Studies uses CEnT-S (the CISIA test that replaced English TOLC-E) or SAT, depending on the round. Master's programs like International Management review your CV, then interview you in English. The call also states the seats reserved for non-EU applicants.
3. Prove your English — at least B2, which the consulate also checks in all four skills. The IELTS or TOEFL minimum is set by the program.
4. Take the selection through Studenti Online — UniBo's own portal.
5. Pre-enroll on Universitaly — after admission, on the official free portal, for one program only.
6. Prepare your documents — for the visa you need either two CIMEA certificates (comparability and authenticity) or a Declaration of Value (DOV). Pakistan, India, Bangladesh and Saudi Arabia use the apostille. Nigeria, Egypt, the UAE, Qatar and Kuwait need consular legalization at the Italian embassy.
7. Get the visa and enroll — upload documents to UniBo's International student administration, then get a tax code, health insurance and a residence permit in Italy.

Timeline for 2027/28: UniBo has not published dates yet. In 2026, first bachelor's rounds ran from February to late April or early May, and in CLaBE non-EU seats existed only in that first round. Have your test score and English certificate ready early. UniBo offices close for part of August, and the visa deadline for 2027/28 is October 31, 2027.

## What to know before you apply
**UniBo does not work with agencies.** The university states it has no recruiters abroad and no agreement with any agency, so you apply yourself. Our role: we assess your chances, prepare your documents, check your application before you send it, and build a plan B at other universities. UniBo staff write only from @unibo.it addresses, and neither UniBo nor ER.GO asks for prepayment for scholarships.

Other honest limits:
- **Seats for non-EU students are few.** In 2026/27: 32 of 190 in CLaBE (first round only), 20 of 180 in International Studies, 30 of 70 in International Management, and 20 non-EU seats against 140 EU seats in Medicine.
- **Medicine in English:** in 2026/27 admission was by test (IMAT). The format for 2027/28 has not been announced.
- **Apply early.** From 2026/27, universities can validate only up to their quota plus 20% on Universitaly. After that the portal blocks new validations.
- **ER.GO paperwork is strict.** Income certificates must come from official authorities, legalized or apostilled and translated into Italian. Self-declarations are not accepted.
- **Full scholarship coverage is not guaranteed.** In 2025/26 every eligible student got the ER.GO grant; the region warns this may be hard to keep.

## Rankings and accreditation
In the QS World University Rankings 2027, UniBo is #123 in the world and third in Italy. In QS by Subject 2026 it is #49 in Arts and Humanities and #73 in Social Sciences and Management. Nine subjects are in the global top 50, including Archaeology (#19), Petroleum Engineering (#24) and Law (#40). UniBo is first in Italy in 16 subjects.

International admission runs through Italy's national Universitaly procedure. For recognition at home, especially for medicine, check your national regulator's rules before you enroll; we help with this during the consultation.

## Housing and student life
Housing is the hardest part. ER.GO, the regional student agency, has only 1,842 places in Bologna for about 97,000 UniBo students. Of the free places, 6% go to non-EU first-year students. In 2026, ER.GO housing and scholarship applications ran June 23 to August 24; late ones were rejected.

A private single room cost around €585 a month on average in August 2026; budget roughly €950–1,250 a month in total. For the visa you need a hotel booking, rental contract or assigned dorm place, not a pending dorm application. ER.GO scholarship holders must earn 20 credits (CFU) by November 30 of the next year, or return the money.

<!-- block: textContent -->
## Student visa
You need a type D study visa from the Italian consulate in your country of residence. Key requirements:
- Validated pre-enrollment on Universitaly.
- €10,179.85 per academic year plus a return ticket; family funds (relatives up to the fourth degree) count, cash and pending scholarships do not.
- Housing proof, health insurance valid in Italy, fingerprints.

Only the consulate decides; a validated application does not guarantee a visa. After arrival, apply for a residence permit within 8 working days (€116.46 the first year). Details: [Italy student visa](/study-in-italy/italy-student-visa).

### How much does UniBo cost for students from Pakistan, India or Nigeria?
In 2026/27, citizens of non-OECD countries whose family income is earned abroad pay a reduced flat fee. It is €1,157.04 a year for a bachelor's and €1,357.04 for a master's. You only need a declaration of income. Fees for 2027/28 are not published yet.

### Does UniBo accept the SAT?
Some programs do. CLaBE (Business and Economics) selects only by SAT score, and International Studies uses SAT in one round. Other programs use CEnT-S, their own exam or a document review. Check the call of your program.

### What IELTS score do I need?
UniBo requires English at B2 level, and the Italian consulate checks B2 in all four skills. The exact IELTS or TOEFL minimum is set by each program.

### Can an agency apply to UniBo for me?
No. UniBo states it works with no agencies or recruiters, so you submit the application yourself. We help you prepare documents, check the application before you send it and plan alternatives.

### Does UniBo offer scholarships to international students?
The last UniBo call for international students, International Talents 2025/26, was for master's students only. No call for 2026/27 has been found. The main option is the need-based ER.GO scholarship, up to €7,171.11 a year in 2026/27.

### Can I work while studying in Bologna?
Yes, with a student residence permit you can work up to 20 hours a week and up to 1,040 hours a year. Do not count on a job to cover your visa funds: the consulate checks them in advance.
