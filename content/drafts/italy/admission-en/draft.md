If you need a student visa for Italy, you apply to a bachelor's or master's program through Universitaly, the official and free national pre-enrolment portal. The university validates your application, and only then can you apply for a visa at the Italian consulate.

Around that step sits a chain of paperwork: proof of 12 years of schooling, CIMEA statements or a Dichiarazione di valore (DOV), an apostille or consular legalization, a language certificate and, for some programs, an entrance test. The rules for international students cover two academic years at once, 2026/27 and 2027/28, although the Ministry of University and Research (MUR) can still amend them.

This guide follows the order in which applicants from Pakistan, India, Nigeria, Bangladesh, Egypt and the Gulf go through the process. Where official 2027 dates are not yet published, we say so.

<!-- block: textContent -->
## How Universitaly pre-enrolment works

Every applicant who needs a visa submits the pre-enrolment request (domanda di preiscrizione) only through this portal. There is no paper alternative and no fee.

- **One program per application.** For most school certificates you choose a single program. Italian law allows studying two degrees at once, but not on a student visa: the visa and residence permit are issued for one course.
- **Each university sets its own window.** The ministry gives no single opening date. Each university publishes its deadlines on its website, except programs with a national competition.
- **Quotas fill up.** Since 2026/27 a university can validate no more applications than its quota for international students plus 20%. After that, new validations are blocked; a place frees up only if someone is refused a visa. Apply early.
- **The university decides what it needs for enrolment.** Documents the consulate requests do not bind the university's decision.

Uni-Italia, a non-profit association, also helps with applications and visas through its centers abroad.

<!-- block: processBlock title="From application to arrival" -->
1. Choose one program and check its requirements and deadline on the university website.
2. Get your school certificate apostilled or legalized, with translations if required.
3. Order CIMEA statements or a DOV, after checking what the university accepts.
4. Take the language test or entrance test the program requires.
5. Submit the pre-enrolment request and upload your documents.
6. Wait for validation. Without it you cannot apply for the visa, except in two cases described below.
7. Apply for a type D study visa at the Italian consulate for your country of residence, by 31 October 2027 for bachelor's and master's programs.
8. Within 8 working days of arrival, apply for a permesso di soggiorno (residence permit) and complete enrolment.

## The 12-year schooling rule

For a bachelor's program you need a school-leaving certificate based on at least 12 years of education. If your country has a compulsory national university entrance exam, you also submit its result.

In Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, Nigeria and Egypt, school usually lasts 12 years, so most applicants meet the rule directly. After 11 years of school, current rules let you cover the missing year with a full first year at a university at home, one year of related post-secondary education, or a foundation course. A 10-year system requires two extra years. These routes give access only to the first year of a bachelor's program.

<!-- block: textContent -->
## CIMEA or DOV: recognizing your school certificate

For the visa, your certificate must come with either two CIMEA documents or a DOV. You do not need both.

- **CIMEA**: an Attestazione di comparabilità (statement of comparability) plus an Attestazione di verifica dell'autenticità (authenticity check). You order them online on the CIMEA-Diplome platform, from your own account.
- **DOV**: a declaration of value issued by the Italian embassy. It covers comparability only; without an authenticity note, that part is still missing.

CIMEA advises checking first whether your university needs its statements at all. If your country is a party to the Lisbon Recognition Convention, the comparability statement is free in the ARDI database (ardi.cimea.it), and you pay only for the authenticity check.

Otherwise, comparability costs around €150 and the authenticity check about €65, with an urgent option for roughly €100 extra. Standard processing takes about 60 working days, or around 30 when urgent. Universities with a CIMEA agreement sometimes get faster results.

Embassies may run their own checks on documents and language. Forged documents are reported to Italian prosecutors and other Schengen countries.

## Apostille or legalization: what applies in your country

Official papers must be certified before they are used in Italy. The route depends on whether your country is a party to the Hague Apostille Convention.

<!-- block: tableBlock -->
| Country | Route | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Pakistan | Apostille | Party since 9 March 2023 |
| India | Apostille | Party since 2005 |
| Bangladesh | Apostille | Party since 30 March 2025 |
| Saudi Arabia | Apostille | Party since 2022 |
| Oman, Bahrain | Apostille | Parties to the convention |
| Nigeria, Egypt | Consular legalization | At the Italian embassy |
| UAE, Qatar, Kuwait | Consular legalization | At the Italian embassy |

<!-- block: textContent -->
Legalization depends on embassy appointments and takes longer than an apostille. If you are from Nigeria, Egypt or the Gulf states above, build that time into your plan.

## Language requirements and study in Italy without IELTS

**English-taught programs.** There is no Italian test. The consulate checks your English: a certificate at B2 or higher in all four skills, or an equivalent certificate issued by the university. Each university sets its own minimum IELTS or TOEFL score.

**Italian-taught programs.** The university must check that your Italian is at least B2, usually with its own test, and uploads the result to your application. A B2 certificate from a recognized body (CLIQ: CILS, CELI, PLIDA and others) exempts you. Foundation courses require the course language at B1 or higher.

### Can you study in Italy without IELTS?

Yes, in the sense that IELTS is not the only accepted proof. Some universities accept other certificates or run their own English test, and the consulate accepts an equivalent university certificate. What you cannot skip is proof of B2 English in all four skills. Confirm in writing what your university and consulate accept.

## Entrance tests: CEnT-S and medicine

Many science, engineering and economics programs use CISIA tests. Since November 2025, the English-language TOLC-E, TOLC-I and TOLC-F have been replaced by one test, CEnT-S (CISIA English Test – Sciences).

- Taken at a university (@UNI) or at home (@HOME).
- Four periods a year: November–January, February–March, April–June and September–October, one attempt in each.
- Around 55 questions in about 110 minutes, across mathematics, reading and data analysis, biology, chemistry and physics.
- About €55 for the November 2025 – October 2026 cycle.

Not every program uses CEnT-S. Some universities run their own tests or admit on documents, so check each program's admission page.

For English-taught medicine, the 2026/27 intake still used an entrance test. The format for 2027/28 has not been announced; the ministry will publish it in a separate note. Follow our [medicine in Italy guide](/study-in-italy/medicine-in-italy) for updates.

## Admission calendar for 2027/28

Most 2027 dates are not published yet. Here is what the rules fix and what each university decides.

<!-- block: tableBlock -->
| Stage | Timing for 2027/28 | Set by |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-enrolment | Window published by each university | University |
| Language and entrance tests | Program dates; CEnT-S in four periods a year | University / CISIA |
| Visa, bachelor's and master's | By 31 October 2027, or earlier if the university says so | Ministry circular |
| Visa, post-graduate Master, PhD, foundation | No fixed deadline, but before classes start | Ministry circular |
| Residence permit | Within 8 working days of arrival | Italian law |
| Final enrolment | By 31 January 2028, or the visa is cancelled | Ministry circular |

<!-- block: textContent -->
Two exceptions allow a visa application before validation ("con riserva"): your certificate has not been issued yet (you need an official provisional certificate, not a self-declaration), or you must sit an in-person language or entrance exam. You then get a type D visa for 100 days. The regular study visa is a 365-day multiple-entry type D visa. See the full checklist in our [Italy student visa guide](/study-in-italy/italy-student-visa).

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="What to know before you apply" -->
- **Italian bureaucracy takes months.** The chain runs from pre-enrolment to CIMEA or DOV, the consulate and the permesso di soggiorno after arrival. Validation does not guarantee a visa appointment or a visa. The consulate assesses "migration risk": family finances, previous education and whether the program makes sense for you. We help you plan the order of steps.
- **The visa has a real financial minimum.** You must show at least €10,179.85 per academic year, plus money for a return ticket. Funds must be legal and traceable, ideally in your own account; relatives up to the fourth degree may support you. Cash does not count, and neither does one large deposit just before the visa. The consulate looks at account history and family income.
- **A DSU scholarship is not money in the bank.** DSU (diritto allo studio) grants depend on family income and assets and are paid by regional agencies. Being eligible ("idoneo") or having applied does not count toward the visa minimum. First-year students who miss 20 CFU (credits) by the deadline repay everything received and lose the fee waiver. See our [DSU scholarship guide](/study-in-italy/dsu-scholarship).
- **Housing is scarce.** For the visa you need a hotel booking, a rental contract, a hosting declaration or an assigned dormitory place; a dormitory application alone does not count. A private room averaged about €704 a month in Milan, €615 in Rome and €585 in Bologna in August 2026.
- **Private academies award different diplomas.** Art, design and fashion academies (AFAM and private institutes) do not all issue the same qualification as a state university. Before paying a deposit, check the program's official status and how the diploma is recognized at home.
- **Other visa requirements.** Health insurance with no limits on emergency hospitalization, and fingerprints for all type D visas.

### What is Universitaly?
It is Italy's official national portal for university applications. International applicants who need a visa must submit their pre-enrolment request through it, free of charge.

### When does pre-enrolment open for 2027/28?
There is no single national date. Each university sets its own window and publishes it on its website. Because universities validate only up to their quota plus 20%, apply as soon as your program's window opens.

### Can I apply to more than one program?
Each pre-enrolment request names one program, and the visa is issued for that one course. Ask us how to plan a backup option for your specific certificate.

### Do I need CIMEA or DOV?
For the visa, you need either the two CIMEA statements (comparability and authenticity) or a DOV from the Italian embassy. For enrolment, the university decides which documents it requires, so check before paying for anything.

### Do Pakistani applicants need an apostille or legalization?
Pakistan joined the Hague Apostille Convention on 9 March 2023, so Pakistani documents get an apostille. Nigeria, Egypt, the UAE, Qatar and Kuwait are not parties, so their documents need consular legalization.

### Is it possible to study in Italy without IELTS?
IELTS is not the only accepted certificate, and some universities run their own English test. However, English-taught programs require proof of B2 in all four skills for the visa.

### What happens after my application is validated?
You apply for a type D study visa at the consulate for your country of residence, by 31 October 2027 for bachelor's and master's programs. After arrival you apply for a residence permit within 8 working days and must enrol by 31 January.
