A Dubai student visa is a residence permit sponsored by your university, or by your parents if they already live in the UAE. It is usually issued for one year, renewed every year, and comes with an Emirates ID card that expires together with the visa.

You do not apply at a consulate yourself. Once you accept an offer and pay what the university asks, it applies for your entry permit. After you land, you do a medical test and Emirates ID biometrics, and the residence visa is issued.

Below is the process for January and September 2027 intakes: sponsors, documents, real costs in AED, work rules and options after graduation. Still choosing a university? Start with our [guide to studying in the UAE](/study-in-uae).

<!-- block: textContent -->
## Who can sponsor your UAE student visa

There are two possible sponsors. The first is the accredited university or college where you study. The second is your parents, if they are expatriates with UAE residence.

Parent sponsorship has limits set by the government:

- sons can be sponsored until age 25;
- daughters can be sponsored regardless of age, as long as they are unmarried.

For most applicants from India, Pakistan, Nigeria or Egypt, the university is the sponsor. Dubai's immigration authority, GDRFA, requires that you are enrolled at an accredited institution, that the university acts as your host and guarantor, and that it is licensed and approved by the relevant regulator. The university also issues a letter stating the length of your program. Family members can live with a student who can provide suitable housing for them.

<!-- block: textContent -->
### Entering the UAE before your residence visa

The safest route is an entry permit arranged by your university. Citizens of Pakistan, Nigeria and Egypt with ordinary passports generally need a visa in advance. Indian citizens can get a 14-day visa on arrival if they hold a US visa or green card, or a UK or EU residence permit; since 13 February 2025 this also covers residence permits from Australia, Canada, Japan, New Zealand, South Korea and Singapore.

If you are already in the UAE, for example on your parents' visa, the university can change your status inside the country, but this costs more.

<!-- block: processBlock title="Step by step: from offer to Emirates ID" -->
1. Accept your offer and pay — for example, Middlesex University Dubai asks for 25% of annual tuition (or the full outstanding balance) plus visa fees and deposit before it applies.
2. Send your documents — passport copy, photo, insurance details and the university's visa forms.
3. The university applies for your entry permit — submit at least 8 weeks before the semester. For September 2027, that means early July 2027 at the latest.
4. Travel to the UAE on the entry permit.
5. Do the medical fitness test — mandatory for residence applicants over 18. It includes a blood test and chest X-ray; unusual results lead to additional tests.
6. Give biometrics for your Emirates ID — the card expires together with your visa.
7. Receive your residence visa — GDRFA's service time is 48 hours once the file is complete. The whole cycle usually takes about 4–6 weeks.

## Documents for a Dubai student visa

The visa file is short. The bigger task is your education documents: the UAE is not a party to the Hague Apostille Convention, so an apostille is not accepted. Egypt and Nigeria are not Hague members; India and Pakistan (since 9 March 2023) are, but still need consular legalization for the UAE.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="What the university and GDRFA usually ask for" -->
- Ordinary passport valid for at least one year (Middlesex rule).
- Photo on a white background.
- Medical fitness certificate and medical insurance.
- Emirates ID application receipt.
- University letter confirming the program length.
- Bachelor's: legalized school certificate and transcript. Master's: legalized bachelor's degree and transcript.
- Under 18: notarized parental consent (Middlesex rule).

<!-- block: processBlock title="How to legalize education documents issued abroad" -->
1. Certification at home — the school, university or national education authority certifies the document, then a local notary.
2. Ministry of Foreign Affairs — stamp from the foreign ministry of the country where you studied.
3. UAE embassy — stamp from the UAE embassy in that country (or that country's embassy in the UAE).
4. UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MoFA) — the final stamp inside the UAE.

<!-- block: textContent -->
You also need equivalency. A school certificate goes to the Ministry of Education (MoE), "Equivalency of General Education Certificate from Abroad (Grade 12)". A university degree goes to the Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research (MoHESR). MoE asks for legalized documents, certified translations of anything not in Arabic or English, and originals of Grade 10, 11 and 12 certificates, which are returned. Processing times are not published, so allow a few weeks to a few months.

You can start studying while this is in progress. University of Birmingham Dubai asks for the documents during the first semester. [Heriot-Watt University Dubai](/study-in-uae/heriot-watt-university-dubai) gives one semester for equivalency and may withdraw a student who does not get it.

## What a UAE student visa costs

Most of the money goes on the university package and deposit. GDRFA fees plus the medical test, in AED:

| Visa issued for more than 2 years | +100 for each extra year |

## Renewal, working while you study and leaving the UAE

The visa is usually issued for one year and renewed annually by your university; Canadian University Dubai, for example, lists a renewal fee of AED 525 (2018 price list). The Emirates ID is renewed with it.

Work needs a permit from the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MOHRE). There are two routes:

- **Student Training and Employment Permit.** A company can hire or train a student aged 15 or over who is already in the UAE. It is valid for 3 months; the employer applies.
- **Part-time Work Permit.** Working for more than one employer requires MOHRE approval.

MOHRE service descriptions list a university no-objection certificate (NOC) for internships, a valid residence visa, a medical certificate and a MOHRE-approved contract. We could not confirm an official weekly-hours cap, so ask your university's careers office first.

When your studies end or the visa is cancelled, GDRFA gives a 60-day grace period to leave or switch to another visa.

## After graduation: Golden Visa and job exploration visa

The UAE does not appear to have a separate UK-style post-study work visa. Graduates usually move to an employer's work visa, a job exploration visa, or, if they qualify, a Golden Visa.

<!-- block: tableBlock -->
| Option | Who qualifies | Duration | Government fees (AED) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Golden Visa, graduates of UAE universities | CGPA of at least 3.5 (class A university) or 3.8 (class B, ministry classification); graduated no more than 2 years ago; degree attested by MoHESR | 10 years | about 1,640 (1,100 + 10 + 10 + 500 + 20), excluding medical test and Emirates ID |
| Golden Visa, graduates of foreign universities | University in the top 100 of a ministry-recognized world ranking; bachelor's GPA of at least 3.5; graduated no more than 2 years ago | 10 years | as above |
| Visit visa for job exploration | Graduate of a top-500 university by ministry classification; bachelor's degree or higher; MOHRE skill level 1–3 | 60, 90 or 120 days | 200 / 300 / 400 + 5% VAT; deposit 1,000 + 20 + 40 in charges |

<!-- block: textContent -->
For the Golden Visa, the key step is attesting your degree with MoHESR, which requires a federally licensed university. Check this before you enroll.

## What to know before you apply

**A KHDA license is not MoHESR accreditation.** Federally, MoHESR and its Commission for Academic Accreditation (CAA) license each institution and accredit each program. In Dubai's free zones, the Knowledge and Human Development Authority (KHDA) licenses universities. For UAE government jobs, regulated professions, further study at a CAA university or the Golden Visa, check that both the university and your program are MoHESR/CAA-accredited. The gap is narrowing: in February 2025 MoHESR licensed 16 Dubai universities, and most large branch campuses now hold both licenses.

**Recognition at home is a separate question.** Your degree is issued by the home university abroad. Rules in India, Pakistan, Nigeria or Egypt depend on your country, so check with the relevant authority at home, especially for regulated careers.

**The visa is a small part of the cost.** Bachelor's tuition at UK and Australian branch campuses is roughly AED 60,000–80,000 a year, and AED 129,000–157,000 at University of Birmingham Dubai (2026/27 prices). Living costs without tuition are about AED 5,500–9,000 a month; Middlesex partner residences start at about AED 2,200 for a shared room (September 2026). Compare [universities in Dubai](/study-in-uae/universities-in-dubai).

**Do not budget on part-time income.** Work needs a MOHRE permit, and no official weekly-hours rule is published in the sources we checked.

**Safety: check official advice.** In 2026 the regional situation changed several times, and in spring UAE universities briefly moved online. As of 21 September 2026, the UK FCDO does not advise against travel to the UAE but warns of regional risks, possible flight cancellations and airspace closures. Check your own government's current advice before booking.

<!-- block: accordionBlock title="Frequently asked questions" -->
### How long does the student visa process take?
Universities advise submitting visa documents at least 8 weeks before the semester starts. The full cycle, from entry permit to medical test and Emirates ID, usually takes about 4–6 weeks.

### How much does a UAE student visa cost?
A university visa package with Emirates ID and insurance usually costs about AED 5,500–7,500, plus a refundable deposit of about AED 2,500–5,000. Applying from inside the UAE costs more.

### Do I need to show a bank balance for the visa?
We did not find a government minimum bank balance for the student residence visa. Universities instead require payment before they apply. For example, Middlesex University Dubai asks for 25% of annual tuition plus visa fees and deposit.

### Can my parents sponsor my student visa if they live in the UAE?
Yes, if they are expatriate UAE residents. Parents can sponsor sons until age 25 and daughters of any age while they are unmarried. Otherwise, your university sponsors you.

### Can I work in the UAE on a student visa?
Yes, but only with a MOHRE permit. The Student Training and Employment Permit is valid for 3 months and is arranged by the employer; a Part-time Work Permit also exists.

### Does the UAE accept an apostille on my certificates?
No. The UAE is not a party to the Hague Apostille Convention, so documents need consular legalization through your foreign ministry, the UAE embassy and the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

### What happens to my visa after I graduate?
When your student visa ends or is cancelled, GDRFA gives a 60-day grace period. You can then switch to an employer's work visa, a job exploration visa of 60–120 days, or a 10-year Golden Visa if you meet the GPA and other criteria.
