An MBA in Dubai at a UK or Australian branch campus costs about 85,000–118,000 AED in tuition at current 2026/27 prices. Most master's degrees fall between roughly 76,000 and 175,000 AED, depending on the university and the subject. You study in Dubai, and the degree is awarded by the parent university in the UK or Australia.

Dubai is a large market for this. In 2024/25, 42,026 students studied at 41 private universities overseen by the Dubai regulator KHDA. Thirty-seven of those universities are branches of foreign universities, and 35% of students came from abroad.

Below we compare five campuses: Middlesex, University of Wollongong in Dubai (UOWD), Heriot-Watt, Birmingham and Curtin, including the risks worth knowing before you pay a deposit.

For the full country overview, see our [Study in UAE](/study-in-uae) guide.

## Entry requirements for masters in Dubai

You need a completed bachelor's degree. The required grade depends on the program. For example, Birmingham's MSc in AI and Machine Learning asks for a 2:1 Honours degree in a numerate subject.

English scores also vary by campus and program:

- Middlesex master's programs: IELTS Academic 6.5 (no band below 6.0), TOEFL iBT 87 or PTE Academic 58.
- Birmingham: IELTS 6.0 or 6.5, depending on the program's language group.
- Other campuses publish their requirements on each program page. We check them against your scores before you apply.

MBA and Executive MBA programs may also look at your work experience. The rules differ between programs, so we compare them for your profile during the consultation.

Your bachelor's diploma and transcript must be legalized for the UAE, and the UAE does not accept the apostille. India and Pakistan are members of the Apostille Convention, and Egypt and Nigeria are not, but all four still go through consular legalization. The usual chain has four steps: your university or education authority and a notary, your foreign ministry, the UAE embassy, then the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs. The ministry MoHESR then issues an equivalency for your foreign degree.

## Full-time, part-time and intakes

Full-time study is the standard route for international students. Curtin and Birmingham, for example, sponsor student visas only for full-time programs. Birmingham's master's programs take 1 year full-time or 2 years part-time. Curtin's master's programs take 12 or 16 months.

Part-time programs and the Executive MBA suit people who already live in the UAE on another residence visa. This includes many expat families in the GCC. Parents who are UAE residents can sponsor sons under 25 and unmarried daughters of any age.

## Student visa and work during your studies

The university sponsors your student residence visa. It is issued for one year and renewed each year. Entering on an entry permit arranged by the university is the most reliable route. Changing status from inside the UAE costs more.

University visa packages differ widely. Heriot-Watt charges 3,315 AED from abroad plus a 3,000 AED refundable deposit. Middlesex charges 5,555.22 AED plus a 2,500 AED deposit. Birmingham (7,068.10 AED), UOWD (8,237 AED) and Curtin (8,820 AED) include a refundable deposit in the total. Visa documents, timing and costs by nationality are covered on our [UAE student visa page](/study-in-uae/uae-student-visa).

A student visa is not a work permit. To hire or train a student, an employer applies to the labor ministry MOHRE for a Student Training and Employment Permit, which is valid for 3 months. A separate Part-time Work Permit also exists. Curtin states that students on its visa cannot work outside these permitted schemes. Plan your budget without counting on part-time income.

## After graduation: jobs and residence options

The UAE has no separate post-study work visa like the UK Graduate Route. Graduates usually move to an employer's work visa. Two other options exist.

- Job Exploration visa: for graduates of universities in the ministry's top-500 list, with at least a bachelor's degree and a qualification at MOHRE skill level 1–3. Fees are 200, 300 or 400 AED for 60, 90 or 120 days, plus 5% VAT and a 1,000 AED deposit.
- Golden Visa: a 10-year residence visa for graduates of UAE universities with a CGPA of at least 3.5 (class A universities) or 3.8 (class B), who graduated within the last two years and have their degree attested by MoHESR.

Because these campuses hold a federal licence, graduates can have their degree attested by MoHESR. After your student visa is cancelled, the Dubai immigration authority allows a 60-day grace period to change status or leave.

## What to know before you apply

### A KHDA licence is not the same as federal accreditation
KHDA licenses universities in Dubai's free zones. MoHESR and CAA accreditation works at federal level, first for the institution and then for each program. If you plan to work in the UAE public sector or a regulated profession, check that your specific program is CAA-accredited. The gap has narrowed: in February 2025 the ministry reportedly licensed 16 Dubai universities, including Middlesex and Curtin. Whether your home country recognizes the degree depends on its own rules, so check with the relevant authority before you enroll.

### The real cost is tuition plus about 12 months of living
Without tuition, a student in Dubai needs roughly 5,500–9,000 AED a month. A room in a partner residence costs 2,200–7,450 AED a month. As a rough example, a one-year MBA at about 85,000 AED plus a year of living costs comes to around 151,000–193,000 AED, before visa fees and deposits. The 50% student discount on Dubai public transport applies only to students aged 23 and under, which excludes many master's students. See our [cost of studying in Dubai](/study-in-uae/cost-of-studying-in-dubai) guide for the full breakdown.

### Work cannot fund your studies
Student work needs an employer-sponsored permit. Plan to cover tuition and living costs from savings, family support or a sponsor.

### The regional situation changed during 2026
In spring 2026, UAE universities briefly moved to online classes several times. As of 21 September 2026, the UK Foreign Office (FCDO, update of 24 July 2026) does not advise against travel to the UAE, but warns of regional risks and possible flight disruption. Check your own government's travel advice before you travel.

### Read deposit and scholarship terms before paying
Deposits differ in size and refund rules, and scholarship terms are often set for one intake only. We review the offer terms with you before you pay anything.

### How much does an MBA cost in Dubai?
At the five campuses on this page, MBA tuition is about 85,000–118,000 AED at 2026/27 prices. Middlesex charges 84,872 AED, and UOWD and Curtin charge about 115,000 AED. Add living costs of roughly 5,500–9,000 AED a month, plus visa fees and deposits.

### Is a master's degree from a Dubai campus the same as one from the UK or Australia?
The degree is awarded by the parent university. Birmingham, for example, states that the transcript mentions Dubai but the degree certificate mentions only the UK. Recognition in your home country depends on local rules, so check with the relevant authority.

### Can I study part-time on a student visa?
Usually not. Curtin and Birmingham, for example, sponsor visas only for full-time programs. Part-time and Executive MBA formats are for people who already hold a UAE residence visa through work or family.

### When should I apply for a 2027 start?
Start preparing documents early, because legalization can take from a few weeks to a few months. Birmingham, for example, has no fixed application deadline and advises applying as early as possible. Submit visa documents at least 8 weeks before classes start.

### Are there scholarships for international master's students?
Yes, as tuition discounts. Middlesex offers an International Study Grant, Birmingham a Postgraduate Chancellor's scholarship of up to 30%, and Curtin a Professional Excellence scholarship. Check the terms for your intake before you apply.

### Can I stay in the UAE after graduating?
Yes, if you find a job and move to a work visa. You can also apply for a Job Exploration visa if you meet its conditions. High-achieving graduates of UAE universities can apply for a 10-year Golden Visa.
