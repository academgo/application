Curtin University Dubai is the Dubai campus of Curtin University, a public university based in Perth, Western Australia. It teaches Curtin's own bachelor's, master's and PhD programs in Dubai International Academic City (DIAC). The campus is institutionally accredited by the UAE Commission for Academic Accreditation (CAA) and licensed by KHDA in Dubai.

It suits students who want Curtin's curriculum in business, IT, cyber security, design, psychology or engineering, with the option to move to Perth later. It also suits expat families in the UAE who want their child close to home.

It is a weaker fit if you need medicine or law, want a university dorm on campus, or plan to work part-time to pay your fees. If you have a school certificate from Nigeria, Egypt or Central Asia, expect to start with an 8-month Foundation year.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Fees on top of tuition" -->
- Application fee: 367 AED, non-refundable, credited toward tuition.
- Registration fee: 3,500 AED for every program when you accept the offer, non-refundable.
- Service fee: 500 AED per trimester or semester.
- Optional university bus: 2,100–3,675 AED per trimester, depending on the zone (Dubai, Sharjah, Ajman).

<!-- block: textContent -->
Tuition is charged by credit, so your offer may show an estimate. Curtin fixes your tuition for the length of study unless you repeat units. You pay at the start of each study period, with installments by post-dated checks or bank standing instruction; late payment costs 500 AED per month.

The refund rule matters for international students. A new student gets 100% of tuition back if they withdraw before the first study period starts or **do not get a UAE visa**. After the census date there is no refund, and the application and registration fees are never returned.

Curtin does not publish percentages for its merit scholarships (Academic Excellence, Women in STEM, Sports and others). It offers two full Dubai Top Achievers scholarships per year for all nationalities, plus grants such as the Financial Aid Bursary and Family Grant. Foundation tuition grants go up to 10,000 AED. You apply separately with a motivation letter, and different types of support cannot be combined.

## Programs and the degree you receive
Bachelor's options include the BBA (5 specializations), Bachelor of Commerce (Accounting, Finance, Marketing, International Business, double majors), Design, Interior Design, BSc Cyber Security, BSc IT, BSc Psychological Science, BAppSc Architectural Science and BEng Mechanical. Master's options are the Master of AI and Master of Cyber Security (12 months), the MBA and Master of Engineering Management (16 months), and a Graduate Certificate in Business Fundamentals. A PhD takes up to 4 years.

According to Curtin, you study for a Curtin University degree, with the curriculum and calendar aligned with Curtin's other campuses. You can transfer to Perth or another Curtin campus if you perform at an appropriate level and get an Australian student visa (Genuine Student requirement). The program generally has to exist at the target campus, so check this before you choose a major.

## Admission requirements for international students
Entry rules depend on your school certificate. The main routes for 2027 applicants look like this.

<!-- block: processBlock title="How to apply to Curtin Dubai for 2027" -->
1. Check your grades. India (CBSE or ISC): 60% in your best 4 subjects and 60% in English; state boards (HSC): 65% plus a separate English test. Pakistan (HSSC or Intermediate): at least 75% (825 of 1,100) plus a separate English test. A-Level: 2 A2 subjects with at least 5 points. IB: 24 points (BBA). US high school diploma: B average plus SAT 1000 or ACT 21. Some courses ask for higher grades.
2. Choose Foundation if needed. Direct bachelor's entry from a Russian, Saudi or UAE state certificate needs 1 year of university first. With a school certificate from Nigeria, Egypt, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan and many other countries, plan on the Foundation route. Foundation entry needs completed Grade 11 or 5 IGCSE/GCSE/O-level subjects at C or above. Foundation lasts 8 months, and you progress to the bachelor's once you meet the academic requirements.
3. Prove your English. Bachelor's: IELTS 6.0 with at least 6.0 in each band. Foundation: IELTS 5.5 (no band below 5.0), or 6.0 in each band for Arts and Media; IGCSE/O-level English at C or higher or SAT 1000 also count.
4. Apply online. Upload your certificates and transcripts, English results, 2 photos and your passport with the visa page. The university then sends an offer and an acceptance pack.
5. Legalize your documents. The UAE does not accept the apostille. The chain is: school or education authority and notary, your Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the UAE embassy in your country, then the UAE MoFA. You also need equivalency of your school certificate from the UAE Ministry of Education.
6. Pick your intake. In 2026, Foundation, business and IT started in January, May and September; engineering, psychology, design and master's started in February and September. Curtin has not published its 2027 calendar yet, so exact dates come closer to the intake. Leave at least 8 weeks for the visa before classes start.

## What to know before you apply
Many school certificates do not give direct entry. For applicants from Nigeria, Egypt and Central Asia, Foundation adds 8 months and 52,500–54,600 AED. We check your certificate against Curtin's table before you pay the application fee.

The first payment is large. For visa confirmation you pay the first study period (100 credits) before arrival, plus the registration fee and visa package. The refund rule covers a visa refusal, but plan the cash flow early.

Work will not pay your fees. On the university's visa you can only work through permitted schemes, such as the 3-month Student Training and Employment Permit arranged by an employer, or a part-time work permit.

Recognition at home differs by country and profession. Check with the relevant authority in India, Pakistan, Nigeria or Egypt before you enroll, especially for regulated careers.

The region saw disruption in 2026: in spring, UAE universities briefly moved online a few times. Check your government's current travel advice before you travel.

## Rankings and accreditation
The UAE federal regulator is the Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research (MoHESR), with the CAA inside it; Dubai free-zone universities are also licensed by KHDA. Curtin Dubai has both, and in Australia Curtin is registered with TEQSA.

In the THE World University Rankings 2026, Curtin University is placed 251–300. According to Curtin, it is in the top 1% in ARWU 2025, 4th for Mining Engineering in QS by Subject 2026, and rated QS Five Stars Plus.

## Housing and student life
Curtin Dubai has no dorm of its own. It works with partner residences: Yugo, The Myriad (DIAC), KSK Homes (DIAC) and DSOA University Residence, and does not publish their prices. For reference, for September 2026 Middlesex Dubai listed The Myriad at 3,050–7,450 AED per month and KSK Homes from 2,850 AED, with furniture, Wi-Fi and utilities included.

Outside tuition, plan roughly 5,500–9,000 AED per month for housing, food, transport and personal costs. The student Nol card gives 50% off public transport, but only up to age 23. The campus has been expanding by 50% since October 2025, and students from 90+ nationalities make English the everyday language.

## Student visa
Curtin Dubai sponsors the student residence visa for full-time students. For a new student from abroad, the package costs 8,820 AED (20–25 working days) or 10,200 AED express (15–20). It includes Emirates ID, medical test, insurance and a refundable 3,000 AED deposit. A change of status inside the UAE costs 7,140 or 8,520 AED.

The visa lasts 1 year; renewal costs 3,730 or 4,530 AED. Expat parents in the UAE can sponsor sons up to 25 and unmarried daughters instead. Full steps are on our [UAE student visa page](/study-in-uae/uae-student-visa).

### How much does Curtin Dubai cost per year?
Most bachelor's programs cost 182,700 AED for 3 years, about 60,900 AED per year, for starts from September 2026. Architecture and Mechanical Engineering come to about 65,100 AED per year. Add the registration, service and visa fees.

### Is Curtin Dubai accredited in the UAE?
Yes. It received CAA institutional accreditation on January 9, 2025, and holds a KHDA license. Check that your specific program is on the CAA list.

### Can I get into Curtin Dubai with CBSE marks?
Yes, if you have 60% in your best 4 subjects and 60% in English. Some courses ask for higher grades. State board (HSC) students need 65% plus a separate English test.

### What do Pakistani students need for Curtin Dubai?
HSSC or Intermediate with at least 75% (825 of 1,100) and a separate English test, such as IELTS 6.0 with 6.0 in each band. This certificate does not meet subject prerequisites, so check your chosen course.

### Can I transfer from Curtin Dubai to Perth?
Yes, if you perform at an appropriate level and get an Australian student visa. The program generally has to exist on the Perth campus. Scholarships from Dubai do not transfer to another campus.

### Can I work while studying at Curtin Dubai?
Not freely. On the university's visa you can only work through permitted schemes, such as the Student Training and Employment Permit or a part-time work permit. Do not count on part-time income to cover tuition.
