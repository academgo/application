The University of Wollongong in Dubai (UOWD) is the Dubai branch campus of the University of Wollongong (UOW), an Australian university that also runs campuses in Hong Kong and Malaysia. It opened in 1993 as the first international Australian university in the UAE. Graduates receive a degree awarded by UOW Australia, and the campus sits in Dubai Knowledge Park.

UOWD suits students who want an Australian degree in business, IT, engineering, media or psychology without moving to Australia, including expat families in the GCC. Strong school-leavers gain the most: high grades open a 3-year bachelor's and merit scholarships of up to 50%.

It is a weaker fit if 63,449 AED a year in tuition is beyond your budget, or if you want medicine, which is not on UOWD's fee lists. Students with WAEC/NECO or a CIS school certificate can only take the 4-year path.

## University of Wollongong in Dubai tuition fees
UOWD charges per subject, and you pay each trimester for the subjects you take. The yearly figures below assume a full load. The fee pages name no academic year: these rates apply to the Autumn 2026 intake, and 2027/28 rates are not published yet.

| Program | Tuition (Autumn 2026 intake, AED, VAT incl.) | Length | Approximate total (our estimate) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bachelor's: Business, BBA, Computer Science, Business Information Systems, Communication & Media, Psychological Science | 63,449 a year | 3 years (24 subjects) or 4 years (32 subjects) | about 190,000 / about 254,000 |
| Bachelor of Engineering (Honours) | 68,909 a year | 4 years (32 subjects) | depends on your subject plan |
| Nursing (Conversion), for registered nurses | 42,466 a year | 2 years | depends on your subject plan |
| Master's, most programs | 9,415.35 per subject | 10–12 subjects | about 94,000–113,000 |

| NCUK International Foundation Year | 60,000 per program | 7–9 months | 60,000 |

<!-- block: textContent -->
You can pay each trimester in full or use the UOWD Payment Plan: 50% before the trimester, 25% in the second month and 25% in the third. If UOWD sponsors your visa, the first trimester must be paid in full. Refunds are tiered (100%, 75%, 50%, then none), with cut-off dates in each trimester's calendar.

### Scholarships for new bachelor's students
The Academic Merit Scholarship is open to any nationality: 15, 25 or 50% off on the 4-year path, or 10, 15 or 25% on the 3-year path. Places are limited and awarded first-come, first-served. Thresholds for the 4-year path:

<!-- block: tableBlock -->
| Curriculum | 50% | 25% | 15% |
|---|---|---|---|
| CBSE and Indian State Boards | 95% | 85% | 80% |
| Thanaweya Amma (incl. Egypt) | 98% | 90% | 85% |
| IB | 40 | 35 | 30 |
| A-levels | AAA | two A grades | one A grade |
| Nigeria WAEC/NECO | not offered | 5 A grades | 4 A grades |

<!-- block: textContent -->
To keep the award, take at least 3 subjects each trimester and hold a weighted average (WAM) of 75, 65 or 55 respectively, with no fails; otherwise the percentage drops. The scholarship does not transfer to Australia. Pakistani HSSC is not listed separately, so we confirm eligibility with the university.

## Programs and the degree you receive
UOWD teaches bachelor's degrees in Business (including the BBA), Computer Science, Business Information Systems, Communication & Media and Psychological Science. The Bachelor of Engineering (Honours) has five majors: Civil, Computer & Autonomous Systems, Electrical & Electronics, Mechanical and Mechatronic. Master's options include the MBA, Engineering Management and Educational Studies; compare them in our guide to [master's and MBA programs in Dubai](/study-in-uae/masters-and-mba-in-dubai).

Every graduate receives a degree awarded by UOW Australia. The bachelor's has two routes. Advanced Entry takes 3 years (24 subjects). Direct Entry adds an Integrated Freshman Year and takes 4 years (32 subjects), with lower grade requirements. If you meet neither, the NCUK International Foundation Year gives a guaranteed place at UOWD or an NCUK university once you meet its requirements.

You can also transfer with credit to UOW campuses in Wollongong or Sydney. The terms are set by the university Registry, so check them before planning around a move.

## Admission requirements for international students
Minimum grades depend on your curriculum and path. The table covers bachelor's programs in Business, CS, IT, Media and Social Science.

<!-- block: tableBlock -->
| Curriculum | 3-year path | 4-year path |
|---|---|---|
| India CBSE Grade 12 | 10.5 points* | 7 points* |
| India ICSE | 77% | 55% |
| Pakistan HSSC | 88% | 65% |
| Nigeria WAEC / NECO | not available | 7+ subjects, max. one "Pass" |
| CIS school certificate | not available | average 3 out of 5 |
| Egypt / Arab countries (Thanaweya Amma) | not available | 70% |
| IB | 25 | 21 |
| A-levels | CCD | 2 AS or 1 A-level at D, plus 5 IGCSEs at D |
| American high school | C (70% / GPA 2.5) + SAT 1100 | C (70% / GPA 2.5) |
| Bangladesh HSC | 4.0 out of 5 | 3.0 out of 5 |

<!-- block: textContent -->
*English or Hindi plus your best 4 subjects, from A1 = 4 to D2 = 0.5. Engineering asks for CBSE 10.5, ICSE 77%, HSSC 88%, IB 25 or A-levels CCD, plus a math and physics placement test; below 60%, you take remedial subjects first.

<!-- block: processBlock title="How to apply to UOWD for 2027" -->
1. Match your grades — use the table above to choose the 3-year or 4-year path, and check the scholarship thresholds.
2. Take an accepted English test — Business needs IELTS Academic 6.0 (3-year) or 5.0 (4-year), TOEFL iBT 79 / 40 or PTE 54 / 42. CS/IT and Engineering need IELTS 6.0 in every band; Social Sciences and Media need 6.5. IELTS Online and TOEFL Home Edition are not accepted; the Accuplacer English Test at UOWD is an alternative.
3. Legalize your school documents — the UAE does not accept an apostille. The chain: school or education authority and notary, your Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the UAE embassy in your country, then the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs.
4. Apply for equivalency — if you did not study the UAE curriculum, you need school certificate equivalency from the UAE Ministry of Education. Until then, UOWD can admit you conditionally with a ministry Letter of No Objection.
5. Pick an intake — January, April or September 2027. UOWD has not published 2027 dates or deadlines yet; we track them and plan your documents backwards from your start date.
6. Pay and start the visa — sponsored students pay the first trimester in full, then UOWD applies for the entry permit.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="What to know before you apply" -->
- **Nigeria and CIS graduates get the 4-year path only.** That adds a year of tuition. The engineering table does not list WAEC/NECO or the CIS certificate, so we confirm engineering entry with the university first.
- The yearly price assumes a full load. You pay per subject, so your bill changes with your trimester plan.
- Scholarships are first-come, first-served, and the discount drops if your average falls.
- Recognition at home depends on your country's rules. Check with your ministry or professional council before enrolling, especially for regulated professions.
- As of September 21, 2026, the UK Foreign Office does not advise against travel to the UAE but warns of possible regional escalation and flight disruption. Check your own government's advice before you book.

## Rankings and accreditation
In the THE World University Rankings 2026, UOW (the parent university) is placed 201–250 worldwide and joint 11th in Australia. The ranking covers UOW as a whole, not the Dubai campus.

UOWD degrees are accredited by the Commission for Academic Accreditation (CAA), part of the federal Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research, and the campus is licensed by KHDA. Quality is overseen by UOW, which is registered with Australia's regulator TEQSA. All UOW business programs hold AACSB accreditation; Computer Science and Business Information Systems are accredited by the ACS. UOWD also lists CIM, CILT, PMI, a CFA Institute affiliation, ACCA, CIPD, CPA Australia and Engineers Australia.

## Student visa
UOWD sponsors your student visa and arranges the entry permit you travel on. The 1-year package costs 8,237 AED from abroad or 9,917 AED from inside the UAE, VAT included. It covers 2,000 AED of insurance, processing, the medical test, Emirates ID and a 2,500 AED Visa Maintenance Charge, whose unused balance is refunded after cancellation.

The entry permit takes 10–15 working days, and the rest usually 7–14 working days, sometimes a month or more. Your passport needs 6+ months of validity. Citizens of Pakistan, Nigeria and Egypt generally need a visa to enter. Expat parents in the UAE can sponsor sons up to 25 and unmarried daughters instead. Full steps are in our [UAE student visa guide](/study-in-uae/uae-student-visa).

<!-- block: accordionBlock title="Frequently asked questions" -->
### Is a UOWD degree Australian?
Yes. Graduates receive a degree awarded by UOW Australia, and in the UAE the degrees are accredited by the CAA. Recognition in your home country depends on its own rules.

### How much does UOWD cost per year?
For the Autumn 2026 intake, most bachelor's programs cost 63,449 AED a year and engineering 68,909 AED, VAT included. You pay per subject each trimester. 2027/28 fees are not published yet.

### What grades do Indian and Pakistani students need?
CBSE: 10.5 points for the 3-year path or 7 for the 4-year path; ICSE: 77% or 55%. Pakistani HSSC: 88% or 65%. Engineering requires the higher score plus a placement test.

### Can Nigerian students apply to UOWD?
Yes, on the 4-year path. You need WAEC or NECO with at least 7 subjects and no more than one "Pass" grade.

### Does UOWD offer scholarships to international students?
Yes. The Academic Merit Scholarship is open to any nationality: 15–50% off on the 4-year path or 10–25% on the 3-year path, while places last.

### Can I transfer from Dubai to Australia?
Yes, with credit, to UOW campuses in Wollongong or Sydney. Terms are set by the university, and merit scholarships do not transfer.
