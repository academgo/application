Heriot-Watt University Dubai is the Dubai campus of Heriot-Watt University, founded in Edinburgh, Scotland, in 1821. Students here graduate with the same Heriot-Watt degree as students in the UK. The campus opened in 2005 as the first British university campus in Dubai and has been in Dubai Knowledge Park since 2021.

It suits students who want a UK degree in English, two intakes a year, a fixed tuition fee and the option to study for a time in the UK or Malaysia.

It suits you less if your tuition budget is below about 78,000 AED a year, or if your grades send you to Foundation first (see the limits below).

## Tuition fees at Heriot-Watt University Dubai for 2027
Fees for the January and September 2027 intakes are published on each program page. International and UAE students pay the same rate.

<!-- block: tableBlock -->
| Program (example) | Fee | Intake on the price list |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation (Degree Entry Programme, e.g. Design Studies), 1 year | 56,280 AED | January 2027, September 2027 |
| Bachelor's, management (e.g. MA (Hons) International Business Management) | 78,650 AED per year | January 2027, September 2027 / January 2028 |
| Bachelor's, engineering and computing (e.g. BEng Electrical & Electronic Engineering, BSc Computer Systems) | 82,264 AED per year | January 2027, September 2027 |
| Master's (e.g. MSc International Business Management with Marketing, full-time) | 99,528 AED for the program | January 2027, September 2027 / January 2028 |

<!-- block: textContent -->
Program pages state these fees include VAT, while the general fees page says they exclude it, so we check the exact figure for your program. MBA fees we confirm for your case.

Your first-year fee stays the same every year until you graduate, if your fee status does not change. Payment:

- 300 AED non-refundable application fee.
- On accepting the offer: 10% of the first-year fee plus a refundable 1,000 AED security deposit.
- The rest: in full or in 4 equal installments per year; 25% of the annual fee is due before the visa application.

**Scholarships.** The Undergraduate Merit Scholarship gives 30%, 40% or 50% off the net first-year fee, spread equally across all years of study. Thresholds: CBSE or Pakistan FBISE HSSC 80%, 85% or 90%+; A-levels BBB, ABB or AAB; IB 28, 33 or 37+; American curriculum 85%, 90% or 95%. You keep it by scoring at least a C each year. WAEC and CIS certificates are not listed, so we ask the university about your case.

A 3% discount applies if you pay the full year upfront: by 1 September for the September intake, or by 1 January for January. The GCC and UAE Regional Student Grant (5,000 AED) is only for students from UAE and GCC schools. Its 2026 round closed on 12 September 2026.

## Programs and the degree you receive
Subjects: business, finance, engineering, computing and AI, data science, psychology, architecture and design, and construction.

- **Foundation (Degree Entry Programme)** — 1 year, if your grades do not meet Year 1 entry.
- **Bachelor's** — 3–4 years, awarded as MA, BSc or BEng (Hons).
- **Master's and MBA** — the MBA is delivered by Edinburgh Business School.
- **PhD**.

You receive the same Heriot-Watt University degree as graduates in Scotland, accredited and approved by Royal Charter in the UK.

Through Go Global, bachelor's students can move to the UK or Malaysia campus for a semester, a year or longer, if their program runs there. You must complete Year 1 and pass all exams, usually without resits.

## Admission requirements for international students
Year 1 entry depends on your school curriculum and program. The course director makes the final decision.

<!-- block: tableBlock -->
| Curriculum | Year 1 entry | Foundation entry |
|---|---|---|
| India (CBSE, ICSE, State Boards) | Management and design 65%; engineering 65% + 60% in math and physics/chemistry; computer science 65% + 60% math; data science 65% + 65% math; psychology 70% | 50% |
| Pakistan (FBISE HSSC) | 70% for management programs | Confirmed on request |
| Nigeria (WAEC, NECO, WASSCE) | Management, psychology, design: 5 subjects at B2. No direct Year 1 entry to engineering, computer science or data science | 5 subjects at C6 |
| UAE (Tawjihiyya) | 80% (Elite/Advanced 75%) | 68% |
| Russia, Kazakhstan (Attestat) | Grade 5 in every subject + IELTS 6.0 | Grades of 3 and above |
| A-levels / IB / American | CCD / 24 points / 70% + SAT 1000 (or ACT 20, or 3 AP at 3+) | — |

<!-- block: textContent -->
Indian students with strong results can join Year 2 directly: 75% for management, 85% (with 80% in math) for engineering and computer science. There is no Year 2 route for WAEC or the CIS Attestat. The university does not publish separate rules for Egypt or Saudi Arabia, so we check them before you apply.

<!-- block: processBlock title="How to apply for 2027" -->
1. Check your grades — compare your results with the table above and pick Year 1, Year 2 or Foundation.
2. Prepare English — IELTS Academic 6.0 (no band below 5.5), IELTS Online accepted, or Duolingo 120 (min 110) or PTE 50–57. Foundation needs IELTS 5.5 or Duolingo 100. Citizens of countries UKVI treats as English-speaking are exempt.
3. Collect documents — passport, school certificate and transcript. The UAE does not accept an apostille: documents need legalization at home, by your Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the UAE embassy, then the UAE MoFA.
4. Apply and pay the 300 AED fee — then receive your offer.
5. Accept the offer — pay 10% of the first-year fee and the 1,000 AED deposit.
6. Start the visa — pay 25% of the annual fee plus visa charges; processing usually takes 15–20 working days.
7. Arrive and get equivalency — the UAE Ministry of Education must recognize your school certificate. You have one semester to obtain it.

<!-- block: textContent -->
For January 2027, Welcome Week runs 11–15 January and classes start on 18 January. Deadlines are not published, and some programs, such as Electrical & Electronic Engineering and Data Sciences, start only in September.

## What to know before you apply
- **CIS certificates need top grades.** A Russian or Kazakh Attestat needs a 5 in every subject plus IELTS 6.0 for Year 1. Otherwise Foundation adds a year and 56,280 AED.
- **WAEC closes direct entry to engineering and computing.** Nigerian applicants go through Foundation for these subjects. Management, psychology and design accept WAEC for Year 1.
- **Equivalency is a real deadline.** Without it within one semester, the university may withdraw you. Start legalization at home before you fly; we prepare the document chain with you.
- **There is no campus housing.** Rooms are rented from private providers, with a contract signed directly with them.
- **Fees are not the lowest in Dubai.** Middlesex University Dubai lists 64,557 AED per year for bachelor's in 2026/27.
- **Recognition at home is decided at home.** The degree is issued by Heriot-Watt University in the UK; check recognition with the relevant authority in your country.
- **Regional situation.** As of 21 September 2026, UK FCDO does not advise against travel to the UAE but warns of regional risks and flight disruption. Check your government's advice before travel.

## Student visa
The university sponsors your visa after a signed unconditional offer and payment of 25% of the annual fee. Your passport must be valid for 6+ months. Current charges, which may change:

- New visa from abroad: 3,315 AED (express 4,828 AED).
- Change of status inside the UAE: 5,200 AED.
- Refundable deposit: 3,000 AED.
- University medical insurance, if you have none: 1,469 AED.

The entry permit is valid for 60 days; the visa lasts 12 months and is renewed yearly. GCC nationals need no student visa, and expat parents in the UAE can sponsor sons up to 25 and unmarried daughters. Full steps: [UAE student visa](/study-in-uae/uae-student-visa).

### How much does Heriot-Watt Dubai cost per year?
Bachelor's fees for 2027 intakes are 78,650 AED per year for management and 82,264 AED for engineering and computing. The Foundation year costs 56,280 AED. Your first-year fee stays the same until you graduate.

### Is the Heriot-Watt Dubai degree the same as in the UK?
Yes. Graduates receive the same Heriot-Watt University degree as students in Scotland. The Dubai programs are also accredited by the UAE's CAA.

### What CBSE percentage do I need?
Management and design need 65%. Engineering needs 65% plus 60% in math and physics or chemistry, and psychology needs 70%. With 50%, you can start with Foundation.

### Does Heriot-Watt Dubai offer scholarships for Indian and Pakistani students?
Yes. The Undergraduate Merit Scholarship gives 30–50% off the first-year fee, spread across all years, for CBSE or FBISE scores of 80%, 85% or 90% and above. You keep it by scoring at least a C each year.

### Can I transfer to Edinburgh or Malaysia?
Yes, through Go Global, if your program runs on that campus. You need to complete Year 1 in full and pass all exams. We confirm fee terms for the transfer period with you.

### When are the 2027 intakes?
Bachelor's programs start in January and September; master's also start in May. Classes for January 2027 begin on 18 January. Some programs, such as Electrical & Electronic Engineering, start only in September.
