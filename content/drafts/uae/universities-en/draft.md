Most universities in Dubai that international students apply to are branch campuses of foreign universities: you study in Dubai and graduate with the home university's degree. This page compares five of them side by side (Middlesex, Heriot-Watt and Birmingham from the UK, Wollongong and Curtin from Australia) by accreditation, tuition, intakes and entry routes.

In 2024/25, 42,026 students studied at 41 private higher education institutions supervised by Dubai's regulator, and 37 of those institutions were branch campuses of foreign universities. International students made up 35% of enrolments.

This guide is written for applicants from India, Pakistan, Nigeria and Egypt, and for expat families already living in the UAE. If your parents are UAE residents, they can sponsor your student residence visa: sons up to age 25, and daughters for as long as they are unmarried.

<!-- block: textContent -->
## KHDA vs CAA: what the two licenses mean for your degree

Dubai universities are regulated at two levels. The Knowledge and Human Development Authority (KHDA) licenses universities in Dubai's free zones, such as Dubai International Academic City (DIAC) and Dubai Knowledge Park (DKP). At the federal level, the Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research (MoHESR) works through its Commission for Academic Accreditation (CAA).

Federal recognition happens in two stages. First the university receives institutional licensure, then each program is accredited separately. A federal license generally lets graduates have their degree and transcript attested by MoHESR, which is how a qualification gets official recognition in the UAE.

Why this matters: if you plan to work in the UAE public sector, enter a regulated profession or continue at a CAA-accredited university, check that both the university and your specific program are accredited. The UAE Golden Visa for top graduates also requires a MoHESR-attested degree.

The gap between the two systems has narrowed. In 2025, MoHESR and KHDA signed agreements to speed up federal licensing, and in February 2025 MoHESR reportedly licensed 16 Dubai institutions at once. All five universities below hold both a KHDA license and CAA accreditation.

For Egyptian applicants, UOWD publishes a clear rule: Thanaweya Amma with 70% gives entry to its 4-year route. The other four universities do not list Egypt on the pages we checked, so ask admissions directly.

Most of these campuses ask for IELTS Academic 6.0 for a bachelor's degree. Minimum band scores differ: Middlesex and Heriot-Watt accept 5.5 in each band, while Curtin wants 6.0 in every band. UOWD does not accept IELTS Online.

## What studying in Dubai really costs

Tuition is only part of the bill. Universities also charge fees when you apply, accept the offer and get the student visa, which the university sponsors.

<!-- block: textContent -->
Living costs come on top. Without tuition, a student in Dubai needs roughly 5,500–9,000 AED a month (about $1,500–2,450), including a residence room, food, transport and personal spending. None of the five universities runs its own dormitory; they partner with private residences. Middlesex's partners for September 2026 charged from 2,200 AED a month for a triple room to 7,450 AED for a studio, with Wi-Fi and utilities included. Students aged 23 or younger can get a Nol card with 50% off Dubai public transport.

Scholarships in Dubai are tuition discounts, not cash. Heriot-Watt's Merit Scholarship gives 30–50% off first-year fees (CBSE 80–90%+), spread across all years. UOWD offers 15–50% on its 4-year route to any nationality. Birmingham's Chancellor's scholarship (15–30%) was published for September 2026 entry only.

For a full budget, see the [cost of studying in Dubai](/study-in-uae/cost-of-studying-in-dubai) and the [UAE student visa guide](/study-in-uae/uae-student-visa).

<!-- block: processBlock title="How to choose a Dubai campus in six steps" -->
1. Decide what the degree is for. If you need it for a UAE government job, a regulated profession or recognition at home, start from accreditation, not from rankings.
2. Check your school certificate against each university's entry table. Find out whether you go straight into year 1 or through a foundation.
3. Calculate the full cost: tuition for every year, plus any foundation year, the visa package, deposits and 12 months of living costs.
4. Match the intake to your timeline. Dubai universities advise submitting visa documents at least 8 weeks before the semester starts.
5. Start document legalization early. The UAE does not accept the apostille: your certificates go through your education authority, your foreign ministry, the UAE embassy and the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs.
6. Apply, accept the offer, pay the deposit and the first tuition installment, then the university applies for your entry permit.

## What to know before you apply

Here is what we tell every family before they apply.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Risks and how to handle them" -->
- **A KHDA license is not the same as CAA accreditation.** All five campuses on this page hold both, but other Dubai institutions may not. Check the university and your exact program on the CAA list before you pay a deposit.
- Recognition at home is decided by your own country's rules. Before you enroll, ask the relevant authority in your country how it treats a degree issued by a UK or Australian university and studied in Dubai. This matters most for regulated professions.
- The real cost is tuition plus housing. A three-year bachelor's at Middlesex costs 193,671 AED in tuition alone at 2026/27 rates; with a foundation year and living costs, budget for four years, not three.
- Work is limited and must be legal. Employers can hire students through an MOHRE Student Training and Employment Permit (valid 3 months) or a part-time work permit, and the employer applies for it. Curtin states that students on its visa cannot work outside these schemes, so do not count on job income.
- Documents take time. Legalization and equivalency can run alongside your first semester, but Heriot-Watt gives you one semester to get equivalency and may withdraw you if it is missing.
- The regional situation changed during 2026. In spring 2026, UAE universities briefly switched to remote learning several times. As of September 21, 2026, the UK Foreign Office (update of July 24, 2026) does not advise against travel to the UAE but warns of the risk of further strikes, flight cancellations and periodic airspace closures. Check your own government's travel advice before you travel.

### Do I get the same degree as students at the home campus?
Middlesex, Heriot-Watt, Birmingham and Wollongong state that Dubai graduates receive the home university's degree. Birmingham adds that your transcript mentions Dubai, but the degree certificate only mentions the UK. Curtin Dubai students also study for a Curtin University degree, with a curriculum aligned to other Curtin campuses.

### What is KHDA?
KHDA is the Knowledge and Human Development Authority, the Dubai government body that licenses universities in the city's free zones. It is separate from federal accreditation by MoHESR and its Commission for Academic Accreditation (CAA). For a degree that is officially recognized across the UAE, check that your program is CAA-accredited.

### Is there an American university in Dubai?
Yes. One example is RIT Dubai, a campus of the Rochester Institute of Technology, a private US university. All its bachelor's and master's programs are accredited by MoHESR, and it holds a KHDA license. This page focuses on UK and Australian campuses; ask us if you want a US-style program compared.

### Can I start year 1 directly with a CBSE, Pakistani or Nigerian certificate?
With CBSE, usually yes: Middlesex and Heriot-Watt ask for 65%, and Curtin asks for 60%. With a Pakistani HSSC, Heriot-Watt (70%), Curtin (75%) and UOWD's 3-year route (88%) offer direct entry, while Middlesex and Birmingham start you on a foundation. With WAEC, only Heriot-Watt publishes direct entry, and only for non-engineering programs.

### How much does a bachelor's degree in Dubai cost?
At the five campuses on this page, bachelor's tuition runs from about 60,900 AED a year at Curtin to 156,718 AED a year for AI and Computer Science at Birmingham (latest published fees). Add the visa package, deposits and roughly 5,500–9,000 AED a month for living costs. Heriot-Watt, Birmingham and Curtin fix your fee for the length of the program.

### Can I stay in the UAE after graduation?
There is no separate post-study work visa like the UK Graduate Route. Graduates usually move to an employer's work visa. Top graduates of UAE universities can apply for a 10-year Golden Visa with a CGPA of at least 3.5 (Class A universities) or 3.8 (Class B), within two years of graduating and with a MoHESR-attested degree.

### When should I apply for the 2027 intakes?
Middlesex and Birmingham do not set fixed application deadlines and advise applying as early as possible. Heriot-Watt has already published fees for January and September 2027, while the others are expected to confirm 2027/28 fees and dates closer to the intake. Leave at least 8 weeks for the student visa before classes start.
