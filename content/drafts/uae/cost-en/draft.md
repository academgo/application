The cost of studying in Dubai for an international bachelor's student at a UK or Australian branch campus is about 60,000–80,000 AED a year in tuition (2026/27 price lists), plus roughly 5,500–9,000 AED a month to live. University of Birmingham Dubai sits well above that range, at about 129,000–157,000 AED a year.

Scholarships here are tuition discounts, not cash. Several are open to applicants from India, Pakistan, Nigeria and Egypt, with published grade thresholds.

Most universities have not yet published 2027/28 fees; Heriot-Watt Dubai has already posted its 2027 prices. Every figure below shows the intake it applies to.

### How fees are charged

UOWD charges per subject each trimester, so your annual total depends on your study load. Curtin quotes one price for the whole program.

Heriot-Watt, Birmingham and Curtin fix your first-year rate until you graduate. Middlesex keeps the net fee the same, but passes on any VAT increase. Repeated units at Curtin and resits at Middlesex (500 AED plus VAT) cost extra.

### Budget for a foundation year if your certificate needs one

Middlesex Dubai places students with Pakistani FSc/HSSC or Nigerian WAEC/NECO certificates on its foundation program, not year 1. At Heriot-Watt, WAEC holders cannot enter engineering or computer science directly. UOWD accepts WAEC/NECO only on its 4-year route. Check your certificate before you compare prices.

### Master's degrees and public universities

Master's programs at branch campuses cost about 76,000–120,000 AED for the whole program. Examples: MBA at Middlesex, 84,872 AED (2026/27); MBA at Curtin, 115,500 AED. Birmingham's International Business MSc costs 166,356 AED (September 2026 intake). Details are on our [master's and MBA page](/study-in-uae/masters-and-mba-in-dubai).

Federal universities accept international students, but are not cheaper. From 2026/27, UAE University charges international bachelor's students 2,661 AED per credit hour, or 319,320–351,252 AED for a 120–132-credit program. Free tuition is for UAE citizens only.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Fees you pay on top of tuition" -->
- **Heriot-Watt:** 300 AED application fee (non-refundable). When you accept the offer, you pay 10% of first-year tuition plus a 1,000 AED refundable security deposit.
- **Curtin:** 367 AED application fee (credited toward tuition), 3,500 AED registration fee on acceptance (non-refundable), and a 500 AED service fee for every trimester or semester. Optional university transport costs 2,100–3,675 AED per trimester.
- **Birmingham:** no application fee. A 5,000 AED deposit on acceptance is non-refundable but counts toward tuition. The university states that fees exclude visa, travel, housing, food and insurance.
- **Middlesex:** a 3,000 AED deposit when you accept the offer; this is also how a grant is secured.
- **Payment plans:** Heriot-Watt allows 4 equal installments, or a 3% discount if you pay the full year in advance. Birmingham splits the year into 3 payments. UOWD takes 50% before the trimester and 25% in each of the next two months.

## Student visa costs by university

Your university sponsors your student residence visa and sells it as a package. Before it applies, it wants part of your tuition paid: 25% of the annual fee at Middlesex and Heriot-Watt, the full first trimester at UOWD, the first study period at Curtin. The visa is issued for one year and renewed annually.

## Student housing and living costs in Dubai

None of the five universities runs its own dormitory. Students rent rooms in partner residences; rent includes furniture, Wi-Fi and utilities, and longer contracts cost less per month.

<!-- block: tableBlock title="Partner residences of Middlesex Dubai, September 2026 intake" -->
| Residence | Monthly rent | Deposit |
|---|---|---|
| ESAW | 2,200 AED (triple room) to 4,300 AED (private room) | 3,000 AED |
| KSK Homes | from 2,850 AED; twin 3,050–4,699, single 4,999–6,550 | 3,000 AED |
| The Myriad (Dubai International Academic City) | 3,050 AED (double) to 7,450 AED (studio) | 3,500 AED |
| Yugo Dubailand | from 3,500 AED | not listed |

<!-- block: textContent -->
Each university negotiates its own rates: Birmingham's 2026/27 price for a shared double at The Myriad is 3,250 AED a month.

Numbeo data from September 2026 puts a single person's monthly spending at about 4,207 AED, excluding rent. A one-bedroom apartment outside the center rents for about 5,507 AED a month.

Students aged 5 to 23 can get a Nol student card (70 AED, valid one year) with 50% off public transport. Over 23, there is no discount; the regular monthly pass is 275 AED.

In total, budget about 5,500–9,000 AED a month in a shared residence room, including food and transport.

## Part-time jobs in Dubai for students: what the rules allow

Plan your budget without work income. A student visa does not give an automatic right to work: Curtin's policy says students on its visa cannot work except through permitted schemes. The legal routes run through the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MOHRE):

- **Student Training and Employment Permit.** A company can hire or train a student aged 15 or older who is already in the UAE. The employer applies, and the permit is valid for 3 months.
- **Part-time Work Permit.** It covers reduced hours. Working for more than one employer requires MOHRE approval.
- **Typical documents.** According to MOHRE service descriptions, you need a no-objection letter from your university, a valid residence visa, a medical certificate and a MOHRE-approved contract.

Confirm any weekly hour limit with your university before you accept a job; working without a permit puts your visa at risk. Part-time jobs in Dubai for students can help with pocket money, not tuition.

## What to know before you apply

**Licence and accreditation are two different things.** In Dubai, KHDA licenses universities in the free zones. At the federal level, the Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research (MoHESR) works through the Commission for Academic Accreditation (CAA): first the institution is licensed, then each program is accredited. If you need your degree for a UAE government job, a regulated profession or further study at a CAA-accredited university, check that both the university and your specific program are accredited.

All five universities on this page hold federal status, with programs accredited by CAA. The degree itself is awarded by the parent university in the UK or Australia. Recognition at home depends on your country's rules, so check with the relevant authority before paying a deposit, especially for regulated fields.

**Tuition is only part of the bill.** Living costs can exceed tuition; add a foundation year if needed, and upfront deposits. Student work permits are short-term and will not fund your studies.

**Check the regional situation before you travel.** In spring 2026, UAE universities briefly switched to distance learning several times. As of 21 September 2026, the UK Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office does not advise against travel to the UAE, but warns of possible escalation in the region, flight cancellations and airspace closures. Check your own government's current advice before you travel.

### How much does it cost to study in Dubai per year?
At UK and Australian branch campuses, bachelor's tuition is about 60,000–80,000 AED a year on 2026/27 price lists. Living costs add roughly 5,500–9,000 AED a month, so a first year comes to about 130,000–200,000 AED, more at Birmingham.

### Can international students get a full scholarship in the UAE?
Rarely. Khalifa University in Abu Dhabi covers up to 100% of tuition at its top tier, and Curtin Dubai gives 2 full scholarships a year to students of any nationality. Most Dubai scholarships are partial discounts.

### Can I work part-time while studying in Dubai?
Only with a MOHRE permit, such as the 3-month Student Training and Employment Permit or a Part-time Work Permit. The employer applies, usually with a no-objection letter from your university.

### Do I need to show a bank balance for a UAE student visa?
Universities focus on tuition payment: 25% of the annual fee at Middlesex and Heriot-Watt, the first trimester at UOWD. Ask your university which financial documents it needs.

### Do tuition fees in Dubai increase every year?
At Middlesex, Heriot-Watt, Birmingham and Curtin, your rate is fixed for the normal length of your degree. Prices for new intakes can rise: Middlesex went from 62,982 AED (2025/26) to 64,557 AED (2026/27).

### Is it cheaper to study at a public university in the UAE?
No. UAE University charges international students 2,661 AED per credit hour from 2026/27, and Zayed University 2,500 AED. Free tuition is only for UAE citizens.
