The University of Birmingham Dubai is the Dubai campus of the University of Birmingham, a UK Russell Group university. You study in Dubai International Academic City and graduate with a University of Birmingham degree certificate that mentions only the UK. Bachelor's programs take three years, master's programs one year full-time.

It suits students who want a UK research university's degree without moving to the UK and can budget roughly 129,000–157,000 AED a year for a bachelor's degree. It is a weaker fit if your budget matches other UK and Australian branches in Dubai, which charge about 60,000–80,000 AED a year. It is also not a route to the UK campus: there are no transfers.

### Scholarships

The Chancellor's Academic Merit Scholarship cut bachelor's tuition by 15–30% every year for September 2026 entry. The top 30% went to A-Level A*AA, IB HL 7,6,6 or CBSE above 90%; 15% needed ABB, IB HL 6,5,5 or CBSE above 75%. There is no separate application: every conditional offer is reviewed.

The 2027 terms have not been announced, so treat any discount as possible, not certain. A further 10% first-year discount in 2026 applied only to UAE residents who paid the deposit early.

## Programs and the degree you receive

Bachelor's programs run three years full-time in Dubai, in business and finance, economics, AI and computer science, engineering, psychology, media and more. The CAA list covers 25 bachelor's and 36 master's programs, including Civil Engineering BEng, Psychology BSc, the MBA, Cyber Security MSc and Master of Public Health. Master's programs take one year full-time or two years part-time and start in September.

The university's FAQ confirms that your transcript will say Dubai, while the degree certificate mentions only the UK.

On most bachelor's programs you can apply to spend your second year at the Edgbaston campus in Birmingham, subject to your grades. Four programs include compulsory UK years: Biomedical Science BSc and MEng Computer Science and Software Engineering (final year), MSci Biomedical and MEng Mechanical Engineering (last two years).

## Admission requirements for international students

You apply through the university's admissions portal, and decisions usually come within 10 working days. Your certificate decides the route: direct entry to year one or the Integrated Foundation Year.

<!-- block: processBlock title="How to apply for 2027 entry" -->
1. Check your grades. A-Level: A*AA to BBB depending on the program (Business Management AAB–BBB; AI and Computer Science A*AA–ABB with an A in Maths). IB: 32 points with HL 6,5,5 to 6,6,6.
2. Indian Standard XII allows direct entry. As a guide from the University of Birmingham's India page, CBSE/ISC needs around 75–90% depending on the program. The Foundation Year asks for 65% (CBSE, ICSE, West Bengal) or 70% (other boards).
3. Pakistan, Nigeria and the Gulf: the university publishes Foundation Year requirements for these certificates. Pakistan HSSC: grade B or 70%. Nigeria WAEC SSCE: five subjects at B3 or better, none below C5. Saudi Thanawiya and UAE Tawjihiyya: 85%. Egyptian certificates are not listed and are assessed individually.
4. Prove your English. Foundation Year: IELTS 5.5 in every skill, TOEFL 72 or PTE 59. Bachelor's: IELTS 6.0 or 6.5, TOEFL 80 or 88, PTE 64 or 67, depending on the program. Indian applicants can use a Standard XII English grade of 70–80% instead.
5. Upload your transcript (first-semester or predicted grades), certificate if you have it, personal statement, reference and English score.
6. Accept the offer and pay the deposit. The visa process starts after an unconditional offer.
7. Legalize your certificate. The UAE does not accept apostilles. The chain is: your school or education authority and a notary, your Foreign Ministry, the UAE embassy in your country, then the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs. The Ministry of Education then issues equivalency for your Grade 12 certificate. You can start classes meanwhile, but must upload the documents during the first semester.
8. Watch the timing. There is no fixed deadline, and September 2027 dates are not announced yet, so apply early once applications open. For the January 2027 Foundation intake, the standard visa deadline is 14 December 2026 (express: 21 December).

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="What to know before you apply" -->
- **Price.** Fees are well above the 60,000–80,000 AED a year at other UK and Australian branches in Dubai. Budget for the whole course plus living costs before you commit.
- **No transfer to the UK.** The exchange year depends on your grades. Programs with compulsory UK years mean a UK visa and UK living costs.
- **Foundation Year may be your route.** For Pakistani, Nigerian and Egyptian certificates, only Foundation entry is published. That adds a year and a fee.
- **Scholarships for 2027 are not confirmed.** Plan on the full fee.
- **Legalization takes weeks.** Start in your final school year, not after you arrive.
- **Recognition at home.** The degree comes from the UK parent university. Recognition in your home country follows that country's rules, so check with the relevant authority.
- **Regional situation.** UAE universities briefly moved online in spring 2026. As of September 2026, UK FCDO does not advise against travel to the UAE but warns of risks and possible flight disruption. Check your own government's advice.

## Rankings and accreditation

The University of Birmingham is ranked =98 in the THE World University Rankings 2026 and 68th in the QS World University Rankings 2027. Both rankings apply to the university as a whole; the Dubai campus is not ranked separately.

In the UAE, the university is licensed by the Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research (MoHESR), and its programs are accredited by the Commission for Academic Accreditation (CAA). It also holds a Higher Education Permit from Dubai's KHDA. As of 21 September 2026, the CAA register lists it as active. Graduates can attest their degree with MoHESR, which counts for public-sector jobs in the Gulf.

## Student visa

The university sponsors your residence visa once you accept an unconditional offer for a full-time program and register online. Its package costs 7,068.10 AED (standard) or 8,444.10 AED (express) from abroad, and 8,698.10 or 10,074.10 AED from inside the UAE. This includes a refundable 3,000 AED visa deposit and 1,031.10 AED medical insurance.

Citizens of Pakistan, Nigeria and Egypt need a visa to enter, so use the entry permit the university arranges. Everyone over 18 has a medical test in the UAE. Expat parents resident in the UAE can sponsor sons under 25 and unmarried daughters themselves. Details are on our [UAE student visa page](/study-in-uae/uae-student-visa).

<!-- block: accordionBlock title="Frequently asked questions" -->
### Is a Birmingham Dubai degree the same as a UK degree?
You receive a University of Birmingham degree certificate that mentions only the UK. Your transcript states that you studied in Dubai.

### How much does Birmingham Dubai cost per year?
For the September 2026 / January 2027 intake, bachelor's fees run from 128,695 AED (Economics BSc) to 156,718 AED (AI and Computer Science BSc) a year. Fees for 2027 will be published later.

### What CBSE percentage do I need?
As a guide, direct entry needs around 75–90% in CBSE or ISC, depending on the program. The Integrated Foundation Year asks for 65% in CBSE, ICSE or West Bengal boards and 70% in other boards.

### Can I transfer to the Birmingham campus in the UK?
No, transfers are not offered. Most bachelor's programs allow an exchange year in Birmingham in year two, depending on your grades.

### Is there an application fee?
No, applying is free for UAE residents and international applicants. You pay the 5,000 AED deposit only when you accept an offer.

### Can I work while studying in Dubai?
Employers can arrange a three-month Student Training and Employment Permit, and a separate part-time work permit exists. Do not count on work to cover tuition or living costs.

### Can I stay in the UAE after graduation?
The UAE has no UK-style graduate visa, so most graduates move to an employer's work visa. Graduates of UAE universities with a CGPA of 3.5 or 3.8, depending on the ministry's classification of the university, can apply for a 10-year Golden Visa within two years of graduating, with a MoHESR-attested degree.
