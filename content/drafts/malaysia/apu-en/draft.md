Asia Pacific University of Technology & Innovation (APU) is a private Malaysian university in Kuala Lumpur, best known for computing, cyber security, engineering and business. It started in 1993 as the Asia Pacific Institute of Information Technology (APIIT) and today teaches more than 80 programs in English, from Foundation to PhD. Many bachelor's and coursework master's programs come with a second degree from De Montfort University (DMU) in Leicester, UK.

APU suits applicants who want an English-taught IT, engineering or business degree at a mid-range private-university price. It also suits students with Standard XII, FSc/HSSC, HSC, WAEC/NECO or Thanaweya A'ama: these certificates give direct entry to year one of a bachelor's degree, without a Foundation year.

It suits you less if you need public-university prices, if you are counting on a merit discount with a national curriculum, or if you plan to stay and work in Malaysia after graduation with a Pakistani, Nigerian, Egyptian or Bangladeshi passport. We explain each of these points below.

## Asia Pacific University tuition fees (2026 price list)
APU publishes fees in Malaysian ringgit (RM). The figures below come from the APU Fee Guide 2026 for international students and do not include the 6% Service Tax (SST). Since 1 July 2025, SST has applied to all fees that APU charges non-Malaysian students, so add 6% to every line.

<!-- block: tableBlock -->
| Program (international students, 2026 prices) | Duration | Tuition |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation | 12 months (3 semesters) | RM 28,400; Engineering route RM 29,200 |
| Diploma | 2 years | RM 54,200 for the program (RM 27,900 per year) |
| Bachelor's: IT, Computer Science, Software Engineering, Data Science, AI, Cyber Security, Business, Accounting, Finance, Psychology, International Relations, Hospitality, Design, Media, Games | 3 years | RM 34,900 / 36,100 / 37,500 by year; RM 108,500 in total |
| Bachelor's: Architecture | 3 years | RM 35,800 / 36,800 / 37,800 by year; RM 110,400 in total |
| Bachelor's: Engineering | 4 years | RM 32,600 / 33,600 / 34,800 / 36,200 by year; RM 137,200 in total |
| MBA (full-time) | 1+ year | RM 42,000; deposits and other fees not included |
| Intensive English | 4 weeks per level, 7 levels | RM 2,750 per level |

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Other costs to budget for (2026, before SST)" -->
- One-time international student registration fee: RM 5,000, including the student card.
- Stage 1, paid when you accept the offer: about RM 3,600. It covers the EMGS visa fee, APU's application and processing fee, and airport pickup.
- Stage 2, paid after the Student Pass is approved: RM 2,000. It covers the EMGS medical check, first-year insurance, i-Kad, eVAL and the Student Pass.
- Personal bond: RM 1,000, returned when you finish your studies if there are no violations. Library deposit: RM 500, refundable (plus an RM 500 lab deposit for Engineering).
- Visa renewal every year: RM 1,100–1,450.
- DMU dual degree: GBP 450 for each level of study, paid at the start of that level.

<!-- block: textContent -->
You pay in ringgit. APU's reference dollar amounts use about 3.80 RM per dollar, while the market rate in September 2026 was about 4.05–4.10, so budget for the difference. The 2027 fees are not published yet; expect possible indexation. More on budgets: [cost of studying in Malaysia](/study-in-malaysia/cost-of-studying-in-malaysia).

APU Merit Awards give 10–30% off tuition to all nationalities, but only for A-Levels (AAA to ABB/AAC), O-Levels/IGCSE (4 to 9 A grades) and the IB (26 to 34+ points). CBSE, FSc, WAEC and other national certificates are not in the award tables. Keeping the award requires a CGPA of 2.50 or higher.

## Programs and the degree you receive
APU's bachelor's degrees cover computing (IT, Computer Science, Cyber Security, Digital Forensics, Software Engineering, Data Science, AI), business and finance (Business, Digital Marketing, Accounting, FinTech, Actuarial Studies), Psychology, International Relations, Design, Games, Media, Architecture, Hospitality and Engineering (Mechatronic, Mechanical, Computer, Electrical & Electronic, Petroleum).

Before a bachelor's, you can take a 12-month Foundation or a Diploma of 24+ months. At the graduate level, APU offers an MBA, MSc degrees in AI, Cyber Security, Data Science and Software Engineering, and PhD and DBA programs. Master's programs are assessed 100% through coursework, with no exams.

With the dual degree option, you graduate with two degrees and two transcripts, from APU and from De Montfort University. DMU has approved 25 bachelor's and 23 master's programs for it. Hospitality students get a dual award with Hotel and Tourism Management Institute Switzerland (HTMi), already included in the fee.

## Admission requirements for international students
APU publishes an official list that shows the entry level for each school certificate. Most Year 12 certificates from our main markets lead straight to year one of a bachelor's degree. Year 10 certificates lead to Foundation or Diploma first. Minimum marks depend on the program, and we confirm them for your certificate before you apply.

<!-- block: tableBlock -->
| Country | Direct bachelor's entry | Foundation or Diploma first |
|---|---|---|
| India | Standard XII (ISC, CBSE AISSCE, state board HSC) | Standard X (ICSE, CBSE AISSE, SSC) |
| Pakistan | HSSC / Intermediate (FSc) | SSC / Matric |
| Bangladesh | HSC / Alim | SSC / Dakhil |
| Nigeria | WAEC / NECO Senior School Certificate | WASC / GCE O Level (before 1989) |
| Egypt and GCC | Thanaweya A'ama (including Al-Azhar), Tawjihiya (Saudi Arabia, UAE, Bahrain), Oman, Qatar, Kuwait certificates | — |

<!-- block: processBlock title="How to apply to APU for 2027" -->
1. Check your entry level against the table above. Some programs add subject rules: Computer Science (Cyber Security) with A-Levels needs two passes (A–D), including mathematics and a science or ICT.
2. Prove your English. IELTS 5.0 (PTE 47) covers IT, Computer Science, Cyber Security, Accounting and Hospitality. Business, Psychology and Media need IELTS 5.5 (PTE 51); Banking & Finance and the MBA need 6.0 (PTE 59). English-medium schooling (IGCSE, A-Levels, IB) can give an exemption.
3. Prepare certified copies. Your school, a notary or a Commissioner of Oaths can certify them. Malaysia is not part of the Apostille Convention, so no apostille is needed; add certified English translations. You also need a Health Declaration Form and a 35×45 mm photo on white.
4. Check your passport: APU wants at least 24 months of validity from the intake date. Applicants from Oman, Sudan and Iran also need a no-objection certificate (NOC).
5. Apply 6–8 weeks before your intake. APU usually decides within 2–3 working days, and predicted grades can get you a conditional offer.
6. Accept the offer and pay Stage 1. APU files your visa application with EMGS; it usually takes 2–3 weeks after a complete file.
7. Watch the 2027 dates. APU runs several intakes a year, but the 2027 calendar is not published yet. We track it for you.

## What to know before you apply
- **Refunds are strict.** Application, processing and registration fees are non-refundable once you accept the offer. Tuition is refunded only if the visa is refused; after approval, never. Choose your program before you pay.
- **No program switch after the visa application.** A new program means a new Student Pass, so we compare programs with you before the offer.
- **Merit Awards skip national certificates.** With CBSE, FSc, WAEC or Thanaweya, budget for full tuition.
- **English has a time limit.** If you start with Intensive English, ministry rules give you no more than 12 months to reach the required level, or you return home. Take IELTS or PTE before applying if you can.
- **Staying after graduation depends on your passport.** Malaysia's 12-month Graduate Pass is open to citizens of 32 countries, including India and the GCC states, but not Pakistan, Nigeria, Egypt or Bangladesh.
- **The DMU degree does not open the UK Graduate Route.** That visa requires study in the UK; a dual degree earned in Kuala Lumpur does not qualify.

## Rankings and accreditation
APU ranks #=528 in the QS World University Rankings 2027, its best result so far, and #20 in the world for International Students. The APU MBA ranks #51 in Asia in the QS Global MBA Rankings 2027. APU is not in the Times Higher Education (THE) World University Rankings, only in the THE Impact Rankings.

In Malaysia, APU holds a 5-star (Excellent) SETARA rating in the Emerging Universities category. It is also a member of the Association of Commonwealth Universities (ACU).

Every program has a Malaysian Qualifications Agency (MQA) code; Computer Science (Cyber Security), for example, is MQA/FA12440. Check your program's code in the Malaysian Qualifications Register (MQR). Engineering degrees are accredited by the Board of Engineers Malaysia (BEM), which links them to the Washington Accord.

## Student visa
Every applicant from our main markets needs a Student Pass through EMGS, even if no visa is required for tourism. The route is: offer, EMGS application, eVAL approval letter, a Single Entry Visa through the official eVISA portal where required, arrival, and a medical check within 7 working days. Nigerian applicants also need a yellow fever vaccination certificate.

EMGS aims to process a complete file in 14 working days; plan for 4–8 weeks. The Student Pass usually lasts up to one year and is renewed yearly. Only master's and PhD students can bring family. The full guide is on our [Malaysia student visa](/study-in-malaysia/malaysia-student-visa) page.

### How much does APU cost per year for international students?
Under the 2026 price list, most bachelor's programs cost RM 34,900–37,500 a year, and Engineering RM 32,600–36,200 a year over four years. Add 6% SST, the RM 5,000 registration fee and visa payments. Fees for 2027 are not published yet.

### Can I enter an APU bachelor's degree directly after Year 12?
Yes. Standard XII, FSc/HSSC, Bangladeshi HSC or Alim, WAEC/NECO, Thanaweya A'ama and GCC Tawjihiya lead straight to year one. Year 10 certificates lead to Foundation or Diploma first.

### What IELTS score does APU require?
It depends on the program. IT, Computer Science, Cyber Security, Accounting and Hospitality need IELTS 5.0; Business, Psychology and Media need 5.5; Banking & Finance and the MBA need 6.0. PTE Academic is also accepted, and some applicants are exempt based on English-medium schooling.

### Is the APU degree recognized in the UK?
If you choose the dual degree option, you receive a separate degree and transcript from De Montfort University in Leicester, in addition to the APU degree. The DMU fee is GBP 450 per level of study. Studying fully in Malaysia does not qualify you for the UK Graduate Route visa.

### Can I get a scholarship at APU with CBSE, FSc or WAEC?
Not through the Merit Awards. APU's undergraduate awards of 10–30% cover only A-Levels, O-Levels/IGCSE and IB results. Master's applicants can receive 10–30% based on bachelor's CGPA.

### Can I work in Malaysia after graduating from APU?
Citizens of 32 countries, including India and the GCC states, can apply for a 12-month Graduate Pass to look for work. Pakistan, Nigeria, Egypt and Bangladesh are not on that list. Long-term work requires an Employment Pass arranged by an employer.
