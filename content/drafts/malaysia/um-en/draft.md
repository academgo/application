Universiti Malaya is Malaysia's oldest university and a public research university in Kuala Lumpur. It began in 1905 as a medical college in Singapore and became an independent university in Kuala Lumpur in 1962. About 32% of its roughly 18,900 students are international.

UM suits strong students who want a public university degree and a top-60 QS ranking. Computer science, engineering, business and medicine are the main English-taught routes for international applicants. Non-medical programs cost a fraction of the MBBS fee.

UM is a weaker fit if you need an undergraduate scholarship or finished only 11 years of school. It also does not suit anyone who wants to avoid Malay entirely: every international student must pass a Malay course.

## Universiti Malaya tuition fees for international students

UM quotes international fees **for the whole program**, not per year, and you pay by semester. The price list applies to the "2025/2026 intake onwards" (updated 22 October 2025). No 2027/28 list is published yet, and fees may change without notice. Fees exclude 6% SST, so add it where it applies.

The one-time admission fee (RM 800 for undergraduates) is already included. Master's programs are also priced per program: Master of AI or Cyber Security RM 48,100, Data Science RM 41,700, MBA RM 79,100.

Two costs sit outside tuition. The application fee is RM 500, non-refundable. After accepting an offer, you pay UM's International Student Processing Fee, which covers EMGS, eVAL, the Student Pass, medical screening, insurance and UM's visa handling. For Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nigeria, Egypt, the UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman and Bahrain it is RM 5,451 (one-year pass) or RM 6,151 (three years); for India RM 5,481 or RM 6,241. The visa fee of RM 17–50 comes on top, and the package is non-refundable even if the visa is refused.

Most programs are taught in English. Some humanities and social science programs also use Malay, and Malay Studies and parts of Islamic Studies run mainly in Malay or Arabic. Every international student must pass a Malay course before graduating.

You receive a Malaysian bachelor's degree from a public university, listed automatically in the Malaysian Qualifications Register (MQR). If you plan to practice medicine or dentistry at home, check your home regulator's rules before applying. ## Admission requirements for international students

UM asks for at least 12 years of schooling. Accepted qualifications include STPM, A-Level, IB, a high school or Grade 12 certificate, or a diploma with a CGPA of at least 3.00. Some programs add an interview and a test.

<!-- block: processBlock title="How to apply to UM for 2027" -->
1. Check the academic entry for your program. Computer Science example: A-Level B in Mathematics plus B in Physics, Computing, Chemistry, Biology or Further Maths; IB 30 points with 5 (HL) or 6 (SL) in math and science; a senior high school certificate with an average of 65% and at least 60% (B-) in math and one science; or a diploma with CGPA 3.00.
2. For MBBS, the bar is higher: A-Level A grades in Biology, Chemistry and Physics or Maths; IB 36 points; a foundation with A in Biology and Chemistry; or a degree with CGPA 3.7. You also need MSAT, BMAT, MCAT or GAMSAT, an interview, a test and a medical report.
3. Take an English test. Most programs: IELTS Academic 5.5, TOEFL iBT 46, PTE Academic 51 or MUET 4.0. Medicine: IELTS 6.0, TOEFL iBT 60, PTE 59 or Cambridge 169. Scores are valid for 2 years; IELTS Indicator, TOEFL Home Edition and IELTS UKVI are not accepted.
4. Prepare certified copies of certificates and transcripts, with an official English translation if needed. A school principal, government officer, Commissioner of Oaths or embassy can certify. Malaysia is not in the Apostille Convention, so an apostille does not replace this.
5. Apply and pay the RM 500 fee. You cannot reapply to the same program in the same intake, so check everything first. A decision usually comes by email in about 4–8 weeks.
6. Accept the offer and pay the processing fee. UM then files your Student Pass through EMGS: eVAL, then a single-entry visa if your country needs one, then arrival and medical screening.
7. Plan the timeline. Applications for the March 2027 intake are open from 7 September 2026 to 30 January 2027. For October 2027, UM has not published dates yet; in the 2026 cycle, undergraduate applications ran February to August and MBBS closed in June. UM publishes exact dates closer to each intake.

## What to know before you apply

**Scholarships are almost nonexistent for international undergraduates.** Of 13 entries on UM's undergraduate aid page, only one is open to them: a PTE-sponsored award that in 2026 was a single GBP 3,000 prize decided by an essay contest. Plan to pay the full fee. Postgraduates have more options: UM Bright Scholarship for PhD by research, and the government's Malaysia International Scholarship (MIS) for master's and PhD, which includes RM 1,500 per month.

The 12-year rule matters. If your school system ends after 11 years, you may not meet it directly. UM's 2025 booklet lists a one-year Foundation, but whether international students can join it in 2027 still needs to be confirmed.

Choose your program carefully. UM's older guidance says you cannot switch programs after accepting an offer; a new application would be needed.

Post-study work depends on your passport. Malaysia's 12-month Graduate Pass is open to citizens of 32 countries, including India and the GCC states, but not Pakistan, Nigeria, Egypt or Bangladesh. For GCC expat families, citizenship counts, not residence.

## Rankings and accreditation

UM ranks #58 in the QS World University Rankings 2026, up from #60 in 2025, and #12 in QS Asia 2026. In the THE World University Rankings 2026 it sits in the 201–250 band. THE subject results: Education =80, Business and Economics 101–125, Law 126–150, Engineering 151–175.

The Malaysian Qualifications Agency (MQA) oversees program quality. As a self-accrediting university, UM has all its qualifications listed in the MQR, where you can check your program.

## Housing and student life

UM's residential colleges (Kolej Kediaman) charge international residents RM 900 per month for a single room and RM 450 for a shared room of 2–6 people. Rooms have fans and shared bathrooms, and places depend on availability.

Off campus, UM estimates rent at roughly RM 800–2,200 per month; the campus is close to Bangsar, Mid Valley and KL Sentral. Overall, a student in Kuala Lumpur needs about RM 2,000–3,500 per month.

Part-time work is limited to 20 hours a week, with immigration approval, during holidays longer than 7 days. Do not build your budget around a job.

<!-- block: textContent -->
## Student visa

You need a Student Pass through EMGS even if you can visit Malaysia visa-free as a tourist. Your passport must be valid for at least 18 months. EMGS's official time is 14 working days for a complete file, but plan for 4–8 weeks. Medical screening follows within 7 working days of arrival, and insurance (included in UM's package) is compulsory. Travelers from Nigeria need a yellow fever certificate. Only master's and PhD students can bring family. Step-by-step details are on our [Malaysia student visa](/study-in-malaysia/malaysia-student-visa) page.

<!-- block: accordionBlock title="Frequently asked questions" -->
### Is UM taught in English?
Most programs are, including computer science, engineering, business and medicine. Some humanities programs also use Malay, and all international students must pass a Malay course before graduating.

### How much does UM cost for international students?
Under the 2025/26 list, whole-program fees start at RM 60,900 for arts, with Computer Science at RM 89,200 and MBBS at RM 673,200. Add 6% SST where it applies, the RM 500 application fee and the visa package of about RM 5,450–6,250.

### What IELTS score do I need for UM?
Most programs require IELTS Academic 5.5, TOEFL iBT 46 or PTE Academic 51. Medicine requires IELTS 6.0, TOEFL iBT 60 or PTE 59. UM does not accept IELTS Indicator, TOEFL Home Edition or IELTS UKVI.

### Does UM offer scholarships for international undergraduates?
Almost none. In 2026 the only one was a single GBP 3,000 PTE award decided by an essay contest. Master's and PhD students have better chances through MIS or UM Bright Scholarship.

### When is the next intake at UM?
Undergraduate intakes are in October and March. Applications for March 2027 are open from 7 September 2026 to 30 January 2027. October 2027 dates are not published yet. MBBS admits only in October, and in 2026 it closed earlier than other programs.

### Can I work in Malaysia after graduating from UM?
It depends on your citizenship. The 12-month Graduate Pass is open to citizens of 32 countries, including India and the GCC states, but not Pakistan, Nigeria, Egypt or Bangladesh. Long-term work requires an Employment Pass arranged by an employer.
