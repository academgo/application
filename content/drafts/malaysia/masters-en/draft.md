Masters in Malaysia are taught at public research universities such as Universiti Malaya and UTM and at private universities such as Taylor's and APU, in coursework, research and mixed-mode formats. Tuition for the whole program ranges from about RM 26,500 to RM 79,100 at the universities on this page (fee lists published 2025–2026), before visa fees, insurance and living costs.

Two things make postgraduate study here different from a bachelor's: master's students can bring their family, and the government's Malaysia International Scholarship (MIS) is open only to master's and PhD applicants.

Every international student needs a Student Pass issued through EMGS (Education Malaysia Global Services), so plan the visa timeline together with the application.

<!-- block: textContent -->
## Coursework, research or mixed mode: which format fits

The format changes how you study, how you are assessed and when you apply.

<!-- block: tableBlock -->
| Format | How you study | Assessment | Suits you if |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coursework (taught) | Scheduled modules, group projects, a final project | Assignments and exams; APU runs its master's on 100% coursework, no exams | You want a career step: MBA, data, AI, management |
| Research | Independent thesis under a supervisor | Thesis and examination | You plan a PhD or an academic or R&D career |
| Mixed mode | Modules plus a larger research project | Both | You want a thesis without giving up taught subjects |

<!-- block: textContent -->
Length depends on the program. UTM prices its master's programs over 3 semesters. APU describes its full-time MBA as "1+ year". Always check the duration on the program page, because it also sets how long your Student Pass must cover.

Deadlines differ by format, too. For September 2026, UTM closed taught and mixed-mode applications on 31 July but kept research applications open until 31 October. Expect a similar split for 2027; universities publish exact dates closer to each intake.

For research programs, agree a topic with a supervisor first; MIS, for example, asks research applicants for a proposal of at least 1,000 words.

Three things change the final number. Since 1 July 2025, private universities add 6% Service Tax (SST) for international students, and fee lists usually exclude it; Universiti Malaya also quotes fees without 6% SST. You pay in ringgit: universities convert to USD at an indicative RM 3.80, while the market rate in September 2026 was around RM 4.05–4.10. And 2027 fee lists are not out yet, so plan on 2026 prices with a possible increase.

Full price lists by university are on our [cost of studying in Malaysia](/study-in-malaysia/cost-of-studying-in-malaysia) page.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Budget beyond tuition" -->
- Application fee: RM 500 at Universiti Malaya, about RM 450 at UTM. At UCSI, the master's application fee is RM 3,600 and already includes the first-year EMGS payment.
- EMGS and immigration fees for the first year: about RM 2,700–3,300 by the official EMGS schedule. Universities often charge their own package instead; at UM it is RM 5,451 for a one-year pass, non-refundable.
- Medical insurance: RM 456–958 a year on EMGS plans.
- Personal bond: usually RM 1,500–2,000 depending on nationality, refunded after you finish.
- Living costs: about RM 2,000–3,500 a month in Kuala Lumpur. UTM estimates RM 1,000–1,500 a month in Johor Bahru with on-campus housing.

## Entry requirements for a master's degree in Malaysia

### Academic record
APU asks for a bachelor's degree in a related field with a CGPA of at least 2.50. Applicants with 2.00–2.49 go through an internal assessment. An unrelated degree with a CGPA of 2.00 or higher can work if you have relevant work experience or take prerequisite courses. At Taylor's, the benchmark is around 2.7 CGPA. Scholarships set the bar higher: MIS needs at least 3.00.

### English
Universities usually ask for about IELTS 6.0–6.5 for a master's. APU asks for IELTS 6.0 (PTE 59) for its MBA and 6.5 for the Master of Finance. EMGS accepts IELTS, TOEFL, MUET, Cambridge English and PTE Academic. Check the test version: Universiti Malaya does not accept IELTS Indicator, TOEFL Home Edition or IELTS UKVI.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Documents you will need" -->
- Degree certificate and transcripts as certified true copies, with a certified English translation if they are in another language.
- Passport valid for at least 18 months when you apply. APU asks for 24 months from the intake date.
- Photo 35×45 mm on a white background.
- Health Declaration Form for the EMGS application.
- Bank statements are not on the standard EMGS student list, but a university may ask for them.

## Popular fields and where to study them

- Business and MBA: offered at public (UM, UTM) and private (Taylor's, APU) universities; the table above shows how far prices differ for the same title.
- Computing, AI and data: APU (AI, Cyber Security, Data Science and Business Analytics, Software Engineering), Universiti Malaya (AI, Cyber Security, Data Science), Monash Malaysia.
- Engineering: the core of UTM, a public engineering and technology university; also UM and Monash.
- Psychology and niche subjects: Taylor's lists Clinical Psychology; UCSI runs more than 150 undergraduate and postgraduate programs.

APU also runs a dual degree with De Montfort University (UK) for coursework master's programs: two degrees, two transcripts, and an extra GBP 450 per level of study.

In the QS World University Rankings 2026, Universiti Malaya was #58 and UTM #153. Compare universities side by side on our [universities in Malaysia](/study-in-malaysia/universities-in-malaysia) page, or open the cards for [Universiti Malaya](/study-in-malaysia/universiti-malaya), [UTM](/study-in-malaysia/universiti-teknologi-malaysia), [Taylor's](/study-in-malaysia/taylors-university), [APU](/study-in-malaysia/asia-pacific-university) and [UCSI](/study-in-malaysia/ucsi-university).

Before you apply, check the program in the Malaysian Qualifications Register (MQR). Each accredited program has a code: "MQA/FA" means full accreditation, "MQA/PA" means provisional. APU's MBA, for example, carries MQA/FA8762 valid until June 2027.

## Scholarships for international master's students

The main government award is the Malaysia International Scholarship (MIS) from the Ministry of Higher Education. It is for full-time master's and PhD study only. It covers tuition for the whole program and pays RM 1,500 a month.

MIS is open to citizens of Egypt, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar, Kuwait and Bahrain, and of Commonwealth countries, including India, Pakistan, Nigeria and Bangladesh. Master's applicants must be under 40, with a CGPA of 3.00 (Second Class Upper) or higher and IELTS 6.0 or TOEFL PBT 550. Medicine, nursing and pharmacy are not priority fields. MIS works at 20 public universities, including UM and UTM, and several private ones. The 2026/27 round is closed, and dates for the next round have not been announced.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Other funding options" -->
- APU postgraduate Merit Award, open to all nationalities: 30% off tuition for a bachelor's CGPA of 3.7+, 20% for 3.5+, 10% for 3.2+.
- MTCP (Malaysian Technical Cooperation Programme), listed by UTM: a government award for applicants from developing countries, up to age 45, CGPA 3.00 and IELTS 6.0.

<!-- block: processBlock title="How to apply: from shortlist to Student Pass" -->
1. Shortlist programs and check each code in the MQR. Confirm the intake: Universiti Malaya accepts master's applications for March 2027 from 7 September 2026 to 30 January 2027.
2. Apply to the university and get an offer. Taylor's asks international applicants to apply at least 9 weeks before the intake; APU recommends 6–8 weeks.
3. Submit the EMGS Student Pass application and pay the fees. You must be outside Malaysia at this point.
4. EMGS runs an academic check and passes the file to immigration. The official timeline is 14 working days for a complete file; plan for 4–8 weeks, longer in peak months.
5. Receive your eVAL (electronic Visa Approval Letter), then apply for a Single Entry Visa on the official eVISA portal. Citizens of Pakistan, India, Nigeria, Bangladesh, Egypt, the UAE and Saudi Arabia all need one; it takes about 48 hours.
6. Fill in the Malaysia Digital Arrival Card no earlier than 3 days before you land.
7. Take the medical check within 7 working days of arrival. The university then submits your passport for the Student Pass endorsement.

## What to know before you apply

### Apostille does not work for Malaysia
Malaysia is not in the Hague Apostille Convention, so an apostille adds nothing here. Universities ask for certified true copies (notary, solicitor, public official or your own university) and a certified English translation. We check each university's format before you pay for translations.

### Everyone needs a Student Pass through EMGS
Visa-free entry is for tourism only: Indian citizens can visit visa-free until 31 December 2026, but not study. Study longer than 3 months needs a Student Pass through EMGS. We prepare and check the file with you; details are on our [Malaysia student visa](/study-in-malaysia/malaysia-student-visa) page.

### International fees are higher, plus 6% SST
APU's MBA costs RM 42,000 for international students and RM 38,800 for Malaysians, and private universities add 6% SST. Compare offers by the international price with tax and visa packages included.

### Work is limited to semester breaks
Students may work up to 20 hours a week, only during breaks or holidays longer than 7 days, with immigration permission obtained through the university. Breaking the rules can cancel your Student Pass; confirm current terms with your university. Do not plan to pay tuition from part-time work.

### The Graduate Pass is not open to every nationality
The Graduate Pass gives graduates 12 months to look for work, with no extension, and only citizens of 32 countries qualify. India, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman and Bahrain are on the list; Pakistan, Nigeria, Egypt and Bangladesh are not. It also needs a Malaysian sponsor earning at least RM 1,500 and a RM 600 fee. Otherwise, staying on means an Employment Pass arranged by an employer; we plan this before you choose a program.

### A UK degree earned in Malaysia does not give the UK Graduate Route
A dual degree or 3+0 program gives you a UK degree, but the UK Graduate Route requires applying from inside the UK on a Student visa after at least 12 months of study there. Choose a dual degree for the qualification, not for UK work rights.

### Family is allowed, with planning
Master's and PhD students can bring dependants. Immigration asks for a 3-month bank statement for dependant applications, so keep your account history clean.

<!-- block: accordionBlock title="Frequently asked questions" -->
### How much does a master's in Malaysia cost for international students?
On 2025–2026 fee lists, tuition for the whole program runs from about RM 26,500 at UTM to RM 79,100 for the UM MBA, plus 6% SST at private universities. Add first-year visa and EMGS fees (about RM 2,700–3,300) and living costs of about RM 2,000–3,500 a month in Kuala Lumpur.

### How long is a master's degree in Malaysia?
It depends on the program. UTM prices its master's programs over 3 semesters, and APU's full-time MBA takes "1+ year". Check the duration on the program page.

### What IELTS score do I need for a master's in Malaysia?
Most programs ask for IELTS 6.0–6.5. APU's MBA requires IELTS 6.0 and its Master of Finance 6.5. PTE Academic, TOEFL, MUET and Cambridge English are also accepted, but check which test versions your university allows.

### Can I bring my spouse and children?
Yes. Immigration allows master's and PhD students to bring dependants. You will need to show a 3-month bank statement for the dependant application.

### Is there a fully funded master's scholarship in Malaysia?
The Malaysia International Scholarship (MIS) covers tuition and pays RM 1,500 a month for master's and PhD students. It is open to Commonwealth countries, including India, Pakistan, Nigeria and Bangladesh, and to Egypt and several GCC countries. You need a CGPA of at least 3.00 and IELTS 6.0; dates for the next round are not yet announced.

### Can I stay in Malaysia after my master's?
Citizens of 32 countries, including India and the GCC states, can apply for a 12-month Graduate Pass. Citizens of Pakistan, Nigeria, Egypt and Bangladesh are not on that list and need an employer-sponsored Employment Pass to stay and work.
