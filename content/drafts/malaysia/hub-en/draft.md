<!-- block: textContent -->
To study in Malaysia, you need three things: a school certificate that meets the university's entry level, an English test score, and a Student Pass issued through EMGS, the official student visa agency. Tuition is paid in ringgit (RM), most programs for international students are taught in English, and private universities run several intakes a year.

Malaysia suits you if you want an English-taught bachelor's or master's at a moderate price, a dual-award degree with a UK university, or a fast start. With Class XII, FSc/HSSC, HSC or WAEC results, private universities such as APU accept you straight into year one of a bachelor's program.

It suits you less if you plan to cover living costs by working during the semester, or if your post-study plan depends on staying and working in Malaysia. The same applies if you want to become a doctor at home after a Malaysian MBBS. We cover each of these limits below, with a way around it.

## How much does it cost to study in Malaysia

The ranges below are for international students, taken from 2026 price lists, and exclude the 6% Service Tax (SST). Prices for 2027 are not published yet, so expect some indexation.

<!-- block: tableBlock -->
| Level and university type | Approximate tuition for international students (2026 price lists, excl. SST) |
|---|---|
| Foundation (1 year) | about RM 28,000–37,500 for the whole program |
| Bachelor's, private Malaysian universities | about RM 35,000–45,000 per year |
| Bachelor's, UK/Australian branch campuses | about RM 48,000–65,000 per year; medicine up to about RM 139,000 per year |
| Bachelor's, public universities (UM, UTM, UPM) | about RM 16,000–33,000 per year |
| Master's (coursework) | about RM 26,500–79,100 for the whole program, depending on university and field |

<!-- block: textContent -->
Public universities such as Universiti Malaya and UTM quote fees for the whole program, not per year, and you pay by semester. Universiti Malaya states on its program pages that its fees do not include the 6% SST. Private universities have charged 6% SST on all fees for non-Malaysians since 1 July 2025.

On top of tuition, budget for:

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Costs outside tuition" -->
- EMGS and immigration fees in year one: about RM 2,700–3,300 by the official EMGS fee schedule, including insurance, medical screening, i-Kad and the visa.
- Student Pass renewal every year: about RM 800–1,300.
- University visa packages, which are often higher than the EMGS minimum. Universiti Malaya charges RM 5,451 for a one-year pass for applicants from Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nigeria, Egypt and most Gulf states, and it is non-refundable.
- One-time international student fees: for example, RM 5,000 registration at APU, or RM 2,800 enrolment plus a RM 3,000 international student charge at Taylor's.
- A personal bond of about RM 1,000–2,000, depending on university and nationality. It is refunded after you finish your studies.

<!-- block: textContent -->
Universities often quote a USD figure "for reference" at an indicative rate of 1 USD = RM 3.80. The market rate in September 2026 was about RM 4.05–4.10 per dollar, so budget in ringgit and allow for the exchange difference. A detailed breakdown by university is on our page about the [cost of studying in Malaysia](/study-in-malaysia/cost-of-studying-in-malaysia).

Scholarships are narrower than many agencies suggest. The Malaysia International Scholarship (MIS) covers full tuition plus RM 1,500 a month, but only for master's and PhD students. Citizens of India, Pakistan, Nigeria, Bangladesh, Egypt and most Gulf states can apply. At bachelor's level, APU gives 10–30% tuition discounts for strong A-Level, IGCSE or IB results, but CBSE, FSc and WAEC are not in its table. If you plan a [master's in Malaysia](/study-in-malaysia/masters-in-malaysia), check MIS first.

## Types of universities in Malaysia

Universities in Malaysia fall into three groups, and the choice affects price, degree certificate and entry rules. Every program you consider should be accredited by the Malaysian Qualifications Agency (MQA). You can check the program's code in the Malaysian Qualifications Register (MQR). "MQA/FA" means full accreditation. "MQA/PA" means provisional accreditation, granted before a program is fully accredited.

<!-- block: tableBlock -->
| | Public universities | Private Malaysian universities | Foreign branch campuses |
|---|---|---|---|
| Examples | Universiti Malaya, UTM, UPM | Taylor's, APU, UCSI, Sunway | Monash (Australia), Nottingham, Heriot-Watt, Southampton (UK) |
| Tuition (2026, international) | about RM 16,000–33,000 per year, quoted per program | about RM 35,000–45,000 per year + 6% SST | about RM 48,000–65,000 per year |
| Degree | Malaysian degree | Malaysian degree; dual award with a UK or Australian partner in some programs | Degree of the foreign university |
| Intakes | usually 2 a year | usually 2–4 a year | depends on campus |
| Scholarships for international undergraduates | almost none | merit discounts at some universities | depends on campus |

<!-- block: textContent -->
A dual award means two degree certificates: one from the Malaysian university and one from its foreign partner. At APU, the partner is De Montfort University (UK), and the dual award costs GBP 450 extra per level. In a 3+0 program, you complete the foreign partner's degree entirely in Malaysia. In 2+1 or 1+2 programs, you transfer abroad, pay the home campus fees and need a new visa; transfer requires a passing grade. Compare all options on our [universities in Malaysia](/study-in-malaysia/universities-in-malaysia) page.

## Admission requirements and steps

All applications from international students go through EMGS (Education Malaysia Global Services), a company under the Ministry of Higher Education. Either the university or the student submits the file. The path from application to arrival looks like this:

<!-- block: processBlock title="From application to Student Pass" -->
1. Choose a program and check its MQA accreditation code in the MQR. Confirm the entry level for your school certificate and the English score required.
2. Apply to the university and receive an offer letter. Accept it and pay the university's first fees, which usually include the EMGS visa fee.
3. The application goes to EMGS. EMGS checks your academic documents and passes the file to immigration. The official processing time is 14 working days for a complete file; plan for 4–8 weeks, longer in peak months. You must be outside Malaysia when the application is submitted.
4. Receive the eVAL (electronic Visa Approval Letter). Applicants who need a visa then apply for a Single Entry Visa only through the official eVISA portal. Processing takes about 48 hours.
5. Complete the Malaysia Digital Arrival Card (MDAC) no earlier than 3 days before arrival, then fly in.
6. Complete the medical screening at an EMGS panel clinic within 7 working days of arrival. The university then applies for the Student Pass endorsement in your passport.

### Which school certificate gets you where

Most private universities publish entry levels by country, not by grades. The table shows APU's official entry list as an example, alongside UTM's minimum grades for engineering. Other universities set their own thresholds.

<!-- block: tableBlock -->
| Country | Certificate that can lead to a bachelor's (APU) | UTM minimum for engineering | Earlier certificate (leads to Foundation or Diploma at APU) |
|---|---|---|---|
| India | Standard XII (CBSE AISSCE, ISC, state HSC) | B1 / B+ in the required subjects | Standard X (CBSE, ICSE, SSC) |
| Pakistan | HSSC / Intermediate (FSc) | 75% in the required subjects | SSC / Matric |
| Bangladesh | HSC / Alim | A+ in the required subjects | SSC / Dakhil |
| Nigeria | WAEC / NECO Senior School Certificate | B2 in the required subjects | GCE O Level (before 1989) |
| Egypt and GCC | Thanaweya Amma, Al-Azhar certificate, Tawjihiya | 80% in the required subjects | — |

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Documents you will need" -->
- Passport valid for at least 18 months at the time of application. Some universities ask for more: APU asks for 24 months from the intake date.
- Photo 35×45 mm on a white background.
- Copies of certificates and transcripts. Universities usually ask for certified true copies and a certified English translation.
- English test: IELTS, TOEFL, PTE Academic, Cambridge English or MUET. For a bachelor's, you usually need IELTS 5.5–6.0; for a master's, 6.0–6.5. Medicine, law and psychology can require more.
- Health Declaration Form.
- Yellow fever vaccination certificate if you travel from a country with yellow fever risk, including Nigeria.

<!-- block: textContent -->
A bank statement is not on the standard EMGS or immigration list for students, though a university can ask for one. Most private universities have 2–4 intakes a year. Universiti Malaya admits undergraduates in October and March; applications for March 2027 are open from 7 September 2026 to 30 January 2027. UTM admits in September and, for some programs, in February. Exact dates for autumn 2027 are not announced yet; universities publish them closer to the intake.

## Malaysia student visa and documents

Visa-free entry for tourism does not allow you to study. Anyone studying in Malaysia for more than 3 months needs a Student Pass issued through EMGS. Immigration exempts only citizens of 51 countries from visas for all purposes, such as the UK, Australia and Canada. India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nigeria, Egypt, the UAE and Saudi Arabia are all on the EMGS list of countries that also need a Single Entry Visa after the eVAL.

The visa itself is inexpensive: RM 50 for Indian citizens and RM 20 for Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nigeria and Egypt. The Student Pass is usually issued for up to 1 year and renewed before it expires. Master's and PhD students can bring their family as dependants; bachelor's students generally cannot.

## What to know before you apply

These points rarely appear in agency brochures. Each is manageable if you plan for it before paying any fees.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Risks and how to handle them" -->
- **Malaysia is not a party to the Apostille Convention.** An apostille from home does not help on its own. Universities ask for certified true copies (by a notary, solicitor, government officer or your school) and a certified English translation. Each university sets its own rules, so we check them before you certify anything.
- **Everyone needs a Student Pass via EMGS.** Visa-free entry for tourists does not cover study. Do not buy tickets before you have both the eVAL and, if required, the Single Entry Visa. UTM, for example, tells students the same.
- **International fees differ from local ones.** At Sunway, BSc Computer Science costs RM 39,800 a year for Malaysians and USD 9,779 a year for international students. Private universities add 6% SST. Comparison sites often quote the local price, so we always work from the international fee schedule.
- **Work is limited to breaks.** Students can work up to 20 hours a week, with immigration approval, only during semester breaks or holidays longer than 7 days. The application goes through your university, and breaking the rules can cancel your Student Pass. Plan your budget without work income.
- **The Graduate Pass is not open to all nationalities.** It gives 12 months, with no extension, to look for work after graduation. Only citizens of 32 countries qualify, including India, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman and Bahrain. Citizens of Pakistan, Nigeria, Egypt and Bangladesh are not on the list. Eligibility depends on citizenship, so a Gulf residence permit alone does not help.
- **A 3+0 UK degree does not give the UK Graduate Route.** You can apply for it only from inside the UK, after at least 12 months of study there on a Student visa. A 2+1 route qualifies only if your UK stay reaches 12 months, so check the course dates. From 1 January 2027, the Graduate visa lasts 18 months.
- **A Malaysian MBBS does not come with a Malaysian housemanship.** Taylor's states that house officer positions in Malaysia are not available to international students, and that India's NMC requirements currently cannot be met in Malaysia. If you plan to practise at home, check your national medical council's rules before applying.

## Living costs, work and student life

A student in Kuala Lumpur needs about RM 2,000–3,500 a month. You spend less in a hostel room or shared house, and more in a private studio or with frequent Grab rides. In Johor Bahru, UTM estimates RM 1,000–1,500 a month with on-campus housing, food and local transport.

Housing is the biggest variable, and university halls are the cheapest start:

<!-- block: tableBlock -->
| University | Option | Monthly rent (official, 2026 or latest) |
|---|---|---|
| APU (Kuala Lumpur) | Satellite residence, triple sharing, utilities included | from RM 410 |
| Universiti Malaya | College room, 2–6 people | RM 450 |
| UTM (Johor Bahru) | Hostel, double room | RM 360 |
| UCSI (Kuala Lumpur) | On-campus hall, twin sharing | about RM 700 |
| Taylor's (Subang Jaya) | Off-campus twin / on-campus twin en-suite | RM 1,250 / RM 1,700 |

<!-- block: textContent -->
Check payment terms before booking. Taylor's residences, for example, require a deposit and six months' rent in advance. Medical insurance is compulsory for the whole study period and must run at least 12 months per policy. EMGS packages cost RM 456–958 a year, and the arrival medical screening costs RM 250.

Work rules are covered above. Current immigration guidance is worded more broadly, so confirm the exact conditions with your university.

<!-- block: accordionBlock title="Frequently asked questions" -->
### What does a year of university in Malaysia cost?
Based on 2026 price lists, bachelor's tuition for international students is about RM 16,000–33,000 a year at public universities and RM 35,000–45,000 at private ones, before 6% SST. Add EMGS and visa fees of about RM 2,700–3,300 in year one, plus living costs.

### Do I need IELTS for Malaysian universities?
You need proof of English: IELTS, TOEFL, PTE Academic, Cambridge English or MUET. For a bachelor's, the usual minimum is IELTS 5.5–6.0; some programs at APU start at 5.0. Students taught in English at school, for example with A-Levels or IB, can often apply for an exemption.

### Can I work while studying in Malaysia?
Yes, but only up to 20 hours a week during semester breaks or holidays longer than 7 days, and only with immigration approval arranged through your university. Do not rely on work income to pay tuition or rent.

### Can I stay in Malaysia after graduation?
Graduates from 32 countries, including India and the Gulf states, can apply for a 12-month Graduate Pass to look for work. Citizens of Pakistan, Nigeria, Egypt and Bangladesh are not eligible. Everyone else needs an Employment Pass arranged by an employer.

### How long does the Malaysia student visa take?
EMGS officially processes a complete file in 14 working days. In practice, plan for 4–8 weeks from application to eVAL, and longer in peak months. The Single Entry Visa through the eVISA portal then takes about 48 hours.

### Do I need an apostille for my documents?
No. Malaysia is not a party to the Apostille Convention, so an apostille does not replace the university's requirements. Universities ask for certified true copies and a certified English translation, and each university sets its own certification rules.

### Are Malaysian degrees recognized?
Check that your program is MQA-accredited by its code in the MQR. Recognition at home is decided by your country's authorities and can be restricted for regulated professions such as medicine. We check this for your country before you apply.
