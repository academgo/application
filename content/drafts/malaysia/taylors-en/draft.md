Taylor's University is a private Malaysian university on the Lakeside Campus in Subang Jaya, Selangor, next to Kuala Lumpur. It grew out of Taylor's College, which dates back to 1969, and it is a Malaysian institution in its own right, not a branch of a foreign university. In the QS World University Rankings 2027 it is placed #272.

It suits students who want a ranked private university, three intakes a year and dual awards with UK, Australian or New Zealand partners. It suits you less if your budget is tight, if you are counting on a scholarship, or if you plan to study medicine and then practice in India.

For the full country overview, see our [Study in Malaysia](/study-in-malaysia) guide.

## Programs and the degree you receive
Taylor's teaches Foundation, Diploma (for example, Culinary and Hotel Management), an American Degree Transfer Program, bachelor's, master's (MBA, Master of Management, Applied Computing, Clinical Psychology and others) and PhD. Its main fields are Hospitality and Tourism, Culinary Arts, Business and Accounting, Computer Science, Architecture and Design, Medicine, Pharmacy, Engineering, Law and Media.

Foundation is run by Taylor's College, a separate institution in the same group. You move to the university only if you meet your degree program's entry requirements.

Some programs are dual awards, with documents from two universities:

- **Computer Science** — with UWE Bristol (UK): certificates and transcripts from both. Data Science, Cyber Security, Mobile Computing and AI specializations are dual award.
- **Business** — with UWE (Bristol Business School) and QUT (Australia).
- **Accounting and Finance** — 3+0 or 2+1 with UWE; 2+1 with the University of Canterbury (New Zealand).
- **Hospitality and Culinary** — according to the university, with Université Toulouse – Jean Jaurès (France).

As a self-accrediting university, Taylor's has its qualifications listed in the Malaysian Qualifications Register (MQR) automatically. Check your program's code in the MQR before applying; it matters for recognition at home.

## Admission requirements for international students
Taylor's publishes exact grade thresholds only for some school systems. Here is the process step by step.

<!-- block: processBlock title="How to apply to Taylor's University for 2027" -->
1. Check your grades. For a bachelor's degree: A Level from DD; IB from 24 points (Computer Science) or 26 (Accounting); AUSMAT ATAR 55–60; other Year 12 certificates with a CGPA of at least 2.50 (Accounting), or any qualification the Malaysian Government recognizes as equivalent. For Indian students, Taylor's says Class 12 graduates can usually enter a bachelor's directly with 65–95% in at least two subjects, depending on the program. For Pakistan (FSc/HSSC), Nigeria (WAEC), Bangladesh (HSC) and Egypt, we check your certificate against the program with the admissions team. If your certificate does not qualify directly, Foundation takes O Level with 5 credits or an equivalent.
2. Check your English. Computer Science needs IELTS 5.5; Accounting and Finance IELTS 5.0 or MUET 3.5; MBBS IELTS 6.0 or TOEFL iBT 60. Foundation needs IELTS 4.0 or TOEFL iBT 31, or Year 11 taught in English. You may be exempt if your pre-university study or diploma was in English. Without the required score, you can take Intensive English: RM 4,660 per 12-week level, intakes in January, May and September.
3. Prepare documents from O Level upward, with official English translations. Malaysia is not part of the Apostille Convention, so an apostille alone does not help: universities ask for certified true copies. For the visa you also need a passport valid for at least 18 months, a 35×45 mm photo on a white background and a health declaration form.
4. Apply online through the TOPAS portal and receive a conditional or final offer.
5. Pay through Flywire and receive your Letter of Acceptance. Taylor's then files your visa application with EMGS.
6. Keep to the timeline. International students should apply at least 9 weeks before the intake, because the eVAL (visa approval letter) takes 6–8 weeks. For 2027, target the February, April or September intake; Taylor's publishes exact dates closer to each intake.

MBBS also requires A Level BBB, ABC or AAC in Biology, Chemistry and Physics or Maths, or IB from 33 with Biology, Chemistry and Maths at HL, plus an interview, a medical check and a minimum age of 18.

## What to know before you apply
**MBBS is not a route to practicing in India or Malaysia.** Taylor's states that house officer (internship) positions in Malaysia are closed to international students, and that India's NMC requirements are currently not possible to meet in Malaysia. If you plan to practice at home, check your national medical regulator's rules before paying; we help you compare medical programs that fit your plan.

**Scholarships are practically closed to outside applicants.** Taylor's scholarships, including the 50% and 70% Merit Scholarship, are for Malaysians and for international students progressing within Taylor's Education Group. Plan your budget on the full fee.

**Refunds are limited.** The enrolment fee and International Student Charge are never refunded. If you withdraw before the semester starts, you get a refund minus RM 500; from the third week of the semester, nothing is refunded. Late payment costs RM 100 per week.

**Post-study work depends on your passport.** Malaysia's 12-month Graduate Pass is open to citizens of 32 countries, including India and the GCC states, but not Pakistan, Nigeria, Egypt or Bangladesh. The UK Graduate Route is also not available after a 3+0 dual award, because it requires at least 12 months of study in the UK.

**Part-time work is restricted.** Students can work up to 20 hours a week only during breaks longer than 7 days, with immigration approval.

## Student visa
You need a Student Pass issued through EMGS, even if you can visit Malaysia visa-free as a tourist. Taylor's files the application after you pay. EMGS officially takes 14 working days for a complete file; in practice, plan for 4–8 weeks. After the eVAL is approved, students from countries such as Pakistan, Bangladesh and Nigeria get a Single Entry Visa through the official eVISA portal, applying from outside Malaysia. Within 7 working days of arrival, you pass a medical check. Full steps are in our [Malaysia student visa guide](/study-in-malaysia/malaysia-student-visa).

<!-- block: accordionBlock title="Frequently asked questions" -->
### How much does Taylor's University cost for international students?
Under the 2026 price list, a 3-year Computer Science degree costs RM 152,187 with all international fees and 6% SST; MBBS costs RM 512,760 for 5 years. The 2027 prices are not published yet.

### What are the Taylor's University entry requirements?
For a bachelor's degree: A Level from DD, IB from 24–26 points, or an equivalent Year 12 certificate, plus IELTS 5.0–6.0 depending on the program. If you do not qualify directly, start with Foundation at Taylor's College.

### Can I get a scholarship at Taylor's?
Rarely. Taylor's scholarships are for Malaysians and for students progressing within Taylor's Education Group, so most international applicants pay the full fee.

### Is Taylor's MBBS recognized in India?
Taylor's itself states that meeting NMC requirements is currently not possible in Malaysia, because international students cannot do their internship there.

### When are the intakes at Taylor's University?
Most bachelor's programs start in February, April and September; MBBS in April and September. Apply at least 9 weeks before the intake to leave time for the visa.

### Is Taylor's a foreign branch campus?
No. It is a private Malaysian university, though some programs are dual awards with partners such as UWE Bristol and QUT.
