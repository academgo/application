UCSI University is a private Malaysian university founded in 1986, with its main campus in Cheras, Kuala Lumpur, and two more campuses in Bandar Springhill and Kuching. It offers over 150 programs from Foundation to PhD, including medicine, pharmacy, engineering, computer science, business, hospitality and music. In the QS World University Rankings 2027 it is placed #282.

UCSI suits students who want three intakes a year, a strong music or hospitality program, or a pathway to finish a degree in the UK or Australia. It suits you less if you need a guaranteed route to a medical license at home, a budget below typical private-university prices, or two diplomas: UCSI offers transfer pathways, not dual awards.

## UCSI University fees for international students
UCSI has no separate fee schedule for international students. Each program page shows "Approximate Total Fees" for the whole course. The university calls it an estimate, because visa and administrative charges differ by country, and a regional counselor gives the detailed breakdown.

Fees are for the whole course, from UCSI program pages checked in September 2026. The pages carry no update date, and 2027 fees are not published yet. Since July 1, 2025, international students at private universities pay 6% Service Tax; UCSI does not say whether these totals include it.

The application fee also covers the first-year EMGS visa fee: RM 3,100 for most programs, RM 3,150 for Engineering, RM 3,400 for Music, RM 3,600 for Medicine, Pharmacy and Master's. From the second year, the EMGS renewal fee depends on your nationality. You pay the amount in your Letter of Admission by bank transfer in US dollars.

## Programs and the degree you receive
UCSI covers medicine, pharmacy, nursing, engineering, architecture, computer science and IT, business, hospitality, music and performing arts, psychology and food science. You receive a UCSI degree unless you transfer abroad through a pathway.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Study routes at UCSI" -->
- **Foundation** — 1 year in 3 semesters. The Science route leads to medicine, pharmacy and engineering; the Arts route to business, IT and accounting.
- **Bachelor's degrees** — 3 years for computer science and business, 4 years for pharmacy, with at least 6 months of work placement.
- **Doctor of Medicine (MD)** — 5 years; years 1–2 at Bandar Springhill next to UCSI Hospital. The program page states it is accredited by the Malaysian Medical Council and the Maldives Medical and Dental Council, and listed by WHO, WFME and the Bangladesh Medical and Dental Council.
- **International Degree Pathways** — transfer to a partner in the UK (2+1, 2+2, 1+2), Australia, New Zealand or Switzerland. UK partners include UWE Bristol, Northumbria, Hertfordshire, Middlesex, Essex, Strathclyde and Dundee. The partner then awards the degree and charges its own fees.
- **Postgraduate** — Master's and PhD programs across the same faculties.

## UCSI University admission requirements for international students
UCSI publishes entry requirements program by program, not one table for every national school system. The steps below show what the university asks for and where we check your case individually.

<!-- block: processBlock title="How to apply to UCSI for 2027" -->
1. **Check your grades.** Computer science: Foundation or Matriculation with CGPA 2.00+, two A-Level subjects, or a qualification MQA treats as equivalent. MD: A-Level BBB/ABC/AAC in Biology, Chemistry and Physics or Maths, or Foundation in Science with CGPA 3.0, plus an interview. Indian MD applicants need an average of 70% in these subjects, NEET and an NMC Eligibility Certificate. Thresholds for FSc/HSSC, WAEC, HSC or Thanaweya are not published, so we assess your certificate individually.
2. **Meet the English requirement.** Computer science: IELTS 5.0 or TOEFL iBT 42. MD: IELTS 6.0 or TOEFL 550 PBT. Foundation: IELTS 4.0. With a lower score, computer science students take Basic English alongside the program, and MD applicants can get a conditional offer. A letter confirming English-medium schooling can exempt you.
3. **Prepare documents.** Certificates with English translations, all passport pages in color (valid at least 18 months), a 35×45 mm photo, a Health Declaration and a medical exam. Travelers from yellow fever risk countries, including Nigeria, need a vaccination certificate. Malaysia is not part of the Apostille Convention, so universities ask for certified true copies and certified translations instead.
4. **Register online and pay the application fee** by bank transfer.
5. **UCSI submits your visa application to EMGS.** The official processing time is 14 working days for a complete file; plan for 4–8 weeks.
6. **Pick your intake.** UCSI usually takes students in January, May and September, and the MD in September only. The 2027 calendar is not published yet, so start documents 4–6 months ahead.

## What to know before you apply
UCSI is open about fees and entry rules, but several limits matter for international students. Each has a practical way around it.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Limitations and how to handle them" -->
- **Fees are whole-course estimates.** Tax, EMGS renewals, insurance and housing may come on top. Ask for the detailed breakdown before paying; we help you read it line by line.
- **The MD does not guarantee a license at home or an internship in Malaysia.** Graduates need housemanship, and UCSI does not state whether international graduates can complete it in Malaysia. Check your home medical council rules before applying.
- **A pathway is not a dual degree.** After transfer, you study under UK rules and fees. The UK Graduate Route requires at least 12 months in the UK on a Student visa, so a short final year there may not qualify.
- **Post-study stay depends on your passport.** Malaysia's 12-month Graduate Pass is open to citizens of 32 countries, including India and GCC states, but not Pakistan, Nigeria, Egypt or Bangladesh.
- **Scholarship criteria are written for A-Level, IB and IGCSE.** The UCSI University Trust Scholarship gives a partial tuition waiver for top results such as A-Levels AAA or IB 36+. CBSE, FSc or WAEC holders fall under "equivalent qualifications".
- **Part-time work is limited** to 20 hours a week during holidays longer than 7 days, with immigration approval. Do not count on it to pay tuition.

## Rankings and accreditation
UCSI is #282 in the QS World University Rankings 2027 and has stayed in the global top 300 for five years in a row. In QS Asia 2026 it is in the top 30 in Asia and 8th in Southeast Asia. Its strongest subjects in QS by Subject 2026 are Music and Performing Arts (both #24 in the world), Art & Design (#29) and Hospitality & Leisure (#47).

Program quality in Malaysia is controlled by the Malaysian Qualifications Agency (MQA), and accredited programs are listed in the Malaysian Qualifications Register (MQR), where you can check any program by its code. UCSI's MD is fully accredited until May 11, 2029, and Pharmacy until April 4, 2028. The Bachelor of Computer Science (AI) has provisional accreditation (MQA/PA), so full accreditation is still pending.

## Housing and student life
On the Kuala Lumpur campus, students live in the Residential Halls, mostly in twin-sharing rooms, with a few single en-suite rooms in block E. According to UCSI's 2025 brochure, a room costs around RM 700 per person per month, paid per semester, with water and electricity included.

Book before you fly. The non-refundable booking fee is RM 1,000 for blocks D and L and RM 2,000 for block E, and students arriving without a booking are not given a room. Off-campus rooms managed by the university group cost roughly RM 950–1,600 a month, with a 12-month lease.

A student in Kuala Lumpur typically needs about RM 2,000–3,500 a month for living costs. UCSI says students from 120 countries have studied there over its history. You must arrive via KLIA or KLIA2; the free airport pickup requires the Pick-Up Form at least 7 working days ahead.

## Student visa
Every international student at UCSI needs a Student Pass through EMGS, even if your passport allows visa-free tourist entry. You receive an eVAL, travel, complete a medical check within 7 working days of arrival, and then the pass is endorsed. It is usually issued for up to one year and renewed annually.

Health insurance recognized in Malaysia is mandatory; EMGS packages cost RM 456–958 a year. Full steps and fees are in our [Malaysia student visa guide](/study-in-malaysia/malaysia-student-visa).

<!-- block: accordionBlock title="Frequently asked questions" -->
### Is UCSI University a good university?
It is ranked #282 in the QS World University Rankings 2027 and in the top 30 in QS Asia 2026. Its Music and Performing Arts programs are #24 in the world in QS by Subject 2026. Whether it fits you depends on your program, budget and plans after graduation.

### How much does UCSI cost for international students?
UCSI publishes approximate total fees for the whole course: RM 103,881 for the 3-year Bachelor of Computer Science, RM 232,726 for 4-year Pharmacy and RM 532,524 for the 5-year MD (checked September 2026). The application fee is RM 3,100–3,600.

### What IELTS score does UCSI require?
IELTS 5.0 for computer science, 6.0 for the MD and 4.0 for Foundation. If your score is lower, UCSI offers English support or a conditional offer for some programs.

### Can Indian students study medicine at UCSI?
Yes. Indian applicants need an average of 70% in Biology, Chemistry and Physics or Maths, a NEET result and an NMC Eligibility Certificate. The MD starts in September only and includes an interview.

### Does UCSI offer a dual degree with UK universities?
No. UCSI offers International Degree Pathways: you transfer to a UK, Australian or New Zealand partner and receive that university's degree. You then pay the partner's fees and need a visa for that country.

### When are UCSI intakes in 2027?
UCSI usually runs intakes in January, May and September; the MD starts in September only. The 2027 calendar is not published yet, so start preparing documents 4–6 months before your intake.
