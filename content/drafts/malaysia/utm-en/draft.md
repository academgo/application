<!-- block: textContent -->
Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM) is a public research university focused on engineering, computing and science. It received university status in 1975, and its main campus is in Johor Bahru, about 300 km south of Kuala Lumpur. At the end of 2025, UTM had 32,004 students, including 4,852 international students.

UTM suits students with strong math and science grades who want an engineering or computing degree, with tuition quoted as one sum for the whole program. It is a weaker fit if your grades are borderline, since the Bridging Programme ended in 2026/27, or if you want business or accounting.

## Tuition fees at Universiti Teknologi Malaysia
UTM quotes undergraduate fees in Malaysian ringgit (RM) for the whole program, not per year. The figures below come from UTM's current fee schedule for international undergraduates, checked in September 2026. The page does not name an academic year, so we confirm the figure for your program and the 2027 intake before you apply.

<!-- block: tableBlock -->
| Program group | Length | Total tuition (RM) |
|---|---|---|
| Engineering (Civil, Electrical, Mechanical, Chemical and others) | 8 semesters (4 years) | 75,000 |
| MJIIT engineering (Electronic Systems, Mechanical Precision, Chemical Process) | 8 semesters (4 years) | 75,000 |
| Computing / IT | 8 semesters (4 years) | 71,000 |
| Science; Landscape Architecture; Quantity Surveying | 8 semesters (4 years) | 63,000 |
| Artificial Intelligence | 6 semesters (3 years) + short semesters | 54,788 (58,508 with 3 short semesters) |
| Architecture (BSc) | 6 semesters (3 years) | 51,620 |

<!-- block: textContent -->
For engineering, this is about RM 18,750 per year. The first semester is higher (RM 14,758 for engineering). By our reading of the schedule, it already includes the RM 6,152 registration fee, so the total should not grow by that amount; we confirm this with UTM.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Costs outside tuition" -->
- Application fee: about RM 450, paid only through the SMARt system (UTM FAQ).
- Conditional Offer and Final Offer: each carries a non-refundable processing fee; UTM does not publish the amounts.
- Personal bond: RM 1,500–2,000, set by immigration according to your nationality and refunded after you graduate.
- Student Pass, insurance and medical check in the first year: roughly RM 2,700–3,300 (our estimate from the EMGS fee schedule).
- Graduation fee: RM 220; an extra semester costs the same as a normal one.
- Housing is not included in tuition (see below).

<!-- block: textContent -->
The registration fee is paid in full, in MYR, through ecommerce.utm.my, telegraphic transfer or CIMB Clicks. For a full budget, see the [cost of studying in Malaysia](/study-in-malaysia/cost-of-studying-in-malaysia).

## Programs and the degree you receive
For 2026/27, UTM's programs for international undergraduates include:

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Undergraduate programs for international students" -->
- Engineering: Civil, Electrical, Electronic, Mechatronics, Biomedical, Mechanical, Aeronautical, Naval Architecture, Chemical (including Gas and Bioprocess), Petroleum, Nuclear, Geomatics.
- Computing: Software Engineering; Computer Science (Networks & Security, Graphics & Multimedia).
- Bachelor in Artificial Intelligence.
- Built environment: Architecture, Quantity Surveying, Urban Planning, Landscape Architecture.
- Science: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology, Biotechnology.
- Human Resource Development; Psychology with HRD.
- MJIIT programs, built on the Japanese model of engineering education.

<!-- block: textContent -->
Most programs start only in September. Electrical, Electronic, Mechatronics, Biomedical, Civil and three MJIIT programs also take students in February. Engineering, computing and science degrees last 8 semesters (4 years). Architecture (BSc), AI and HRD take 6 semesters (3 years) plus short semesters.

Most undergraduate programs run in Johor Bahru; MJIIT programs are in Kuala Lumpur. Check the campus for your program.

You receive a UTM bachelor's degree, listed automatically in the Malaysian Qualifications Register (MQR) because UTM is self-accrediting. Programs for international students are delivered in English, according to UTM's admissions pages, plus a basic Malay language course among the general subjects.

If you do not meet direct entry, UTMSPACE runs a one-year Foundation (ASASI UTM) open to international students, with a separate application. For postgraduate study, a coursework Master in Engineering costs RM 31,000 for 3 semesters; see our guide to a [master's in Malaysia](/study-in-malaysia/masters-in-malaysia).

## Admission requirements for international students
UTM asks for a senior high school certificate, an English certificate and, for Architecture and Industrial Design, an interview. Engineering grades are checked in math, physics and chemistry or biology; computing in math plus two subjects; science in three subjects.

<!-- block: tableBlock -->
| School qualification | Minimum grade per subject (engineering) |
|---|---|
| Pakistan: HSSC / Intermediate (FSc) | 75% |
| India: CBSE (AISSCE), ISC, State HSC | B1 / B+ |
| Nigeria: WAEC WASSCE / NECO SSCE | B2 |
| Bangladesh: HSC / Alim | A+ |
| Egypt (Thanaweya Amma, Al-Azhar), Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, Bahrain | 80% |
| GCE A-Level | C (Electrical Engineering: B) |
| IB Diploma / IBCP | 4 (Electrical Engineering: 5; Computer Science: Math 5) |

<!-- block: processBlock title="How to apply to UTM for 2027" -->
1. Check your grades — compare each required subject with the table above. Science and social science programs use the same grade across three subjects.
2. Meet the English requirement — IELTS 5.5, TOEFL iBT 46 (Home Edition accepted), PTE Academic 51 (Online accepted), MUET 4.0 or Cambridge 160. Scores are valid for 2 years. A-Level, IB and some international school graduates may be exempt.
3. Prepare documents — certified true copies of certificates and transcripts with certified English translations. Malaysia is not part of the Apostille Convention, so an apostille is not needed. Your passport must be valid for at least 18 months. Add a 35×45 mm photo on a white background and the Health Declaration Form.
4. Apply online through UTM SMARt — a complete application is processed within about one working day, and the offer follows after all assessments.
5. Get your offer letters — the Eligibility Letter is free. A Conditional Offer and then a Final Offer follow, each with a processing fee. Only the Final Offer confirms admission and is used for the visa.
6. Plan the 2027 timeline — for September 2026, UTM accepted applications in two phases between April and July. Dates for February and September 2027 are not yet published; UTM announces them closer to each intake.
7. Apply for the Student Pass through EMGS — start as soon as you hold the Final Offer.

## What to know before you apply
UTM has clear limits. Here is what applicants often learn too late, and how to handle it.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Honest limitations" -->
- No Bridging Programme from 2026/27. Older pages may still mention it. If your grades fall short, look at the UTMSPACE Foundation or a university with a pathway program.
- If your English is below the minimum, UTM lets you take an Intensive English Programme on campus, but you have at most 2 semesters to meet the requirement. It is safer to arrive with IELTS 5.5 or higher.
- No financial aid for international undergraduates. Budget the full tuition plus living costs from your own funds.
- You cannot change your program or university after admission, due to immigration rules. Choose carefully before you apply.
- Accounting, Digital Marketing, Management and Mechanical Engineering (Manufacturing) are not offered to new students in 2026/27.
- Part-time work is limited to 20 hours per week, and only during semester breaks or holidays longer than 7 days, with immigration approval.
- After graduation, the 12-month Graduate Pass is open only to citizens of 32 countries. India and the GCC countries are on the list; Pakistan, Nigeria, Egypt and Bangladesh are not.

## Rankings and accreditation
UTM ranks #153 in the QS World University Rankings 2026, up from #181 in 2025 and #188 in 2024. In the Times Higher Education World University Rankings 2026, it is in the 401–500 band. THE data show 28% international students and 10.1 students per staff member.

UTM is self-accrediting under the Malaysian Qualifications Agency (MQA), and its qualifications appear in the searchable MQR. If you plan to work in a regulated profession at home, such as engineering, check how your home authority recognizes Malaysian degrees before you enroll.

## Housing and student life
UTM has more than 12,000 hostel places in 10 residential colleges on the Johor Bahru campus. Monthly rates:

<!-- block: tableBlock -->
| Room type | Price per month (RM) |
|---|---|
| Double room | 360 |
| Single room | 450 |
| Single room with bathroom | 600 |
| Executive room | 750–1,200 |
| K Hotel | 395–650 |
| Family apartment | 480–1,100 |

<!-- block: textContent -->
UTM estimates living costs at around RM 1,000–1,500 per month if you live on campus, including food and local transport in Johor Bahru. After you arrive, you must complete your medical check at the UTM Health Center within 3 days. Your insurance must be recognized in Malaysia and valid for at least 12 months; EMGS plans cost RM 456–958 per year.

## Student visa
You need a Student Pass even if your country is visa-free for tourism. The route runs through EMGS: offer, application, visa approval letter (eVAL), entry visa if required, arrival, medical check and endorsement. EMGS aims for 14 working days on a complete file, but UTM advises planning 3–6 weeks. Do not buy flights before your approval letter and entry visa are issued. Full steps are in our [Malaysia student visa guide](/study-in-malaysia/malaysia-student-visa).

<!-- block: accordionBlock title="Frequently asked questions" -->
### How much does UTM cost for international students?
Tuition is quoted for the whole program: RM 75,000 for engineering, RM 71,000 for computing and RM 51,620 for Architecture (BSc), per UTM's current fee schedule. Add the bond, visa costs and housing.

### What IELTS score does UTM require?
IELTS 5.5 for undergraduate programs, or TOEFL iBT 46, PTE Academic 51, MUET 4.0 or Cambridge 160. Results are valid for 2 years.

### What grades do I need from Pakistan or India?
For engineering, Pakistani applicants need 75% per required subject in HSSC / FSc. Indian applicants need B1 / B+ per subject in CBSE, ISC or a State HSC.

### Does UTM offer scholarships to international undergraduates?
No. UTM does not offer financial aid to international undergraduates. Funding exists for postgraduates: the UTM ASCEND Bright Talent Scholarship for PhD and the Malaysia International Scholarship for master's and PhD.

### When does UTM accept applications for 2027?
Dates for February and September 2027 are not yet published. For September 2026, applications ran in two phases between April and July. February is open only for selected programs.

### Is UTM in Kuala Lumpur?
The main campus, where most undergraduates study, is in Johor Bahru, about 300 km from Kuala Lumpur. UTM also has campuses in Kuala Lumpur (MJIIT programs) and Pagoh.
