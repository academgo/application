METU (Middle East Technical University, in Turkish Orta Doğu Teknik Üniversitesi, or ODTÜ) is a state university in Ankara, founded in 1956. Every bachelor's program is taught in English, and tuition for international students is $1,600–2,400 a year (2026/27).

METU suits strong applicants who want engineering, architecture, the natural sciences or the social sciences at a low price. Your grades or test scores need to be high: for most applicants from Pakistan, Egypt and the Gulf, that means a school certificate of 90% or more.

It does not suit you if you want medicine, dentistry, pharmacy or law. METU has no faculties for these subjects. It also does not suit applicants who plan to use IELTS, which METU does not accept.

## METU fees for international students

Tuition depends on the faculty, not on your nationality. The fees below are from METU's 2026/27 price list for the Ankara campus and are unchanged from 2025/26.

<!-- block: tableBlock -->
| Program group | Per semester (2026/27) | Per year |
|---|---|---|
| Engineering and Architecture (all programs) | $1,200 | $2,400 |
| Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Molecular Biology & Genetics, Statistics | $1,200 | $2,400 |
| Psychology, Sociology, History, Philosophy | $800 | $1,600 |
| Economic and Administrative Sciences; Education | $800 | $1,600 |
| Master's and PhD | $375 | $750 |

<!-- block: textContent -->
You pay in Turkish lira at the Central Bank of Turkey's rate from the week before each payment period. The English prep year costs the same as a semester of your program. These fees apply for the normal length of the program plus two semesters; after that, they rise by 50%. METU has not published 2027/28 fees yet.

Discounts reward results after you enroll. From the second year, a CGPA of 3.00 gives a 50% discount and 3.50 gives a full waiver, for up to 10% of international students. A 25% family discount applies if a parent lives legally in Turkey and another family member studies at a Turkish state university. Students funded by their own government or another tuition-covering scholarship pay no fees, and METU takes [Türkiye Bursları](/study-in-turkey/turkiye-burslari) scholarship holders.

## Admission requirements for international students

You apply with one qualifying exam or school certificate. METU publishes minimums, not cut-off scores, and states that meeting them "does not guarantee admission." Decisions are based on a holistic evaluation.

<!-- block: tableBlock -->
| Route | METU minimum |
|---|---|
| School certificate: Pakistan, Egypt, Bangladesh, UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, Bahrain, Iraq, Jordan | 90% |
| School certificate: India | 80% |
| Saudi Arabia | 90% in the secondary certificate and 90% in Qudurat |
| Nigeria (also Ghana, Liberia, Gambia, Sierra Leone) | WAEC SSCE: total of 15 or less across 5 subjects, 3 related to the program |
| SAT | 1200; engineering, architecture and science programs also need Math 700 |
| ACT / IB Diploma | 27 / 33 |
| A levels | ABB, two subjects related to the program |
| TR-YÖS | Top 5% of test takers |

<!-- block: processBlock title="How to apply to METU for 2027/28" -->
1. Check your route against the table above. SAT and TR-YÖS results are valid for two years.
2. Prepare your English. A certificate is optional at application but strengthens it. To skip the prep year, you need TOEFL iBT 75, PTE Academic 65 or 60 in METU's own EPE exam.
3. Collect documents: photo, passport data page, school certificate (or a provisional one), transcripts for all school years and your exam results.
4. Apply yourself on the METU online portal. Use a personal e-mail, but not Hotmail, Live or Outlook. After uploading, "freeze" the application, or it will not be reviewed.
5. Watch the calendar. For 2026/27, the main window ran June 1 – July 12, 2026, plus an extra period on August 26–28. METU publishes 2027/28 dates closer to the intake.
6. Legalize your school certificate. Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, Saudi Arabia, Oman and Bahrain use an apostille. Nigeria, Egypt, the UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Iraq and Jordan need consular legalization: education ministry, foreign ministry, then the Turkish consulate. Add a notarized Turkish translation.
7. Get Denklik, the Turkish recognition of your school certificate, through the e-Denklik system. Each university sets its own deadline for it after conditional admission.
8. Apply for your student visa. For 2026/27, classes start on September 28, 2026.

<!-- block: textContent -->
YÖK, the Turkish higher education council, also requires universities to ask for a financial guarantee. METU does not publish a fixed amount, so check it when you apply. For all routes into Turkish universities, see our [admission requirements guide](/study-in-turkey/admission-requirements).

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="What to know before you apply" -->
- **METU does not work with agencies.** The university states that applicants must apply individually. No agency, including AcademGo, can apply for you or offer a special channel. We help you assess your chances, prepare documents and test scores, and plan alternatives.
- IELTS taken on or after December 24, 2022 is not accepted. Book TOEFL iBT or PTE Academic; results are valid for two years.
- Without an accepted English score, you take METU's EPE exam after admission. If you fail, you spend a year in the English prep class.
- For Pakistan, Egypt, Bangladesh and the Gulf, 90% is the minimum, not a safe score. By the university's own figures, most programs take roughly the top 2% of Turkish national exam takers.
- There are 444 places for the whole university. Apply to two or three alternatives in parallel.
- No medicine at METU. See our guide to [medicine in Turkey](/study-in-turkey/medicine-in-turkey).

## Middle East Technical University rankings and accreditation

METU is ranked 351–400 in the THE World University Rankings 2026. Its best THE subject is Education Studies (#93); Engineering is in the 201–250 band and Computer Science in 251–300. In the QS World University Rankings, METU placed #=269 in 2026 and #305 in 2027.

According to METU, all 13 bachelor's programs in its Faculty of Engineering are accredited by the Engineering Accreditation Commission of ABET. If you plan to work as an engineer at home, check your country's recognition rules before you apply.

## Housing and student life

METU has 19 dormitories and guest houses, and around 7,000 students live on campus, according to the university. Most dormitories are single-sex. Monthly rent for 2026/27 is fixed in lira:

<!-- block: tableBlock -->
| Room type | Monthly fee (2026/27) |
|---|---|
| 4-person room (Dormitories 3–7) | 2,812 TL |
| 4-person room (İsa Demiray, Faika Demiray, Refika Aksoy) | 4,187 TL |
| 2-person room (İsa Demiray, Faika Demiray, Refika Aksoy) | 5,504 TL |
| Dormitory 19 (2+2) | 6,309 TL |

<!-- block: textContent -->
According to METU, international students pay the same dormitory fees as Turkish students. You apply after registration, places are allocated by score in weekly rounds, and a place is not guaranteed. Students must be inside the dormitory by midnight.

The campus has indoor and outdoor pools, gyms, sports fields and a 24/7 medical and counseling center. Ring buses run inside the campus, and the metro and buses reach central Ankara from 07:00 to midnight.

## Student visa and residence permit

Citizens of Pakistan, India, Nigeria and Egypt need a visa. The e-Visa is for tourism and business, so the safest option is a student visa from a Turkish consulate. Saudi and UAE citizens enter visa-free for up to 90 days in any 180.

After enrollment, you apply for a student residence permit on e-ikamet and submit documents through the university. The card costs 964 TL in 2026, with no extra permit fee for students, and processing takes up to 90 days. Bachelor's students can work only from the second year, part-time and with a work permit. Details are in our [student visa and residence permit guide](/study-in-turkey/student-visa-and-residence-permit).

## Other options in Turkey

If your grades are below METU's thresholds, or you want medicine or IELTS-based admission, private universities are the usual plan B. English-taught bachelor's programs at most private universities cost roughly $4,000–10,000 a year. Compare [Bahçeşehir University](/study-in-turkey/bahcesehir-university), [Altınbaş University](/study-in-turkey/altinbas-university) and [Istanbul Medipol University](/study-in-turkey/istanbul-medipol-university), or another state option, [Istanbul University](/study-in-turkey/istanbul-university). <!-- block: accordionBlock title="Frequently asked questions" -->
### How much are METU fees for international students?
For 2026/27, bachelor's tuition is $2,400 a year for engineering, architecture and the natural sciences, and $1,600 a year for social sciences, economics and education. Master's and PhD programs cost $750 a year. Fees for 2027/28 are not yet published.

### Which programs at METU are taught in English?
All bachelor's programs are taught in English. If you have no accepted English score, you may first need a one-year English prep class.

### What grades do I need to get into METU from Pakistan or India?
Pakistan requires at least 90% in the school certificate, India at least 80%. You can also apply with SAT 1200, plus Math 700 for engineering, architecture and science. These are minimums, and admission is competitive.

### Does METU accept IELTS?
No, not if taken on or after December 24, 2022. To skip the prep year, you need TOEFL iBT 75, PTE Academic 65 or 60 in METU's EPE exam.

### Can an agency apply to METU for me?
No. METU does not work with third parties in recruitment, and you submit the application yourself. AcademGo can check your chances, help prepare documents and test scores, and plan alternatives.

### Are there scholarships at METU?
Mainly after the first year: a CGPA of 3.00 gives 50% off and 3.50 a full waiver, for up to 10% of international students. Türkiye Bursları holders and government-funded students pay no tuition.

### Is there on-campus housing at METU?
Yes. There are 19 dormitories and guest houses, with rooms from 2,812 TL a month in 2026/27. Places are allocated after registration, and a place is not guaranteed.

### When should I apply to METU for 2027/28?
Dates for 2027/28 are not announced yet. For 2026/27, the main window ran from June 1 to July 12, 2026. Take your SAT or TOEFL early so the results are ready in time.
