## Important: METU does not work with agencies

Per the fact sheet, METU officially states it does not accept applications through agencies. Say this plainly and calmly: applicants apply to METU themselves via the university portal. AcademGo helps differently: we assess your chances against METU's requirements, help you prepare documents and test scores (SAT, TOEFL), and build a plan B at private universities in parallel. Never write "we apply for you" or imply special channels.
