Medical universities in Turkey offer a six-year MD program, and a growing number teach it in English. For international applicants there are two routes: private (foundation) universities with English-taught medicine and published fees, and a small number of places at competitive state universities. Most of the private programs sit between about $24,000 and $40,000 a year on 2026/27 price lists.

Two issues matter more than the fee. First, "English-taught" does not always mean English with patients: at least one major program moves clinical training into Turkish from year four. Second, your degree has to work at home. The medical councils of India, Pakistan and Nigeria each set their own conditions, and none of them has confirmed recognition of any specific Turkish program.

Below: verified programs, costs, admission for the 2027/28 intake and what to check before paying a deposit. For the wider picture, see our [Study in Turkey](/study-in-turkey) guide.

## MBBS in Turkey: total cost

Turkish universities award a six-year MD (Doctor of Medicine), not a degree called MBBS. On 2026/27 price lists, English-taught medicine at private universities costs roughly $24,000–40,000 a year. Medicine taught in Turkish is cheaper: about $18,000–23,000 a year at Üsküdar and İstinye.

Over six years, tuition alone at 2026/27 prices adds up to roughly $144,000–240,000 at mainstream private universities. Prices are reviewed every year, and no university has published 2027/28 fees yet. İstinye, for example, states that its prices apply to 2026/27 only.

State universities cost less but take few international students. Hacettepe charged $12,000 a year for medicine in 2025/26. Istanbul University set 476,100 TL for 2026/27, about $10,000 depending on the exchange rate.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Costs to add to the tuition fee" -->
- **English preparatory year**, if you do not pass the exemption test: $17,000 at Medipol, $6,000 at Başkent, $5,500 at Acıbadem. At Altınbaş and Istanbul University the prep year costs the same as a year of the program.
- **Living costs in Istanbul**: about $700–1,300 a month, depending on whether you share or rent alone.
- **Discounts** exist but are specific to each university. Medipol gives 10% for paying the year in one sum and a 15% country scholarship to citizens of Pakistan, India, Nigeria, Egypt and Bangladesh (not GCC countries). Altınbaş lists lower totals for payment in two installments ($22,500) or in one sum ($21,250).
- **Türkiye Bursları**, the government scholarship, requires at least 90% in school grades for medicine, dentistry and pharmacy. See our [Türkiye Bursları guide](/study-in-turkey/turkiye-burslari).

## Language: English lectures, Turkish clinics

Turkish regulations define two kinds of foreign-language programs. A "fully" foreign-language program teaches the entire curriculum in that language. A "partly" foreign-language program needs only 30% of credits in English. Both include a preparatory language year unless you pass an exemption test.

Even a fully English program can move to Turkish at the bedside. At Medipol's International School of Medicine, years one to three are entirely in English. In years four to six, theory stays in English, but clinical practice and clinical exams are in Turkish.

To enter year four, international students need Turkish at **C1**. The school runs compulsory Turkish courses from A1 to C1 in years one to three, or you can pass the university's Turkish exam.

Other universities are less precise. BAU and Altınbaş write that clinical training happens "in English and/or Turkish" and set no fixed Turkish level. Istanbul University's language rules require C1 by the start of the third year for students who enter with B2 Turkish. How this applies to English-taught medicine is not stated, so ask the admissions office. At Sağlık Bilimleri University, students on Turkish or 30%-English programs who lack C1 Turkish two years after enrollment are dismissed. Whichever program you choose, start Turkish in year one.

## Admission requirements for medicine

Private universities admit mainly on school results and do not require TR-YÖS, the exam for foreign applicants. State universities rely on exams and set much higher thresholds. The details are on our [admission requirements page](/study-in-turkey/admission-requirements).

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="What medical programs ask for (2026 cycle)" -->
- **Medipol**: at least 60% in the school certificate for Pakistan, India, Nigeria (WAEC) and GCC countries. If you submit SAT, the minimum is 1000; the ACT minimum for medicine is 22.
- **BAU**: 83 places a year for international medical students and about 1,500 applications, according to the university. Selection uses grades and a motivation letter.
- **Istanbul University**: national school certificates are not accepted. You need SAT 1440, ACT 32.33, IB 36.33 or TR-YÖS 300 (out of 500) as a minimum.
- **Sağlık Bilimleri University (state)**: minimums of TR-YÖS 400, SAT 1300, ACT 28 or IB 40. No more than 20% of places in a program go to one country.
- **English for BAU medicine**: TOEFL iBT 96 or PTE Academic 78. **BAU, Altınbaş and Istanbul University do not accept IELTS**; Medipol accepts IELTS Academic 6.5.

<!-- block: textContent -->
A minimum score does not guarantee a place. Altınbaş gives priority to applicants with a TR-YÖS result. In 2026 the exam ran in April and October; 2027 dates are not yet published, and results stay valid for two years.

Hacettepe works with no agencies, so you apply yourself; we help prepare documents and check the application before you submit.

<!-- block: processBlock title="How admission to a private medical school works (2027/28 intake)" -->
1. Online application — at Medipol, one per semester, decided within a week.
2. Conditional acceptance letter — sets the deposit and its deadline.
3. Deposit — non-refundable at Medipol and BAU, and counted toward tuition.
4. Official acceptance letter — needed for the student visa at a Turkish consulate (required for Pakistan, India, Nigeria and Egypt).
5. Documents — an apostille for India, Pakistan and Bangladesh; consular legalization for Nigeria, Egypt, the UAE and Qatar.
6. Registration in Turkey, the preparatory year if needed, then the six-year program.

## Accreditation and licensing at home

A Turkish MD lets you practice at home only if your national council accepts it. Check this with the council before you apply, not after graduation.

**TEPDAD and the US.** TEPDAD is recognized by the World Federation for Medical Education (WFME) until July 2033. Since 2024, ECFMG certification, which you need for US residency, is linked to graduating from a school accredited by a WFME-recognized agency. In Turkey that means TEPDAD. Altınbaş, Üsküdar, Nişantaşı, İstanbul Gelişim and Atılım have no medical program on the TEPDAD list (checked 21 September 2026).

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Pakistan: PM&DC conditions for studying abroad (from the 2024-25 session)" -->
- F.Sc. (Pre-Medical) of at least 60% and MDCAT of at least 50% for school graduates from Pakistan.
- The university must appear in the list maintained by ECFMG.
- PM&DC Student Registration Certificate before you leave (free, at srf.pmdc.pk).
- At least 6,200 hours of teaching, at least 80% attendance, at least five years of study.
- National Registration Examination (NRE) before a full license. Graduates who did not meet these conditions cannot sit the NRE.

<!-- block: textContent -->
We have not found a PM&DC list of recognized Turkish universities. Given the ECFMG condition, choose a TEPDAD-accredited program.

**India: NMC FMGL Regulations 2021.** Permanent registration requires a course of at least 54 months with a 12-month internship at the same foreign university. The degree must be taught in English. You must also be eligible for a license in the country of study "at par" with its citizens, complete a 12-month supervised internship in India and pass the NMC exit test. The whole course must be in one university in one country and finished within 10 years.

This raises three open questions. Clinical years at several "English" programs are partly or fully in Turkish. The Turkish internship sits inside the six years, not on top. And a foreign graduate may practice in Turkey only in private facilities, after diploma recognition, with Turkish at B level or higher, a work permit and within Ministry of Health planning. Whether NMC treats this as a license "at par" is unconfirmed. NMC compatibility of Turkish programs is not confirmed, so before applying, get a written answer from the university on each FMGL point.

**Nigeria: MDCN.** To sit the assessment exam for foreign graduates, your school must be listed in the World Directory of Medical Schools. You must also prove the right to practice in the country of study, with a valid license and a Certificate of Good Standing from the regulator, not the university. In June 2025 the fee was ₦190,000 for Nigerian citizens and ₦300,000 for non-Nigerians.

**UAE.** The unified requirements of April 2025 ask general practitioners for a medical degree of at least five years, one year of internship and, for foreign-trained doctors, two years of clinical experience after internship. For Saudi Arabia, Qatar and Egypt, check with the regulator directly.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="What to know before you apply" -->
- **Six years of fees**: budget for the full course plus a possible prep year. Prices are revised yearly.
- **Turkish is part of the degree**: plan for C1-level clinical Turkish at some programs, even when lectures are in English.
- **Recognition first**: confirm TEPDAD status and your council's conditions before paying.
- **Deposits**: Medipol and BAU deposits are non-refundable. Altınbaş refunds its prepayment only after an official visa refusal, keeping $250.
- **Small quotas**: Istanbul University had 4 English medicine places for international students in 2026.

## Studying dentistry in Turkey (and pharmacy)

Dentistry in Turkey takes five years, plus a prep year if needed. English-taught dentistry at private universities costs about $22,000–36,000 a year (2026/27), from Üsküdar to Medipol. At Altınbaş dentistry is 30% English and 70% Turkish, so you must prove both languages. State places are tiny: Istanbul University offered 2 in English dentistry for international students in 2026, at 309,465 TL a year.

Pharmacy also takes five years. In English it costs $18,000 at Medipol and $15,000 at BAU (2026/27), and $12,000 at Altınbaş (2025/26 list). Istanbul University charges 238,050 TL for 2026/27. Hacettepe charged $8,000 a year for dentistry and pharmacy in 2025/26.

<!-- block: accordionBlock title="Frequently asked questions" -->
### How much does MBBS in Turkey cost?
English-taught medicine at private universities costs roughly $24,000–40,000 a year (2026/27). State universities such as Hacettepe and Istanbul University cost about $10,000–12,000 a year but have very few places. 2027/28 fees are not yet published.

### Can I study medicine in Turkey in English?
Yes. Medipol, Acıbadem, BAU, Koç, Hacettepe and Istanbul University all offer English-taught medicine. Clinical training may still involve Turkish, as at Medipol's International School of Medicine in years four to six.

### Do I need to speak Turkish?
Not for admission to English-taught programs, but you will need it with patients. Medipol's International School of Medicine requires C1 Turkish before year four and offers courses to get there.

### Is a Turkish medical degree recognized by NMC or PM&DC?
Neither council has confirmed any specific Turkish university. NMC requires English-medium teaching, an internship on top of the course and a license at par with citizens. PM&DC requires a university on the ECFMG list, a Student Registration Certificate before departure and the NRE.

### Can I take USMLE after studying in Turkey?
US residency requires ECFMG certification. Since 2024 this is linked to a school accredited by a WFME-recognized agency, which in Turkey is TEPDAD. Check your program's TEPDAD status and its end date.

### How long is the program?
Medicine takes six years, with the internship year included. Dentistry and pharmacy take five years. Add one preparatory language year if you do not pass the English exemption test.

### Is NEET required for Indian students?
According to an Indian embassy advisory citing NMC, you must pass NEET-UG before studying abroad if you want to take FMGE later. Check the current NMC rules before you apply.
