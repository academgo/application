<!-- block: textContent -->
Most international students need two documents to study in Turkey. The first is the Turkey student visa, which you get from a Turkish consulate before you travel. Citizens of Pakistan, India, Nigeria and Egypt with ordinary passports need one. The second is the student residence permit (öğrenci ikamet izni), which you apply for after you enroll, through your university.

The visa gets you into the country; the residence permit lets you stay. This guide covers both for the 2027/28 intake, including costs and where applicants usually get stuck.

If you are still choosing a university, start with our overview of [studying in Turkey](/study-in-turkey) and the [admission requirements](/study-in-turkey/admission-requirements).

<!-- block: textContent -->
## Do you need a student visa for Turkey?

Visa rules follow your passport, not your country of residence. Citizens of Pakistan, India, Nigeria and Egypt with ordinary passports need a visa. Citizens of Saudi Arabia and the UAE enter visa-free, but the visa-free stay is limited, so they also apply for a residence permit after enrollment.

<!-- block: tableBlock -->
| Passport | Entry to Turkey | 30-day e-Visa | School documents |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pakistan | Visa required | Only with a valid Schengen, US, UK or Irish visa or residence permit | Apostille |
| India | Visa required | Only with a valid Schengen, US, UK or Irish visa or residence permit | Apostille |
| Nigeria | Visa required | Not available | Consular legalization |
| Egypt | Visa required | Ages 15–45: a valid Schengen, US, UK or Irish visa or residence permit, plus travel with specific airlines | Consular legalization |
| Saudi Arabia | Visa-free, up to 90 days in any 180 days | Not needed | Apostille |
| UAE | Visa-free, up to 90 days in any 180 days | Not needed | Consular legalization |

<!-- block: textContent -->
If you are an Indian or Pakistani national living in Dubai or Riyadh, the rules for your passport apply. A GCC residence permit is not on the list of documents that qualify you for an e-Visa. For other nationalities, check the Turkish Ministry of Foreign Affairs visa page or ask us before you book anything.

<!-- block: textContent -->
## Turkey student visa requirements

The Turkish consulate in your country sets the exact student visa checklist, and it can change, so confirm it before booking an appointment. Several documents are needed for the visa, the residence permit or both, so start on them now.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Documents to prepare early" -->
- **Passport** valid at least 60 days longer than the residence permit period you will request.
- **Admission documents** from your university. After enrollment, it issues a student certificate (öğrenci belgesi), which the residence permit requires.
- **School certificate** with an apostille or consular legalization, plus a notarized Turkish translation.
- **Health insurance plan:** state insurance (GSS) or a private policy covering the full permit period.
- **Proof of address plan:** a notarized lease, a dormitory letter, or a notarized undertaking from the person hosting you.
- **Two biometric photos** for the residence permit form.

<!-- block: textContent -->
### Can you study on an e-Visa?

Officially, the e-Visa is for tourism and business trips only. For study, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs recommends a student visa from a Turkish consulate. Turkish law does allow students to apply for a student residence permit from inside the country.

Our advice: the safest route is to enter on a student visa. With an e-Visa or visa-free entry, some universities accept your residence permit documents, but confirm this with your university in advance.

## Legalizing your documents

Turkey accepts a foreign school certificate only after its origin is confirmed. The method depends on whether your country is a member of the 1961 Hague Apostille Convention.

<!-- block: tableBlock -->
| Method | Countries | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| Apostille | Pakistan (since March 2023), India, Saudi Arabia (since December 2022), Bangladesh (since March 2025), Oman, Bahrain, Morocco, Tunisia, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Kyrgyzstan, Azerbaijan, Tajikistan | Get an apostille from the authority in your country |
| Consular legalization | Nigeria, UAE, Egypt, Qatar, Kuwait, Iraq, Jordan | Ministry of Education, then Ministry of Foreign Affairs, then the Turkish consulate |

<!-- block: textContent -->
The Turkish translation must be notarized. If you translate the document outside Turkey, the Turkish consulate certifies the translation.

Bachelor's applicants also need recognition of their school certificate (Denklik) from the Turkish Ministry of Education. You apply through e-Denklik; from abroad, you go through the Turkish education attaché or consulate. Each university sets its own deadline for submitting Denklik. At Yıldız Technical University, for example, it is 90 days from enrollment, or the enrollment is cancelled.

Master's and PhD applicants do not need Denklik, except for programs with clinical practice in medicine. A recognition letter from the Council of Higher Education (YÖK) for your previous university is enough; the YÖK fee was 1,250 TL in 2026.

## Turkey student residence permit, step by step

You apply for the residence permit after you arrive and enroll. Since students submit through their university, you do not book an appointment at the migration office yourself.

<!-- block: processBlock title="How to get your student residence permit" -->
1. Enroll at your university and get your student certificate (öğrenci belgesi) with an e-signature or stamp, or download it from e-Devlet.
2. Fill in the "Öğrenci İkamet İzni" form at e-ikamet.goc.gov.tr, then print and sign it. Do this before your visa or visa-free period ends. Online applications made within 10 days after it ends are still considered, but do not count on that margin.
3. Pay the residence permit card fee and keep the receipt.
4. Submit the full set of documents to the responsible office at your university, not to the migration office. The university forwards them to the provincial migration office (İl Göç İdaresi).
5. Wait for the decision: up to 90 days from the date your complete file is submitted. With your application document (müracaat belgesi) and fee receipt, you can leave and re-enter Turkey if each trip is shorter than 15 days.
6. Receive your card by mail at your address or the university's address.
7. Register your address (adres tescil) within 20 working days after your first permit is approved.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Documents for your first application" -->
- Signed application form from e-ikamet
- Copy of your passport and, if you have one, your visa
- Two biometric photos
- Receipt for the card fee
- Proof of address: notarized lease, dormitory letter, or notarized undertaking from your host
- Health insurance: a private policy or a GSS document
- Student certificate from your university

<!-- block: textContent -->
You do not need a UETS electronic address for your first application. It is required when you renew the permit or change its type, and not at all for students under 18.

## Costs and processing times

Students pay only for the permit card. Figures are official for 2026; check current amounts before you pay.

<!-- block: tableBlock -->
| Item | Amount or time (2026) | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Residence permit card fee | 964 TL | No exemptions for any nationality |
| Residence permit fee (harç) | 0 TL | Students at Turkish universities are exempt |
| Decision on the permit | Up to 90 days | Counted from a complete file |
| Address registration | Within 20 working days | After the first permit is approved |
| YÖK recognition letter (master's, PhD) | 1,250 TL | Only if you need it |

<!-- block: textContent -->
The consular visa fee and processing time depend on the consulate in your country. We check them for your city when we prepare your file.

## Where you can live in Istanbul

Your address affects your permit, so check it before you sign a lease. Since July 2022, neighborhoods (mahalle) where foreigners make up more than 20% of the population have been closed to new registration of foreigners. According to the migration office, Istanbul has 54 such neighborhoods.

Since October 2022, 10 Istanbul districts have also been closed to first-time residence permit applications: Avcılar, Bahçelievler, Bağcılar, Başakşehir, Esenler, Esenyurt, Fatih, Küçükçekmece, Sultangazi and Zeytinburnu. Students have one exception: the restriction does not apply if their university, or its faculty, institute or school, is in the same closed district. If you study in another district, the exception does not apply.

Before you sign, check the exact address in e-ikamet or with your university. Students admitted to [Istanbul Medipol University](/study-in-turkey/istanbul-medipol-university), for example, can ask its international office.

## Health insurance

You need health insurance for the residence permit, and the policy must cover the whole period you apply for. The cheapest route is usually GSS, the state health insurance. You can register for it within 3 months of enrollment. In that case you do not need a separate private policy, but you must tell the migration office that you have GSS.

If you miss the 3-month window, you need a private policy. The migration office also accepts an SGK provision document, a GSS application to SGK, or a certificate under a bilateral social security agreement.

<!-- block: textContent -->
## Working on a student permit

A student residence permit does not stop you from working, but you need a separate work permit (çalışma izni). Your employer applies for it through the e-İzin system of the Ministry of Labour.

- **Bachelor's and associate degree students** can work only after finishing their first year, and only part-time.
- **Master's and PhD students** have neither restriction.
- Work permits for students in entertainment and domestic work are not approved. Türkiye Bursları scholarship holders also need approval from YTB.

Working without a permit is illegal work. It can lead to a fine, deportation and an entry ban of up to 5 years.

<!-- block: textContent -->
## After graduation

Your student residence permit ends on your graduation date. From then, you have 10 days to apply for a permit on a new basis. Graduates of Turkish universities can get a short-term residence permit if they apply within 6 months of graduation.

If you plan to stay in Turkey long term, your years on a student permit count at half value toward a long-term residence permit.

## What to know before you apply

These are the risks we see most often, and how to handle them.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Risks and how to avoid them" -->
- **Visa refusals happen.** The consulate decides, and no agency can guarantee a visa. Apply as soon as you have your admission documents, and make sure names, dates and amounts match across every paper. We check your file before you submit it.
- **Payments to the university.** Refund rules differ between universities. Get the refund conditions in writing before you pay any deposit.
- **Deadlines.** Submit the e-ikamet form before your visa or visa-free period ends. Overstaying can lead to a fine, deportation and an entry ban of up to 5 years.
- **Losing the permit.** The permit is cancelled if you can no longer continue your studies, if you use it for another purpose, or if there is a deportation decision or entry ban.
- **Changing university or city.** If you transfer within the same city or change faculty, notify the authorities within 20 days. If you move to another city, apply for a new permit within 10 days.
- **Language courses.** A Turkish course (TÖMER) you take on your own gives only a short-term permit, at most twice, and only if you entered on a language-course or student visa. If your university sends you to TÖMER, you get a student permit.

<!-- block: accordionBlock title="Frequently asked questions" -->
### Do Pakistani, Indian and Nigerian students need a visa for Turkey?
Yes. Citizens of Pakistan, India and Nigeria with ordinary passports need a visa. Pakistanis and Indians can get a 30-day e-Visa only if they hold a valid Schengen, US, UK or Irish visa or residence permit, and Nigerians cannot get an e-Visa at all. For study, a student visa from a Turkish consulate is the recommended route.

### Can I enter on an e-Visa and apply for a residence permit?
The law allows students to apply from inside Turkey, but the e-Visa is officially for tourism and business. Enter on a student visa if you can; otherwise, confirm in advance that your university will accept your file.

### How long does the student visa take?
Processing times are set by each Turkish consulate and vary by city and season. Apply as soon as you have your admission documents. After you arrive, the residence permit decision takes up to 90 days from the date your complete file is submitted.

### Do I need to show bank statements?
For the student residence permit, proof of funds is not required by default: your own statement is enough unless the migration office asks for documents. The consulate may have its own requirements for the visa, so check its current checklist.

### What if my Turkish student visa is refused?
Find out the reason if the consulate gives one, and fix the gap before applying again. Tell your university straight away and ask whether you can move your start date. We can review a refused file with you.

### Can I work on a student residence permit?
Yes, with a separate work permit that your employer applies for. Bachelor's students can work part-time after their first year; master's and PhD students have no such restriction.

### What happens after graduation?
Your student permit ends on your graduation date, and you have 10 days to apply on a new basis. Graduates can get a short-term residence permit if they apply within 6 months.
