## Structure

1. **Intro** (textContent, no H2): what Medipol is in 3 sentences (type, city, hospitals, English-taught programs) + who it suits and who it doesn't.
2. **Key facts table** (tableBlock): founded, type, campuses, students, English-taught programs, affiliated hospitals, accreditation — ✅ facts only.
3. **H2: Medipol University tuition fees 2026/27** — tableBlock by program (Medicine variants, Dentistry, Pharmacy, Engineering, Business/Psychology/Architecture…), USD/year + advance payment discount. Explain the difference between the Medicine options (International School of Medicine vs other English tracks) per fact sheet. Note that 2027/28 fees are published by the university before the intake.
4. **H2: Studying Medicine and Dentistry at Medipol** — curriculum language, clinical training in Medipol hospitals, Turkish requirement for clinical years, licensing at home (e.g., PMDC/NMC/MDCN) only as "check with your national medical council" unless ✅ in fact sheet.
5. **video slot:** "Medipol campus and hospital tour", 90 sec.
6. **H2: Admission requirements for international students** — processBlock: documents, exams accepted, English proficiency, application → acceptance letter → payment → visa.
7. **H2: What to know before you apply** — honest risks: cost of 6-year Medicine, Turkish for clinical years, preparatory English year if you fail the proficiency test, student visa at the consulate (no e-Visa conversion), living costs in Istanbul.
8. **surveyBlock** — quiz.
9. **H2: Ranking and recognition** — only sourced rankings/accreditations; no "world-class".
10. **H2: Student visa, residence permit and living costs** — short, international section of `turkey.md`; link to hub.
11. **H2: Scholarships and discounts** — from fact sheet only.
12. **H2: Other options in Turkey** — 1 paragraph: Bahçeşehir, Altınbaş (links), METU / Istanbul University for strong SAT/YÖS applicants.
13. **accordionBlock** — FAQ, 6–8 questions.
14. **cta** — free consultation: check eligibility for Medicine/Dentistry, compare with 2–3 alternatives, visa guidance.
