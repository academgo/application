Istanbul Medipol University is a private foundation university founded in 2009 and best known for Medicine, Dentistry and Pharmacy. It has campuses on both sides of Istanbul, trains medical students in Medipol hospitals and lists 27 bachelor's programs taught fully in English. Fees are in US dollars, and a six-year Medicine degree is a serious financial commitment.

Medipol suits applicants who want a clear entry route based on school grades, not an entrance exam, and who can fund the full degree. It is a weaker fit if you need the cheapest path to a medical degree or expect every clinical year to be in English.

For other programs, the English prep year costs the same as a year of the program. Medipol has two medical faculties. The School of Medicine teaches mainly in Turkish, with at least 30% of courses in English, and from 2026/27 it also lists a 100% English track. The International School of Medicine is a separate English-taught faculty, but it is not on the current fee list for international students, so ask whether it accepts international applicants for 2027/28.

Fees for 2027/28 are not published yet. The university sets tuition every year, so treat these Medipol University fees as a reference, not a quote.

## Studying Medicine and Dentistry at Medipol
Medicine takes six years and Dentistry five, plus an English prep year if you do not pass the exemption requirement. Dentistry comes in 100% English and 30% English versions, with clinical training in the dental hospital on the Haliç campus.

The language changes during the degree. At the International School of Medicine, Years I–III are fully in English. In Years IV–VI, lectures and theory exams stay in English, but clinical practice and clinical exams are in Turkish. For the new 100% English track, the university has not published the clinical-year language, so get the answer in writing.

At the International School of Medicine, international students must reach **C1 Turkish** to work with patients. You can meet this through Turkish prep, compulsory courses Türkçe I–V (A1 in Year I to C1 in Year III) or a university exam. It must be done before Year IV, and it is a condition for starting clinical years. A specific TÖMER certificate is not required. The 30% English track also requires a Turkish test or a Turkish prep year.

According to the university, Medipol Mega University Hospital is accredited by Joint Commission International, and medical, dental and nursing students train in the Mega Hospitals complex. Public sources place it in Bağcılar on the European side, while the dorms are in Kavacık, so plan for the commute.

If you are comparing MBBS in Turkey options, check first with your national medical council (PMDC, NMC or MDCN) how it treats clinical training delivered in Turkish. To study medicine in Turkey privately in English, expect roughly $30,000–60,000 a year; our [Study in Turkey guide](/study-in-turkey) compares the options.

## Admission requirements for international students
Medipol University admission requirements start with your school certificate. The threshold is 60% for most countries, including Pakistan, India, Egypt, Nigeria (WAEC), the Gulf states and Kazakhstan; for Uzbekistan, Kyrgyzstan and Tajikistan it is an average of 3 out of 5. Seats in each program are limited.

<!-- block: processBlock title="How to apply, step by step" -->
1. Check your grades — 60% for most countries. The university also lists SAT (1000+) and ACT (22+ for Medicine and Dentistry). YÖS is not required.
2. Prepare English proof — IELTS Academic 6.5, TOEFL iBT 84 or PTE Academic 71 (online and home versions not accepted), or pass Medipol's exemption exam with 75.
3. Apply online — one application per semester, programs ranked by priority. Upload your certificate, transcript and passport. Review takes up to one week.
4. Pay the deposit — after the Conditional Acceptance Letter, pay the advance tuition deposit by the deadline. It counts toward tuition but is non-refundable.
5. Get the Official Acceptance Letter — use it to apply for a student visa at the Turkish consulate.
6. Legalize documents — apostille for Pakistan, India, Saudi Arabia and Bangladesh; consular legalization for Nigeria, the UAE, Egypt, Qatar, Kuwait, Iraq and Jordan.
7. Register in Istanbul — bring originals with Turkish translations and the equivalence certificate (Denklik) for your school diploma, requested via e-Denklik or a Turkish consulate.

Dates for the 2027/28 intake have not been announced; the university publishes them closer to the intake.

## Medipol University ranking and recognition
Medipol is in the 801–1000 band of the THE World University Rankings 2026, up from 1501+ a year earlier, and 9th of 109 Turkish universities. It is not in the overall QS World University Rankings, but appears in QS by Subject 2026 (701–850) and QS Europe (601–650).

For future doctors, accreditation matters more. TEPDAD accredits the School of Medicine until 2031 and the International School of Medicine until 2030, and TEPDAD is recognized by WFME until July 2033. That is relevant for the USMLE route, as ECFMG has required WFME-recognized accreditation since 2024; confirm current rules with ECFMG.

## Student visa, residence permit and living costs
Citizens of Pakistan, India, Nigeria and Egypt need a visa. Saudi and UAE citizens enter visa-free for 90 days in 180, but the Turkish foreign ministry recommends a student visa for study. Some universities accept permit applications after entry on an e-Visa or visa-free, so confirm this with Medipol first.

After enrollment you apply for a student residence permit on e-ikamet and submit the file through the university. The card costs 964 TL in 2026, students pay no permit fee, and processing takes up to 90 days. Register for state insurance (GSS) within three months of enrollment, or buy a private policy. Bachelor's students can work part-time from the second year with a work permit.

Medipol has about 2,400 dorm beds in Kavacık; international students register in person with the International Office, which quotes current prices. More on visas in our [Study in Turkey hub](/study-in-turkey).

## Scholarships and discounts
Each Medipol University scholarship is a tuition discount. The university does not say whether discounts combine, so count them separately until your offer letter shows the total.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Discounts for the 2026/27 intake" -->
- Special Country Scholarship — 15% for citizens of about 60 countries, including Pakistan, India, Nigeria, Egypt, Bangladesh, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan and Kyrgyzstan. Gulf states are not listed.
- SAT — 10% for 1400+ in Medicine, Dentistry and English Pharmacy; 5–15% for 1100+ in other programs.
- Lump-sum payment — 10%.
- Family — 10% each when relatives study together; partner schools — 5%.
- Merit from Year 2 — top three students with GPA 2.91+ get 100%, 50% or 25% off.

<!-- block: textContent -->
Discounts may work differently in the prep year. For full funding, the government's Türkiye Bursları scholarship needs 90% grades for Medicine, Dentistry and Pharmacy, with applications from 10 January to 20 February; see our [Türkiye Bursları guide](/study-in-turkey/turkiye-burslari).

### Is Medipol recognized?
Medipol was established by Turkish law in 2009. Its medical faculties hold TEPDAD accreditation, recognized by WFME. Recognition for work in your country depends on your national authority.

### How much is Medicine at Medipol per year?
In 2026/27, the 100% English track costs $40,000 and the 30% English track $34,000, with 10% off for lump-sum payment. Fees for 2027/28 are not published yet.

### Can I study Medicine at Medipol in English?
Theory, yes. At the International School of Medicine, clinical practice and exams in Years IV–VI are in Turkish. The clinical-year language of the new 100% English track has not been published.

### Do I need YÖS or SAT?
No. Admission rests mainly on your school certificate, usually 60%+. A SAT score of 1400+ gives 10% off Medicine and Dentistry.

### Do I need to learn Turkish?
For Medicine, yes: the International School of Medicine requires C1 before Year IV, and the 30% English track requires a Turkish test or prep year. For other programs it helps in daily life; the Turkish Foundation School costs $1,390 (2026/27).

### Can I work while studying?
Yes, part-time from the second year, with a work permit your employer applies for.

### What is the Medipol University acceptance rate?
Medipol does not publish one. It sets minimum requirements and limited seats, so apply early with a complete file.
