# Brief: Study in Turkey hub (EN)

- **URL:** `/study-in-turkey`
- **Type:** country-hub (parent of all EN Turkey pages)
- **Audience:** international applicants 17–25 and parents from Pakistan (main), India, Nigeria, Egypt, Bangladesh, GCC expat families. Bachelor's after high school is the main scenario; master's second.
- **Intent:** "Is Turkey a good, affordable option, how do I get in, what visa do I need, what are the catches?"
- **Fact sheets:** `content/facts/turkey.md` (international sections), `content/facts/turkey-medipol.md` (as the example of private fees/admission). Do NOT use the RU hub text — write for this audience from the facts.

## Structure

1. **Intro** (no H2): who Turkey suits and who it doesn't, in 3 short paragraphs; primary keyword in the first sentence.
2. **H2: How much does it cost to study in Turkey** — tableBlock: state vs private universities (bachelor's in English, Medicine, Dentistry, master's) — ranges with year.
3. **H2: Universities in Turkey: state vs private** — comparison table (admission, English, competition, cost, who it suits).
4. **H2: Admission requirements** — processBlock (6 steps) + three routes: school grades (private), TR-YÖS, SAT/IB/A-level. English proof. Link to admission page.
5. **video slot:** "Applying to a private university in Istanbul: from online form to acceptance letter", 90 sec.
6. **H2: Student visa and residence permit** — who needs a visa (Pakistan, India, Nigeria, Egypt), apostille vs consular legalization by country, e-ikamet after arrival. Link to visa page.
7. **surveyBlock**
8. **H2: What to know before you apply** — honest risks: 30% English programs and prep year, Turkish for clinical years in Medicine, Denklik deadline, address rules for the residence permit in Istanbul, non-refundable deposits, visa refusals.
9. **H2: Türkiye Bursları and other ways to pay less** — what the scholarship covers, competition, plan B (private university discounts). Link.
10. **H2: Living and working as a student** — cost of living range, work permit rules by year.
11. **H2: Universities we work with** — Medipol, Bahçeşehir, Altınbaş (private), METU, Istanbul University (state) — 1–2 sentences each + links. Use only facts available in the fact sheets; for universities without facts yet, keep to "private university in Istanbul" + link.
12. **video slot:** "A day as an international student in Istanbul", 60 sec.
13. **accordionBlock** — FAQ 6–8.
14. **cta** — free consultation: check your grades and budget, shortlist 3–4 programs, map your visa route.
