<!-- block: textContent -->
The cost of studying in Hungary for an international student is usually tuition of about €6,000–10,000 (or $6,000–8,000) a year for a non-medical bachelor's degree, plus roughly €800–1,300 a month to live in Budapest. English-taught medicine costs more: about €15,800–18,700 or $16,750–17,900 a year at the four classic medical universities.

Each university sets its own fees and currency: Semmelweis and Szeged bill in euros, Debrecen and Pécs in US dollars. So every price here is in the university's currency, with the year of the price list.

Below: fees by university type, living costs by city, insurance, a sample budget and scholarships. For the wider picture, see our [study in Hungary](/study-in-hungary) guide.

## Tuition fees for bachelor's and master's programs

Outside medicine, the gap between state and private universities is small. For example, the private [Budapest Metropolitan University (METU)](/study-in-hungary/metropolitan-university-budapest) charges about €7,000–10,000 a year, while the state Budapest University of Technology and Economics (BME) charges €6,400–7,000 a year for a bachelor's degree.

A few details change the final bill. Debrecen adds an application fee of $150 and an entrance fee of $350, and its fee already includes health insurance. METU warns that its fees may rise each year in line with inflation. Debrecen has not yet published its 2026/27 price list for non-medical programs, so check the university's site before you plan.

Private universities such as [IBS Budapest](/study-in-hungary/ibs-budapest) and [McDaniel College Budapest](/study-in-hungary/mcdaniel-college-budapest) publish their own fees; we confirm current figures during a consultation.

## Living costs in Budapest and Debrecen

A realistic figure for a student in Budapest is about €800–1,300 a month. The lower end means a shared flat or a dorm room; the upper end means your own apartment. Debrecen and other regional cities are about 15–25% cheaper, from around €650 a month.

## Health insurance and residence permit costs

Health insurance is mandatory for a student residence permit: you need a policy or enough money to cover medical costs. Many universities include insurance in tuition. Debrecen and Szeged do this.

If your university does not, a full-time foreign student can sign a health care agreement with NEAK, the national health insurance fund. The contribution is linked to 30% of the minimum wage, which gives about 96,840 HUF a month in 2026.

The residence permit itself has a fee. The first student permit costs €110 at a Hungarian consulate. Inside Hungary, it costs 26,000 HUF online through Enter Hungary or 39,000 HUF in person. Renewal online costs 26,000 HUF. Stipendium Hungaricum holders pay no permit fee. Full steps are on our [Hungary student visa](/study-in-hungary/hungary-student-visa) page.

Hungary's immigration office (OIF) does not set a fixed minimum amount for your bank account. Figures you see online are estimates. What you do need is proof that you can pay tuition and living costs for the whole year, through bank statements, income documents or a scholarship.

<!-- block: processBlock title="How to plan your study budget for Hungary" -->
1. Pick two or three programs and download each university's current price list. Note the currency and the year.
2. Add one-off costs: application, entrance exam, registration and any deposit.
3. Choose your city and housing type, then multiply the monthly estimate by 12.
4. Check whether health insurance is included. If not, add the NEAK contribution or a private policy.
5. Add the residence permit fee, flights and document legalization in your home country.
6. Prepare proof of funds that covers the full total for the first year.

## Scholarships and ways to lower the cost

The Stipendium Hungaricum scholarship is the main way to study in Hungary without paying tuition. In 2026/27 it covered:

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="What Stipendium Hungaricum covers (2026/27)" -->
- Free tuition.
- A monthly stipend of 43,700 HUF (about €110) for 12 months a year for bachelor's, master's and one-tier master's students.
- A dormitory place or 40,000 HUF a month toward rent.
- Medical care under Hungarian law plus extra insurance of up to 65,000 HUF a year.
- No fee for the student residence permit or its renewal.

<!-- block: textContent -->
Pakistan, India, Nigeria, Egypt and Bangladesh are on the list of partner countries. Among Gulf states, the UAE, Oman and Bahrain are on the list, while Saudi Arabia, Qatar and Kuwait are not. You can apply only through your country of citizenship, and your application counts only if your country's partner institution nominates you. In 2026/27 the program included 30 universities and about 900 programs, including medicine, dentistry and pharmacy.

The 2027/28 call is not yet published. Based on previous years, the deadline is expected around mid-January 2027. Read the full details on our [Stipendium Hungaricum](/study-in-hungary/stipendium-hungaricum) page.

Fee-paying students also have options:

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Other ways to reduce costs" -->
- Pay the full year at once where it is cheaper. At Debrecen, medicine costs $16,900 paid at once versus $17,900 by semester (2026/27).
- Enroll early. Szeged gives a €300 discount on the first semester to medical students who enroll by 30 April (2026/27).
- Choose a regional city. Living in Debrecen is about 15–25% cheaper than in Budapest.
- Pick a university that includes health insurance in tuition.
- Work part time. Students may work up to 30 hours a week during the study period and full time for up to 90 days a year outside it.

## What to know before you apply

Here is what we tell every student before they commit money.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Honest points about costs in Hungary" -->
- **Stipendium Hungaricum is competitive and partial.** Your country, the university and the Tempus Public Foundation all assess you. A pass at the university does not guarantee the scholarship. The foundation itself says 40,000 HUF does not cover rent in big cities, especially Budapest. Plan your own money for living costs anyway.
- **Scholarship holders must learn Hungarian.** In year one, you attend a Hungarian course and take an exam at the end of semester two. Skip the course and the stipend stops. Fail the exam and it drops to 30,000 HUF a month.
- **Medicine starts with an entrance exam.** Semmelweis tests biology, chemistry and English online, followed by an interview. Debrecen tests biology plus chemistry or physics, in writing and orally. The program is six years long, so budget for all six, not just year one.
- **Governance at state universities is changing.** Since 2021, Semmelweis, Debrecen, Szeged and Pécs have been run by public foundations. Under current plans, this model is being reformed and the foundations are due to close on 1 August 2027. They remain state universities, and the change does not affect your degree. Fees for later years may be revised, so check each year's price list.
- **Budapest is not cheap.** Old estimates of $500–800 a month are too low. Plan for about €800–1,300 a month in the capital.

### How much does it cost to study in Hungary per year?
A non-medical bachelor's degree costs about €6,000–10,000 or $6,000–8,000 a year in tuition. Living costs add about €800–1,300 a month in Budapest or from about €650 in Debrecen. English-taught medicine costs about €15,800–18,700 or $16,750–17,900 a year.

### Is studying in Hungary free for international students?
Tuition is free only for Stipendium Hungaricum holders; everyone else pays the university's fee. The scholarship adds a stipend and housing support but does not cover all living costs.

### Can Pakistani and Indian students get Stipendium Hungaricum?
Yes. Pakistan and India are partner countries, as are Nigeria, Egypt and Bangladesh. You apply through the program and must be nominated by your country's partner institution, such as HEC in Pakistan or UGC in India.

### How much money do I need to show for a Hungarian student visa?
Hungary's immigration office does not set a fixed minimum amount. You must show you can cover tuition and living costs for the year. Bank statements, income documents or a scholarship award all count as proof.

### Is health insurance included in tuition?
It depends on the university. Debrecen and Szeged include health insurance in their fees. Otherwise you can sign a NEAK health care agreement, which costs about 96,840 HUF a month in 2026, or show another form of cover.

### Can I work while studying in Hungary?
Yes. Students may work up to 30 hours a week during the study period. Outside it, you can work full time for up to 90 days a year.

### Which is cheaper for medicine, Debrecen or Semmelweis?
Debrecen charges $16,900 a year if paid at once (2026/27). Semmelweis charges €9,340 per semester, about €18,680 a year, for the intake with a 31 May 2027 deadline. The currencies differ, so compare them at the exchange rate on the day you pay.
