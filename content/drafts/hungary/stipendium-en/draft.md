Stipendium Hungaricum is a Hungarian government scholarship that pays your tuition, gives you a monthly stipend and helps with housing. You can only apply if your home country has signed up to the program and nominates you. Pakistan, India, Nigeria, Egypt and Bangladesh are all on the list.

Hungary's Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Trade runs the program, and the Tempus Public Foundation (TPF) coordinates it. The scholarship exists only through bilateral agreements with more than 100 "Sending Partners," the countries allowed to nominate students. In 2026/27, 30 universities offered about 900 programs taught in foreign languages. These included one-tier programs in medicine, dentistry and pharmacy.

TPF has not yet published the official call for 2027/28. In past years the round opened in November and closed in mid-January, so expect a deadline around mid-January 2027. All amounts and dates on this page come from the 2026/27 round unless we say otherwise.

<!-- block: textContent -->
## Who can apply for Stipendium Hungaricum

Your citizenship decides whether you can apply, not where you live. You apply through your own country, and your application is only valid if that country's Sending Partner nominates you. The Sending Partner is usually the education ministry or a national scholarship agency.

<!-- block: tableBlock -->
| Country | On the 2026/27 list (full degree programs) | Who nominates you |
|---|---|---|
| Pakistan | Yes | Higher Education Commission (HEC) |
| India | Yes | University Grants Commission (UGC) |
| Nigeria | Yes | Federal Scholarship Board |
| Egypt | Yes | Cultural and Missions Sector |
| Bangladesh | Yes | Secondary and Higher Education Division |
| UAE, Oman, Bahrain | Yes | Your country's partner body (see the official partners list) |
| Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait | No | Not eligible |

<!-- block: textContent -->
A few rules catch people out:

- If you live in the Gulf with a Pakistani, Indian or Egyptian passport, you apply through your country of citizenship, not your country of residence.
- Hungarian dual citizens, refugees and holders of permanent residence in Hungary cannot apply.
- You must be at least 18 by 31 August of the year your studies start. For 2026/27, that meant being born on or before 31 August 2008. Dance programs are the only exception.

The scholarship covers bachelor's, master's and one-tier master's programs (such as medicine), as well as doctoral programs.

## What the scholarship covers in 2026/27

The package is broad, but the stipend is small. TPF has not announced the 2027/28 amounts yet. The figures below are for 2026/27.

<!-- block: tableBlock -->
| Item | 2026/27 |
|---|---|
| Tuition | Free |
| Monthly stipend (bachelor's, master's, one-tier master's) | 43,700 HUF (about €110), 12 months a year |
| Monthly stipend (PhD) | 140,000 HUF in phase 1 (4 semesters), 180,000 HUF in phase 2 (4 semesters) |
| Housing | A dormitory place **or** 40,000 HUF a month toward rent |
| Health care | Care under Hungarian law, plus extra insurance of up to 65,000 HUF a year |
| Student residence permit fee | Waived, for both the first permit and extensions |

<!-- block: textContent -->
TPF itself calls this partial support. It says 40,000 HUF does not cover rent in large cities, especially Budapest. As a rough guide, a student in Budapest needs about €800–1,300 a month. Regional cities such as Debrecen cost roughly 15–25% less, from about €650 a month. Before you apply, plan how your family will cover the gap. Our [cost of studying in Hungary](/study-in-hungary/cost-of-studying-in-hungary) guide has the breakdown.

## How to apply: steps and timeline

The application itself is free. TPF recommends applying directly, without an agency. You submit the application yourself. We help you choose programs, prepare documents and check everything before you send it.

<!-- block: processBlock title="The application process (2026/27 dates as a guide)" -->
1. Check your Sending Partner's rules. Your ministry or agency sets its own nomination conditions and may ask for extra documents or its own form.
2. Choose up to two programs, in order of priority. After the deadline you cannot change the programs or their order.
3. Prepare your documents. Pakistan, India and Bangladesh issue apostilles. Nigeria, Egypt, the UAE and Qatar do not, so your certificates go through consular legalization: education ministry, then foreign ministry, then the Hungarian embassy or consulate. Translate documents into English or Hungarian.
4. Submit your application to TPF and your Sending Partner by the deadline. In the last round, that was 15 January 2026 at 14:00 CET. For 2027/28, expect a deadline around mid-January 2027.
5. Wait for nomination. In 2026/27, countries had to send their nominations by 28 February.
6. Take the university's entrance exam or interview, held between mid-March and late May. Universities score candidates out of 100.
7. Get your result, expected between late June and mid-July. TPF makes the final allocation. A good university score does not guarantee a scholarship.
8. Apply for your visa and residence permit in July–August. Start as soon as you get a positive result, because the official process can take up to 30 days. In 2026/27, scholarship holders had to arrive by 30 September.

<!-- block: textContent -->
You apply for the student residence permit at the Hungarian embassy or consulate in your home country. Being a scholarship holder does not guarantee a visa. For the documents and the process, see our [Hungary student visa](/study-in-hungary/hungary-student-visa) guide.

## Choosing programs and writing your motivation letter

With only two choices, each one matters. We usually suggest pairing the program you want most with a second one where your grades and background fit comfortably. The program list changes each year, so check it again when the 2027/28 call is published.

Medicine is the most competitive choice. For self-funding applicants, Semmelweis tests medical biology, medical chemistry, general English and biomedical English online, then holds an interview. Debrecen sets written tests in biology and in chemistry or physics, followed by an oral exam. Expect the same level of preparation for a scholarship place. Read more in our guide to [medicine in Hungary](/study-in-hungary/medicine-in-hungary).

A strong motivation letter answers three questions:

- Why this program? Name the courses or specializations that match your goals.
- Why Hungary, and why this university?
- What will you do after graduating, and how does it connect to your home country?

Keep it specific and honest. A reviewer reads hundreds of letters that call Hungary "a beautiful country with a rich history." A concrete plan stands out more than general praise.

## Your chances and your obligations

Competition is high, and three parties assess you: your Sending Partner, the university and TPF. There is no official acceptance rate, so treat any figure you see online with caution. The safest approach is a strong application plus a backup plan.

Winning the scholarship comes with obligations. In your first year you must attend a Hungarian language course and pass an exam at the end of the second semester. If you do not attend, you lose the stipend. If you attend but fail, your stipend drops from 43,700 HUF to 30,000 HUF a month.

Your home country may add its own conditions, such as returning after graduation. Only your Sending Partner can confirm these, so ask before you accept the nomination.

## What to know before you apply

We would rather you hear this from us now than find out in Budapest.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Honest points, with a way forward" -->
- **It is not guaranteed.** You need a nomination from your country, a university score and final approval from TPF. Solution: apply for paid programs at the same time as your scholarship application (see plan B below).
- **It does not cover everything.** The stipend is about €110 a month, and the rent support does not cover a room in Budapest. Solution: budget €800–1,300 a month for Budapest, or consider a regional city.
- **Medicine is demanding.** English-taught medicine has entrance exams in the sciences and English, and the workload stays heavy for six years. Hungary's accreditation agency, MAB, is recognized by WFME until 31 March 2032. Before you choose, check what your home medical regulator requires to recognize the degree.
- **University governance is changing.** Since 2021, Semmelweis, Debrecen, Szeged, Pécs and Corvinus have been run by public trust foundations. Hungarian media report that this model is being reversed, with the foundations due to close on 1 August 2027. These remain state universities, and the same accredited university issues your degree.
- **You will need some Hungarian.** Scholarship holders must take the first-year language course and exam. It is part of the deal, not an optional extra.
- **A scholarship is not a visa.** Consulates assess your application separately. Solution: prepare your permit documents while you wait for your result.

## Plan B: studying in Hungary without the scholarship

If you are not nominated or do not get a place, you can still study in Hungary as a fee-paying student. Paid deadlines fall later than the scholarship results. Semmelweis accepts medicine applications until 31 May 2027, and Pécs until 30 June 2027. That gives you time to run both routes side by side.

### Is Stipendium Hungaricum fully funded?
Not fully. It covers tuition and gives you a monthly stipend (43,700 HUF in 2026/27), a dormitory place or 40,000 HUF a month toward rent, and health cover. TPF itself says this is partial support. In Budapest, rent and daily costs are well above these amounts.

### When is the deadline for 2027/28?
The official call has not been published yet. In the 2026/27 round, the deadline was 15 January 2026 at 14:00 CET. Based on past years, expect the round to open around November 2026 and close around mid-January 2027.

### Can students from Pakistan apply?
Yes. Pakistan is a Sending Partner, and the Higher Education Commission (HEC) handles nomination. You submit your application to TPF and follow HEC's own rules and deadline. Your application only counts if HEC nominates you.

### I live in the UAE or Saudi Arabia but hold another passport. Can I apply?
You apply through your country of citizenship, not residence. For example, an Indian citizen living in Dubai applies through India's nominating body. Citizens of Saudi Arabia, Qatar and Kuwait were not eligible in 2026/27. Citizens of the UAE, Oman and Bahrain were.

### Can I apply through an agency?
TPF recommends applying directly, and the application is free. You submit it yourself through the official system. An adviser like AcademGo can help you choose programs, prepare and translate documents, and check your application before you send it.

### Do I have to learn Hungarian?
Yes, to a basic level. Scholarship holders must attend a Hungarian course in their first year and pass an exam at the end of the second semester. If you fail the exam, your stipend drops to 30,000 HUF a month. If you do not attend, you lose it.

### Can I work while on the scholarship?
A student residence permit lets you work up to 30 hours a week during the study period. Outside it, you can work full time for up to 90 days a year. Check that any job fits around your classes and the Hungarian course.

### How many programs can I choose?
Up to two, in order of priority. You cannot change the programs or their order after the deadline, so decide carefully before you submit.
