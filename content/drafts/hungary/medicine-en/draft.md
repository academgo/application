You can study medicine in Hungary fully in English at four public universities: Semmelweis in Budapest, Debrecen, Szeged and Pécs. Tuition is about €15,800–18,700 or $16,750–17,900 a year, and every university sets its own entrance exam in biology, chemistry or physics, and English.

The program is a single six-year degree. Semmelweis University, for example, runs General Medicine over 12 semesters and awards the Doctor of General Medicine degree. Applicants from Pakistan, India and Bangladesh often search for "MBBS in Hungary", but the Hungarian degree is structured as a six-year single-cycle program, not a British-style MBBS.

Below: costs, exams, the scholarship route and what to check before the 2027/28 intake.

For the full country overview, see our [Study in Hungary](/study-in-hungary) guide.

## Where to study medicine in Hungary in English

All four are public universities. They differ in city, currency, exam format and deadlines.

- **Semmelweis University (Budapest)** is the only one of the four in the capital. Life in Budapest costs more than in regional cities. Read more on our [Semmelweis University page](/study-in-hungary/semmelweis-university).
- **University of Debrecen** also teaches dentistry in English at $17,500 a year (2026/27). See the [University of Debrecen page](/study-in-hungary/university-of-debrecen).
- **University of Szeged** includes basic health insurance in Hungary in its tuition fee.
- **University of Pécs** has already published its 2027/28 dates: the application deadline is June 30, 2027, and classes start on September 1, 2027.

Since 2021, all four universities have been run by public trust foundations rather than directly by the state. This model is being reformed in 2026–2027 (more on this below). They remain public universities, and your degree is issued by the same accredited institution.

## Entrance exams: what each university tests

There is no national medical entrance test in Hungary. Each university runs its own exam, and Semmelweis and Pécs offer online formats.

- **Semmelweis:** online tests in Moodle, 20 questions each, in medical biology, medical chemistry, general English and biomedical English. Then an interview in person or on Zoom about your motivation and English. Exam sessions run once or twice a week from early March to the end of June, and the decision comes within two weeks.
- **Debrecen:** a written test in biology (compulsory) plus chemistry or physics of your choice, 45 minutes each. Then an oral exam in the same subjects.
- **Szeged:** biology, chemistry (or physics), and English.
- **Pécs:** a comprehensive written and oral exam, which can be taken online.

Revise biology and chemistry at final-school-year level and practice answering in English, in writing and aloud.

## Preparatory year: when it makes sense

If your chemistry or physics is weak, or your school system is quite different from the exam content, a foundation year can help. The University of Debrecen lists a Foundation Year at $6,000 (2025/26 price list). Ask the university whether completing it affects admission to medicine, and check the price for 2027/28 before you pay.

A foundation year adds a year and its living costs to your budget. For strong applicants, it is usually better to prepare at home and take the exam directly.

<!-- block: processBlock title="How to apply to a Hungarian medical university (2027/28 intake)" -->
1. Choose one or two universities and check the exam format, currency and deadline for each.
2. Prepare documents: your school-leaving certificate and transcripts, translated into English or Hungarian if needed, and a valid passport.
3. Apostille or legalize your school certificate early. Pakistan (since March 9, 2023), India and Bangladesh (since March 30, 2025) issue apostilles. Nigeria, Egypt and the UAE are not in the Apostille Convention, so documents go through consular legalization.
4. Apply online and pay the application or entrance fee.
5. Sit the entrance exam and interview (Semmelweis runs sessions from early March to late June).
6. After admission, pay the deposit or first-semester fee as the university requires.
7. Apply for a student residence permit at the Hungarian consulate in your country. See [the Hungary student visa guide](/study-in-hungary/hungary-student-visa).

<!-- block: textContent -->
Each Hungarian university recognizes your foreign school certificate itself for admission, so there is no separate nostrification step before you apply. Documents that are not in English or Hungarian can be translated into English. Whether an apostille is required depends on the university, but having it done in advance prepares you for both enrollment and the residence permit.

## Stipendium Hungaricum for medicine

Stipendium Hungaricum is a Hungarian government scholarship, and in 2026/27 it included single-cycle programs in medicine, dentistry and pharmacy. It works only through agreements with partner countries. Pakistan, India, Nigeria, Egypt and Bangladesh are on the list, as are the UAE, Oman and Bahrain. Saudi Arabia, Qatar and Kuwait are not.

The scholarship covered the following in 2026/27:

- free tuition;
- a monthly stipend of 43,700 HUF (about €110) for 12 months a year;
- a dormitory place or 40,000 HUF a month toward rent;
- medical care under Hungarian law plus extra insurance up to 65,000 HUF a year.

Your application is valid only if your country nominates you. The nominating bodies include HEC in Pakistan, UGC in India, the Federal Scholarship Board in Nigeria, the Cultural and Missions Sector in Egypt, and the Secondary and Higher Education Division in Bangladesh. You can choose up to two programs, and the university scores you out of 100 through an exam or interview. A good result at the university does not guarantee the scholarship: the final decision is made by Tempus Public Foundation.

The 2027/28 call has not been published yet. Based on previous years, the deadline is expected around mid-January 2027. Details are on our [Stipendium Hungaricum page](/study-in-hungary/stipendium-hungaricum).

## Hungarian in the clinic

The medical program is taught in English, but patients in Hungarian hospitals speak Hungarian. From the clinical years onward, you will need at least basic Hungarian to take a history and talk to patients. Plan to learn the language from your first year, and ask each university how Hungarian is built into the curriculum.

For Stipendium Hungaricum holders, the rule is formal. In the first year, scholarship holders must attend a Hungarian course and pass an exam at the end of the second semester. Those who do not attend lose the stipend. Those who attend but fail receive 30,000 HUF a month instead of 43,700 HUF.

## Recognition of a Hungarian medical degree

Hungary's accreditation agency, MAB, is recognized by the World Federation for Medical Education (WFME) until March 31, 2032. It is the Hungarian accreditor that holds WFME recognition, not the individual universities.

Recognition at home is a separate question. Medical regulators in Pakistan, India, Nigeria, Egypt and Bangladesh each set their own rules for doctors trained abroad, including licensing exams and internship requirements. Check the current rules of your national regulator before you apply, not after graduation.

## What to know before you apply

A medical degree in Hungary is a serious commitment of six years and a large budget. Here is what we tell every family.

**Stipendium Hungaricum is competitive and does not cover everything.** You need a nomination from your own country, plus a university result and a final decision by Tempus Public Foundation. The 40,000 HUF housing allowance does not cover rent in large cities, especially Budapest, and Tempus Public Foundation says so itself. Always plan a fee-paying option in parallel.

**Getting in is only the first step.** Entrance exams test biology, chemistry or physics, and English at a real level. The pre-clinical years are demanding, and not every student moves to the next year on time. Prepare before arrival, not during the first semester.

**University governance is changing.** Since 2021, Semmelweis, Debrecen, Szeged and Pécs have been managed by public trust foundations. According to Hungarian media, these foundations are planned to be dissolved on August 1, 2027, and control returned to the state. For students, this does not change the degree: it is issued by the same accredited university.

**Hungarian will be needed.** English covers the lectures, but not the conversations with patients during clinical training.

**Living costs are real.** A student in Budapest needs roughly €800–1,300 a month, depending on whether you share a room or rent your own flat. In Debrecen and other regional cities, it is about 15–25% less, from roughly €650 a month. This comes on top of tuition.

**Work will not pay for medical school.** Students may work up to 30 hours a week during term, but the medical timetable leaves little room for that.

### Is there MBBS in Hungary?

Hungarian universities offer a six-year single-cycle medical program rather than a British-style MBBS. Semmelweis awards the Doctor of General Medicine degree after 12 semesters. This is the program international applicants usually mean when they search for MBBS in Hungary.

### How much does a medical degree in Hungary cost?

Tuition at the four public medical universities is about €15,800–18,700 or $16,750–17,900 a year. Semmelweis and Szeged charge in euros, Debrecen and Pécs in dollars. Add one-time application, exam and registration fees, plus living costs of roughly €800–1,300 a month in Budapest or from about €650 in regional cities.

### Do Hungarian medical universities have an entrance exam?

Yes. Each university runs its own exam, usually in biology, chemistry or physics, and English, often followed by an interview or oral exam. Semmelweis, for example, uses online tests in Moodle and then an interview in person or on Zoom.

### What are the tuition fees at Semmelweis University?

For the intake with the May 31, 2027 deadline, Semmelweis lists €9,340 per semester, about €18,680 a year. One-time fees include a $400 entrance procedure fee, a $2,000 deposit counted toward the first semester, and a $230 registration fee.

### What are the fees and deadline at the University of Pécs?

The University of Pécs charges $16,750 a year for General Medicine in English: $9,000 for the first semester and $7,750 for the second. The application fee is $200, the exam $250 and registration $220. The deadline is June 30, 2027, with classes starting on September 1, 2027.

### Can I study medicine for free with Stipendium Hungaricum?

Yes, if your country is a partner and nominates you. In 2026/27 the scholarship covered tuition, a monthly stipend and housing support for single-cycle programs, including medicine. The competition is strong, and you apply yourself through the official system, so plan a fee-paying option as well.

### Do I need to speak Hungarian to study medicine?

Not for admission: the program is taught in English. You will need Hungarian during clinical training to communicate with patients, so start learning it in your first year.
