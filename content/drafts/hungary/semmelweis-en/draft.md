Semmelweis University is a medical university in Budapest that traces its history back to 1769. It has six faculties and around 16,000 students, and roughly a third of them are international students from 130 countries (university data). International applicants mostly come for three long programs taught in English: General Medicine, Dentistry and Pharmaceutical Sciences.

Semmelweis suits applicants who are strong in biology and chemistry, comfortable in English and ready to budget about €18,680 a year for medicine. You take the entrance tests online from home.

It is a poor fit if you need a low-cost option, a guaranteed dorm room or a program without an entrance exam.

## Programs and the degree you receive

The three long programs are one-tier (undivided) degrees. You don't need a separate bachelor's first.

- **General Medicine** — 6 years, 360 ECTS, Doctor of General Medicine (MD). The program is usually described as 2 years of theory, 1 preclinical year and 3 clinical years with block rotations in small groups.
- **Dentistry** — 5 years, Doctor of Medicine in Dentistry (DMD).
- **Pharmaceutical Sciences** — 5 years, Doctor of Pharmaceutical Sciences.

English BSc programs include Nursing, Midwifery, Dietetics, Physiotherapy, Optometry and Health Care Management. English MSc programs include Clinical Translational Medicine, Nursing, Physiotherapy, Psychobiology and Systemic Psychology.

If your biology and chemistry need work, there is a Medical Preparatory Year. It is run by [McDaniel College Budapest](/study-in-hungary/mcdaniel-college-budapest) in partnership with Semmelweis, not by Semmelweis itself. According to the university, 90% of its students get into the program they chose. For a wider look at the field, see [studying medicine in Hungary](/study-in-hungary/medicine-in-hungary).

## Admission requirements for international students

Semmelweis selects students mainly through its own entrance exam. The university itself recognizes your foreign school certificate. You must be at least 18 by the end of May of your first year of study. The university says it prefers applicants under 30.

English at upper-intermediate level is expected. A certificate is not required, because your English is tested in the exam. IELTS or TOEFL scores do not replace the exam. Applicants with a BSc or higher in a related field taught in English can ask the committee for an exam exemption. It is not automatic.

<!-- block: processBlock title="How to apply for September 2027" -->
1. Prepare your documents — school certificate and transcripts, passport. Documents not in English or Hungarian need a certified translation into either language. We recommend an apostille or consular legalization early, because you may need certified originals at enrollment and for the residence permit.
2. Create one account in SEMAPHOR and submit your application. The opening date for the 2027 cycle is announced by the university; the deadline is 31 May 2027. Pay the $400 admission procedure fee.
3. Take the online tests in Moodle: Medical Biology, Medical Chemistry, General English and Biomedical English. Each test has 20 questions and 20 minutes, under Safe Exam Browser with Zoom supervision.
4. Attend the interview online or in person in Budapest. It covers motivation, English and oral questions on the subjects.
5. Get the decision within 2 weeks of the exam. There is a waiting list. If needed, you can retake the exam once in the same calendar year, at least a week later.
6. Pay the $2,000 deposit within 14 days of admission. Under the current rules, the first semester's tuition and the $230 enrollment fee are due by 31 July.
7. If you finished school in 2027, upload your final certificate by 21 August 2027. Then apply for your visa.

<!-- block: textContent -->
Exams run once or twice a week from spring to early summer; exact dates appear in SEMAPHOR after you apply.

Pakistan (since 9 March 2023), India, Bangladesh, Saudi Arabia, Oman and Bahrain issue apostilles. Nigeria, Egypt, the UAE, Qatar and Kuwait are not in the Apostille Convention. Applicants from these countries need consular legalization, which usually goes through the education ministry, the foreign ministry and the Hungarian embassy.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="What to know before you apply" -->
- **Hungarian is part of the course.** It is taught as a subject in the English programs. Students usually need Hungarian to talk to patients from the third year. Start early and treat it as a real subject.
- **Dorm places are not guaranteed.** Semmelweis has 5 dormitories with more than 900 places. Places go by social and financial criteria, and applications open in Neptun in August for about 15 days. That is too late for most visa students, so plan private housing.
- **The deposit is non-refundable.** Before enrollment, fees are refunded except the $2,000 deposit. The $400 fee is refunded only if you withdraw before the deadline. A proportional refund is possible if you stop studying for a valid reason, including visa problems.
- **Visa students may have to pay early.** The university can ask for 1–2 semesters of tuition before you apply for the visa. Plan your funds for this.
- **Check licensing at home.** If you plan to practice in Pakistan, India, Nigeria or elsewhere, check your home regulator's rules for foreign medical graduates before you apply.
- **Apply directly.** Semmelweis accepts applications directly from students. You submit your own application in SEMAPHOR. We help you prepare documents, check the application before you submit it, prepare for the exam and build a plan B.

## Rankings and accreditation

In the THE World University Rankings 2026, Semmelweis is placed 251–300 worldwide and 176–200 in Medical and Health. In ShanghaiRanking (ARWU) 2025, it is 601–700 overall, 51–75 in Pharmacy and Pharmaceutical Sciences, 151–200 in Clinical Medicine and 201–300 in Dentistry. In the QS World University Rankings by Subject 2026, it is 291 in Life Sciences and Medicine, according to the university. US News Best Global Universities 2026 ranks it 164th in Clinical Medicine.

On accreditation, the Hungarian Accreditation Committee (MAB) is recognized by WFME until 31 March 2032. WFME recognizes the Hungarian accreditor, not individual universities.

Semmelweis is a state medical university. Since 2021 it has been run by a public trust foundation, and its governance model is being reformed in 2026–2027.

## Housing and student life

Most international students rent privately. In central districts, a room costs from about €300 and a studio from about €500 per month. The university's Semmelweis Housing service helps you search. You need an address in Hungary for your residence permit, so sort housing out before you travel.

Plan roughly €800–1,300 per month for living in Budapest in total. A shared flat or dorm puts you near the lower end; your own apartment puts you near the top. Health care is already included in tuition during active semesters.

## Student visa

Applicants from visa-required countries, including Pakistan, India, Nigeria, Bangladesh and Egypt, apply for a residence permit for studies at the Hungarian embassy or consulate in their country. The consular fee is €110. Processing takes 15 days to prepare the case and up to 60 days for a decision.

You need proof of admission, proof of funds for tuition and living costs, a Hungarian address, health insurance, a valid passport and photos. There is no fixed official minimum balance. With the permit, you can work up to 30 hours a week during term. After graduation, you can apply for a permit of up to 9 months to look for a job or start a business. Details are on our [Hungary student visa](/study-in-hungary/hungary-student-visa) page.

## Other options

If Semmelweis is out of budget or your exam result falls short, compare other medical schools in Hungary. Medicine in English at the four classic Hungarian medical universities costs roughly €15,800–18,700 or $16,750–17,900 a year: Semmelweis and Szeged charge in euros, Debrecen and Pécs in dollars. The [University of Debrecen](/study-in-hungary/university-of-debrecen) also teaches medicine in English. For fully funded study, see [Stipendium Hungaricum](/study-in-hungary/stipendium-hungaricum); for the full country picture, start with [studying in Hungary](/study-in-hungary).

<!-- block: accordionBlock title="Frequently asked questions" -->
### How much does Semmelweis cost for international students?
For the September 2027 intake, medicine and dentistry cost €9,340 per semester, about €18,680 a year. Pharmacy costs €5,820 per semester. One-time fees are $400 for admission, a $2,000 deposit and $230 for enrollment. Dentistry and pharmacy add material fees from the second year.

### Is the Semmelweis entrance exam held in Pakistan or India?
No. Semmelweis does not hold exams abroad. You take four online tests from home under Zoom supervision, then an interview online or in person in Budapest.

### What is the Semmelweis application deadline for 2027?
The deadline for Medicine, Dentistry and Pharmacy for September 2027 is 31 May 2027. Exams run from spring to early summer, so applying early gives you more exam dates and more time for the visa.

### Do I need IELTS or TOEFL for Semmelweis?
No certificate is required. Semmelweis expects upper-intermediate English and tests it in the exam and interview. A standardized test score does not replace the entrance exam.

### Can I study at Semmelweis on Stipendium Hungaricum?
Yes, Semmelweis takes part in the program. Pakistan, India, Nigeria, Egypt, Bangladesh, the UAE, Oman and Bahrain are partner countries; Saudi Arabia, Qatar and Kuwait are not. Your home country must nominate you, and the Tempus Public Foundation recommends applying directly, free of charge. The 2027/28 deadline is expected around mid-January 2027, but the official call is not yet published.

### Is Semmelweis accredited by WFME?
WFME recognizes the Hungarian Accreditation Committee (MAB), which accredits Hungarian universities, until 31 March 2032. WFME does not accredit individual universities directly.
