If you want to study in Hungary, you are looking at one of the most affordable ways to get an English-taught degree inside the European Union. Business, engineering, IT, design and full six-year medicine are all taught in English, and the country runs its own government scholarship, Stipendium Hungaricum, for students from more than 100 partner countries.

Hungary suits applicants who want an EU degree without Western European fees, and future doctors ready to sit a real entrance exam in biology and chemistry. It also suits students from Pakistan, India, Nigeria, Egypt and Bangladesh, whose countries are all on the scholarship list.

It is a weaker fit if you need a guaranteed place, expect the scholarship to cover all your costs, or plan to live in Budapest on a very tight budget. Below you will find fees by level, the types of universities, the admission steps, the visa route and the risks we explain to every family before they apply.

## How much does it cost to study in Hungary?
Outside medicine, a bachelor's degree taught in English costs about €6,000–10,000 or $6,000–8,000 per year, based on 2026/27 price lists. The gap between state and private universities is smaller than most people expect. Medicine is a separate price category.

Fees for 2027/28 are mostly not published yet, so the table shows the latest official price lists. Some universities bill in euros, others in US dollars; we quote each one in its own currency.

## Types of universities in Hungary
Universities in Hungary fall into four groups, and the group decides which diploma you receive and whether the scholarship applies. The table below is the quickest way to see the difference.

<!-- block: tableBlock -->
| Type | Examples | Diploma you receive | Stipendium Hungaricum |
|---|---|---|---|
| State university | BME, ELTE | Hungarian state-accredited degree | Yes, at participating universities |
| State university run by a public foundation since 2021 | Semmelweis, Debrecen, Szeged, Pécs, Corvinus | Hungarian state-accredited degree | Yes, at participating universities |
| Private Hungarian university | Budapest Metropolitan University (METU) | Hungarian degree | Only 5 creative programs at METU |
| Foreign institution operating in Hungary | McDaniel College Budapest (US), IBS Budapest (joint programs with the University of Buckingham, UK) | American BA (McDaniel); University of Buckingham degree plus IBS degree | No |

<!-- block: textContent -->
The "foundation" group needs a short explanation. Since 2021, several large state universities have been run by public foundations instead of directly by the state. This model is being reformed in 2026–2027 (details in the "What to know" section below). These universities stay public; do not confuse them with private schools.

## Admission requirements
Each university admits international students directly through its own online portal and checks your school certificate itself. You do not need a separate recognition procedure before applying. Most bachelor's programs ask for a completed secondary school certificate, English at roughly intermediate level or higher, and an online interview or test. Medicine adds a subject exam.

<!-- block: processBlock title="How to apply to a Hungarian university" -->
1. Choose 3–4 programs and check each one's requirements, deadline and fee currency. Medicine deadlines for the September 2027 intake are 15 May 2027 at Debrecen and 31 May 2027 at Semmelweis.
2. Prepare your documents: school certificate and transcript, passport, CV and photos. Get the certificate apostilled or legalized in your home country, and translated into English if it is in another language.
3. Submit the online application in the university's portal and pay the application fee.
4. Take the entrance exam or interview. At Semmelweis, the tests are online in Moodle (medical biology, medical chemistry, general and biomedical English), followed by an interview. At Debrecen, it is a written test in biology plus chemistry or physics, then an oral exam; online is possible if you cannot travel.
5. Receive your letter of acceptance and pay tuition as the university instructs. Some universities require payment before they issue the letter for your visa; at IBS, for example, non-EU students pay the first year plus registration up front.
6. Apply for the student residence permit at the Hungarian embassy or consulate in your country.

| Pakistan | Apostille (since 9 March 2023) | Eligible | HEC |

Consular legalization usually runs through your Ministry of Education, then your Ministry of Foreign Affairs, then the Hungarian embassy. Translations can be into English; you do not have to use one specific Hungarian translation office to apply. For GCC expat families, one rule matters: scholarship applications go through the student's country of citizenship, not the country of residence.

## Student visa and documents
Citizens of Pakistan, India, Nigeria, Bangladesh and Egypt apply for a residence permit for the purpose of studies at the Hungarian embassy or consulate in their home country. Once it is approved, you get an entry visa and then collect your residence card after arrival in Hungary, usually within 30 days.

What you will need:
- proof of admission from your university;
- proof of funds for the whole year, covering tuition and living costs (there is no fixed official minimum balance);
- proof of accommodation, meaning an address in Hungary;
- health insurance;
- a valid passport and photos.

The consular fee is €110; Stipendium Hungaricum scholars pay nothing. By law, the authority has 15 days to prepare the case and up to 60 days to decide, so apply as soon as your acceptance letter arrives. Step-by-step details are on our [Hungary student visa](/study-in-hungary/hungary-student-visa) page.

## What to know before you apply
AcademGo tells applicants what other agencies leave out. Here are the five points we cover with every family, each with a way to deal with it.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Five things to plan for" -->
- **Stipendium Hungaricum is competitive and nomination-based.** Your application counts only if your country's Sending Partner nominates you; then the university tests you and the Tempus Public Foundation makes the final decision. A good exam result alone does not guarantee the scholarship. Apply to fee-paying programs in parallel as plan B.
- **The scholarship does not cover everything.** In 2026/27 it included free tuition, 43,700 HUF (about €110) per month, a dormitory place or 40,000 HUF per month toward rent, and health care. The program itself says the rent support does not cover costs in big cities, especially Budapest. Budget your own money on top.
- **English-taught medicine is not "admission without exams."** Every classic medical university tests biology and chemistry (or physics) and interviews you in English. The pace is demanding, and universities do not publish official dropout figures. If your science is weak, a preparatory year (Debrecen's Basic Medicine Course or McDaniel's Pre-Med track) is the honest route, though it does not guarantee a place.
- **University governance is changing.** Semmelweis, Debrecen, Szeged, Pécs and Corvinus have been run by public foundations since 2021. According to Hungarian media, these foundations are planned to close on 1 August 2027, with rights returning to the state. The change does not affect your diploma: the same accredited university issues it.
- **You will need some Hungarian.** Scholarship holders must attend a Hungarian course in year one; those who skip it lose the stipend. Medical students need working Hungarian to talk to patients from year three.

<!-- block: textContent -->
On recognition back home: Hungary's accreditation agency (MAB) is recognized by WFME until 31 March 2032. That is a statement about the accreditor, not about each university. If you plan to practice medicine in your home country, check your medical council's current rules before you apply; we go through this on the [medicine in Hungary](/study-in-hungary/medicine-in-hungary) page.

## Living costs, work and student life
A student in Budapest needs roughly €800–1,300 per month. The lower end means a shared flat or a dormitory, the upper end a studio of your own. In Debrecen and other regional cities, plan for roughly 15–25% less, starting from about €650 per month.

<!-- block: tableBlock -->
| Item | Budapest | Debrecen |
|---|---|---|
| Living costs without rent (Numbeo, September 2026) | about €758 per month | about €677 per month |
| Rent | Room from about €300, studio from about €500 (Semmelweis housing guide) | Apartment 120,000–140,000 HUF per month (University of Debrecen) |
| Public transport | Monthly pass 8,950 HUF; student pass 3,450 HUF | — |

<!-- block: textContent -->
Find housing early. Semmelweis allocates its dormitory places on social criteria in August, which is too late for most visa applicants. McDaniel College has no dormitory at all. Debrecen tells fee-paying students to arrange their own accommodation before September. You also need an address for your residence permit.

On a student residence permit you can work up to 30 hours per week during the semester, and full time for up to 90 days a year outside it. After graduation, you can apply for a residence permit to seek a job or start a business for up to 9 months. From there, you can move to a Hungarian Card or an EU Blue Card without leaving the country.

### Are there English-taught programs in Hungary?
Yes. Hungarian universities teach bachelor's, master's and one-tier medical programs fully in English. In 2026/27, the Stipendium Hungaricum program alone listed about 900 foreign-language programs at 30 universities. You will still take some Hungarian language classes, especially in medicine and on the scholarship.

### Is studying in Hungary cheap?
It is cheaper than most of Western Europe, but not free. English-taught bachelor's programs cost about €6,000–10,000 or $6,000–8,000 per year (2026/27), and medicine costs roughly €15,800–18,700 or $16,750–17,900. Living costs in Budapest run about €800–1,300 per month.

### Who can apply for Stipendium Hungaricum?
Citizens of partner countries, including Pakistan, India, Nigeria, Egypt, Bangladesh, the UAE, Oman and Bahrain. Saudi Arabia, Qatar and Kuwait are not on the list. You apply through your country of citizenship and need a nomination from its Sending Partner. Applications for 2027/28 are expected to open in November 2026, with a deadline around mid-January 2027; you can choose up to two programs.

### Can an agency apply for Stipendium Hungaricum for me?
No, and you should not pay anyone to do it. The Tempus Public Foundation recommends applying directly, and applying is free. AcademGo helps you choose programs and prepare documents; you submit the application yourself. Details are on our [Stipendium Hungaricum](/study-in-hungary/stipendium-hungaricum) page.

### Do I need IELTS to apply?
Often not. Semmelweis checks your English during its own exam, and a certificate is not required. METU and McDaniel also do not require a certificate at entry, although McDaniel asks for TOEFL by the end of year two. IBS asks for IELTS 6.0 or an equivalent test for bachelor's programs.

### Can I work while studying in Hungary?
Yes. With a student residence permit, you can work up to 30 hours per week during the semester and full time for up to 90 days a year outside it. Do not count on part-time work to pay tuition; plan your budget without it.

### Can I stay in Hungary after graduation?
You can apply for a residence permit to look for a job or start a business for up to 9 months, from inside Hungary. After that, you can switch to a Hungarian Card, an EU Blue Card or a guest self-employment permit if you qualify.

### Do universities in Hungary recognize my school certificate?
Yes. The university you apply to recognizes your certificate itself; no separate procedure is needed for admission. We still recommend getting an apostille or consular legalization early, as universities and the consulate may ask for certified originals.
