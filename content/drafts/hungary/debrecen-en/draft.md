The University of Debrecen is a large state university in eastern Hungary that teaches medicine, dentistry, pharmacy and more than 100 other programs in English, according to the university. It has about 28,000 students, and almost 7,000 of them are international. Its history goes back to the Reformed College of Debrecen, founded in 1538, and the university in its current form was created on January 1, 2000.

Debrecen suits applicants who want an English-taught medical degree in the EU, are strong in biology and chemistry, and are ready to pass a written and oral entrance exam. It also suits students who want engineering, business or IT at fees of roughly $6,000–10,000 a year (2026/27).

It is a poor fit if you want to avoid science exams, expect guaranteed student housing without a scholarship, or plan to apply for medicine in late spring. For the September 2026 intake, medicine and dentistry closed early because of the number of applications.

Health insurance, the medical checkup and the student card are included in the tuition fee. You pay by bank transfer to the university's USD account; cheques and cash are not accepted. The final amount for your program is the one stated in your Letter of Acceptance.

Read the refund rules before you pay. The application fee, the entrance exam fee and the seat reservation fee are non-refundable. Tuition is refunded only if your visa or residence permit is refused, and you need documents to prove it.

For non-medical programs the university publishes only ranges for 2026/27, so we confirm the exact fee for your program. To compare costs across Hungary, see the [cost of studying in Hungary](/study-in-hungary/cost-of-studying-in-hungary).

## Programs and the degree you receive

Medicine is the flagship English program. It lasts 12 semesters (360 ECTS) and leads to the Doctor of Medicine degree (dr. med., M.D.), with studies starting in September. Dentistry and Pharmacy take 5 years each.

The sixth year is a clinical rotation in internal medicine, surgery, gynecology, neurology, psychiatry and pediatrics. Health programs include Physiotherapy, Nursing and Public Health BSc, plus master's degrees such as Molecular Biology MSc.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Non-medical fields taught in English" -->
- Business and Management
- Engineering: chemical, civil, electrical, mechanical and others
- Computer Science and Informatics
- Agriculture

- Humanities and Social Sciences
- Levels: foundation year, bachelor's, master's and PhD

Medicine is on the Main Campus, engineering on Ótemető, and informatics and public health on Kassai. The Faculty of Health Sciences is in Nyíregyháza. To compare Hungarian medical schools, see [medicine in Hungary](/study-in-hungary/medicine-in-hungary).

## Admission requirements for international students

For medicine, dentistry, pharmacy and the Basic Medicine Course, admission rests on your school certificate and an entrance exam held in English. Your English is assessed at the same exam, so the medicine page does not ask for a separate IELTS.

<!-- block: processBlock title="How to apply for September 2027" -->
1. Check your school results. The university evaluates your foreign certificate itself. We compare your grades (FSc, CBSE, WAEC and others) with what the program expects.
2. Plan your English. For medical programs, English is tested at the entrance exam. For other programs, we confirm which certificate your program accepts.
3. Prepare documents: application form, school certificate and transcript, CV, medical certificate, proof of citizenship and the $150 application fee receipt. Documents in other languages need a certified English or Hungarian translation.
4. Certify documents early. Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, Saudi Arabia, Oman and Bahrain issue an apostille. Nigeria, Egypt, the UAE, Qatar and Kuwait are not part of the apostille system, so documents need consular legalization.
5. Apply online in the university's StuArt system and pay the $350 entrance procedure fee.
6. Take the entrance exam: a written test in biology (compulsory) and chemistry or physics, 45 minutes each, then an oral exam in the same subjects. You sit it in Hungary, at an exam site abroad or, if you cannot travel, online. Results: admission, a Basic Medicine Course place, another program, or rejection.
7. Watch the timeline. The final deadline for September 2027 is May 15, 2027; the university publishes the opening date and exam dates closer to the intake.

<!-- block: textContent -->
If your science is weak, the Basic Medicine Course is a two-semester pre-medical year from September, or an intensive version from January to July. Completing it successfully gives direct admission to Medicine, Dentistry or Pharmacy.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="What to know before you apply" -->
- **Hungarian is part of a medical degree.** The program is in English, but the university requires basic Hungarian for talking to patients from year 3. According to the language department, teaching starts with a two-week intensive course before semester 1.
- **Medicine fills up before the deadline.** In 2026 the medical and dental intake closed early. Apply in autumn or winter, not in May.
- **Housing is on you unless you have a scholarship.** Fee-paying students organize their own accommodation, and the university warns that it is very hard to find after September.
- **Budget for more than tuition.** A rented apartment costs about 120,000–140,000 HUF a month, according to the university. Our estimate for total living costs is from about €650 a month.
- **Recognition at home is your regulator's decision.** The university lists recognition by several foreign medical boards, but these are its own statements. Check your medical council's rules for foreign graduates before you enroll.
- **The management model is changing.** Since 2021 the university has been managed by a public trust foundation. According to Hungarian media, these foundations are to be dissolved on August 1, 2027, and management returns to the state. Your diploma and its accreditation are not affected.

## Rankings and accreditation

Debrecen placed 563rd in the QS World University Rankings 2026. In the THE World University Rankings 2026 it is in the 801–1000 group, with Medical and Health in the 501–600 group and a student-to-staff ratio of 12.2. According to media reports, it placed 600th in QS 2027.

Hungary's accreditation agency, MAB, is recognized by WFME until March 31, 2032. This recognition belongs to the national agency, not to a single university.

## Housing and student life

The university has 4,943 dormitory places, including 922 in the Campus Hotel. Stipendium Hungaricum scholars pay 40,000 HUF a month for a dormitory place; for other students, the Senate sets fees each year.

Fee-paying students usually rent privately. The university's accommodation page lists Auguszta Hotel and Student Hostel (about 300 places) and Study K&M Hotel. You may work up to 30 hours a week during the study period and full-time outside it, for up to 90 days a year.

<!-- block: textContent -->
## Student visa

Applicants from Pakistan, India, Nigeria, Bangladesh and Egypt apply for a residence permit for the purpose of studies at the Hungarian embassy or consulate in their country. The consular fee is €110. The decision can take up to 60 days, so apply as soon as you have your Letter of Acceptance. You need proof of admission and funds for the year, a Hungarian address, health insurance (included in Debrecen's tuition), a passport and photos. There is no fixed minimum bank balance. The full process is in our [Hungary student visa](/study-in-hungary/hungary-student-visa) guide.

<!-- block: accordionBlock title="Frequently asked questions" -->
### How much does medicine cost at Debrecen?
In 2026/27, medicine costs $16,900 a year if you pay the full year at once, or $17,900 if you pay by semester. The sixth year has a separate fee structure starting at $8,950. Fees for 2027/28 are not published yet.

### Do I need IELTS for medicine at Debrecen?
The medicine program page does not require a separate IELTS. Your English is assessed during the entrance exam, which is held in English. For non-medical programs, we confirm the accepted certificates for your specific program.

### Can I take the Debrecen entrance exam online?
Yes, if you cannot travel to Hungary or to one of the university's exam sites abroad. Exam dates and sites for 2027/28 have not been published yet; we track them for you.

### What is the deadline for September 2027?
The final deadline is May 15, 2027, and it applies to all programs. For medicine and dentistry, do not wait for it: the 2026 intake closed early because of high demand.

### Is a Debrecen medical degree recognized in my country?
Recognition is decided by your country's medical council, not by the university. The degree is issued by an accredited Hungarian university, and Hungary's accreditation agency is recognized by WFME until March 31, 2032. Check your regulator's rules for foreign graduates before you apply.

### Is the University of Debrecen public or private?
It is a state university that has been managed by a public trust foundation since 2021. According to Hungarian media, these foundations are to be dissolved on August 1, 2027, and management returns to the state. Your diploma and its accreditation are not affected.

### Can I study at Debrecen on a scholarship?
Yes, the university takes part in Stipendium Hungaricum, including in medicine. The scholarship covers tuition, a monthly stipend and a dormitory place or a housing allowance. Saudi, Qatari and Kuwaiti citizens are not eligible.
