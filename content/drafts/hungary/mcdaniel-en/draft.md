McDaniel College Budapest is the European branch campus of McDaniel College, a private liberal arts college in Westminster, Maryland, USA. Students take a four-year Bachelor of Arts taught in English, and the degree is conferred by McDaniel College in the United States. In Hungary, the campus is registered as a foreign institution of higher education, not as a Hungarian university.

It suits students who want an American-style bachelor's in business, economics, psychology, communication, political science or art, with an option to spend time on the main campus in Maryland. It also runs a Pre-Medical track for students who plan to apply to Hungarian medical schools.

It does not suit you if you want a Hungarian degree, a medical degree, a Stipendium Hungaricum scholarship or a student dorm. None of these are available at McDaniel in Budapest.

<!-- block: textContent -->
Full tuition is due by course registration, paid by transfer in euros. Pre-Med students who need a visa pay the full amount in the first installment.

International applicants can get the Admission Merit Discount of €500 per year. You qualify with one of these: TOEFL iBT 80 or IELTS 6.5 plus a school average of at least "C"; GCSE grades A–C in six or more subjects; or a US diploma with GPA 2.5 and SAT 1100 or ACT 21. You keep it while your GPA stays at 2.5 or higher. Discounts do not stack and are lost if payment has not arrived by the first day of classes.

On top of tuition, a student in Budapest needs roughly €800–1,300 a month to live. Our [cost of studying in Hungary](/study-in-hungary/cost-of-studying-in-hungary) guide breaks this down.

## Programs and the degree you receive

McDaniel in Budapest offers only bachelor's degrees: no master's and no medicine. Internships and study abroad are available.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Majors in Budapest" -->
- Art and Art History
- Business Administration
- Communication
- Economics
- Political Science (International Studies)
- Psychology

<!-- block: textContent -->
Double majors are possible, for example art and communication or business and economics.

The degree is a Bachelor of Arts from McDaniel College in the United States, not a Hungarian diploma. This matters when you plan a master's or a job at home: it will be assessed as a US degree.

After your first year in Budapest, you can move to the main campus in Maryland. You need a GPA of at least 2.0 and, for non-native speakers, TOEFL iBT 80 or higher. Budapest students can study up to two semesters in Maryland at a discounted tuition cost; the college does not publish the size of the discount. In the US, tuition, housing and meals must be paid in full before Orientation.

### Pre-Medical and Pre-Veterinary track

The Pre-Med track is a preparation course, not a degree. It prepares students for the entrance exams of Semmelweis University, the University of Szeged, the University of Pécs and the University of Veterinary Medicine Budapest, in medicine, dentistry, pharmacy, physiotherapy, nursing, veterinary medicine and other fields.

There is **no guaranteed admission** to medical school after Pre-Med. You apply to the medical university yourself and sit its entrance exam. According to McDaniel, almost 90% of its Pre-Med students get into the programs they wanted. Students with A grades in all subjects get 10% of the fee back. Insurance, textbooks and housing are not included.

Medical school is a separate cost: about €15,800–18,700 or $16,750–17,900 a year at the four classic Hungarian medical universities. See [medicine in Hungary](/study-in-hungary/medicine-in-hungary).

## Admission requirements for international students

McDaniel uses rolling admissions: applications are reviewed as they arrive. The college looks at school grades, English, the essay, motivation and the recommendation, and, where applicable, its own entrance exam.

<!-- block: processBlock title="How to apply for 2027/28" -->
1. Check your school certificate — you must have finished secondary school and be eligible for university in your home country. British-curriculum students need GCSEs in six subjects; US diploma holders need SAT or ACT scores. Certificates from Pakistan, India, Nigeria, Bangladesh or Egypt are assessed by the college itself.
2. Plan your English — TOEFL or IELTS is not required to apply, but a good working knowledge of English is. You must meet the college's TOEFL requirement by the end of your second year.
3. Prepare the documents — application form, an essay of at least 300 words, school certificate and transcript with a certified English translation, one recommendation letter in English, CV, photo and passport scan. If you finish school in 2027, you can send the final certificate later.
4. Certify documents for Hungary — Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, Oman and Bahrain use the apostille. Nigeria, Egypt, the UAE, Qatar and Kuwait are not in the Apostille Convention, so their documents need consular legalization.
5. Watch the timeline — for fall 2026, the deadline for students who need a visa was June 4, 2026. Dates for 2027/28 are not published; based on the 2026 pattern, visa applicants should aim for early June 2027 at the latest.
6. Pay and apply for the visa — pay the €100 processing fee. After admission, take the college's student status letter to the Hungarian consulate.

<!-- block: textContent -->
Business Administration and Economics students take a Math Proficiency Test before their major courses; Psychology students must pass it by the end of year 1.

## What to know before you apply

These are the limits other agencies rarely mention. Each one is manageable if you plan for it.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Honest limitations" -->
- It is an American degree, not a Hungarian one. Check how your home country treats a US bachelor's for the job or master's you plan. We go through this at the consultation.
- There is no dormitory. Apartments near the college start at €500 a month plus utilities, so budget rent from day one.
- There is no medicine degree. Pre-Med is a preparation course, and admission to Semmelweis, Szeged or Pécs depends on their own exam.
- Stipendium Hungaricum is not a route here: as far as we have checked, McDaniel is not in the scheme. Pakistan, India, Nigeria, Egypt, Bangladesh, the UAE, Oman and Bahrain are eligible at participating universities; Saudi Arabia, Qatar and Kuwait are not. See [Stipendium Hungaricum](/study-in-hungary/stipendium-hungaricum).
- Refunds are limited. After classes start, you get 80% of tuition back in week 1, 60% in week 2, 40% in week 3, 20% in week 4, then nothing. Pre-Med fees are not refundable after registration.
- The English test only moves later. The TOEFL requirement must be met by the end of year 2, so we recommend taking it early.
- Only six majors. For engineering, IT or health sciences, a larger Hungarian university fits better.

## Rankings and accreditation

Our fact base has no verified ranking for the Budapest campus, so we do not quote one. What we can confirm is accreditation and legal status.

McDaniel College is accredited by the Middle States Commission on Higher Education (MSCHE), a US regional accreditor; the accreditation was reaffirmed on November 6, 2023. Budapest is a branch campus of this college. In Hungary, it is registered with the Office of Education as a foreign institution of higher education, code FI 82534.

## Housing and student life

The college has no dormitory housing. Apartments near the college start at about €500 a month plus utilities; the college's fees page estimates housing at €3,500–4,500 per semester. The Housing Service helps students find a place after arrival. You will need a housing registration form for your residence permit, so arrange the apartment early.

With a study residence permit you can work up to 30 hours a week during the semester, and full time for up to 90 days a year outside it. Plan your budget without counting on this income.

<!-- block: textContent -->
## Student visa

Students from Pakistan, India, Nigeria, Bangladesh, Egypt and other visa countries apply for a study residence permit at the Hungarian embassy or consulate in their country. The consular fee is €110. The immigration authority (OIF) prepares the case within 15 days and decides within 60 days.

McDaniel lists the documents: its student status letter, a bank statement (yours, or a parent's with a permission letter), the housing registration form and health insurance. OIF sets no fixed minimum balance; show funds for a full year of tuition and living costs. After graduation you can apply for a permit to look for work for up to 9 months. Full steps: [Hungary student visa](/study-in-hungary/hungary-student-visa).

<!-- block: accordionBlock title="Frequently asked questions" -->
### Is McDaniel a Hungarian university?
No. It is the Budapest branch campus of McDaniel College, a private US college in Maryland. In Hungary it is registered as a foreign institution of higher education, and you receive a US Bachelor of Arts.

### How much does McDaniel College Budapest cost per year?
Under the 2026/27 price list, tuition is €9,200 a year in years 1–2 and €9,800 in years 3–4. Add the €100 processing fee, about €230 a year for insurance and rent from €500 a month. Fees for 2027/28 are not published yet.

### Do I need IELTS or TOEFL to apply?
Not to apply, but you need good working English. You must meet the TOEFL requirement by the end of year 2. TOEFL iBT 80 or IELTS 6.5 at entry, with a school average of at least "C", also gives a €500-a-year discount.

### Does Pre-Med guarantee admission to medical school?
No. You apply to the medical university yourself and pass its entrance exam. McDaniel reports that almost 90% of its Pre-Med students get into their chosen programs; that is the college's own figure.

### Are there student dorms?
No. Students rent private apartments, from about €500 a month plus utilities near the college. The college's Housing Service helps after arrival.

### Can I transfer to the campus in the United States?
Yes, after your first year, with a GPA of at least 2.0 and TOEFL iBT 80 or higher for non-native speakers. Budapest students can spend up to two semesters in Maryland at a discounted tuition cost.

### What is the deadline for 2027/28?
It is not published yet. For fall 2026, visa-requiring students had to apply by June 4, 2026, so for 2027 aim for early June at the latest and start earlier to leave time for the visa.
