Most universities in North Cyprus admit international students on documents alone: a school-leaving certificate with transcripts, a passport and, if you have one, an English test score. As a rule there is no entrance exam, and if your English is not yet strong enough, you start with a preparatory English year on campus.

Simple entry is only half of the picture. Medicine, dentistry and pharmacy set grade minimums, and recognition outside Turkey depends on the program you choose. Below is the full admission route for the 2027/28 intake.

If you are still comparing countries, start with our overview of how to [study in North Cyprus](/study-in-north-cyprus).

<!-- block: textContent -->
## Entry requirements: school certificate, grades and no entrance exams

For a bachelor's degree you need a secondary school certificate (or an equivalent) and your transcript of grades. Universities in the TRNC generally do not run their own entrance exams for international applicants. The International Office reviews your documents and decides.

What changes from program to program is the grade bar. EMU, for example, sorts programs into four levels of requirements:

1. Medicine (the highest bar).
2. Dentistry and pharmacy.
3. Engineering, architecture and health sciences.
4. All other programs.

For medicine, EMU asks for 3 A-Levels (biology and chemistry, plus mathematics or physics) at grade B or higher, or an IB score of at least 30 out of 42. Applicants with WAEC, national certificates from Pakistan, India or the Gulf, or other school systems are assessed against the university's own table. If you plan to study medicine, your biology and chemistry grades matter more than anything else in the file.

## English requirements and the preparatory year

English is the main language of teaching at most programs you will look at. Your test score decides whether you go straight into year one or spend a year in English preparatory school first. At EMU, an IELTS or TOEFL certificate is valid for 2 years, so an old score may not count.

No certificate is not a barrier to admission. At CIU, you take the university's English exam after arrival. The first attempt is free, a second costs €100, and later attempts cost €150 each. If you do not pass, you join the Foundation (English Preparatory) School. CIU's scholarship also covers this Foundation year.

The prep year adds time and money, so plan for it honestly:

- **EMU, 2026/27:** English Preparatory School costs $3,520–4,613 per year depending on your scholarship, plus $250 per semester for books and exams.
- **GAU, 2025/26 price list:** the preparatory English year is listed at €7,700 before any discount. GAU has not yet published its 2026/27 table.
- A prep year means a 4-year bachelor's takes 5 years in total. Medicine at EMU is 6 years plus a prep year if needed.

If you can take IELTS or TOEFL at home before you travel, it often saves a full year. We help applicants decide whether a test is realistic for them before they apply.

<!-- block: processBlock title="How to apply: step by step for 2027/28" -->
1. Check the program: confirm that it is taught in English and is on the recognition list you need (see the section on choosing a program below).
2. Prepare documents: school certificate and transcript, passport scan, English certificate if you have one, and a photo.
3. Apply online through the university portal, for example apply.emu.edu.tr for EMU or the CIU online portal.
4. Receive a Conditional Acceptance Letter. At CIU, the International Office assesses your file and states the scholarship and the initial deposit in this letter.
5. Pay the initial deposit shown in the letter and receive the Unconditional Acceptance Letter.
6. Arrange travel. Flights to Ercan airport go only through Turkey, so check whether you need a Turkish visa for your route.
7. Enter the TRNC with your acceptance letter. Since 24 August 2026 the TRNC runs an e-Visa system; citizens of Nigeria, Armenia and Syria must get the e-Visa before travel, while most other nationalities receive a visa on arrival.
8. Apply online for the student residence permit (Öğrenci İkamet İzni) within about 30 days, then complete the medical check at an authorized clinic in the TRNC.

## Documents: what to prepare and how translations work

The standard document pack is short. At CIU, the application requires:

- the application form;
- your school certificate or equivalent (O/A-Level, WAEC/NECO and others) with transcripts;
- a scan of your passport or birth certificate;
- the signed Rules and Regulations of the university.

GAU asks for a certified and translated copy of the certificate and transcript, a passport copy, an English certificate if available, and a photo.

Two details surprise many families. First, at registration you bring the originals together with a certified English translation. Second, at CIU the originals stay with the university until graduation. This is a TRNC requirement. Make certified copies for yourself before you leave home.

If your documents are not in English, translation is needed. Whether your home country also requires legalization or an apostille on the school certificate depends on the university and your country. We check this per applicant before you submit.

## Intakes and dates for 2027/28

Universities have not yet published their application deadlines for 2027/28. The academic year usually starts in September, and many programs also admit students in a spring semester. Exact dates and which programs accept spring entry are published by each university closer to the intake.

For reference, CIU's 2026/27 calendar had autumn classes starting on 21 September 2026 and spring classes starting on 8 February 2027. Medicine at CIU follows its own schedule. Use these dates only to understand the rhythm of the year, not as 2027/28 deadlines.

Apply early for medicine, dentistry and pharmacy. Grade review takes longer, and the scholarship percentage is set at the application stage.

## How to choose among universities in North Cyprus with recognition in mind

Choosing a program here is not only about the price and the city. The key question is where you plan to use your degree after graduation.

- **Check the program, not only the university.** Turkey recognizes TRNC universities under a Turkey–TRNC agreement. International graduates who want to use the degree in Turkey apply for equivalence (denklik) at YÖK, Turkey's Council of Higher Education. Bachelor's programs that are not in the ÖSYS guide (Turkey's central admission guide) are not considered in the denklik procedure.
- **Verify the university with YÖK.** You can check whether YÖK recognizes a specific university through the YÖK "Okul Tanıma Belgesi" service (okultanima.yok.gov.tr).
- **Know the METU NCC exception.** Graduates of the [METU Northern Cyprus Campus](/study-in-north-cyprus/metu-northern-cyprus-campus) receive a diploma from METU in Ankara, a Turkish state university, signed by the METU rector.
- **For medicine, read the program structure.** Medicine at [EMU](/study-in-north-cyprus/eastern-mediterranean-university) is a joint program with Marmara University: the first 3 years in Famagusta, the last 3 years in Istanbul, with a diploma signed by both faculties. The clinical years are in Turkey, which means a separate stay and separate costs there.
Compare specific options on our cards for [Cyprus International University](/study-in-north-cyprus/cyprus-international-university), [Near East University](/study-in-north-cyprus/near-east-university) and [Girne American University](/study-in-north-cyprus/girne-american-university).

## Scholarships and discounts at admission

Private universities usually show a "base" price and then apply an automatic scholarship for international students. CIU gives all international bachelor's students 50%, plus academic scholarships of 50–100% by grades. At EMU, the percentage is set by your grades and program and stated in your acceptance letter.

Medicine and dentistry are the main exception. At EMU, the scholarship for them is 30–45% instead of 60–75%. At Near East University, the automatic 50% discount does not apply to medicine and dentistry.

As a rough guide for 2026/27, most English-taught programs cost approximately €3,000–6,500 or $4,500–6,500 per year after standard discounts, and medicine from about €11,000 to $18,000 per year. For full price tables, see [tuition fees and living costs in North Cyprus](/study-in-north-cyprus/tuition-fees-and-living-costs).

## What to know before you apply

AcademGo tells applicants what many agencies leave out, and then shows the whole route in advance. Here are the points to settle before you pay any deposit.

**Recognition.** The Turkish Republic of Northern Cyprus (TRNC) is recognized only by Turkey. Degrees from its universities are not automatically recognized in the EU, and the Republic of Cyprus does not recognize them at all. The TRNC is not part of the Bologna Process. The working path goes through Turkey: universities are licensed and accredited by the local regulator YÖDAK and recognized by Turkey's YÖK; international graduates need YÖK denklik to use the degree in Turkey. This does not mean "recognized nowhere": the University of Southampton in the UK, for example, accepts North Cyprus bachelor's degrees for master's study, and US credential evaluators assess such degrees individually. Every other country decides on its own. Details are on our page on [degree recognition](/study-in-north-cyprus/degree-recognition).

**Legalization.** An apostille cannot be issued in the TRNC. Diplomas are certified by the Turkish Embassy in Lefkoşa, usually through the university's student office, and are then processed in Turkey: sworn translation, notary, and an apostille or consular legalization by the relevant Turkish authority.

**Your home country.** Ask your own recognition authority before you apply, especially for medicine or any regulated profession. According to GAU's founding rector in 2020, Jordan, Egypt, Iran, Iraq and the UAE stopped recognizing TRNC degrees. If you plan to work in the Gulf or in Egypt, check this first.

**Entry and the Green Line.** You fly in via Turkey. The Republic of Cyprus treats entry through Ercan and northern ports as illegal. According to the UK government, this can mean a fine, refusal of entry to or exit from the Republic of Cyprus, and a ban on returning to the north. If your goal is Europe, North Cyprus is not the route to it.

**Discounts and refunds.** Discounts often exclude medicine and dentistry. At CIU, deposits are refunded only if your visa is refused, with a letter from the embassy; no other reason qualifies. Read these rules before paying.

**Residence.** A student residence permit is mandatory for every international student and is applied for online after arrival. Our guide to the [student visa and residence permit](/study-in-north-cyprus/student-visa-and-residence) covers the steps.

With this in mind, North Cyprus is one of the most affordable starts for an English-taught degree, with simple admission.

<!-- block: accordionBlock title="Frequently asked questions" -->
### Is there an entrance exam for North Cyprus universities?
As a rule, no. International applicants are admitted on their school certificate and transcripts. Medicine, dentistry and pharmacy set minimum grades; at EMU, medicine requires 3 A-Levels at grade B or an IB score of at least 30.

### Can I apply without IELTS?
Yes. Without a certificate you take the university's English test or start with a preparatory English year. At CIU, the first attempt at the English exam is free; at EMU, the prep year costs $3,520–4,613 in 2026/27 plus books and exams.

### What documents do I need to apply?
A school certificate with transcripts, a passport scan, an English certificate if you have one, and a photo. At registration you bring the originals with a certified English translation, and CIU keeps the originals until graduation.

### When do applications open for 2027/28?
Universities have not yet published their 2027/28 deadlines. The academic year normally starts in September, and many programs also take students in spring. We track official announcements and plan your application around them.

### Is a degree from North Cyprus recognized in my country?
It depends on your country and your program. Universities are accredited by YÖDAK and recognized by Turkey's YÖK, but recognition elsewhere is decided individually by each country or university. Check with your national authority before you apply, especially for medicine.

### Does the 50% scholarship apply to medicine?
Often not in full. At Near East University the automatic 50% discount does not cover medicine and dentistry, and at EMU the scholarship for these programs is 30–45%. Check the exact percentage in your acceptance letter.
