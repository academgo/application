Near East University (NEU) is a private university in Nicosia (Lefkoşa), the capital of North Cyprus. According to the university and public sources, it was founded in 1988 and is one of the largest universities in North Cyprus, with more than 25,000 students from over 100 nationalities. Its campus includes a university hospital, which matters if you are looking at medicine, dentistry or pharmacy taught in English.

NEU suits applicants who want an English-taught degree at a moderate price, a large campus and a simple admission process based on documents. For most bachelor's programs, international students get an automatic 50% scholarship, so fees are among the lower ones for English-taught study.

It does not suit you if your goal is to work in the EU, in the Republic of Cyprus, or in a country that does not accept degrees from North Cyprus. North Cyprus is recognized only by Turkey. Read the section on limitations below before you apply.

## Programs and the degree you receive

NEU teaches medicine, dentistry, pharmacy and veterinary medicine in both English and Turkish. According to secondary sources, medicine takes 6 years and dentistry 5 years. The university also teaches engineering in English, and NEU reports that several engineering programs received ASIIN accreditation with the EUR-ACE label. The full list of English-taught programs sits in the university's official table, and we check your chosen program against it.

Graduates receive a Near East University degree. The university is accredited by YÖDAK in North Cyprus and recognized by Turkey under the intergovernmental agreement. The degree does not become Turkish automatically for international graduates. To use it in Turkey, you apply to YÖK for an equivalence certificate (denklik).

One detail can save you years. YÖK only considers bachelor's programs that appear in the Turkish central admission guide (ÖSYS). All master's graduates from North Cyprus universities go through the denklik process. Before you choose a program, check that it is on that list. Our [degree recognition guide](/study-in-north-cyprus/degree-recognition) explains the full route.

## Near East University admission requirements for international students

NEU admits most applicants on documents, without an entrance exam. The steps below cover the 2027/28 intake. NEU has not yet published dates for 2027/28; the university announces them closer to the intake.

<!-- block: processBlock title="How to apply to NEU for 2027/28" -->
1. Check your grades — NEU accepts national school certificates and international curricula such as GCSE/IGCSE, IB and SAT/ACT. For medicine, expect stricter requirements; we confirm the minimum grades for your curriculum.
2. Prove your English — IELTS 5.5 or an equivalent TOEFL score. Without a certificate, you take NEU's English exam. If you do not pass it, you start with a preparatory English course.
3. Prepare your documents — school certificate, transcript and passport. Documents not in English or Turkish need an official translation. We tell you in advance whether your country requires attestation of school documents.
4. Submit the application — send the package through the university's application system and receive an offer letter with the fee for your program.
5. Confirm the place — pay the deposit only after you see the official fee, including all charges, for your program.
6. Plan travel and the residence permit — arrange your visa and flights, then apply for the student residence permit after arrival.

<!-- block: textContent -->
Our page on [admission requirements in North Cyprus](/study-in-north-cyprus/admission-requirements) compares requirements across universities.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="What to know before you apply" -->
- **North Cyprus is recognized only by Turkey.** The Republic of Cyprus does not recognize NEU degrees. You cannot use them for regulated professions there, such as medicine or law.
- North Cyprus is not part of the Bologna Process. In the EU, recognition is individual, through an ENIC-NARIC center or a specific university. For regulated professions such as medicine, a North Cyprus degree does not qualify for automatic EU recognition.
- The degree can still open doors abroad case by case. For example, the University of Southampton (UK) accepts North Cyprus bachelor's degrees for master's entry. US credential evaluators such as ACEI assess each degree individually.
- Recognition at home is decided at home. The rector of another North Cyprus university said in 2020 that Jordan, Egypt, Iran, Iraq and the UAE had stopped recognizing North Cyprus degrees. If you plan to work in Egypt or the Gulf, check with your recognition authority before you apply. We help you prepare that request.
- An apostille cannot be issued in North Cyprus. The Turkish Embassy in Lefkoşa certifies graduates' diplomas, usually through the university's student office. The documents are then translated, notarized and apostilled or legalized in Turkey.
- Do not rely on older claims that NEU's pharmacy program is ACPE-accredited. The ACPE database lists NEU's international MPharm program as discontinued.
- The Republic of Cyprus treats entry through Ercan Airport as illegal. If you fly to the north via Turkey, you may be refused at crossing points to the south or fined in the Republic of Cyprus. If Europe is your goal, plan for that from the start.

## Rankings and accreditation

We did not find a ranking for NEU that we could verify from a primary source, so we do not quote one here. What we can confirm is the formal status. NEU operates under YÖDAK, the higher education regulator of North Cyprus, which licenses and accredits universities there. Turkey recognizes North Cyprus universities through an intergovernmental agreement, and YÖK handles equivalence for foreign graduates.

You can check whether YÖK recognizes a specific university through the YÖK "Okul Tanıma Belgesi" service. Program accreditations can help when a university or employer abroad evaluates your degree. They do not replace recognition by a state.

## Housing and student life

NEU has dormitories on campus. We did not find 2026/27 dormitory prices from a reliable source, so we confirm them with your offer. According to public sources, the main campus covers about 98 hectares and also has a hospital, a school and a large library. The university hospital opened in 2009, a year after the medical faculty.

Daily life in North Cyprus runs in Turkish lira, while NEU bills tuition in euros. Lira prices rise often because of high inflation, so plan your living budget in EUR or USD. Our [North Cyprus overview](/study-in-north-cyprus) covers cities, transport and daily life.

## Student visa and residence permit

Most international students get their entry visa for North Cyprus on arrival, valid up to 90 days within 180. Since 24 August 2026, North Cyprus has an e-Visa system, and citizens of Nigeria must get an e-Visa before travel. Flights to Ercan Airport go only via Turkey, so check the Turkish visa rules for your passport too.

After arrival, you apply online for the student residence permit. Next comes a medical check at an authorized clinic in North Cyprus; certificates from other countries are not accepted. Apply within about 30 days of your visa start date. The permit does not allow you to work; a job needs a separate work permit arranged by the employer. The full process is on our [student visa and residence page](/study-in-north-cyprus/student-visa-and-residence).

### Is Near East University recognized?
NEU is accredited by YÖDAK in North Cyprus and recognized by Turkey under an intergovernmental agreement. International graduates need a YÖK equivalence certificate to use the degree in Turkey. The Republic of Cyprus does not recognize it, and in other countries recognition is decided individually.

### How much does NEU cost for international students?
For 2026/27, a typical bachelor's program costs about €2,665–2,800 a year after the automatic 50% scholarship. Medicine costs about €11,400 a year without that scholarship. These are approximate figures; the official price list confirms the exact fee.

### Does the 50% scholarship apply to medicine at NEU?
No. The automatic 50% scholarship for international students does not cover medicine or dentistry. NEU has separate merit scholarships for these programs, and eligibility depends on your grades.

### What IELTS score do I need for NEU?
NEU asks for IELTS 5.5 or an equivalent TOEFL score. Without a certificate, you can take the university's English exam. If you do not pass, you start with a preparatory English course.

### Is there an entrance exam at NEU?
Most programs admit international students on documents, without an entrance exam. Medicine usually has stricter grade requirements. We confirm the minimum grades for your curriculum before you apply.

### Can I use an NEU degree to work in Europe?
Not automatically. North Cyprus is outside the Bologna Process, and EU countries assess each degree individually. Regulated professions such as medicine do not get automatic EU recognition with a North Cyprus degree.

### Can I work while studying at NEU?
The student residence permit alone does not allow you to work. A job in North Cyprus needs a separate work permit, arranged by the employer. Working without one can lead to a fine and deportation.
