Degrees from North Cyprus universities are recognized by Turkey, but they are not automatically recognized in the European Union, and the Republic of Cyprus does not recognize them at all. Everywhere else, the decision is made case by case: by a recognition body, a university or an employer in the country where you want to use the degree.

This is the part many agencies skip. North Cyprus is one of the most affordable places to get an English-taught bachelor's degree, with simple entry through Turkey. Whether it suits you depends on where you plan to work or study after graduation.

Below we explain how the system works (for the wider picture, see our [North Cyprus study guide](/study-in-north-cyprus)), where a TRNC degree is accepted, how documents are legalized, and what to check before you pay a deposit.

<!-- block: textContent -->
## Why recognition works differently in North Cyprus
The Turkish Republic of Northern Cyprus (TRNC) declared independence in 1983. UN Security Council Resolution 541 of the same year called the declaration legally invalid and asked states not to recognize any Cypriot state other than the Republic of Cyprus. As a result, the TRNC is recognized only by Turkey.

This has direct consequences for students:
- The TRNC is not part of the Bologna Process or the European Higher Education Area, so there is no "automatic" route through the European recognition network.
- EU law is suspended in the northern part of the island under Protocol 10 of Cyprus's Accession Treaty.
- The TRNC is not a party to the 1961 Hague Apostille Convention, so a diploma cannot be apostilled there. According to law firms that handle these documents, legalization has to go through Turkey.

None of this makes a TRNC degree worthless. You just need to know in advance where it works.

<!-- block: textContent -->
## YÖDAK and YÖK: who accredits North Cyprus universities
Two bodies matter, and they do different jobs.

**YÖDAK** is the TRNC's own higher education regulator. It opens universities and programs, supervises them and carries out their institutional accreditation.

**YÖK** is Turkey's Council of Higher Education. It does not accredit TRNC universities. Turkey recognizes them under an intergovernmental agreement on mutual recognition of universities, signed on 16 January 2009 and in force since 24 August 2011, with a supplementary agreement signed on 13 October 2015 and in force since 4 October 2016.

The correct wording is "accredited by YÖDAK, recognized by YÖK". If a university or agency says "accredited by YÖK", treat it as a red flag about accuracy.

Most large universities in the north are private: Near East University (NEU), Cyprus International University (CIU) and Girne American University (GAU). Eastern Mediterranean University (EMU) is generally described as the state university of the TRNC.

## Denklik: what international graduates need to use the degree in Turkey
A TRNC diploma counts as a Turkish one without extra steps only for Turkish citizens who were admitted through Turkey's central exam, ÖSYM. International students are not in that group.

If you are from Pakistan, Nigeria, India, Egypt, Bangladesh or any other country and want to use your degree in Turkey, you apply to YÖK for **denklik** (an equivalence certificate). Three rules from YÖK's procedure matter most:
- Bachelor's programs that are not listed in Turkey's ÖSYS program guide are not considered for denklik at all.
- Master's programs not recognized by YÖK are not considered either.
- Every master's graduate from a TRNC university goes through denklik, regardless of citizenship.

Denklik is a document for use in Turkey. It is not international legalization, and it does not mean "recognized worldwide". Whether another country accepts it is up to that country.

The same certificate is needed for a master's or PhD in Turkey; the pre-application goes through YÖK's system.

## Where a North Cyprus degree works and where it does not
**Republic of Cyprus.** Its Ministry of Education states that it does not recognize northern universities or their diplomas. It also says no country except Turkey recognizes them. That second claim is the position of one side of the conflict, not a fact: UK universities do accept TRNC degrees. Either way, you cannot work in a regulated profession in the Republic of Cyprus (doctor, lawyer, licensed engineer) with a TRNC diploma.

**EU.** Outside Cyprus, recognition is individual, through the ENIC-NARIC center or university of a specific country. In our reading of EU rules, a TRNC medical degree does not fall under automatic recognition of regulated professions (Directive 2005/36/EC).

**UK and US.** A master's in the UK is a real path. The University of Southampton publishes equivalencies for a TRNC bachelor's (Lisans Diplomasi): a 2:1 is about 7.0/10 or 3.0/4.0, a 2:2 is about 6.5/10 or 2.5/4.0, and the degree must be accredited by YÖK/YÖDAK. In the US, evaluators such as ACEI look at the university's status, YÖK recognition and program accreditation.

**Gulf and Middle East.** In December 2020, GAU's founding rector said that Jordan, Egypt, Iran, Iraq and the UAE had stopped recognizing TRNC degrees. This is a statement by an interested party, not an official list. If you plan a career in Egypt or the Gulf, confirm the rules before you enroll.

## Program accreditation: useful, but not a substitute
Some programs hold international accreditations. [EMU](/study-in-north-cyprus/eastern-mediterranean-university) lists ABET for 8 engineering bachelor's programs, plus FIBAA, ASIIN, NAAB and others. For AACSB, EMU is a member, which is not the same as accreditation. [CIU](/study-in-north-cyprus/cyprus-international-university) lists FIBAA for business programs and MÜDEK for several engineering programs. [NEU](/study-in-north-cyprus/near-east-university) reports ASIIN accreditation for some engineering programs.

Program accreditation does not make a degree recognized in the EU, but it can help in an individual assessment. ABET, for example, matters to American evaluators.

Be careful with "UK ENIC recognition" claims. According to UK ENIC's published packages, membership is a paid database subscription, not accreditation or recognition.

<!-- block: processBlock title="How a North Cyprus diploma is legalized for use abroad" -->
1. Certification in the TRNC. For use in Turkey, the diploma must be certified by the TRNC Ministry of Education and the Turkish Embassy in Lefkoşa. Diplomas usually go to the embassy's consular section as a batch through the university's student office (Öğrenci İşleri); this is based on a 2017 embassy notice, so confirm the steps with your university.
2. Sworn translation in Turkey. According to law firms that handle TRNC documents, the next steps take place in Turkey, starting with a sworn translation.
3. Turkish notary. The translation is certified by a notary in Turkey.
4. Apostille or consular legalization. An authorized Turkish authority adds an apostille for Hague Convention countries. For other countries, consular legalization is used.
5. Recognition in the receiving country. Your home country, employer or university still makes the final decision on recognition.

## The METU Northern Cyprus Campus exception
METU Northern Cyprus Campus (METU NCC) works differently. Its graduates receive a diploma from METU, the Turkish state university in Ankara, signed by the METU rector and issued together with main-campus graduates.

In practice, this is a Turkish document, so the legalization chain is simpler. If recognition is your main concern, compare [METU Northern Cyprus Campus](/study-in-north-cyprus/metu-northern-cyprus-campus) with the other options.

<!-- block: processBlock title="Checks to make before you apply" -->
1. Confirm your program is in the ÖSYS guide. Without this, YÖK will not consider the bachelor's degree for denklik. Our [admission requirements](/study-in-north-cyprus/admission-requirements) page covers the rest of the application.
2. Check the university's recognition status in YÖK's "Okul Tanıma Belgesi" service (okultanima.yok.gov.tr or e-Devlet).
3. Ask your home country's recognition body how it treats TRNC degrees in your field, in writing if possible.
4. If you plan a UK master's, read the target university's equivalency page for Northern Cyprus.
5. For medicine, law or engineering, ask the licensing body where you want to practice, not only the education ministry.

## What to know before you apply

- **Recognition.** The TRNC is recognized only by Turkey. Degrees are not automatically recognized in the EU, and the Republic of Cyprus does not recognize them. The working route is YÖDAK accreditation plus YÖK recognition, and as an international graduate you will need denklik to use the degree in Turkey.
- **Entry goes through Turkey.** In practice, direct flights to Ercan Airport operate only from Turkey. See our [student visa and residence guide](/study-in-north-cyprus/student-visa-and-residence).
- **Crossing to the South carries risk.** The Republic of Cyprus treats entry through Ercan or northern ports as illegal entry. According to the UK Foreign Office, consequences can include a fine for illegal entry, being refused entry to or exit from the Republic of Cyprus, and a ban on returning to the north. Canada warns that you may be turned back at buffer-zone crossings if you arrived through Ercan. According to the European Commission, in 2024 officers at one British Sovereign Base crossing refused passage to 4,394 people. To cross legally, a non-EU citizen generally needs a Republic of Cyprus residence permit, or a valid travel document plus a Cyprus visa if required, and should check the rules first.
- **Discounts often exclude medicine.** Most private universities advertise an automatic discount for international students, often 50%. At NEU it does not apply to medicine and dentistry, which are around €11,380 a year in 2026/27. Check the [tuition fees and living costs](/study-in-north-cyprus/tuition-fees-and-living-costs) page for the full picture.
- **If Europe is your goal, choose Europe.** If you want to live and work in the EU, a TRNC degree is the long way around. AcademGo also works with universities in Poland, Hungary, Spain, Italy and the Republic of Cyprus, and we will compare routes honestly on a consultation.

Who North Cyprus suits: students who want an affordable English-taught degree and plan to work or study further in Turkey, go to a UK master's, or return to a home country where they have confirmed recognition in advance.

<!-- block: accordionBlock title="Frequently asked questions" -->
### Is a North Cyprus degree recognized in Europe?
Not automatically. The TRNC is outside the Bologna Process, so each EU country assesses TRNC degrees individually through its ENIC-NARIC center or universities. The Republic of Cyprus does not recognize them at all.

### Are TRNC universities accredited by YÖK?
No. They are accredited by YÖDAK, the TRNC regulator. Turkey's YÖK recognizes them under an intergovernmental agreement in force since 2011, which is a different thing from accreditation.

### Is a TRNC diploma equal to a Turkish diploma for international students?
No. Only Turkish citizens admitted through ÖSYM get that status automatically. International graduates must apply to YÖK for denklik, and bachelor's programs outside the ÖSYS guide are not considered.

### Can I do a master's in the UK with a North Cyprus bachelor's?
Yes, at universities that accept it. The University of Southampton, for example, accepts a TRNC Lisans Diplomasi if the degree is accredited by YÖK/YÖDAK and you meet its grade equivalencies. Check each target university's policy.

### Can I get an apostille on my diploma in North Cyprus?
No. The TRNC is not a party to the Hague Apostille Convention. The diploma is certified by the Turkish Embassy in Lefkoşa and then processed in Turkey through translation, a notary and an apostille or consular legalization.

### Will my home country recognize a North Cyprus medical degree?
It depends on your country's recognition and licensing bodies, and no one can guarantee the result. Ask them before you apply, in writing if possible. In 2020, GAU's founding rector said several Middle Eastern countries, including Egypt and the UAE, had stopped recognizing TRNC degrees.

### Should I study in North Cyprus if I want to work in the EU?
Usually not. If your goal is an EU career, especially in a regulated profession, an EU university is the more direct route. North Cyprus fits students who plan to stay in Turkey, apply for a UK master's or return home with recognition confirmed.
