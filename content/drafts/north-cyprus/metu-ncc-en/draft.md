METU Northern Cyprus Campus (METU NCC) is not a local North Cyprus university. It is a campus of Middle East Technical University (METU, Ankara), a Turkish state university, and it follows the same academic rules and standards as the main campus. Graduates of METU Northern Cyprus receive a METU diploma signed by the METU rector and join the graduation ceremony in Ankara.

The campus is small, around 3,150 students according to the university, and focused on engineering plus a few business and social science programs. Tuition for international students is $9,450 a year (2026/27), and competitive scholarships of 25% to 100% are available for strong SAT, ACT, IB or A-Level results.

It suits applicants who want an engineering degree from a Turkish state university, taught in English, with a clear diploma status. It does not suit students who want medicine, dentistry or pharmacy (the campus has none), or those looking for the lowest possible tuition in North Cyprus.

## Tuition fees at METU NCC

Undergraduate tuition for the international quota is $9,450 a year for first-year students in 2026/27. On top of tuition, every student pays a $500 registration fee each semester. It can be paid in two parts and covers documents, exams, the student ID, the diploma and the campus health center. The university invoices in US dollars.

The 2027/28 price list is not published yet. Budget using the 2026/27 figures and expect them to change. The reduced rate of $6,300 a year is not a general discount: it applies only to one child of METU campus staff, children of METU graduates, and siblings of current undergraduates.

### Scholarships and the exam scores behind them

Scholarships cut tuition by 25% to 100% and are awarded on academic results. Places are limited, so meeting a threshold does not guarantee an award. The scholarship page is labeled 2025–2026, but its base price matches the 2026/27 fee. The university also accepts A-Level, BTEC and AP results for scholarships.

The $500 registration fee per semester applies at every scholarship level. To keep a scholarship, you need a CGPA of at least 2.00 out of 4.00; on academic probation it is suspended. Students in the English prep program need a score of 50 or more to keep it. Separate merit scholarships (for a GPA of 3.50 or higher) and need-based support scholarships also exist. For a comparison with other universities, see [tuition fees and living costs in North Cyprus](/study-in-north-cyprus/tuition-fees-and-living-costs).

## Admission requirements for international students

METU NCC sets minimum grades by the country where you finished secondary school. Each country has its own criteria page, and the university states that meeting the minimum does not guarantee admission. An alternative route is TR-YÖS, where applicants need a result in the top 10 percentiles. Our general guide to [admission requirements in North Cyprus](/study-in-north-cyprus/admission-requirements) covers the other universities.

<!-- block: processBlock title="How to apply to METU NCC for 2027/28" -->
1. Check the criteria for your school system. Find the page for the country where you completed secondary school (Pakistan, Nigeria, India, Egypt, a GCC country and so on) and compare your grades with the minimums.
2. Decide on international exams. The university accepts all major international exams, including SAT, ACT, IB and A-Level. Scholarships are awarded on these results, so a strong SAT or IB score can lower your tuition significantly.
3. Prove your English. To skip the prep program, the published minimums are TOEFL iBT 75, IELTS 6.0 or PTE 55, with results valid for 2 years. Teaching English as a Foreign Language requires more: TOEFL iBT 86, IELTS 7.0 or PTE 65. Without a certificate, you take the METU English Proficiency Exam on campus or join the prep program.
4. Prepare your documents. Usually this means your secondary school certificate and transcripts, your passport and your English certificate. Translation and certification requirements depend on your country.
5. Apply online at apply.ncc.metu.edu.tr. Upload your documents and exam results in the portal.
6. Plan the 2027 timeline. METU NCC has not published application deadlines for 2027/28. The university releases the exact dates closer to the intake, so prepare exams and documents early.
7. After admission, arrange travel and your residence permit. See the student visa section below.

## What to know before you apply to METU Northern Cyprus

North Cyprus (the Turkish Republic of Northern Cyprus) is recognized only by Turkey. Diplomas from local North Cyprus universities are not recognized automatically in the EU and not recognized at all in the Republic of Cyprus. METU NCC is different because the diploma comes from METU in Ankara. Still, recognition in your home country is decided by your country's authority, case by case. Check the rules at home before you apply. Our page on [degree recognition in North Cyprus](/study-in-north-cyprus/degree-recognition) explains the full route.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Honest limitations and how to handle them" -->
- No medical programs. There is no medicine, dentistry or pharmacy at METU NCC. If you plan a medical career, compare [Eastern Mediterranean University](/study-in-north-cyprus/eastern-mediterranean-university) or [Near East University](/study-in-north-cyprus/near-east-university).
- Higher sticker price. Most programs at other North Cyprus universities cost roughly $4,500–6,500 a year after their standard discounts. At METU NCC, the price drops to that level only with a scholarship, and scholarships depend on exam scores.
- The $6,300 rate is not for everyone. It covers only children of METU staff or graduates and siblings of current students. Do not budget with it unless you qualify.
- A prep year may add time. Without the required English score, you start with the English Preparatory Program before your degree.
- The Green Line matters. The Republic of Cyprus treats entry through Ercan airport as illegal entry. You may be refused at crossing points to the south and fined in the Republic of Cyprus.
- A student residence permit does not give you the right to work. A job with a local employer requires a separate work permit arranged by the employer.
- Daily costs are paid in Turkish lira, and lira prices keep rising. Plan your living budget in dollars or euros.

## Rankings and accreditation

The key status fact is the campus's link to METU. METU NCC is attached to METU in all academic and administrative matters and applies the same standards as the Ankara campus. All graduates receive a Europass Diploma Supplement, according to the university.

Program accreditation is available for part of the engineering portfolio. On October 19, 2021, ABET's Engineering Accreditation Commission accredited several METU NCC bachelor's programs, including Electrical and Electronics, Mechanical, Computer and Civil Engineering. Aerospace, Industrial and Software Engineering were not on that list. Program accreditation helps with individual evaluation by employers and universities, but it is not the same as state recognition. We check the current accreditation status of your program before you apply.

We do not quote rankings on this page, because we publish only figures we have verified at the source.

## Housing and student life

On-campus dormitories cost $1,429.50–2,962.50 per semester, depending on the building and the number of beds in the room. There is also a one-time refundable deposit of $200. These figures come from the university's 2025–2026 page; 2026/27 rates may differ.

The campus sits in the Güzelyurt (Morphou) area, 5 km from the town. It has laboratories, a library, a sports complex and a Culture and Convention Center. With about 3,150 students from 46+ countries, it is a quiet, study-focused campus rather than a big-city experience.

We do not publish a monthly living budget for North Cyprus, because we have no reliable source for it. We estimate your costs for food, transport and phone during the consultation.

## Student visa and residence permit

Most students reach North Cyprus through airports in Turkey, since direct flights to Ercan operate mainly from Turkey. Check early whether you need a Turkish visa for your connection. North Cyprus introduced an e-Visa on August 24, 2026. According to published reports, Nigerian citizens must get the e-Visa before travel, while most other nationalities receive a visa on arrival.

Every international degree student needs a student residence permit. You apply online through the immigration department's system, reportedly within 30 days of entry. Then you pass a medical check at an authorized clinic in North Cyprus and pay the fees online. Universities report each student's status to the immigration system every three months, and withdrawal from studies cancels the permit.

Full steps and fees are on our page about the [student visa and residence permit in North Cyprus](/study-in-north-cyprus/student-visa-and-residence).

### Is the METU NCC degree the same as a METU Ankara degree?
Graduates receive a METU diploma signed by the METU rector and join the graduation ceremony in Ankara. The campus follows the same academic rules and standards as METU in Ankara. This is the main difference from local North Cyprus universities, whose diplomas are issued in North Cyprus.

### How much does METU NCC cost per year?
In 2026/27, undergraduate tuition for international students is $9,450 a year plus a $500 registration fee per semester. Dormitories cost $1,429.50–2,962.50 per semester by the university's 2025–2026 figures. The 2027/28 price list is not published yet.

### Can I get a scholarship at METU NCC?
Yes, the university offers 25%, 50%, 75%, 90% and 100% tuition scholarships based on SAT, ACT, IB, A-Level and other international results. For example, a 50% scholarship requires at least SAT 1200 with Math 670, ACT 29 or IB 36. Places are limited, and you must keep a CGPA of 2.00 or higher.

### Do I need the SAT to apply?
Admission criteria depend on the country where you finished school, and each country has its own page. Scholarships are awarded on international exam results such as the SAT, ACT or IB. We check the page for your school system and tell you whether an exam is required or only recommended.

### Does METU NCC offer medicine?
No. There is no medicine, dentistry or pharmacy at METU NCC. Future doctors usually compare Eastern Mediterranean University and Near East University.

### What English score do I need?
To skip the English Preparatory Program, the published minimums are TOEFL iBT 75, IELTS 6.0 or PTE 55. Teaching English as a Foreign Language requires TOEFL iBT 86, IELTS 7.0 or PTE 65. Without a certificate, you can take the METU English Proficiency Exam on campus or join the prep program.

### When is the application deadline for 2027?
METU NCC has not published its 2027/28 deadlines. The university announces exact dates closer to the intake. Prepare your exams and documents in advance so you can apply as soon as the portal opens.

### How do I apply to METU NCC?
You apply online at apply.ncc.metu.edu.tr. Upload your school certificate, transcripts, passport and exam or English results. We can check your file against the criteria for your country before you submit.
