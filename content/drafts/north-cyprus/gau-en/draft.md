Girne American University (GAU) is a university in Kyrenia (Girne), on the north coast of Cyprus, founded in 1985. Its main campus, Karmi Campus, is in Karaoğlanoğlu. Teaching is mainly in English, with some programs in Turkish, according to the university.

GAU suits students who want an affordable English-taught bachelor's or master's degree, a simple application by documents, and a clear price list in euros. International students accepted to bachelor's and master's programs get a 50% tuition scholarship for the whole study period.

GAU is not the right choice if your goal is a degree recognized in the EU, work in the Republic of Cyprus, or a guaranteed licence at home. Northern Cyprus is recognized only by Turkey, and that shapes what the diploma can do for you. We explain this below, together with the route that works.

Every program adds **€500 a year** in registration and activity fees. For most bachelor's programs, that makes a realistic total of about €4,350–4,500 a year after the scholarship, based on 2025/26 prices.

GAU's payments cover the application, tuition, registration, activity fees, VAT and medical insurance. A separate diploma fee is charged at graduation. The university offers an installment plan, so you do not have to pay the whole year at once.

For medicine and pharmacy, the scholarship page lists no exception, but it does not confirm the 50% either. Before you plan a medicine budget, get the final fee in writing from GAU. We request it for you as part of the application check.

## Programs and the degree you receive
GAU's price list covers a wide range of bachelor's programs. They include architecture and design, engineering, law, business, communication, education, political science, humanities, sport, tourism and a maritime school. Health programs include nursing, health sciences, pharmacy and medicine. GAU also teaches aviation: aviation management, pilot training and an ATPL course.

There is no dentistry program in GAU's price list. The university also runs associate degrees, master's programs and PhDs. The teaching language can differ by program, so check it for your specific course before you apply.

Your diploma is issued by GAU, a university licensed and accredited by YÖDAK. Turkey recognizes universities in Northern Cyprus under an agreement between the two, and YÖK is the Turkish authority involved. This does not turn your degree into a Turkish one. An international graduate who wants to use the diploma in Turkey applies to YÖK for an equivalence certificate (denklik).

One practical point: YÖK does not consider bachelor's programs that are missing from ÖSYS, Turkey's central admissions guide. If a route through Turkey matters to you, check that your program is listed there. You can also check a university's status with YÖK's "Okul Tanıma Belgesi" service.

## Admission requirements for international students
Admission at GAU is based on documents plus English level. The admissions pages list no subject entrance exams for international students. The steps below show the full route for the 2027/28 intake.

<!-- block: processBlock title="How to apply to GAU for 2027/28" -->
1. Check your grades — you need a completed secondary school certificate (or equivalent) with a transcript. Minimum grades for your curriculum (WAEC, A-Level, HSSC, CBSE, Thanaweya Amma and others) depend on the program; we check them against GAU's current rules.
2. Prove your English — IELTS 5.0, TOEFL iBT 60, TOEFL PBT 497, PTE 40 or Cambridge FCE grade C for most programs. Pharmacy, English language programs and master's degrees need IELTS 5.5, TOEFL iBT 65, PBT 513, PTE 46 or FCE grade B. Duolingo is not on GAU's list.
3. No certificate? Take the FES test — the Foundation English School exam. The pass mark is 60/100 for most programs and 80/100 for pharmacy, English philology, translation, English language teaching and master's. Below the pass mark, you study a foundation English year first.
4. Prepare documents — school certificate and transcript, passport, photo and an English certificate if you have one. Documents not in English must be translated and certified by the Ministry of Education before you arrive.
5. Apply online — upload scans at apply.gau.edu.tr. GAU sends a provisional letter that states the deposit amount.
6. Pay the deposit — after payment you receive the official acceptance letter and a receipt. You need both for the visa and residence permit.
7. Plan the timeline — GAU has not published intake dates for 2027/28. The university usually publishes them closer to the admission cycle; start collecting documents early, because certification at home takes time.

## What to know before you apply
AcademGo tells you what many agencies leave out. These are the real limits of a GAU degree and how to work around them.

The Turkish Republic of Northern Cyprus is recognized only by Turkey. The Republic of Cyprus does not recognize universities in the north or their diplomas. Northern Cyprus is not part of the Bologna Process, so there is no automatic recognition in the EU. EU countries and universities review such degrees case by case. For example, the University of Southampton (UK) accepts Northern Cyprus bachelor's degrees for master's entry, with the required grades and YÖK/YÖDAK accreditation.

The Middle East needs special attention. In 2020, GAU's founder-rector said that Jordan, Egypt, Iran, Iraq and the UAE had stopped recognizing TRNC diplomas. If you plan to work in Egypt or the Gulf, check recognition with the authority there before you apply. The same applies to medicine and other licensed professions in Pakistan, India, Nigeria or Bangladesh.

Legalizing your diploma also works differently. An apostille cannot be issued in Northern Cyprus. Diplomas are certified by the Turkish Embassy in Lefkoşa, usually through the university's student office. The document is then translated, notarized and apostilled or legalized by the relevant Turkish authorities. The final decision on recognition stays with the receiving country. Our [degree recognition guide](/study-in-north-cyprus/degree-recognition) shows the full route.

Money is the other risk. GAU states that all payments to the university are **non-refundable**, including the deposit. The official pages describe no exception for a visa refusal. Pay the deposit only after your documents, budget and travel route are checked.

Two more points. A student residence permit does not give you the right to work; a job needs a separate work permit arranged by the employer. The Republic of Cyprus treats entry through Ercan airport as illegal entry, so a trip to the south can end at the checkpoint, with a possible fine.

## Rankings and accreditation
GAU is licensed and accredited by YÖDAK, the higher education regulator of Northern Cyprus. Turkey recognizes it under the Turkey–TRNC agreement. We publish only what we can confirm from official sources, so this page lists no rankings or program-level accreditations for GAU.

## Housing and student life
GAU runs two dormitories. Elysium Court is on campus and houses female students. Knowledge Village (Bilgi Köyü) houses male students. A third dorm, Gordon Bennet, is temporarily closed.

The dorm page shows both 2025–2026 and 2026–2027 labels, so confirm the year of these prices before booking. You can pay in up to 8 interest-free installments or all at once, in EUR or TL. Dorms offer 24/7 security, cleaning, laundry and a shuttle.

Daily expenses in Northern Cyprus are paid in Turkish lira, and lira prices rise often. Plan your living budget in EUR or USD. ## Student visa
Northern Cyprus has an e-Visa system from 24 August 2026. Nigerian citizens must get an e-Visa before travel. Most other nationalities receive a visa on arrival for up to 90 days within 180 days. There are no direct flights to Ercan airport except from Turkey, so check whether you need a Turkish visa for the transit.

After arrival, every international student needs a student residence permit. You apply online through the TRNC Immigration Department system, usually within 30 days. A medical check follows at an authorized clinic in Northern Cyprus; certificates from other countries are not accepted. GAU students handle the permit application themselves. The permit fee for 6–12 months is 1,015 TL, according to the Immigration Department in September 2026. Full details are in our [North Cyprus student visa and residence permit guide](/study-in-north-cyprus/student-visa-and-residence).

<!-- block: accordionBlock title="Frequently asked questions" -->
### Is GAU a recognized university?
GAU is licensed and accredited by YÖDAK in Northern Cyprus, and Turkey recognizes it under the Turkey–TRNC agreement. The Republic of Cyprus does not recognize it, and there is no automatic recognition in the EU. In other countries, the recognition authority or university decides case by case.

### How much does GAU cost per year for international students?
Under the 2025/26 price list, most bachelor's programs cost €7,700–8,000 before the 50% scholarship. With the scholarship and the €500 annual fees, that is about €4,350–4,500 a year. The 2027/28 table is not published yet.

### Does the 50% scholarship apply to medicine?
GAU's scholarship page covers all bachelor's and master's programs and lists no exception. It does not confirm medicine and pharmacy directly. Get the final fee for these programs in writing before paying a deposit.

### What IELTS score do I need for GAU?
Most programs need IELTS 5.0 or TOEFL iBT 60. Pharmacy, English language programs and master's degrees need IELTS 5.5 or TOEFL iBT 65. Without a certificate, you take GAU's Foundation English School exam.

### Is the GAU deposit refundable if my visa is refused?
GAU states that all payments to the university are non-refundable, including the deposit. Its official pages describe no exception for a visa refusal. Check your visa route and documents before you pay.

### Does GAU offer dentistry?
No. Dentistry is not in GAU's price list. Medicine and pharmacy are available.

### Can I apply to GAU through an agent?
Yes. GAU works with approved representatives who recruit students on its behalf. You can also apply directly at apply.gau.edu.tr.

### Is a GAU degree accepted in Egypt or the UAE?
Do not assume it is. In 2020, GAU's founder-rector said that Egypt, the UAE, Jordan, Iran and Iraq had stopped recognizing TRNC diplomas. Check with the recognition authority in your target country before you apply.
