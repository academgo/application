<!-- block: textContent -->
The cost of studying in Cyprus's north is roughly €3,000–6,500 or $4,500–6,500 a year for most English-taught bachelor's programs after the university's standard discount (2026/27). Medicine costs far more: from about €11,000 to $18,000 a year, and the big discounts usually do not apply to it.

That puts North Cyprus among the most affordable places to start an English-taught degree. But the price on a university banner is rarely the price you pay. Each university uses its own currency, discount rules and annual fees, and daily life is paid in Turkish lira.

Below are the official 2026/27 numbers side by side, medicine separately, and a sample annual budget. We also cover what low tuition does not buy: automatic recognition of your degree in the EU.

<!-- block: textContent -->
## How North Cyprus tuition fees work: base price, discount, fees

Most private universities in North Cyprus publish a high "base" price and then give international students an automatic scholarship. The usual discount is 50% (Cyprus International University, Near East University, Final International University) and 75% at Cyprus West University. Eastern Mediterranean University (EMU), the public university, applies scholarships of 60–75% on most programs.

Three things change the final number:

- **Medicine and dentistry.** These are usually excluded from the standard discount or get a smaller one. At Near East University there is no 50% discount on medicine and dentistry; at EMU the medicine scholarship is 30–45%.
- **Annual fees on top of tuition.** Girne American University adds €500 a year, Final International University $890–990, METU Northern Cyprus Campus $500 per semester. EMU's table already includes VAT, registration and the Student Services Fund.
- **The price year.** Always check which academic year a price belongs to.

So compare "tuition after the standard discount plus annual fees", not base prices.

Medical programs are the most expensive rows in any fee table, and the standard discount usually shrinks or disappears. Plan for the full price.

## Student accommodation in North Cyprus

University dormitories are the easiest housing cost to plan, because the price is fixed for the year and usually includes bills. EMU's 2026/27 dormitory prices for international students, paid in full for the year, are a good benchmark.

That works out to roughly $120–400 a month per bed. Electricity, water, internet and cleaning are included; the summer semester is not. Paying in 4 installments costs more: $1,540–5,100 a year depending on the room.

## Currency: invoices in EUR or USD, daily life in Turkish lira

North Cyprus has no currency of its own. It uses the Turkish lira (TRY, TL), and all government fees, including the residence permit, are set in lira. Universities, however, invoice tuition in euros or US dollars: EMU and METU NCC in USD, GAU, CIU, NEU and CWU in EUR.

You can usually pay the university in TL, USD, EUR or GBP by bank transfer or online. Food, transport and other everyday costs are paid in lira, and lira prices rise quickly. The net minimum wage went from 52,738 TL in January 2026 to 61,677 TL from July 2026, about 17% in six months, according to local media.

The practical rule: plan your budget in EUR or USD, and expect lira prices to change during your studies.

<!-- block: textContent -->
Residence permit fees, per the Immigration Department (Muhaceret Dairesi) as of September 2026: 690 TL for up to 6 months, 1,015 TL for 6–12 months, 2,025 TL for 12–24 months, plus stamp duty. The process is on our [student visa and residence page](/study-in-north-cyprus/student-visa-and-residence).

We do not give a monthly living-cost figure because we have not found a reliable source. We estimate it with you for your city and housing.

Do not plan to cover costs with a local part-time job. A student residence permit alone does not give the right to work. Working for an employer in North Cyprus requires a separate work permit arranged by the employer; working without one can lead to a fine and deportation.

<!-- block: processBlock title="How to check the real price before you pay" -->
1. Find the university's official fee table and note the academic year and currency.
2. Check that the standard discount applies to your program; medicine and dentistry are often excluded.
3. Add all annual fees: registration, service fund, VAT, books.
4. Ask the university in writing what conditions you must meet to keep the scholarship in later years.
5. Add the prep year if you have no IELTS or TOEFL score.
6. Add housing, the residence permit and living costs, and keep a reserve for lira inflation.

## What to know before you apply

Low tuition comes with limits you should understand before paying a deposit. Other agencies often skip them; we lay them out upfront.

- **Recognition.** The Turkish Republic of Northern Cyprus (TRNC) is recognized only by Turkey. Degrees from its universities are not automatically recognized in the EU, and the Republic of Cyprus does not recognize them at all. North Cyprus is not part of the Bologna Process, so recognition in other EU countries is decided case by case.
- **The working path.** Universities are licensed and accredited by the local regulator YÖDAK. Turkey recognizes TRNC universities under an intergovernmental agreement, through its Council of Higher Education, YÖK. An international graduate who wants to use the degree in Turkey applies to YÖK for an equivalence certificate (denklik). Bachelor's programs that are not listed in Turkey's ÖSYS directory are not considered for denklik, so check your program before you enroll. Details are on our [degree recognition page](/study-in-north-cyprus/degree-recognition).
- **Your home country decides.** Recognition at home is always an individual decision by your national authority. The founding rector of GAU said in 2020 that Jordan, Egypt, Iran, Iraq and the UAE had stopped recognizing TRNC degrees. If you plan to work in the Gulf or in Egypt, and especially in medicine, check with the licensing body before you apply.
- **Entry is through Turkey.** There are no direct flights to Ercan airport except from Turkey. From August 24, 2026, Nigerian citizens need an e-Visa before arrival; most other nationalities get a visa on arrival.
- **Crossing to the south carries risks.** The Republic of Cyprus treats entry via Ercan as illegal entry. The UK government warns of fines and refusal of entry or exit; in 2024, 4,394 people were refused at one British base checkpoint alone. If your goal is Europe, North Cyprus is not a shortcut to it.
- **Discounts often exclude medicine.** Budget medical programs at close to full price.

We help you check the recognition route and your home-country rules before you pay anything.

### How much does it cost to study in North Cyprus per year?
Most English-taught bachelor's programs cost roughly €3,000–6,500 or $4,500–6,500 a year after the standard discount (2026/27). Medicine runs from about €11,000 to $18,000 a year. Add annual fees, housing, the residence permit and living costs to get the full budget.

### Is the 50% scholarship in North Cyprus real?
Yes. CIU, NEU and FIU officially give international bachelor's students 50%, and CWU gives 75%. It is applied to a high base price, so compare the final figure. Medicine and dentistry are often excluded or discounted less.

### How much does medicine cost in North Cyprus?
At EMU, medicine costs $14,510–18,140 a year in 2026/27 with a 30–45% scholarship. At NEU it is about €11,380 with no 50% discount, and GAU's base price is €13,000 plus €500 in fees (2025/26). FIU and CWU do not offer medicine.

### What costs are there besides tuition?
Expect annual registration or service fees (for example €500 at GAU, $500 per semester at METU NCC), a preparatory English year if you have no IELTS or TOEFL, housing, and the student residence permit. The permit costs 1,015 TL for 6–12 months plus stamp duty and a Health Fund contribution.

### Can I work part-time to pay for my studies?
Not on a student residence permit alone. You need a separate work permit arranged by an employer in North Cyprus. Working without one can lead to a fine and deportation, so plan your budget without local job income.

### Are fees for the 2027/28 intake published?
Universities publish each year's table closer to the intake; GAU's 2026/27 table was not out when we checked in September 2026. Use 2026/27 figures as a guide and confirm the final price before you pay.
