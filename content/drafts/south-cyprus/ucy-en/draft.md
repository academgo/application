The University of Cyprus (UCY) is a public university of the Republic of Cyprus, founded by law in 1989 and based in Nicosia. It is a state university of an EU member country, and its master's fees start at €5,125 for the whole program, not per year. That makes it an affordable route to an EU postgraduate degree for students from Pakistan, India, Nigeria, Bangladesh, Egypt and the GCC.

The catch is language. Almost all UCY bachelor's programs are taught in Greek, and non-EU applicants must prove good knowledge of Greek. For most international students, UCY is a master's or PhD destination.

UCY suits you if you already hold a bachelor's degree and want an affordable master's taught in English. It does not suit you if you want an English-taught bachelor's in business, IT or medicine. For that, compare the private universities in the "Other options" section below.

For the full country overview, see our [Study in Cyprus](/study-in-cyprus) guide.

## University of Cyprus fees for international students

UCY publishes one bachelor's fee for non-EU students and a set of master's fees per program. Master's fees cover the entire program (at least three semesters), which is why they look low next to private universities.

For master's programs, you pay a non-refundable €500 deposit when you accept your place, then €700 twice each semester. The MBA uses a €550 deposit plus installments. The fees page does not list a separate master's rate for non-EU students, so we confirm the exact amount for your program before you apply.

The entry permit requires proof of funds for tuition and living costs, so plan the whole budget, not only the fee.

## Programs and the degree you receive

UCY runs bachelor's, master's and doctoral programs. Graduates receive a degree from a public university of an EU member state. Cyprus has an official ENIC-NARIC recognition center, KYSATS. For regulated professions, check recognition with your home country's authority.

**Bachelor's programs.** Eight semesters (maximum twelve) and 240 ECTS. Semesters run September–December and January–May, and attendance is compulsory. Teaching is in Greek, except English Studies (English), French and European Studies (French) and Turkish Studies (Turkish).

**Master's and PhD programs.** The language is Greek and/or English and/or another international language, stated for each program. A master's takes at least three semesters, up to eight, and carries 90–120 ECTS. International students on programs not taught in Greek must take at least one "Greek Language and Culture" course.

If you want to learn Greek, the UCY School of Modern Greek runs courses from A1 to C2, online or on campus. The fee is €25 registration plus €500 per level.

## University of Cyprus admission requirements for international students

Requirements differ between bachelor's and master's. Below is the master's route, with bachelor's differences noted.

<!-- block: processBlock title="How to apply to UCY for 2027/28" -->
1. Choose the program and check its language. Each master's program states whether it is taught in English, Greek or both.
2. Check your grades. Bachelor's applicants are assessed on GCE/GCSE, IB or equivalent results, or on UCY's own special exams, against each faculty's cut-off. Master's applicants submit their bachelor's transcripts.
3. Confirm the language proof. Bachelor's applicants need a certificate of good knowledge of Greek. For English-taught master's programs, the language requirement is set per program; we confirm it for yours.
4. Prepare documents. For a master's: CV, transcripts, degree certificate (or a letter confirming expected graduation), a motivation essay of up to two pages and two reference letters (three in some departments). For a bachelor's: school certificate, Greek certificate, proof of citizenship and passport.
5. Certify documents. Cyprus requires official translation and certification. India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Saudi Arabia, Oman and Bahrain use the apostille. Nigeria, Egypt, the UAE, Qatar and Kuwait are not in the Apostille Convention, so documents go through consular legalization.
6. Apply online. Master's applications go through UCY's online portal, and you can apply to several programs. The fall intake deadline is usually 31 March; in 2026 it was extended to 15 May. The spring deadline is usually 31 October. Bachelor's applications are usually in April. UCY publishes exact 2027 dates closer to the intake.
7. Accept your place and start the visa. Pay the €500 master's deposit, then contact UCY's International Support Office for the entry permit process.

<!-- block: textContent -->
UCY accepts applications directly through its own portal. You submit the application yourself. AcademGo's role is to assess your chances, prepare and certify your documents, review the application before you send it, and build a parallel plan B at other universities.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="What to know before you apply" -->
- **Bachelor's = Greek.** Apart from three language programs, UCY bachelor's degrees are taught in Greek. If you do not speak Greek, target a master's or choose an English-taught private university.
- Master's fees are low, but the budget is not. You still need proof of funds for living costs and a bank guarantee (€850 for India, Pakistan, Bangladesh and Nigeria).
- Housing on campus is limited. There are 208 single rooms, and many students rent privately.
- Dates for 2027 are not published yet. Plan around the usual 31 March master's deadline and prepare documents by winter.
- Post-study stay is for master's graduates only. Graduates with a master's or higher can stay up to 12 months to look for work; bachelor's graduates cannot.
- Cyprus is in the EU but not yet a full Schengen member. A Cypriot student residence permit does not give free travel across the Schengen area.
- Some cost data is old. UCY's living-cost estimate dates from 2022, so treat it as a guide only.

## Housing and student life

UCY halls are on the Aglantzia campus: 12 buildings with 208 single rooms. First-year and international students get type B rooms. Each has a sink and air conditioning, with a shared bathroom and kitchen for every eight rooms. Places are limited, so some students live in apartments rented by the university and others find housing themselves.

UCY does not publish hall prices on its open pages, so we confirm them before you arrive. The university's 2022 estimate for living costs in Nicosia is about €600–770 per month without rent. The University of Nicosia estimates a minimum of €12,000 per year for living in the city.

Master's students may also apply for teaching assistantships. According to UCY's older FAQ, these pay about €342 or €683 per month for up to 10 months.

Students with a residence permit can work up to 20 hours a week during semesters and up to 38 hours during holidays, in permitted sectors.

## Student visa

UCY applies for your entry permit; you do not apply yourself. You must be outside Cyprus when the application is filed, not in the country on a tourist visa. The official processing time is one month with a complete file.

Documents include a valid passport, a bank statement from your home country, a police certificate, medical tests, the admission letter, academic documents and a bank guarantee. The guarantee is €850 for India, Pakistan, Bangladesh and Nigeria, and €350 for Egypt and GCC countries. The law also allows a group guarantee paid by the university. Migration rules require paying at least the first semester before entry. After arrival, you register within 7 days. Full details are on our [Cyprus student visa page](/study-in-cyprus/cyprus-student-visa).

<!-- block: accordionBlock title="Frequently asked questions" -->
### Does UCY teach in English?
Master's and PhD programs can be taught in Greek, English or both; the language is stated for each program. Bachelor's programs are taught in Greek, except English Studies, French and European Studies and Turkish Studies.

### How much does UCY cost for international students?
Non-EU bachelor's students pay €6,834 per year. Standard master's programs cost €5,125 for the whole program on the 2026/27 fees page. Self-funded programs such as the MBA (€10,250) cost more.

### Can I do a bachelor's at UCY without Greek?
Only in English Studies, French and European Studies or Turkish Studies. All other bachelor's programs require a certificate of good knowledge of Greek. International students without Greek usually choose a master's at UCY or an English-taught bachelor's at a private university.

### When is the UCY master's deadline for 2027?
UCY has not yet published 2027 dates. The fall deadline is usually 31 March, and in 2026 it was extended to 15 May. We recommend preparing documents by winter 2026/27.

### What documents do I need for a UCY master's?
You need a CV, transcripts, your degree certificate, a motivation essay of up to two pages and two reference letters (three in some departments). Documents must be officially translated and certified, with an apostille or consular legalization depending on your country.

### Can I work after graduating from UCY?
Master's and PhD graduates can stay in Cyprus for up to 12 months to look for work or start a business. This option is not available to bachelor's graduates. During studies, you can work up to 20 hours a week in term time.
