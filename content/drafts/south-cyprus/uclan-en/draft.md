UCLan Cyprus is the Cypriot branch campus of the University of Lancashire (formerly the University of Central Lancashire, UCLan), a British university based in Preston. It opened in 2012 and describes itself as the first British university in Cyprus. Graduates receive a double-awarded degree: two certificates, one from the University of Lancashire in the UK and one from the Cyprus campus.

The campus is in Pyla, in the Larnaca district of the Republic of Cyprus, an EU member state. Teaching is in English, and the bachelor fee is €9,000 per year (2026/27), the same for all students.

It suits applicants who want a British-structured degree in an EU country at a fee slightly below the main private universities in Nicosia. It does not suit future doctors, dentists or pharmacists, or anyone planning to stay and work in Cyprus after a bachelor's degree.

## UCLan Cyprus tuition fees

Tuition is one price for everyone, including non-EU students. The figures below are from the 2026/27 price list. Fees for the 2027/28 intake have not been published yet, so treat these as a guide and confirm before you pay.

| Bachelor's: English Language & Literature, Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Web Design, Media Production | 30% reduction | Program-specific discount |
| Bachelor's: Mathematics & Statistics | 40% reduction | Program-specific discount |

Bachelor's scholarships are 50% or 40% of the base fee, so you pay €4,975 or €5,970 per year (2026/27). Grade thresholds are published only for Cypriot and Russian certificates and the IB (35/45 for 50%, 30/45 for 40%). For South Asian, African and Gulf curricula the award depends on the university's own assessment. Master's students can receive up to 30% after an interview.

## Programs and the degree you receive

The university teaches through five schools:

- Business & Law
- Computing, Engineering & Mathematics
- Health & Wellbeing
- Arts, Media & Communication
- Institute of Professional Studies

Psychology is accredited by the British Psychological Society, IT programs by BCS, and accounting and finance by ACCA. If you plan a career in these fields, check that your exact program carries the accreditation.

Every graduate receives two certificates, one from the UK university and one from the Cyprus campus. The university also says its joint programs allow transfers between the Cyprus and UK campuses; the conditions are not published, so ask before you count on it. For medicine, see [medicine in Cyprus](/study-in-cyprus/medicine-in-cyprus).

## Admission requirements for international students

For a bachelor's program you need a school-leaving certificate or 96 UCAS points from relevant A-levels, plus IELTS 5.5 or an equivalent. The university does not publish minimum grades for national curricula such as CBSE, HSSC or WAEC, so we check your grades with the university before you apply. For a master's you need a bachelor's degree at Lower Second (2:2) level or equivalent, IELTS 6.5, a motivation letter, a CV and two references.

Accepted alternatives to IELTS include IGCSE/GCSE English, A-level, IB, Michigan, SAT and PTE. The university also runs its own online test, Password Skills Plus, for €120. Citizens of the UK, Ireland, the USA, Canada (if educated in English), Australia and New Zealand are exempt.

<!-- block: processBlock title="How to apply for the 2027/28 intake" -->
1. Check eligibility — compare your certificate and English score with the requirements above. Your passport should be valid for at least two years.
2. Prepare and certify documents — school certificate and transcripts in the original language and English. Cyprus requires official translation and certification. India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Saudi Arabia, Oman and Bahrain use an apostille; Nigeria, Egypt, the UAE, Qatar and Kuwait need consular legalization.
3. Submit the online form and pay the €50 application fee — the university assesses your file and issues a conditional offer.
4. Pay the deposit and visa fee — after that, the university files your entry permit with the Migration Department and the Ministry of Education.
5. Complete the visa file — proof of funds, police clearance, medical tests in your home country and a bank guarantee (see the visa section below).
6. Arrive in September — the main intake. Exact 2027 deadlines are not on the university's public pages; the university publishes them closer to the intake, so start the steps above early.

## What to know before you apply

- **No medicine, dentistry or pharmacy.** If a medical career is the goal, compare [University of Nicosia](/study-in-cyprus/university-of-nicosia) and [European University Cyprus](/study-in-cyprus/european-university-cyprus).
- **The campus is in Pyla, not Nicosia or central Larnaca.** Watch a campus video or visit before you decide.
- **Scholarship thresholds are not published for your curriculum.** Budget at the full €9,000 (2026/27) and treat any scholarship as a bonus.
- **The deposit amount differs between university pages, and refunds after a visa refusal are not described.** Get both in writing before you pay.
- **Staying to work needs a master's.** The 12-month post-study stay applies only to master's graduates and above.
- **Cyprus is in the EU but not a full Schengen member.** A Cypriot student permit does not work like a Schengen residence permit for travel.

## Rankings and accreditation

The university is accredited by the UK Quality Assurance Agency and by CYQAA, the Cyprus Agency of Quality Assurance and Accreditation in Higher Education. Individual programs carry British Psychological Society, BCS and ACCA accreditation. We do not quote a ranking here: the only ranking on the university's site is several years old.

For employers and recognition bodies at home, the key facts are the UK and Cyprus quality assurance and the double award. Recognition rules differ by country and profession, so check with your national authority before you enroll.

## Housing and student life

The Student's Nest is the residence about a minute's walk from the Pyla campus. According to the university, it has 171 en-suite rooms, plus apartments from one to five bedrooms and penthouses. Prices are not published online, so request them with your offer.

On a student permit you can work up to 20 hours a week during semesters and 38 hours a week in holidays, in sectors approved by the Minister of Labour. The local labour office approves the contract first. Treat part-time work as extra income, not as a way to pay tuition.

## Student visa for UCLan Cyprus

The university, not the student, applies for your entry permit. The official processing time is one month with a complete file; incomplete files take longer. You must apply from outside Cyprus, not while visiting on a tourist visa.

- You need a bank letter and statement proving funds for living, study and return. The official list sets no fixed amount.
- The bank guarantee is €850 for citizens of India, Pakistan, Bangladesh and Nigeria, and €350 for Egypt and the Gulf states. A university can post a group guarantee instead.
- Within 7 days of arrival you register and apply for a residence permit, issued for up to one year and renewed annually based on academic progress.

Step-by-step details are on our [Cyprus student visa page](/study-in-cyprus/cyprus-student-visa).

### Is UCLan Cyprus a British university?
It is the Cyprus branch campus of the University of Lancashire (formerly the University of Central Lancashire) in the UK. Graduates receive two certificates, one from the UK university and one from UCLan Cyprus. Quality is overseen by both the UK Quality Assurance Agency and CYQAA in Cyprus.

### How much are UCLan Cyprus fees for international students?
On the 2026/27 price list, bachelor's tuition is €9,000 per year and a standard master's is €9,500 for the whole 90-ECTS program. Non-EU students pay the same tuition as local and EU students. Fees for 2027/28 have not been published yet.

### What IELTS score does UCLan Cyprus require?
IELTS 5.5 for bachelor's programs and IELTS 6.5 for master's programs, or an equivalent such as PTE, IGCSE English or IB. You can also take the university's online Password Skills Plus test for €120.

### Can I study medicine at UCLan Cyprus?
No. The university does not offer medicine, dentistry or pharmacy. In Cyprus, medicine in English is available at the University of Nicosia (€24,000 per year) and European University Cyprus (€23,000 per year), both on 2026/27 price lists.

### Can international students get a scholarship?
Bachelor's scholarships are 50% or 40% of the base fee, meaning €4,975 or €5,970 per year (2026/27). Thresholds are published only for Cypriot, Russian and IB certificates, so for other curricula the university decides case by case. Master's students can receive up to 30% after an interview.

### Can I work in Cyprus after graduating from UCLan Cyprus?
Only master's graduates and above can stay up to 12 months to look for work or start a business. Bachelor's graduates do not get this option. During studies you can work up to 20 hours a week in semesters in approved sectors.
