European University Cyprus (EUC) is a private university in Nicosia, the capital of the Republic of Cyprus, an EU member state. It started in 1961 as Cyprus College and received university status from the Republic of Cyprus in 2007. International applicants mostly look at EUC for one reason: medicine, dentistry and veterinary medicine taught fully in English inside the EU.

EUC suits you if you want an EU-accredited degree, have strong grades in biology and chemistry or math, and your family can cover €23,000 a year for medicine plus living costs. It is a good fit for business, IT and psychology students who want an English-taught bachelor's at a mid-range EU price.

It is not the right choice if you need a scholarship to make the numbers work, or if you are counting on a part-time job to pay rent. It is also not a shortcut into medicine: the grade bar is high and there is an interview.

## Programs and the degree you receive

EUC has seven schools, but the English-taught programs that bring most international students are in health.

**Medicine (MD).** Six years, 360 ECTS, taught in English on the Nicosia campus. Clinical exposure starts in year one. Placements run in hospitals in Cyprus, Greece, Germany, the US and Tanzania. EUC states that the school meets all requirements for ECFMG certification, the route to US and Canadian licensing exams. According to the university, the MD was accredited by CYQAA in February 2020 through a procedure based on WFME standards.

**Dentistry (BDS).** Five years in English. The program uses simulation labs and a dental clinic on campus.

**Veterinary Medicine (DVM).** An English-taught option at €21,000 a year.

**Bachelor's degrees.** Business, IT, psychology and pharmacy sit in the €9,240 band; biomedical sciences and physiotherapy in the €10,980 band. Some programs, including nursing, law and education, are taught only in Greek. Always check the language of instruction for your exact program before you apply.

EUC's Frankfurt branch is a separate medical campus in Germany with its own fees and visa rules. This page covers Nicosia only.

For a wider view, read our [medicine in Cyprus](/study-in-cyprus/medicine-in-cyprus) guide.

## Admission requirements for international students

The steps follow EUC's published rules; 2027 dates follow the pattern EUC uses each year.

<!-- block: processBlock title="How to apply to EUC for 2027 entry" -->
1. Check your grades. For medicine, dentistry and veterinary medicine, EUC asks for an average of at least 90% in your school certificate, with biology plus one of chemistry, math or physics. For other bachelor's programs, you need completed secondary education.
2. Get an English certificate. EUC accepts IELTS Academic 6.5+, TOEFL iBT 79+, PTE Academic 66+, A-level English grade C+ or IB English A 5+. Non-EU students who need a visa and all dentistry applicants cannot use EUC's internal English test, so book an official exam early.
3. Prepare documents. Academic documents must be in English. Translations must be official and certified. Documents from India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Saudi Arabia, Oman and Bahrain need an apostille; documents from Nigeria, Egypt, the UAE, Qatar and Kuwait need consular legalization.
4. Apply online. For health programs: application form, €200 fee, school certificate, an academic reference sent directly by your referee, and a motivation letter in English. Volunteer experience counts. Shortlisted applicants are invited to an interview.
5. Pay the deposit. After you receive an offer, non-EU students pay the €3,000 tuition deposit by bank transfer before the visa file goes in.
6. Submit your visa documents on time. EUC's visa deadlines for non-EU students are August 31 for the October intake and January 15 for the February intake. Visa documents are due no later than 3 months before the semester starts. For the October 2027 intake, plan to have your documents ready by early summer 2027; EUC publishes the exact 2027 dates closer to the intake.

## What to know before you apply

Better to know these before you pay the deposit than after.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Honest limitations" -->
- **No real scholarship route for international students.** EUC's financial aid is "awarded to native students only", and medicine, dentistry and veterinary medicine are excluded from scholarships. Budget for the full fee. The high school scholarships (25–75%) are written for graduates of Cypriot schools.
- **Recognition at home is your responsibility to check.** EUC's MD is accredited by CYQAA to WFME standards, and graduates can go for ECFMG certification. EUC does not publish recognition decisions for specific countries. If you plan to practice in India, Pakistan, Egypt, Nigeria or the Gulf, check your national medical council's current rules before you apply. We help you prepare that check.
- **Work will not cover your costs.** Cyprus allows students to work up to 20 hours a week during semesters and 38 hours during breaks, only in sectors approved by the Ministry of Labour, and each contract needs approval first. EUC's own FAQ adds that non-EU students can work only after six months from their first entry into Cyprus.
- **Staying after graduation is limited.** The 12-month stay to look for work or start a business applies only to master's graduates and above, not to bachelor's or MD graduates.
- **Cyprus is not a full Schengen member.** A Cypriot student residence permit does not give you automatic free travel across Schengen.
- **The €3,000 deposit is paid before the visa decision.** EUC does not publish what happens to it if the visa is refused. Ask for written refund terms before you pay; we help you get them.

## Rankings and accreditation

EUC is a private university accredited by CYQAA, the national quality agency of Cyprus. According to the university, it is placed in the 801+ band of the THE World University Rankings overall and in the 601+ band for medicine, dentistry and health. EUC also reports a QS Stars 5-star rating for its MD program.

For medicine, WFME-based accreditation and ECFMG eligibility matter more than rankings. Degree recognition in Cyprus is handled by KYSATS, the national ENIC-NARIC center.

## Housing and student life

EUC's student residences are about 5 minutes on foot from the Engomi campus, and there are private apartments in the same area. According to EUC, rent runs about €600–1,000 per month, with other living costs of about €600–900 per month. You need a lease or proof of accommodation for your residence permit, so sort housing before you fly.

## Student visa

Students from outside the EU need an entry permit before travel. The university, not the student, files the entry permit application with the Cypriot authorities. You cannot apply while in Cyprus on a tourist visa.

What EUC asks for in the visa file:

- passport valid for at least 2 years;
- English-language academic documents and proof of English at B2 or above;
- police clearance certificate issued within the last 6 months;
- tests for HIV, hepatitis B and C and syphilis, plus a chest X-ray, done within the last 4 months;
- bank documents showing at least €7,000 (an EUC requirement, not a fixed amount set by Cypriot law);
- at least €2,000 in cash on arrival.

Cyprus also requires a bank guarantee that depends on your citizenship: €850 for India, Pakistan, Bangladesh and Nigeria, €350 for Egypt, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman and Bahrain. The university can provide a group guarantee for students; confirm with EUC how this works for your file.

Official processing takes 1 month with a complete file (EUC says typically 3–4 weeks). Within 7 days of arrival you register with the migration authorities and apply for a residence permit (government fee €140). The permit is issued for up to 1 year and renewed annually based on academic progress. Full details are on our [Cyprus student visa](/study-in-cyprus/cyprus-student-visa) page.

<!-- block: accordionBlock title="Frequently asked questions" -->
### Is EUC a good university for medicine?
EUC's MD is a 6-year, English-taught program accredited by CYQAA under WFME-based standards, and graduates are eligible for ECFMG certification. Clinical exposure starts in year one. Whether it is right for you depends on your grades, budget and where you plan to practice.

### How much does medicine at EUC cost?
For 2026/27, the MD costs €23,000 a year, plus a €200 application fee and €70 registration per semester. Optional clinical training in the US in years 4–6 adds €6,000 a year. Fees for 2027/28 are not published yet.

### What grades do I need for EUC medicine?
EUC asks for an average of at least 90% in your school certificate. You also need biology plus one of chemistry, math or physics. Shortlisted applicants are invited to an interview.

### What IELTS score does EUC require?
IELTS Academic 6.5 or higher. EUC also accepts TOEFL iBT 79+ and PTE Academic 66+. Non-EU students who need a visa cannot use EUC's internal English test.

### Does EUC offer scholarships to international students?
EUC's financial aid is published as being for native students only, and medicine, dentistry and veterinary medicine are excluded from scholarships. Plan your budget at the full fee.

### When is the deadline for non-EU students?
Under EUC's current rules, the visa deadline is August 31 for the October intake and January 15 for the February intake. Visa documents are due at least 3 months before the semester. EUC publishes exact 2027 dates closer to the intake.
