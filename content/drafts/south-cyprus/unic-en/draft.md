The University of Nicosia (UNIC) is a private university in Nicosia, the capital of the Republic of Cyprus, an EU member state. According to the university, it started in 1980 as Intercollege and became a university in 2007. It describes itself as the largest university in Cyprus and reports over 11,000 students from over 70 countries.

All UNIC bachelor's, master's and doctoral degrees are accredited by the Republic of Cyprus. Bachelor's and master's programs are available in English, and the medical school runs a 6-year Doctor of Medicine (MD) in English.

UNIC suits you if you want an EU-accredited degree in English, have solid school grades and can show funds for the first year of study. It is a weaker fit if your budget is well below €10,000 a year for tuition alone, or if you plan to work in the EU straight after a bachelor's degree.

Every non-EU applicant also pays a non-refundable application fee of €60 and a non-refundable student visa fee of €150. UNIC also charges an International Student Guarantee of €400, which is refunded when you leave the university. Recurring fees include registration (€30 per semester) and health insurance (€175 per year).

Medical students have extra one-off payments: an administration fee of €1,500 and a seat deposit of €3,500. Yearly insurance adds €180 (medical), €300–1,250 (malpractice, rising in years 5–6) and €50 (accident). For a full budget, see our page on the [cost of studying in Cyprus](/study-in-cyprus/cost-of-studying-in-cyprus).

## Programs and the degree you receive

UNIC has eight schools: Business; Education; Humanities and Social Sciences; Law; Life and Health Sciences; Medical School; Sciences and Engineering; and Veterinary Medicine. English-taught bachelor's and master's programs cover business, law, architecture, IT and engineering, international relations, psychology and more.

Your degree is issued by UNIC and accredited by the Republic of Cyprus. UNIC holds the ECTS Label and the Diploma Supplement Label. In Greece, the recognition body D.O.A.T.A.P. treats UNIC as equivalent to Greek universities.

The medical school was set up in cooperation with St George's, University of London. The university calls it the first and largest medical program in Cyprus, with over 500 students. The degree you receive is the UNIC Doctor of Medicine.

- **6-year MD:** 360 ECTS over 12 semesters, taught in English.
- **5-year graduate-entry MD:** 300 ECTS, for applicants who already hold a bachelor's degree.
- **Clinical years 5–6:** by default in Cyprus and partner sites. As an option, you can train in the UK/USA for an extra €6,000 per year or in Switzerland/Germany for an extra €3,000 per year.

If medicine is your main goal, compare options on our [medicine in Cyprus](/study-in-cyprus/medicine-in-cyprus) page.

## Admission requirements for international students

For bachelor's programs, UNIC asks for a recognized school-leaving certificate with an average of at least 7.5/10 or 15/20. Applicants below that can be admitted with extra support in the first year, and some programs set higher requirements.

For English, bachelor's applicants need IELTS 6.0 or TOEFL iBT 72, or A-level English at grade C, or IB English HL 4. With IELTS 4.5 or higher, you can take UNIC's internal placement test and study with language support. Master's applicants need an accredited bachelor's degree and IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 79. If your first degree was taught in English, you can be exempt.

The MD has stricter entry rules:
- a school certificate of 90%, with similar grades in biology and one of chemistry, physics or mathematics;
- or A-levels ABB, or IB 32 points (HL 16);
- IELTS 6.5 with 6.5 in writing and at least 6.0 in other parts, or TOEFL iBT 79;
- a mandatory online interview.

<!-- block: processBlock title="How to apply to UNIC for 2027/28" -->
1. Check your grades against the program. We compare your certificate with UNIC's 7.5/10 or 15/20 benchmark, or with the MD's 90% rule in biology and chemistry, physics or math.
2. Get your English score in time for the deadline.
3. Certify your documents. India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Saudi Arabia, Oman and Bahrain use an apostille. Nigeria, Egypt, the UAE, Qatar and Kuwait need consular legalization. Everything must be officially translated and certified.
4. Submit the application and pay the fees: €60 application fee and €150 student visa fee, both non-refundable.
5. Receive your offer. After a conditional or unconditional offer, UNIC files your entry permit application with the Migration Department and the Ministry of Education.
6. Pay at least the first semester. Cyprus requires non-EU students to pay at least the first semester before entry and study full time (30 ECTS per semester).
7. Travel and register. Within 7 days of arrival, register in the register of foreign nationals and apply for a temporary residence permit.

<!-- block: textContent -->
UNIC's general deadlines are late June for the Fall intake (September–January), late October for Spring (February–May) and late February for Summer. Exact 2027/28 dates are announced closer to each intake. The MD pages do not list separate deadlines, so for medicine we confirm the timeline with you at the consultation.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="What to know before you apply" -->
- **Proof of funds is concrete.** UNIC asks for a bank letter on your sponsor's solvency (issued within 6 months) and a 1-month bank statement showing at least the first year's tuition.
- **Bank guarantee depends on your passport.** The state guarantee is €850 for India, Pakistan, Bangladesh and Nigeria, and €350 for Egypt, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman and Bahrain. A university can lodge a group guarantee instead.
- **Medical tests have an expiry date.** HIV, syphilis, hepatitis B/C tests and a TB chest X-ray must be no older than 4 months. The police certificate must be no older than 6 months.
- **Cyprus is in the EU but not yet a full Schengen member.** A Cypriot student permit does not give the same travel rights as a Schengen permit.
- **Work is limited.** Students can work up to 20 hours a week in term and 38 hours in holidays, only in approved sectors and with a contract approved by the labor office.
- **Post-study stay is for master's graduates.** Graduates of master's level and above can stay up to 12 months to look for work or start a business. Bachelor's graduates do not get this option.
- **Medical licensing is decided at home.** UNIC's MD is accredited by CYQAA to WFME standards and listed in WDOMS. Licensing rules differ by country, so check your national medical regulator's requirements before you commit.

## Rankings and accreditation

All UNIC degrees are accredited by the Republic of Cyprus. Programs are evaluated by CYQAA, the national quality agency registered in EQAR.

For medicine, the 6-year and 5-year MD programs are accredited by CYQAA against WFME standards, and CYQAA itself holds WFME recognition status. The program is listed in WDOMS. UNIC notes that since 2024 ECFMG (USA) only certifies graduates of programs that meet WFME standards.

UNIC is a member of the EUA and the ACU. We publish ranking positions only after checking them in the ranking tables.

## Housing and student life

UNIC's official residences in Nicosia are called UNIC Residences; according to the university, they include the SIX, U and Triangle buildings. Room prices are not published on the open pages, so we request current rates when you get your offer.

For budgeting, UNIC estimates yearly living costs as follows: shared housing €4,000–6,000 or a private room €6,000–10,000; food and personal costs €3,350–5,000; books €400–500. The medical school gives a minimum of €12,000 per year for living in Nicosia. Your residence permit also requires a rental contract.

<!-- block: textContent -->
## Student visa

You do not apply for a Cyprus student entry permit yourself. After your offer, UNIC files it with the Migration Department and the Ministry of Education. The official processing time is 1 month with a complete file, and longer if documents are missing.

After arrival, you have 7 days to register and apply for a temporary residence permit. The decision can take up to 3 months. The full document list, fees and timeline are on our [Cyprus student visa](/study-in-cyprus/cyprus-student-visa) page.

<!-- block: accordionBlock title="Frequently asked questions" -->
### Is the University of Nicosia accredited?
Yes. All UNIC bachelor's, master's and doctoral degrees are accredited by the Republic of Cyprus, an EU member state. The MD is accredited by CYQAA against WFME standards and listed in WDOMS.

### How much does UNIC cost for international students?
For 2026/27, non-EU bachelor's tuition is €10,320–11,820 per year, depending on the program. The 6-year MD costs €24,000 per year, and most master's programs cost €14,490–15,750 in total.

### What IELTS score do I need for UNIC?
Bachelor's programs need IELTS 6.0 or TOEFL iBT 72. Master's programs and the MD need IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 79; for the MD, writing must be 6.5 and other parts at least 6.0.

### When is the application deadline?
UNIC's general deadlines are late June for the Fall intake and late October for the Spring intake. Exact dates for 2027/28 are announced closer to each intake.

### Can I work while studying at UNIC?
Yes, within limits. Students can work up to 20 hours a week during semesters and 38 hours a week in holidays, in approved sectors and with an approved contract.

### Does UNIC offer scholarships?
MD students can receive financial aid of up to 20% based on need, after enrollment and an interview. The CyprusAid scholarship is open only to citizens of Kenya, Uganda, Rwanda, Zambia, Botswana, Mauritius, Ghana, Senegal, Tanzania and Madagascar. For other programs, we check the available options for your case at the consultation.
