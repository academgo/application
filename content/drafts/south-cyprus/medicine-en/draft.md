You can study medicine in Cyprus fully in English at two private universities in Nicosia: the University of Nicosia (UNIC) and European University Cyprus (EUC). Both offer a 6-year, 360 ECTS Doctor of Medicine (MD), with 2026/27 tuition of €24,000 a year at UNIC and €23,000 a year at EUC.

Cyprus here means the Republic of Cyprus, an EU member state. Both MD programs are accredited by the national quality agency CYQAA according to WFME standards, the benchmark that matters for ECFMG certification in the United States. Recognition in your home country is a separate question, and we cover it below.

This page is for applicants from Pakistan, India, Nigeria, Bangladesh, Egypt and the Gulf who are planning for the 2027/28 intake. Fees for 2027/28 are not published yet, so every price below is from the 2026/27 price lists.

For the full country overview, see our [Study in Cyprus](/study-in-cyprus) guide.

## MBBS in Cyprus or MD: what degree you actually get

Many students from South Asia search for "MBBS in Cyprus". In Cyprus, the undergraduate-entry medical degree is called an MD (Doctor of Medicine). It takes 6 years and 360 ECTS, split into 12 semesters of 30 ECTS each.

- **University of Nicosia Medical School.** According to the university, it runs the first and largest medical program in Cyprus, with over 500 students from around the world. It was set up in collaboration with St George's, University of London, but the degree is awarded by UNIC. UNIC also offers a 5-year graduate-entry MD (300 ECTS) for applicants who already hold a bachelor's degree.
- **European University Cyprus School of Medicine.** Opened in 2013. Clinical exposure starts in year 1, with training in hospitals in Cyprus, Greece, Germany, the United States and Tanzania. EUC also runs a separate medical branch in Frankfurt, Germany; the fees and visa rules on this page are for the Nicosia campus only.

If you are also considering dentistry, EUC launched the first dental program in Cyprus in 2017: a 5-year BDS taught in English.

For full profiles, see the [University of Nicosia](/study-in-cyprus/university-of-nicosia) and [European University Cyprus](/study-in-cyprus/european-university-cyprus) pages.

## Cost of medical school in Cyprus

Tuition is only part of the bill. Both universities charge one-off fees before you arrive and annual insurance or registration fees. All figures below are from the 2026/27 price lists for non-EU students.

| Cost item | UNIC | EUC |
|---|---|---|
| Tuition per year | €24,000 | €23,000 |
| Application fee (one-off) | €60 | €200 |
| Other one-off payments | Administrative fee €1,500; seat deposit €3,500; visa/registration fee €150 | €3,000 tuition deposit, paid before visa documents are sent |
| Refundable guarantee | International Student Guarantee €400 | — |
| Recurring fees | Medical insurance €180/year; malpractice insurance €300/year (years 1–4) and €600–1,250/year (years 5–6); accident insurance €50/year | Registration €70 per semester |
| Optional clinical training abroad | UK/USA +€6,000/year or Switzerland/Germany +€3,000/year (years 5–6) | USA €6,000/year (years 4–6) |
| Financial aid | Up to 20% need-based, after admission and interview | Medicine is excluded from EUC scholarships |

<!-- block: textContent -->
Living costs are the second big line. UNIC estimates the minimum cost of living in Nicosia at €12,000 per academic year. EUC gives rent of €600–1,000 a month near its campus, plus €600–900 a month for other expenses. Budget with these figures, not with tuition alone.

On top of university fees you pay Cyprus state fees and a bank guarantee (see the visa section below). For a full breakdown by city and program type, see [cost of studying in Cyprus](/study-in-cyprus/cost-of-studying-in-cyprus).

## Entry requirements for the MD

Both schools ask for strong science grades and a recognized English certificate. The bar is similar, but the details differ.

<!-- block: tableBlock -->
| Requirement | UNIC | EUC |
|---|---|---|
| School grades | 90% average, with similar grades in biology and one of chemistry, physics or mathematics | Average of at least 90%, with biology and one of chemistry, mathematics or physics |
| Other qualifications | A-levels ABB; IB 32 (HL 16) | Equivalent national scales accepted (e.g. 18/20) |
| English | IELTS 6.5 (writing 6.5, other bands at least 6.0) or TOEFL iBT 79 | IELTS Academic 6.5, TOEFL iBT 79 or PTE Academic 66 |
| Interview | Online interview, required | Interview for shortlisted applicants |
| Other documents | School certificate, passport, English certificate | Academic reference sent directly by the referee; motivation letter in English; volunteering counts |

<!-- block: textContent -->
One detail catches many non-EU applicants. EUC's internal English test is not available to non-EU students who need a visa, so you must have an official IELTS, TOEFL or PTE result before you apply. Book the test early; it is often the item that delays the whole file.

For both schools, admission requirements for other programs are on our [universities and admission](/study-in-cyprus/universities-and-admission) page.

## Accreditation and recognition of a Cyprus medical degree

Accreditation in Cyprus is handled by CYQAA, the national quality agency. UNIC's 6-year and 5-year MD programs are accredited by CYQAA under WFME standards, and CYQAA itself holds WFME recognition status. The UNIC program is listed in the World Directory of Medical Schools (WDOMS) and was designed in line with EU Directive 2005/36/EC on professional qualifications.

EUC's MD is also accredited by CYQAA following WFME standards. EUC states that its school meets all requirements for ECFMG certification, the route US-bound graduates need.

What these facts do not settle is licensing in your home country. Neither university publishes a country-by-country list of approvals from bodies such as India's NMC or Pakistan's PMDC. Your home regulator sets its own rules, and they can change. Before you pay a deposit, check your regulator's current requirements for foreign medical graduates and keep a written copy.

For recognition of degrees inside Cyprus, the state body is KYSATS, the national ENIC-NARIC information center.

## How to apply and get the student visa

For a medical program, the visa part takes as long as admission itself. The key rule: the university, not the student, applies for your entry permit.

<!-- block: processBlock title="From application to arrival in Nicosia" -->
1. Take IELTS, TOEFL or PTE and collect school transcripts — the English score and 90% grade average come first.
2. Apply online and pay the application fee (€60 at UNIC, €200 at EUC) — attach the motivation letter and reference where required.
3. Attend the interview — online at UNIC, for shortlisted applicants at EUC.
4. Accept the offer and pay the required deposit — at EUC, non-EU students transfer €3,000 toward tuition before visa documents are sent.
5. Prepare visa documents — passport, police certificate, medical tests (HIV, syphilis, hepatitis B and C, chest X-ray), financial proof, bank guarantee; all officially translated and certified.
6. The university submits your entry permit application — the official processing time is 1 month with a complete file; you must apply from outside Cyprus, not on a tourist visa.
7. Arrive and register within 7 days — apply for a temporary residence permit and give biometrics.

<!-- block: textContent -->
Legalization depends on where your documents come from. India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Saudi Arabia, Oman and Bahrain use the apostille. Nigeria, Egypt, the UAE, Qatar and Kuwait are not part of the Apostille Convention, so their documents need consular legalization.

Timing matters too. EUC asks for visa documents at least 3 months before the semester starts. For the 2026/27 cycle its non-EU visa deadlines were 31 August for the October intake and 15 January for spring; EUC publishes the 2027/28 dates closer to the intake. UNIC does not list separate MD deadlines, so confirm the dates with the admissions office. The full visa process is on our [Cyprus student visa](/study-in-cyprus/cyprus-student-visa) page.

## What to know before you apply to study medicine in Cyprus

**This is the Republic of Cyprus, not North Cyprus.** UNIC and EUC are in Nicosia, in the EU-member Republic of Cyprus. Universities in the north of the island operate under a different system and different recognition rules. Check the country on every offer letter and payment request.

**Financial requirements depend on your nationality.** The official bank guarantee is €350 for citizens of Egypt, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman and Bahrain, among others. It is €850 for citizens of India, Pakistan, Bangladesh and Nigeria, among others. A university may provide a group guarantee instead. The official list sets no fixed bank balance, but universities do: UNIC wants a statement showing at least the first year's tuition, and EUC asks for at least €7,000 plus €2,000 in cash on arrival. You must also pay at least the first semester before entry.

**Public universities teach bachelor's programs mostly in Greek.** At the University of Cyprus, international bachelor's students first take a year of Greek to B2 level. That is why most international medical students in Cyprus choose the English-taught private schools. See [University of Cyprus](/study-in-cyprus/university-of-cyprus) and [Cyprus University of Technology](/study-in-cyprus/cyprus-university-of-technology) for their options.

**Work is limited and will not cover medical fees.** Students may work up to 20 hours a week during semesters and 38 hours in holidays, only in sectors approved by the Ministry of Labour and only after the labor office approves the contract. EUC tells its non-EU students they can work only 6 months after first entry. With a full-time MD schedule, plan to fund your studies without a job.

**Post-study stay is not automatic.** Graduates can stay up to 12 months to look for work, but only at master's level and above (EQF level 7). Ask the university in writing how your medical degree is classified.

**Cyprus is in the EU but not a full Schengen member.** A Cyprus student residence permit does not give free travel across the Schengen area. Plan clinical rotations abroad with this in mind.

**Nicosia is your budget base.** Both medical schools are in Nicosia. Coastal cities such as Limassol are generally more expensive to live in, so do not plan on commuting from there.

**Progress is checked every year.** The residence permit is issued for up to 1 year and renewed annually, with your transcript checked at each renewal. Failing years puts the permit at risk, not just your grades.

<!-- block: accordionBlock title="Frequently asked questions" -->
### Can I study medicine in Cyprus in English?
Yes. The University of Nicosia and European University Cyprus both teach a 6-year MD fully in English in Nicosia. You need IELTS 6.5 or an equivalent certificate and around a 90% school average with biology.

### How much does MBBS in Cyprus cost per year?
For 2026/27, MD tuition for non-EU students is €24,000 a year at UNIC and €23,000 a year at EUC. Add one-off fees, insurance and living costs; UNIC estimates at least €12,000 a year for living in Nicosia. Prices for 2027/28 are not yet published.

### Is a Cyprus medical degree recognized in India or Pakistan?
Both MD programs are accredited by CYQAA under WFME standards. Neither university publishes approval by specific regulators such as NMC or PMDC, so check your home regulator's current rules for foreign graduates before you apply.

### Can I take the USMLE route after graduating in Cyprus?
UNIC states that its MD meets the WFME condition ECFMG has applied since 2024. EUC states that its school meets all requirements for ECFMG certification. Certification itself depends on ECFMG's own exams and rules.

### Are there scholarships for medicine in Cyprus?
UNIC offers need-based aid of up to 20% for MD students, decided after admission and an interview. EUC excludes medicine, dentistry and veterinary medicine from its scholarships.

### Is there an entrance exam for medicine in Cyprus?
Neither university lists a separate entrance exam in its requirements. Selection is based on school grades, an English certificate and an interview; EUC also asks for a motivation letter and an academic reference.

### Can I work while studying medicine in Cyprus?
Students may work up to 20 hours a week in term and 38 in holidays, in approved sectors only, with a contract approved by the labor office. EUC says its non-EU students can start only 6 months after first entry. Do not count on work income to pay tuition.

### Does a Cyprus student visa let me travel in Europe?
Not automatically. Cyprus is an EU member but not yet a full Schengen member, so a Cyprus student residence permit does not work like a Schengen permit.
