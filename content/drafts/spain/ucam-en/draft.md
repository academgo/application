UCAM (Universidad Católica San Antonio de Murcia) is a private Catholic university founded in 1996, with its main campus in Murcia and further campuses in Cartagena, Madrid and Málaga. For international applicants it offers eight official bachelor's degrees taught in English, from business and psychology to dentistry and veterinary medicine, plus a range of English-taught master's programs. According to the university, it has more than 10,000 international students.

UCAM suits students who want an official Spanish degree in English at a private university, with fees for most English bachelor's programs around €7,350–7,950 a year. It is also a practical option for graduates looking for an English-taught master's in Spain.

It does not suit you if you want medicine in English: UCAM teaches medicine only in Spanish. Dentistry and veterinary medicine also require Spanish during the degree.

<!-- block: textContent -->
Tuition is only part of the first-year bill. On top of it, UCAM charges one-time and yearly fees:

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Fees on top of tuition (bachelor's)" -->
- Application fee: €150, non-refundable.
- Pre-registration (preinscripción): €390 for English programs, non-refundable.
- Seat reservation: €1,100 in Murcia and Cartagena, €1,500 in Madrid, €2,000 for medicine and dentistry. It is credited toward your tuition.
- File opening on first enrollment: €450.
- Student insurance: €96.12 per year.
- Master's programs: pre-registration €280 (English) and reservation €1,200.

<!-- block: textContent -->
UCAM does not list merit or country scholarships for international students. Its discounts are family-based (5% or 10%, depending on how many family members study at UCAM) or social, and they cannot be combined. Budget for the full fee.

## Admission requirements for international students
UCAM's English programs ask for a school certificate valid for university access in Spain, English at B2 and a standard document set. For most applicants from outside Europe, the hardest step is recognition of the school certificate.

<!-- block: processBlock title="How to apply to UCAM for 2027/28" -->
1. Choose the program and check the language — English B2 for most programs; dentistry adds Spanish B2 before year 3, veterinary needs Spanish B1 from the start. International Relations accepts any certificate on UCAM's list or an individual assessment.
2. Check your school curriculum — IB Diploma, European Baccalaureate and EU school certificates go through a UNEDasiss credential. Certificates from India, Pakistan, Nigeria, Egypt, Bangladesh and most other systems must be homologated to the Spanish título de Bachiller by the Ministry of Education. UNEDasiss does not do homologation.
3. Prepare documents — passport, school certificate and grades, motivation letter, CV and an English B2 certificate. For homologation you need grades for at least the last 3 years of school.
4. Certify and translate — India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Saudi Arabia, Oman and Bahrain use the apostille. Nigeria, Egypt, the UAE, Qatar and Kuwait need full legalization: Ministry of Education, then Foreign Ministry, then the Spanish consulate. All documents need an official translation.
5. File for homologation early — the application is online, the state fee is about €48, and the legal review period is 3 months from a complete file. Real processing often takes longer.
6. Apply and pay — application fee €150, then pre-registration €390 and the seat reservation. While homologation is pending, UCAM can give conditional admission, valid for 1 year. If the documents do not arrive in time, the admission is cancelled.
7. Timeline — UCAM usually accepts applications from the second half of March to the first half of October; exact 2027/28 dates are published closer to the intake. Your visa application must go in at least 2 months before classes start, so aim to have your admission letter by spring 2027.

## What to know before you apply
Several details catch UCAM applicants out. Plan for them early.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Honest limitations" -->
- No medicine in English. Medicine is Spanish-only, and admission uses two routes: the Spanish PAU exam with a score of at least 11.00 out of 14, or a transfer from another medical school with at least 30 ECTS recognized. If you want medicine, we compare English-taught options elsewhere.
- Dentistry is not fully in English. Clinical practice is in Spanish, and you need Spanish B2 before year 3. Language courses are included in Cartagena — start Spanish in your first semester anyway.
- Non-EU students pay more for some programs. Medicine costs about €18,900 for non-EU students against €14,200 for EU students; veterinary medicine costs €12,990 against €11,600.
- Fees are largely non-refundable. If you cancel more than 28 days before the start, the application fee, pre-registration and 20% of the reservation are kept. Within 28 days, all three are kept in full. After the program starts, there are no refunds.
- Homologation takes time. Start it as soon as you have final grades. Conditional admission covers you for 1 year only.
- Recognition at home depends on your country. If you plan to practice dentistry or psychology at home, check the local regulator's rules before you enroll.

## Rankings and accreditation
We only publish accreditation facts we have checked against official sources. UCAM was established by decree of the Bishop of Cartagena in 1996, and the Region of Murcia authorized its first programs by Decreto 32/1999. The English-taught bachelor's and master's programs are listed by UCAM as official degrees. According to the university, the English Dentistry degree is verified by ANECA, Spain's quality agency, and the Business Administration degree holds ACCA accreditation. We do not quote rankings here because we have not verified a source for them.

## Housing and student life
UCAM runs a housing portal, housing.ucam.edu, with residences and shared apartments in Murcia, Cartagena and Madrid. In Murcia, options include Residencial Los Jerónimos next to the main campus, Micampus and GURU. Residences with meals start from around €800 per month in 2026/27 listings; apartments are cheaper.

The university is known for sport: according to public sources, UCAM athletes won 11 of Spain's 17 medals at the Rio 2016 Olympics.

As a university student in Spain, you may work up to 30 hours a week without a separate permit, as long as work does not interfere with study. Work must be in the region where your permit was issued, which for UCAM Murcia is the Region of Murcia. Family members who join you cannot work. Read more on [studying and working in Spain](/study-in-spain/study-and-work-in-spain).

## Student visa
For a UCAM bachelor's degree, Spain issues a study permit (estancia por estudios) for the full length of the program. You apply at the Spanish consulate at least 2 months before classes start. The authorization is decided within 7 days, and the consulate then issues the visa within 1 month. After arrival you apply for your TIE card within 1 month.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Main visa requirements" -->
- Funds of 100% IPREM per month: €600, or €7,200 for a year (2026). Tuition is not part of this sum.
- Private health insurance with no co-payments, no waiting periods and no coverage limit.
- Police clearance from every country you lived in over the last 5 years, if you are 18 or older.
- A medical certificate for the visa application.
- A passport valid for at least 1 more year.

<!-- block: textContent -->
If your visa is refused, UCAM refunds the seat reservation (not the administrative fees), provided you applied before the program start with genuine documents and funds, and you have the refusal letter. You can also defer once, for up to 1 year, by asking in writing more than 28 days before the start. Full details are on our [Spain student visa](/study-in-spain/spain-student-visa) page.

<!-- block: accordionBlock title="Frequently asked questions" -->
### Does UCAM teach medicine in English?
No. Medicine at UCAM is taught only in Spanish. The English-taught health options are dentistry, which switches to Spanish for clinical practice, and bilingual veterinary medicine. If you need medicine in English, we can compare universities in other countries.

### How much does UCAM cost for international students?
On the current price list (2026/27), English bachelor's programs cost about €7,350–7,950 a year. Dentistry in Cartagena is about €15,200 and veterinary medicine about €12,990 for non-EU students. Add the €150 application fee, €390 pre-registration and the seat reservation.

### Is a UCAM degree official?
UCAM lists all its English-taught bachelor's and master's programs as official Spanish degrees. It also offers non-official diplomas, so check that your program is marked "Official".

### Do I need to homologate my school certificate?
Yes, if your certificate is from outside the EU and not from a country with a reciprocity agreement. Homologation to the Spanish título de Bachiller is done by the Ministry of Education, and UCAM can admit you conditionally for 1 year while it is processed.

### Is the reservation fee refunded if my visa is refused?
Yes, under conditions. UCAM refunds the reservation fee if your documents are genuine, you showed funds, you applied for the visa before the program start and you have the refusal letter. Administrative fees are not refunded.

### Can I stay in Spain to work after graduating from UCAM?
Graduates of official degrees can switch to a work permit without leaving Spain. Graduates at bachelor's level or above can also apply for a job-search permit of up to 24 months. Our [study and work in Spain](/study-in-spain/study-and-work-in-spain) guide explains the conditions.
