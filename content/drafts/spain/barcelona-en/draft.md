The University of Barcelona (Universitat de Barcelona, UB) is a public university in Catalonia, founded in 1450. It ranks =165 in the QS World University Rankings 2027 and =145 in the THE World University Rankings 2026 — first in Spain in both tables. THE counts 45,016 students, 12% of them international.

The headline number for applicants from outside the EU: a full bachelor's degree at UB costs the same as for local students, about €1,202 a year in 2026/27. Master's programs are priced differently for non-EU students, at €82 per credit for most programs.

UB suits students ready to study in Spanish and Catalan, and graduates targeting one of its English-taught master's. It is a poor fit if you need a fully English bachelor's or want to skip entrance exams.

## University of Barcelona tuition fees

Bachelor's prices at UB are the same for everyone on a full degree, including non-EU students. Master's prices depend on your status. The table uses the 2026/27 price list.

<!-- block: tableBlock -->
| Program | Non-EU price per credit | Approx. cost per year (60 credits) | Price list |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bachelor's (Grau), full degree | €17.69 | about €1,061 tuition + about €141 fees = about €1,202 | 2026/27 |
| Official master's, most programs | €82 | about €4,920 tuition + fees = about €5,060 | 2026/27 |
| Master's that qualify for a regulated profession (habilitant) | €71 | depends on program length | 2026/27 |
| Selected business and economics master's, e.g. International Business | €95–110 | about €6,740 at €110 per credit | UB list labeled 2025/26 |
| MSc in Economics (taught in English) | €82 | €4,920 tuition; optional intro courses €246 | 2026/27 |

<!-- block: textContent -->
The annual fees on top of tuition are €69.80 for file management, €70.00 for learning support and €1.12 for school insurance (students under 28). The €90 per credit rate applies only to Study Abroad and non-degree students, not to full degrees.

For master's pricing, a student stay permit (estancia por estudios) does not count as residence, so non-EU students pay the non-EU rate. The 2026/27 price decree came out in June 2026; 2027/28 prices are not published yet. We confirm the exact fee for your program before you apply.

## Programs and the degree you receive

UB offers bachelor's, master's and doctoral programs across a network of city campuses, including Diagonal, Mundet and Bellvitge (medicine and health sciences).

Most bachelor's programs are taught in Catalan and Spanish. The language of each subject is announced before the year starts. You can usually write assignments and exams in Catalan or Spanish.

English-taught options are limited and mostly at master's level. The MSc in Economics is taught entirely in English and asks for English at B2. At bachelor's level, English Studies and International Business may be options; check each program's language before you apply.

## Admission requirements for international students

For a bachelor's, you apply through Catalonia's central system; for a master's, directly through the UB portal. You submit the application yourself; we prepare and check everything with you first.

<!-- block: processBlock title="Bachelor's at UB: steps for 2027/28 entry" -->
1. Check whether you need homologation — IB Diploma holders and graduates of EU school systems enter without it. Graduates of other systems (India, Pakistan, Nigeria, Egypt, Bangladesh and others) must have their school certificate recognized as the Spanish título de Bachiller.
2. Apply for homologation with Spain's Ministry of Education — submit your certificate and grades for at least the last 3 years, with apostille or legalization and an official translation. By law the ministry decides within 3 months of a complete file, so start early.
3. Take the entrance exams — non-EU applicants sit either the PAU in Catalonia (Spanish language and literature, a foreign language, history or history of philosophy, one subject exam) or the PCE exams through UNEDasiss. There is no route based on the certificate alone.
4. Get your admission grade — in Catalonia it is 0.4 × your exam average + 0.6 × your school grade, plus up to 4 points from subject exams, for a maximum of 14. Through UNEDasiss, without PCE exams your score cannot exceed 6 out of 10.
5. Plan the exam calendar — UNEDasiss accreditation runs online from February 4 to December 1. The 2027 exam calendar is not out yet; based on 2026, expect registration in March–April 2027 and exams at the end of May 2027. The 2026 May–June exams took place in Spain.
6. Apply through Catalonia's preinscripció — in 2026 it ran June 2–26, with the first allocation on July 10. Exact 2027 dates are published closer to the intake. A September round covers only remaining places.

<!-- block: processBlock title="Master's at UB: steps for 2027/28 entry" -->
1. Choose your program and check its language requirement — English-taught programs usually ask for B2.
2. Watch the program's own deadlines — applications open from January, and each program sets its own periods. The MSc in Economics accepts 2027/28 applications from December 1, 2026 to June 8, 2027. UB advises non-EU applicants to use the first period to leave time for the visa.
3. Pay the fees — €30.21 per application; an application is not reviewed until it is paid. If your bachelor's is from outside the EU, UB checks its level for a fee of €218.15. You do not need to homologate your bachelor's degree for this.
4. Prepare certified documents — after admission, foreign degrees usually need apostille or legalization and a translation.
5. Receive the admission letter — UB's academic office issues it for your visa application.

<!-- block: textContent -->
Apostille or legalization depends on your country. India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Saudi Arabia, Oman and Bahrain use the apostille. Nigeria, Egypt, the UAE, Qatar and Kuwait are not in the Hague Convention, so documents go through the full legalization chain: your education ministry, your foreign ministry, then the Spanish consulate.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="What to know before you apply" -->
- **Language is the main barrier.** Catalonia's target for 2025/26 is 80% of bachelor's teaching hours in Catalan. UB generally recommends at least B1 in the language of your subjects. If you do not speak Spanish or Catalan, focus on the English-taught master's.
- Bachelor's entry requires exams and homologation. Plan a full year: homologation (3 months by law from a complete file), exam preparation and the June application. A missing stamp or translation delays everything, so we check documents before you file.
- Master's fees are not the local rate. At €82 per credit, most programs cost about €5,060 a year for non-EU students; some business programs cost more.
- We did not find UB's own scholarships for non-EU students on full degrees. Nigerian and Egyptian applicants can look at the MAEC-AECID África-Med Máster scholarship (30 places in 2026/27). If you hold it, you cannot later switch to a work permit on the post-study route.
- Barcelona is not cheap. Budget about €1,100–1,450 a month with a shared room.

## Rankings and accreditation

UB is a public university of Catalonia. In the QS World University Rankings 2027, published in June 2026, it ranks =165 and first in Spain, ahead of Complutense (199) and UAB (211). In QS 2026 it was 160th. In the THE World University Rankings 2026, UB ranks =145, also first in Spain.

## Housing and student life

UB runs the Col·legi Major Penyafort-Montserrat in the Diagonal area, next to the pharmacy faculty, with metro line L3 and tram nearby. It has 380 places in single and shared rooms, with full board (three meals a day) and cleaning, from October 1 to June 30. The 2025/26 price was about €10,400 for the academic year. Applications for 2026/27 opened in January 2026, so apply early in the year you start.

Most international students rent a room in a shared flat. A room in Barcelona costs about €600 a month on average. Living costs without rent are about €827 a month, and a monthly transport pass is about €22.80. With a shared room, plan on about €1,100–1,450 a month.

## Student visa

Non-EU students apply for a Spanish student visa at the consulate at least 2 months before classes start. Extranjería decides on the permit within 7 days, then the consulate issues the visa within 1 month. You need proof of funds of 100% of IPREM for each month: €600 a month, or €7,200 for a year, not counting tuition. You also need full private health insurance without copayments, waiting periods or coverage limits, a police certificate if you are 18+ and staying over 6 months, and a passport valid for at least 1 more year.

During a university program you can work up to 30 hours a week without a separate permit, within Catalonia. After graduation, bachelor's and master's graduates can apply for a job-search permit of up to 24 months. Full steps are on our [Spain student visa](/study-in-spain/spain-student-visa) page and our guide to [studying and working in Spain](/study-in-spain/study-and-work-in-spain).

### How much does UB cost for international students?
A full bachelor's degree costs €17.69 per credit for everyone, about €1,202 a year including fees (2026/27). Most master's programs cost non-EU students €82 per credit, about €5,060 a year. Some business programs cost €95–110 per credit.

### Can I study at UB in English?
Yes, but mainly at master's level. The MSc in Economics is taught entirely in English with a B2 requirement. Most bachelor's programs are taught in Catalan and Spanish, so check the language of each program before applying.

### Can I get into a UB bachelor's without entrance exams?
Not if your school certificate is from a non-EU system. You need homologation of your certificate and then either the PAU in Catalonia or the PCE exams through UNEDasiss. IB Diploma holders do not need homologation.

### Do I need to homologate my bachelor's degree for a UB master's?
No. UB checks the level of your non-EU degree and whether it gives access to a master's in your country, for a fee of €218.15. This check does not count as homologation for other purposes, such as regulated professions.

### When should I apply for a UB master's for 2027/28?
Applications open from January, and each program has its own periods. Some start earlier: the MSc in Economics accepts applications from December 1, 2026 to June 8, 2027. UB advises non-EU applicants to apply in the first period to leave time for the visa.

### Does UB offer scholarships for international students?
We did not find UB scholarships for non-EU students on full degrees. Nigerian and Egyptian applicants can apply for the MAEC-AECID África-Med Máster scholarship. For India, Pakistan, Bangladesh and the Gulf, we found no MAEC-AECID program in 2026/27.

### Can I work while studying at UB?
Yes. University students can work up to 30 hours a week without a separate permit, if work does not interfere with studies. Work is allowed within Catalonia, where your permit was issued.
