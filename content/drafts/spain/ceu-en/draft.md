CEU San Pablo University (Universidad CEU San Pablo, often shortened to USP-CEU) is a private Catholic university in Madrid, recognized by Spanish law in 1993. It teaches on two campuses: Moncloa in central Madrid and Montepríncipe in Boadilla del Monte, where the Medicine faculty is based. Its English-taught degrees include Dentistry, Pharmacy, Physiotherapy, Business and International Relations, and Medicine runs in a bilingual English–Spanish track.

It suits international applicants who want an official Spanish degree taught fully or partly in English and who can budget for private tuition of €9,300–23,100 a year (2026/27 price list). It suits future doctors only if they are ready to reach Spanish B2 before admission.

It is not the right fit if you need Medicine fully in English, or if your budget is closer to public university fees. For those cases, we compare other Spanish universities below.

The place reservation is paid once. The enrollment fee is charged every year and is €2,000 for double degrees. For Medicine students who started in 2026/27, the enrollment fee is €4,200 in year two and €3,300 in each later year. Tuition stays at €19,500 a year.

You can pay the year in 9 monthly installments by SEPA direct debit, or pay upfront and get a 3% discount. These are 2026/27 prices. Before paying for 2027/28, we check the current price list for your program.

Scholarships exist, but none are reserved for specific nationalities. Medicine offers 10 CEU Merit Academic Excellence scholarships for the 10 best admission scores. General CEU Merit awards range from €2,500 to €7,500, and we confirm with the university whether your case qualifies.

## Programs and the degree you receive

Every Grado on this page is an official Spanish bachelor's degree. Medicine is registered in the national RUCT register (code 2502164) and leads to the regulated profession of Médico. Dentistry also has an officially published study plan and leads to a regulated profession.

**Medicine is not taught fully in English.** The bilingual track, offered since 2026/27, mixes English and Spanish credits. Students train at partner hospitals such as the HM Hospitales group, Vithas Pardo-Aravaca and Hospital Beata María Ana, plus the university's own simulation hospital.

Dentistry in English lasts 5 years, with 1,300+ hours of clinical practice in 45 treatment units. Students see patients from year three.

Other English-taught degrees include Pharmacy, Physiotherapy, Business Administration & Management, International Relations & EU, Computer Engineering, Data Science & Engineering, and Digital Communication. Law and Biotechnology run in bilingual format.

Some programs add "International Dual Degrees" with partners such as Boston University. The university states these foreign certificates are not official degrees; your official qualification is the Spanish Grado.

## Admission requirements for international students

Most degrees follow one path: online application, tests, faculty interview, decision. Admission becomes final only after Spain recognizes your school certificate.

<!-- block: processBlock title="How to apply to Universidad CEU San Pablo" -->
1. Choose the program and language track. For bilingual Medicine, you need an English B2 certificate before the tests and Spanish B2 before admission.
2. Apply online and complete the steps in the applicant portal (Portal del Futuro Alumno).
3. Take the online tests. Everyone sits an English test. Depending on the program, there is also a Spanish, math, or biology and chemistry test.
4. Attend the interview with the faculty. For Dentistry, the score is 60% grade average and 40% competency test, plus the interview; manual dexterity matters.
5. Start the homologation of your school certificate to the Spanish Bachillerato. Students from India, Pakistan, Nigeria, Egypt and most non-EU systems need it. The law sets 3 months to decide once the file is complete.
6. Prepare documents: the official school certificate and grades for at least the last 3 years. Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, Saudi Arabia, Oman and Bahrain use an apostille. Nigeria, Egypt, the UAE, Qatar and Kuwait need legalization through the education ministry, the foreign ministry and the Spanish consulate.
7. Get an official translation. A sworn translator, a Spanish consulate abroad or an official translator abroad with a legalized signature are all accepted for homologation.
8. Pay the place reservation after your offer. For Medicine, you have 5 working days, or the place goes to the next person on the waiting list.

### Medicine: 2027/28 selection rules and dates

For Medicine, the university has already published the 2027/28 process. Your score is 60% average grade of the first year of Bachillerato (or its equivalent), 35% biology and chemistry test, and 5% English. The Spanish university entrance exam (PAU) does not count.

International applicants do not need the PAU. They need the homologation credential, plus an "Informe de Equivalencia" that converts their grades to the Spanish scale. The university refers applicants to an external service for this paid report.

You can take the test at Montepríncipe or online. For other programs, the university publishes 2027/28 deadlines closer to the intake, and we track them for you.

## What to know before you apply

- **Medicine needs Spanish.** There is no fully English Medicine degree. The bilingual track requires Spanish B2 before admission and at least 7 in the English part of the test. If you are starting Spanish from zero, plan a language year or look at Dentistry in English.
- Payments are hard to get back. The place reservation is non-refundable if you do not complete enrollment, except in cases covered by consumer law. The enrollment fee is not refunded. If you withdraw after paying the year, tuition is refunded by month, from the month after your written notice.
- Medicine applicants who miss the document deadline lose the place without a refund. Ask the university in writing what happens to your payments if your visa is refused.
- Homologation takes time. You can usually enroll conditionally meanwhile, but enrollment is cancelled if homologation is refused.
- "CEU" is a group of four separate universities. CEU Cardenal Herrera (Valencia), Abat Oliba CEU (Barcelona) and CEU Fernando III (Seville) have their own rules and fees.
- Madrid is not cheap. A student sharing a flat needs roughly €1,100–1,450 a month on top of tuition (2026 estimate).

We help you plan around each point: Spanish language timing, document legalization, homologation, and a backup list of 2–3 universities.

## Rankings and accreditation

Universidad CEU San Pablo is recognized as a private university by Ley 8/1993. Medicine is listed in the national RUCT register, and the Dentistry plan is published in the state gazette (BOE). This makes your degree official in Spain.

We do not quote ranking positions until we verify them at the source. Recognition at home, especially for Medicine and Dentistry, depends on your national medical or dental council; check it before applying.

## Housing and student life

Most first-year international students choose between the university residence and a shared flat. The Colegio Mayor Universitario San Pablo, founded in 1944, sits near Moncloa. It offers full board and single or double rooms with a private bathroom. Admission is by application form and interview; prices are not published on its website, so we request them for you.

A room in a shared Madrid flat costs around €550 a month (idealista, August 2026). Medicine and Dentistry are at Montepríncipe, outside the center, so factor in the commute.

The university also sells a €600 Hospitality Package, paid with the place reservation and non-refundable except in justified cases. It covers visa and NIE/TIE help, document recognition, A1–B1 Spanish courses, a bank account, a SIM card and housing search.

## Student visa

Non-EU students need a Spanish student visa (estancia por estudios). Apply at the consulate at least 2 months before classes start. Show at least €600 per month of stay (€7,200 a year), not counting tuition, plus health insurance without co-payments or coverage limits, a medical certificate and a police clearance. Your passport must be valid for at least 1 more year.

University students can work up to 30 hours a week within the Community of Madrid without a separate permit. Graduates can apply for a job-search residence permit for up to 24 months. The full process is on our [Spain student visa page](/study-in-spain/spain-student-visa).

### Can I study Medicine in English at CEU San Pablo?
Not fully. Medicine is offered in Spanish and in a bilingual English–Spanish track. The bilingual track requires an English B2 certificate, a score of at least 7 in the English part of the test and Spanish B2 before admission.

### How much does Universidad CEU San Pablo cost per year?
On the 2026/27 price list, tuition ranges from €9,300 (Physiotherapy in Spanish) to €23,100 (Dentistry in English). Medicine costs €19,500 a year. Add a one-time place reservation and a yearly enrollment fee, usually €950 and €1,500.

### Do I need the PAU to apply?
No. Applicants from non-EU school systems need homologation of their school certificate instead, and Medicine also requires an Informe de Equivalencia of your grades.

### When can I apply for 2027/28?
For Medicine, the first round runs September 9 – October 8, 2026, with the test on October 16, 2026. Two more rounds follow, the last closing April 1, 2027. For other programs, the university publishes dates closer to the intake.

### Is the place reservation refundable?
Generally no. It is non-refundable if you do not complete enrollment, except in cases covered by consumer law, and the enrollment fee is not refunded either. Ask the university in writing about visa refusal before you pay.

### Are there scholarships for international students?
There are no scholarships reserved for specific countries. Medicine applicants compete for 10 academic excellence scholarships based on admission scores. We check with the university whether other CEU Merit awards apply to you.

### Can I work while studying at CEU San Pablo?
Yes. University students can work up to 30 hours a week within the Community of Madrid without a separate permit, as long as work does not interfere with studies.
