Universidad Europea is a private university network in Spain, with campuses in Madrid, Valencia, Alicante, Málaga and Tenerife. The Madrid university was recognized by Spanish law in 1995, and today the group teaches dozens of bachelor's and master's programs in English. Official fees for 2026/27 run from €6,240 to €24,120 a year, plus one-time reservation and enrollment fees.

It suits you if you want an English-taught official degree in business, engineering, psychology, physiotherapy or dentistry and can cover private-university fees. It does not suit you if you want medicine in English (medicine here is taught only in Spanish) or the lowest possible tuition.

## Programs and the degree you receive

The network is made up of separate universities, each issuing degrees in its own name. Madrid programs lead to a degree from Universidad Europea de Madrid; Valencia and Alicante programs, from Universidad Europea de Valencia.

There are two kinds of qualifications. Official degrees (Grado, Máster Universitario) are listed in RUCT, Spain's national register of programs. Private degrees (títulos propios) appear in the price list as "Private Master's Degree", Postgraduate Diploma or Specialization. An official degree is the safer choice for recognition at home, though a título propio still qualifies you for a student stay permit. The Foundation Year is not an official degree.

English-taught bachelor's programs in Madrid include business, economics, marketing, international relations, architecture, aerospace, computer and biomedical engineering, cybersecurity, biotechnology, physiotherapy, psychology, sport sciences, design and videogames. Valencia teaches psychology, business, marketing, international relations and tourism in English.

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Health programs: what the official records say" -->
- Medicine (Madrid): official Grado, RUCT code 2500083, 6 years, 360 ECTS, 200 places, taught in Spanish only.
- Dentistry (Madrid): RUCT code 2500917, 5 years, 300 ECTS, 280 places, classes in English or Spanish. Clinical practice in years 4 and 5 is in Spanish.
- Dentistry (Valencia and Alicante): RUCT code 2502867, 5 years, 300 ECTS, 200 places in Valencia and 80 in Alicante. B2 level (CEFR) is required if you study in a language that is not your native one.

## Admission requirements for international students

Every applicant takes the university's own tests, and most non-EU school graduates also need their certificate recognized in Spain.

<!-- block: processBlock title="How to apply to Universidad Europea from abroad" -->
1. Check which route your school system takes. IB, European Baccalaureate and EU-school graduates apply through UNEDasiss accreditation. Graduates from India, Pakistan, Nigeria, Egypt, Bangladesh and most other countries need homologation of their certificate to the Spanish Bachiller by the Ministry of Education.
2. Start the homologation early. You submit your certificate and grades for at least the last 3 years. By law the ministry decides within 3 months of a complete file; in practice it can take longer. Meanwhile the receipt (volante) lets you enroll provisionally, but a refusal can cancel the enrollment.
3. Certify your documents. India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Saudi Arabia, Oman and Bahrain use the apostille. Nigeria, Egypt, the UAE, Qatar and Kuwait need legalization: education ministry, foreign ministry, then the Spanish consulate. Add an official translation, and allow several weeks.
4. Take the university tests (€150, non-refundable). A 30-minute competency test, an English test and a motivation interview or letter; Spanish-taught programs add a Spanish test. Homologation applicants without UNEDasiss also sit a General Knowledge test (minimum 5 out of 10).
5. Meet the program's selection rules. In Dentistry, your grade average weighs 80% and the test or interview 20%. Medicine requires a science track and B2 Spanish on the university's test.
6. Reserve your seat. Pay the reservation fee within 7 days of the decision (Medicine: 5 working days).
7. Plan the 2027 timeline. Admission is rolling and 2027/28 dates are not announced. The visa application goes in at least 2 months before classes, so file homologation well before spring 2027.

## What to know before you apply

<!-- block: bulletsBlock title="Honest limits and how to handle them" -->
- No medicine in English. Some websites advertise it, but the program does not exist. Reach Spanish B2 first or consider English-taught dentistry.
- Dentistry in English still needs Spanish. Clinical years 4 and 5 are in Spanish, so start lessons in year 1.
- Year one costs more than tuition. In Madrid the test, reservation and file fees add €2,050–4,550.
- No scholarships aimed at non-EU students. Discounts exist (15% for alumni, 5% if a relative studies there), plus education loans.
- Before paying the reservation fee, ask admissions in writing what happens if your visa is refused.
- The Foundation Year is not a degree; check with us whether it fits your visa plan.
- Public universities cost less: at Complutense, non-EU students pay about €6,823–8,186 a year (2026/27).

## Housing and student life

The Villaviciosa campus has its own residence, XIOR Picasso-Velázquez, with 545 places, meals, cleaning and sports complex access. The last published prices (2023/24) started at about €1,270 a month for a shared room; confirm the current rate.

A room in a shared flat costs around €550 a month in Madrid. Budget roughly €1,100–1,450 a month in Madrid and €950–1,200 in Valencia. According to the university, about 40% of its students are international, from more than 110 countries.

## Student visa

You apply at the Spanish consulate at least 2 months before classes. The permit decision takes 7 days, then the consulate issues the visa within 1 month. Key requirements: €600 per month for living costs (€7,200 a year, tuition excluded), health insurance without co-payments or waiting periods, a passport valid 1 more year, a medical certificate and, for adults, criminal record certificates for the last 5 years.

Students can work up to 30 hours a week without a separate permit, within the region that issued it. Graduates of official degrees can apply for a job-search permit of up to 24 months. Full details are on our [Spain student visa page](/study-in-spain/spain-student-visa).

<!-- block: accordionBlock title="Frequently asked questions" -->
### Does Universidad Europea teach medicine in English?
No. Medicine in Madrid is taught only in Spanish, and applicants must reach B2 Spanish on the university's own test. English-taught dentistry is available in Madrid, Valencia, Alicante and Málaga, but clinical practice in years 4 and 5 is in Spanish.

### How much does it cost for international students?
Bachelor's tuition runs from €6,240 to €24,120 a year under the 2026/27 price list. In the first year you also pay a €150 test fee, a reservation fee of €1,100–3,600 and a one-time enrollment file fee of €550–800. Madrid charges EU and non-EU students the same.

### Do I need IELTS or TOEFL?
The university runs its own English test covering reading, grammar, vocabulary and listening for applicants from non-English-speaking countries. The minimum level is not published for most programs. We check the current requirement for your program before you apply.

### Is the degree official?
Grado and Máster Universitario programs are official Spanish degrees registered in RUCT. Programs marked as private degrees, and the Foundation Year, are not official degrees. Recognition in your home country follows your own government's rules, so check them before you choose.

### When should I apply for September 2027?
Admission is rolling, and the university has not announced 2027/28 dates yet. Since document certification and homologation take months, start in late 2026 or early 2027. The visa application must go in at least 2 months before classes begin.

### Can I work while studying?
Yes, up to 30 hours a week without a separate permit, as long as work does not interfere with studies. You can only work in the region that issued your permit.
